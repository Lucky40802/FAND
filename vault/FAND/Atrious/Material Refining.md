# Material Refining

Refining combines several lower-grade materials into one of a higher grade, so low-level loot keeps its value as the party grows. Grades are explained in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

## Within a realm
Combine **3 materials of the same type and grade** (for example, three Human 4 Ore / Metal) into **1 material one grade higher** (Human 5) of the same realm and type.

- **Who can refine:** the profession that works that material type (see Material Types in Material Grading). [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavengers]] can refine organic types (hide, bone, blood, essence), at disadvantage.
- **Check:** d20 + profession crafting bonus vs the design DC for the *result's* grade (see the table in [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]).
- **Time:** 1 day per grade of the result.
- **Failure:** one input is lost. A natural 1 ruins them all.

## Crossing into the next realm
Grade 10 of a realm can be refined into grade 1 of the next realm up, but it's harder:

- **5 materials** of grade 10 and the same type, not 3;
- a **binding agent** from the destination realm (any Essence, Blood, or Crystal of that realm);
- a **Realm Forge** of the destination realm, or the home realm of a god whose contract you hold at Apostle rank or higher;
- the check is at disadvantage.

For example, five Human 10 Adamantine plus a Mysterious binding agent could become one Mysterious 1 metal.

## Limits
- **Primordial** materials can't be made by refining. They're only found.
- **Supplies** can't be refined.
- Inputs must share a **type**. Mixed types can't be combined.
- The result's **name and property** are the refiner's choice from among the inputs. A refined material is a new named material; give it a name and write it into the [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]] and the materials database if you want to reuse it.

## Why bother
| From | To | Materials needed |
|---|---|---|
| Human 1 | Human 5 | 81 |
| Human 7 | Human 10 | 27 |
| Human 10 | Mysterious 1 | 5 plus a binding agent |

Refining is cheaper than it looks for mid-grade materials and punishing for low ones. Past the first two realms, it's usually faster to travel and gather (see [[FAND/Atrious/Realm Travel|Realm Travel]]).
