# Primordial Gathering

Ores, stone, crystals, plants, and other materials gathered in the [[Primordial Realm]] (tier 10). Anyone can try to gather; characters with the [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] profession gather more and better, while parts taken from corpses (hide, bone, blood, organs, essences) come from [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] and are listed in the Bestiaries. The material type decides which profession crafts with it (see [[FAND/Atrious/Master Professions|Master Professions]]).
