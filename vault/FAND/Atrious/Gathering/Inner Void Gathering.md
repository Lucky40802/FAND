# Inner Void Gathering

Ores, stone, crystals, plants, and other materials gathered in the [[Inner Void]] (tier 9). Anyone can try to gather; characters with the [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] profession gather more and better, while parts taken from corpses (hide, bone, blood, organs, essences) come from [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] and are listed in the Bestiaries. The material type decides which profession crafts with it (see [[FAND/Atrious/Master Professions|Master Professions]]).

| Hollow Ore | Inner Void 7 | 44 | Ore / Metal | Nullifying | Deep Inner Void | Harvesting | Metal that is mostly nothing |
