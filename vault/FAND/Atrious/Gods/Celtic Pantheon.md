# Celtic Pantheon

## Morrigan - Goddess of Fate
Goddess of war, fate, and death, often appearing as a crow over battlefields to claim the fallen.

## Cernunnos - God of Wild Things
Horned god of wild animals, the forest, and fertility.

## Brigid - Goddess of Healing
Goddess of healing, poetry, and smithcraft, later syncretized into the Christian Saint Brigid.

## Dagda - Father of the Celtic Gods
Father-figure of the Tuatha Dé Danann, god of abundance, wisdom, and druidry, owner of a cauldron that never runs empty.

## Lugh - God of Craftsmanship
God of skill and craftsmanship, master of every trade at once, associated with the harvest festival Lughnasadh.
