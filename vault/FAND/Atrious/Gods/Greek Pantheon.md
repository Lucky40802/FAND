# Greek Pantheon

## Zeus - King of the Olympian Gods
Ruler of Olympus and god of sky and thunder, who overthrew his father Cronus to lead the second generation of gods.

## Hera - Queen of the Gods
Zeus's wife and queen of Olympus, goddess of marriage and family, often defined in myth by her jealousy of Zeus's affairs.

## Poseidon - God of the Sea
Brother of Zeus and god of the sea, earthquakes, and horses, quick to anger and slow to forgive a slight.

## Demeter - Goddess of the Harvest
Goddess of agriculture and the harvest, whose grief over her daughter Persephone's abduction is said to have created the seasons.

## Athena - Goddess of Wisdom
Goddess of wisdom and strategic warfare, born fully armored from Zeus's head.

## Apollo - God of Prophecy and Music
God of the sun, music, prophecy, and healing, twin brother of Artemis.

## Artemis - Goddess of the Hunt
Goddess of the hunt, wilderness, and the moon, fiercely protective of her independence.

## Aphrodite - Goddess of Love and Beauty
Goddess of love, beauty, and desire, born from sea foam.

## Hephaestus - God of the Forge
God of fire, blacksmiths, and craftsmen, the only Olympian associated with a physical disability, honored for his skill regardless.

## Hermes - Messenger of the Gods
Messenger god of travelers, trade, and thieves, known for speed and cunning.

## Dionysus - God of Wine and Revelry
God of wine, festivity, and religious ecstasy, capable of both joyous celebration and destructive madness.

## Persephone - Queen of Spring
Demeter's daughter, abducted by Hades to become queen of the underworld, splitting her year between the two realms and bringing spring when she returns.

## Hecate - Goddess of Magic
Goddess of magic, witchcraft, and crossroads, associated with the night and the moon.

## Nyx - Goddess of Night
Primordial goddess of night, powerful enough that even Zeus is said to fear her.

## Hypnos - God of Sleep
God of sleep, twin brother of Thanatos (death), living in a cave where the sun never reaches.

## Nemesis - Goddess of Balance
Goddess of retribution, who brings down those who succumb to hubris or take more than their share of fortune.

## Eos - Goddess of the Dawn
Goddess of the dawn, who rises each morning from the edge of the world to announce the sun's arrival.

## Selene - Goddess of the Moon
Goddess who personifies the moon itself, driving her chariot across the night sky.

## Helios - God of the Sun
God who personifies the sun, driving a chariot of fire across the sky from east to west each day.

## Tyche - Goddess of Fortune
Goddess of luck and fortune, whose favor determines the rise and fall of cities as much as individuals.
