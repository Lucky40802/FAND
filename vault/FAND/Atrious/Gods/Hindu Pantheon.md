# Hindu Pantheon

## Brahma - The Creator God
The creator god of the Hindu trimurti, who brought the universe into being at the start of each cosmic cycle.

## Vishnu - The Preserver
The preserver god of the trimurti, who maintains cosmic order and descends to earth in avatars whenever balance is threatened.

## Shiva - The Transformer
The transformer and destroyer god of the trimurti, whose destruction clears the way for renewal.

## Ganesha - God of Beginnings
Elephant-headed god of new beginnings, wisdom, and the remover of obstacles.

## Lakshmi - Goddess of Fortune
Goddess of wealth, fortune, and prosperity, consort of Vishnu.

## Saraswati - Goddess of the Arts
Goddess of knowledge, music, and the arts, consort of Brahma.

## Indra - God of Thunder
King of the Devas and god of thunder, storms, and war.

## Varuna - God of Cosmic Order
God of the sky, oceans, and cosmic law, keeper of rita, the order underlying the universe.

## Surya - God of the Sun
The sun god, who rides a chariot pulled across the sky by seven horses.

## Parvati - Goddess of Devotion
Goddess of love, devotion, and fertility, consort of Shiva and mother of Ganesha.
