# Norse Pantheon

## Odin - Allfather of the Norse Gods
Allfather of the Aesir, god of wisdom, war, and death, who traded an eye for knowledge at Mimir's well.

## Thor - God of Thunder
God of thunder and protector of Asgard, wielder of the hammer Mjolnir.

## Freya - Goddess of Love
Goddess of love, fertility, and war, who claims half the warriors who die in battle for her own hall.

## Tyr - God of Justice
God of law and heroic glory, who sacrificed his hand to bind the wolf Fenrir.

## Baldur - God of Light
The most beloved of the Aesir, god of light and purity, whose death was engineered by Loki.

## Mimir - God of Wisdom
A being renowned for wisdom, whose severed head Odin kept and consulted for counsel.

## Skadi - Goddess of Winter
Giantess and goddess of winter, skiing, and the hunt, who married into the Aesir after a failed vengeance quest.

## Njord - God of the Sea
God of the sea, seafaring, and wealth, father of Freyr and Freya.

## Idun - Goddess of Youth
Keeper of the golden apples that keep the Aesir eternally young.

## Forseti - God of Reconciliation
God of justice and reconciliation, said to settle disputes so fairly that all parties leave satisfied.
