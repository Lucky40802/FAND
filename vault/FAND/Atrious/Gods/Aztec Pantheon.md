# Aztec Pantheon

## Quetzalcoatl - The Feathered Serpent
The feathered serpent god of wind, learning, and civilization, one of the central deities of Mesoamerican mythology.

## Tlaloc - God of Rain
God of rain, fertility, and water, both a giver of life-sustaining rain and a bringer of storms and drought.

## Xipe Totec - God of Renewal
God of spring, agriculture, and renewal, whose myths involve shedding an old skin for a new one.

## Chalchiuhtlicue - Goddess of Water
Goddess of lakes, rivers, and running water, consort of Tlaloc.

## Tonatiuh - God of the Warrior Spirit
The sun god who demanded sacrifice to keep the sun moving across the sky, and patron of warriors.
