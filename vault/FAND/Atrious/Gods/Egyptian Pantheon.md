# Egyptian Pantheon

## Ra - God of the Sun
Sun god and king of the gods, who sails across the sky by day and through the underworld by night.

## Osiris - God of Rebirth
God of the afterlife and resurrection, murdered and dismembered by his brother Set before being restored to rule the underworld.

## Isis - Goddess of Magic
Goddess of magic and motherhood, who reassembled Osiris's body and protected their son Horus.

## Horus - God of the Sky
Falcon-headed sky god, avenger of his father Osiris and patron of kingship.

## Thoth - God of Knowledge
Ibis-headed god of writing, knowledge, and the moon, credited with inventing language.

## Bastet - Goddess of Protection
Cat goddess of protection, home, and fertility.

## Hathor - Goddess of Music
Goddess of music, love, and joy, often depicted as a cow or a woman with cow's horns cradling the sun.

## Sobek - God of the Nile
Crocodile-headed god of the Nile, associated with strength, fertility, and military prowess.

## Khnum - God of Creation
Ram-headed god said to have shaped humankind from clay on a potter's wheel.

## Neith - Goddess of Weaving
Goddess of weaving, hunting, and war, sometimes credited as a creator deity who wove the world into being.
