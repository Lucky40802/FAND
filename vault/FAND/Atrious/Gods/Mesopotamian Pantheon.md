# Mesopotamian Pantheon

## Enki - God of Wisdom
God of wisdom, water, and creation in Sumerian mythology, patron of crafts and civilization.

## Enlil - God of the Wind
God of wind, air, and storms, and chief of the Sumerian pantheon for much of its history.

## Inanna - Goddess of Love
Goddess of love, beauty, and war, whose descent into the underworld is one of the oldest recorded myths.

## Anu - God of the Sky
The sky god and, in the earliest Sumerian tradition, the supreme deity from whom other gods derived their authority.

## Utu - God of Justice
Sun god and god of justice, who judges the living and the dead by the light he casts on all things.
