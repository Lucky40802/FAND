# Japanese Pantheon

## Amaterasu - Goddess of the Sun
The sun goddess and most revered deity in Shinto, said to be the ancestor of the Japanese imperial line.

## Susanoo - God of Storms
God of storms and the sea, Amaterasu's brother, known for both destructive rages and heroic deeds.

## Tsukuyomi - God of the Moon
God of the moon, brother of Amaterasu, whose falling-out with her is said to explain why day and night are separate.

## Izanagi - God of Creation
Primordial god who, with his sister-wife Izanami, created the islands of Japan and the first gods.

## Inari - God of Prosperity
Deity of rice, fertility, and prosperity, often depicted with fox messengers called kitsune.
