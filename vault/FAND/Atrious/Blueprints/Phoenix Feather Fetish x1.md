# Phoenix Feather Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Very Rare*

A fetish incorporating Phoenix Feather (Creature Harvest). A feather voluntarily shed by a phoenix. The feather glows with warm light. Items incorporating phoenix feathers grant the bearer resistance to fire damage. Once — when the bearer would die, the phoenix feather activates, restoring them to 1 HP. Single use. Bonus: Resurrection ward.
