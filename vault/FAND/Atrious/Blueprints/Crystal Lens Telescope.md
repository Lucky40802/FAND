# Crystal Lens Telescope

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Common*

A folding telescope with crystal lenses ground to magnify 10x. Objects 1000ft away appear as if at 100ft. Used by scouts, navigators, and snipers. The crystal lenses resist fogging that would obscure glass equivalents.
