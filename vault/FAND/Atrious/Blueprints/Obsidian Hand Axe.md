# Obsidian Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Obsidian head creates an edge sharp enough to part silk but requires careful use. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Obsidian Greataxe|Obsidian Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Obsidian): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
