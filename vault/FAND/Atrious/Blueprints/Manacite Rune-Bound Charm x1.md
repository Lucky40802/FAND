# Manacite Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Manacite (Arcane Material). A crystal that forms in areas of intense magical activity. It absorbs ambient magical energy constantly. Once per day, touching a manacite object allows a spellcaster to recover one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower. The crystal dims after use and recharges over 24 hours. Bonus: +1 spell slot recovery.
