# Heat Rune

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a metal plate that generates mild warmth when touched. Used to warm beds, keep food warm, and prevent pipes from freezing. Generates heat equivalent to a candle. Cannot start fires.
