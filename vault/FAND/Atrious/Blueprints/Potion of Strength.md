# Potion of Strength

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A fizzing red liquid. Grants +4 to Strength (to a maximum of 22) for 1 hour. The veins of the drinker are briefly visible through the skin.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Fortitude|Potion of Fortitude]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
