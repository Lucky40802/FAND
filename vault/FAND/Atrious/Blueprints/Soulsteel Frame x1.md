# Soulsteel Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Very Rare*

A frame incorporating Soulsteel (Metal). A metal created by compressing soul energy into a physical medium. Grey and slightly translucent. Weapons made from soulsteel deal necrotic damage and can directly damage the soul rather than the body — effective against creatures that are incorporeal or immune to physical damage. Bonus: +2 Necrotic.
