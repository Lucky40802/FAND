# Voidmetal Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Very Rare*

A slab incorporating Voidmetal (Metal). A metal that should not exist. Voidmetal appears black and slightly absorbs light. It cuts through magical barriers and force fields as easily as physical matter. Difficult to work — the metal itself resists being shaped. Bonus: +3 Force.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidmetal): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
