# Spring Steel Trap

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A jaw trap with a spring mechanism and trip plate. Sets with a foot lever. Effective for medium animals and unobservant humanoids.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Forged Steel Spurs|Forged Steel Spurs]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Anvil|Steel Anvil]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Manacles|Steel Manacles]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
