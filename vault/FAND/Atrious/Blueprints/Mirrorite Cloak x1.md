# Mirrorite Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Mirrorite (Arcane Material). A silver metal that perfectly mirrors its surroundings. So reflective that divination magic finds nothing there — it shows only the surroundings. Armour from mirrorite has a 20% chance of reflecting a spell back at its caster. Weapons of mirrorite deal normal damage to targets that rely on sight. Bonus: Spell reflection chance.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Mirrorite): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
