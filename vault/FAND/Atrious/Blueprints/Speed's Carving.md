# Speed's Carving

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Legendary*

This carving only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Speed]] directly, bound into a core of Voidsap Wood (+1 Force). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidsap Wood): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
