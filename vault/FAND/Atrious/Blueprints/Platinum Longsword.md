# Platinum Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Platinum blade conducts magical energy without loss.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Platinum Greatsword|Platinum Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Platinum Shortsword|Platinum Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Platinum): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
