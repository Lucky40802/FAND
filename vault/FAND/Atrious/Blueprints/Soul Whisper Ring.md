# Soul Whisper Ring

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A simple silver ring that allows the wearer to hear the last words of a corpse once per day. The words are exactly what the creature said in their final moment — no interpretation, no context. Just the words.
