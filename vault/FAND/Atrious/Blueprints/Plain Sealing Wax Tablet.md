# Plain Sealing Wax Tablet

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A plain tablet built from sealing wax, taught early in Glyph Inscription training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Plain Sealing Wax Vessel|Plain Sealing Wax Vessel]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/True North Glyph|True North Glyph]].
