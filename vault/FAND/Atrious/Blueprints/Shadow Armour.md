# Shadow Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Uncommon*

A full suit of armour using shadowsteel plates and veilcloth backing. Equivalent to medium armour in protection. In dim light or darkness, the wearer is invisible — not merely concealed but genuinely invisible.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials, AC) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Doublet|Shadow Doublet]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
