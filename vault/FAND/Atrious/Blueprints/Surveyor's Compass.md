# Surveyor's Compass

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A brass compass with a bubble level and a folding sight arm for taking bearings. More accurate than a standard compass. The casing is waterproof. Used by explorers, military engineers, and cartographers.
