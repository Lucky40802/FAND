# Steel Flail

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A steel ball on a chain attached to a short haft. The chain allows the ball to swing around parries and over shields. Requires significant training — the momentum of the chain is difficult to redirect mid-swing.
