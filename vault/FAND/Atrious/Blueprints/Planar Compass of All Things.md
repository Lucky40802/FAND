# Planar Compass of All Things

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Legendary*

A compass with 44 needles, one for each known plane and several for planes not yet named. The navigator can select any plane and be shown the most direct route to it.
