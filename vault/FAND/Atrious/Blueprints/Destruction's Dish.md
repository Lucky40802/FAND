# Destruction's Dish

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Legendary*

This dish only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Destruction]] directly, bound into a core of Liquid Fire (+2d6 Fire). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
