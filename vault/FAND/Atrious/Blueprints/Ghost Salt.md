# Ghost Salt

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

Salt mixed with ground ghost-bone and silver dust. Sprinkled across a doorway or threshold, it prevents incorporeal undead from crossing for 8 hours. A full circle of ghost salt prevents entry from all directions…
