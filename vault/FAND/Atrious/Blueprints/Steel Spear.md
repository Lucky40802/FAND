# Steel Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Steel tip holds a fine edge through extended combat. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Lance Head|Steel Lance Head]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Quarterstaff|Steel Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
