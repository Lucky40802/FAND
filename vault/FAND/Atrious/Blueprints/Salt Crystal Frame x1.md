# Salt Crystal Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Rare*

A frame incorporating Salt Crystal (Common Material). Common salt, but sufficient concentration has spiritual significance. Line of salt crystals prevents spirits of CR 2 or lower from crossing. Applied to wounds, it causes an additional 1d4 damage but prevents infection. Bonus: Spirit ward.
