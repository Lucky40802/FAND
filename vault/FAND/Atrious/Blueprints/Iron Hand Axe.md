# Iron Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Iron head is reliable and widely repairable but rusts without care. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Greataxe|Iron Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Iron): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
