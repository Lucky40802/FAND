# Null Powder Draught x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A draught incorporating Null Powder (Alchemical). A grey powder produced by grinding void nullstone. When applied to an object, it suppresses magical auras for 1 hour. Widely used by thieves to evade Detect Magic. Weapons coated in null powder deal non-magical damage regardless of what they're made of — useful against creatures that can only be har Bonus: Magic suppression. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.
