# Abyssal Bronze Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Abyssal Bronze head weeps acid and leaves wounds that resist magical healing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Abyssal Bronze Mace|Abyssal Bronze Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
