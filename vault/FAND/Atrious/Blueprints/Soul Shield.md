# Soul Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A tower shield with a soulsteel face. Provides +3 AC. Once per long rest, the wielder can summon the spirit of a previous shield-bearer to take one attack for them.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
