# Shadowsteel Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A standard shield. The Shadowsteel face is silent and deals shadow damage, partially incorporealising wounds.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
