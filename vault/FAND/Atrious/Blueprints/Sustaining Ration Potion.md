# Sustaining Ration Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A thick brown liquid. Provides full nutritional sustenance for one day when consumed. No food or water required after drinking. Tastes like slightly sweet porridge. Not pleasant but effective for long expeditions.
