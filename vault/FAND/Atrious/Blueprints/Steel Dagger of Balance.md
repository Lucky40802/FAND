# Steel Dagger of Balance

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A throwing dagger balanced so precisely that it can be thrown from blade or handle equally well. The steel is folded four times for a fine, hard edge. Beloved by assassins and competitive throwers.
