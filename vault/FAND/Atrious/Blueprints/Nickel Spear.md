# Nickel Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Nickel tip is corrosion-resistant and slightly magnetic. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Nickel Quarterstaff|Nickel Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
