# Black Dragon Scale Balisong

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Balisong reforged with Black Dragon Scale (Dragon Material) worked into its construction. Scales from a black dragon. Armour provides resistance to acid. Weapons deal +1d6 acid damage. Objects struck by black dragon scale weapons corrode — non-magical armour loses 1 AC permanently on a critical hit. Bonus: +1d6 Acid.

## Material Property
**Corrosive** (Potency 1, from Black Dragon Scale): Weapon: +1d6 acid damage, and a hit lowers the target's Gear AC by 1 until repaired. Armor: acid resistance.
