# Necrotic Plate

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

Full plate with soulsteel rivets and a wraithbone backing. The wearer is immune to the Frightened condition and has advantage on saves against spells. Undead treat the wearer as neutral unless attacked.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
