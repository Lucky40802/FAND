# Stormglass Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Stormglass head crackles with static charge and discharges lightning. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Stormglass Greataxe|Stormglass Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Stormcharged** (Potency 3, from Stormglass): Weapon: +3d6 lightning or thunder damage. Armor: lightning resistance.
