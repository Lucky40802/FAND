# Mithral Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Mithral head is lighter than steel with superior edge retention and perfect enchantment acceptance. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mithral Greataxe|Mithral Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Mithral): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
