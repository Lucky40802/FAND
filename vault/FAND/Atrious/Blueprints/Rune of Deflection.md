# Rune of Deflection

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A rune carved into armour that causes physical projectiles to deflect slightly. The wearer gains +1 AC specifically against ranged weapon attacks. The rune does not affect magical projectiles like magic missiles.
