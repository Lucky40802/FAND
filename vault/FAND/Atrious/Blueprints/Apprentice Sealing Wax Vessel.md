*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Apprentice Sealing Wax Tablet|Apprentice Sealing Wax Tablet]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Scrying Block Glyph|Scrying Block Glyph]].
