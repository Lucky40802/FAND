# Star Altitude Measurer

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Common*

An unusual artifact, built for reading the stars and the space between them using Meridian Glass. Made to a standard rather than to impress.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Emergency Beacon - Material Plane|Emergency Beacon - Material Plane]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Simple Meridian Glass Compass|Simple Meridian Glass Compass]].
