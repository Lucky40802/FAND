# Deepstone Mace

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A flanged one-handed bludgeon. The Deepstone flanges is twice as dense as granite and delivers crushing force that bypasses hardness.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Deepstone Warhammer|Deepstone Warhammer]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Deepstone): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
