# The Red Sun

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Legendary*

An orb of pure blood crystal set in adamantine. Once per day, it releases a pulse of blood-fire in a 100ft radius. Every living creature in the radius takes 6d8 necrotic damage as their blood heats and burns from inside.
