# Runic Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A shield with abjuration runes across its face. Adds +1 to AC beyond a normal shield. Once per day, the runes can negate a spell of 3rd level or lower.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Abjuration Ward Stone): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
