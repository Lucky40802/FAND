# Amber Draught II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Very Rare*

A draught incorporating Amber (Common Material). Ancient fossilised tree resin. Creatures fully submerged in amber are perfectly preserved — suspended animation with no aging. Items made from amber are beautiful and valuable. Amber can sometimes preserve magical properties of items embedded within it. Bonus: Creature preservation.
