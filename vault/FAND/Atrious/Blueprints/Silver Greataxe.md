# Silver Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Silver construction burns supernatural creatures that rely on regeneration.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Hand Axe|Silver Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Silver): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
