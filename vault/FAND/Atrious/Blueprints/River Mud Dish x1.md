# River Mud Dish x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Very Rare*

A dish incorporating River Mud (Common Material). Ordinary river mud. Applied to skin it provides camouflage (+2 Stealth in natural terrain) and masks scent (disadvantage on scent-based tracking for 4 hours until it dries). Essential wilderness survival material. Bonus: +0. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/River Mud Dish II|River Mud Dish II]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
