# Dragon Claw Pendant

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Common*

A single dragon claw, cleaned, dried, and set in copper wire as a pendant. The claw is from a juvenile dragon — small enough to wear comfortably. Carries the faint elemental resonance of the dragon it came from.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bone Stylus|Bone Stylus]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
