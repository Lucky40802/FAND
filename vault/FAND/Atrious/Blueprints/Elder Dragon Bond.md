# Elder Dragon Bond

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A ritual bond rather than a physical object. Creates a permanent mental link between the tamer and an adult or elder dragon. The dragon retains its intelligence and must agree to the bond.
