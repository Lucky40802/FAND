# Ichor Bronze Khanjar Sword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Khanjar Sword reforged with Ichor Bronze (Metal) worked into its construction. Bronze alloyed with distilled Ichor from aberrant creatures. Yellow-green in colour and slightly corrosive. Weapons deal +1d4 acid damage. Targets hit must CON save DC 13 or take 1d4 additional acid at the start of their next turn. Bonus: +1d4 Acid.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Ichor Bronze): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
