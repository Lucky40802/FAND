# Titanium Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Titanium Crossbow|Titanium Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Titanium Shortbow|Titanium Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
