# Strength Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph inscribed onto an object that, when the object is touched and the activating phrase spoken, temporarily increases the carrier's STR score by 4 for 1 minute. Single use — the glyph fades after activation.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Alarm Glyph|Alarm Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Heat Glyph|Heat Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silence Glyph|Silence Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Translation Rune|Translation Rune]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
