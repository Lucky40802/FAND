# Solarite Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Very Rare*

A automaton incorporating Solarite (Metal). A bright golden metal that only forms in locations of extreme concentrated sunlight over many centuries. Solarite weapons deal +2d4 radiant damage. Undead take maximum damage from solarite weapons. Fiends have disadvantage on attacks against a creature wearing solarite armour. Bonus: +2d4 Radiant.
