# Coldstone Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Rare*

A fragment incorporating Coldstone (Gemstone & Crystal). Stone from the deepest arctic caverns where the temperature never rises above absolute minimum. Perpetually cold to touch. Weapons deal +1d6 cold damage. Targets hit must CON save DC 13 or have their movement speed reduced by 10ft until the start of their next turn. Bonus: +1d6 Cold, Slow on hit.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Coldstone): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
