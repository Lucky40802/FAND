# Silver Dragon Scale Kalis

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Kalis reforged with Silver Dragon Scale (Metal) worked into its construction. Scales from a silver dragon. Armour provides resistance to cold. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage. The weapon also has +1d4 radiant — silver dragon scales carry both elements. Bonus: +1d4 Cold.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Silver Dragon Scale): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
