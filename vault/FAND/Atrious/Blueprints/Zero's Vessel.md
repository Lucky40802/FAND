# Zero's Vessel

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

This vessel only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Zero]] directly, bound into a core of Chaos Matter (Random effect on each hit). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
