# Obsidian Gem Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Rare*

A focus incorporating Obsidian Gem (Gemstone & Crystal). Formed at the border of shadow and fire. Obsidian channels void and shadow energy. Weapons set with obsidian gems deal shadow damage and leave targets partially incorporeal. Bonus: +2 Shadow.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Obsidian Gem): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
