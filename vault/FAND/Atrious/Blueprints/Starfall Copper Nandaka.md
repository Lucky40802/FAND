# Starfall Copper Nandaka

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Nandaka reforged with Starfall Copper (Metal) worked into its construction. Copper extracted from meteorites that fell through multiple planar layers before impact. Slightly blue-tinted. Weapons deal +1d6 radiant damage. Conducts magic more efficiently — spellcasting foci of starfall copper have +1 to spell attack rolls. Bonus: +2.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Starfall Copper): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
