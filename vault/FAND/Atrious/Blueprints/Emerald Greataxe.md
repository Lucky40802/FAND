# Emerald Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Emerald construction channels nature's poison and resonates near living plants.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Emerald Hand Axe|Emerald Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Emerald): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
