# Crystal Ball — Decoration

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Common*

A sphere of clear crystal, perfectly round, 3 inches in diameter. No magical properties. Used for display, carnival performances, and as a focus for meditation. Occasionally mistaken for a genuine scrying focus.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Lens — Magnifying|Crystal Lens — Magnifying]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Lens|Crystal Lens]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Prism — Light Split|Crystal Prism — Light Split]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glass Bead|Glass Bead]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
