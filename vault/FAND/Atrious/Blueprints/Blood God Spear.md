# Blood God Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Legendary*

A spear formed from condensed blood magic. Every hit heals the wielder for the full damage dealt. Critical hits deal 6d10 and create a blood fountain in a 15ft radius dealing 2d10 to all other creatures.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
