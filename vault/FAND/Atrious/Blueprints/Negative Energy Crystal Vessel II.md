# Negative Energy Crystal Vessel II

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Negative Energy Crystal (Planar). Crystal from the Negative Energy Plane. Constantly drains life energy from nearby creatures — bearers lose 1 HP per minute but gain necrotic damage. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic. Undead are healed by the energy rather than harmed. Bonus: Death radiation.
