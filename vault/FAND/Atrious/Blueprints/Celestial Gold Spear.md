# Celestial Gold Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Celestial Gold tip glows with divine light and is catastrophic to fiends. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Celestial Gold Quarterstaff|Celestial Gold Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Gold): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
