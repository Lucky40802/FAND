# Fire Crystal Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Uncommon*

A dagger blade of ruby crystal. Extremely sharp, deals fire damage, and shatters on a critical failure — dealing its damage to the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Evocation Crystal): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
