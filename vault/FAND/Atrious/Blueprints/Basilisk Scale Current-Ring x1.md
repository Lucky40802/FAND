# Basilisk Scale Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Basilisk Scale (Creature Harvest). Scales from a basilisk, carefully removed after death. Armour incorporating basilisk scales grants resistance to petrification effects. Shields of basilisk scale can be angled to reflect a basilisk's gaze — the basilisk must save against its own petrification. Bonus: Petrify gaze ward.
