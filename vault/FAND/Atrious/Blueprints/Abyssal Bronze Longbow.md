# Abyssal Bronze Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A 6-foot bow. The Abyssal Bronze stave weeps acid and leaves wounds that resist magical healing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Abyssal Bronze Crossbow|Abyssal Bronze Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Abyssal Bronze Shortbow|Abyssal Bronze Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Abyssal Bronze): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
