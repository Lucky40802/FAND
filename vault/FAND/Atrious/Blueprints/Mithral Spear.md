# Mithral Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Mithral tip is lighter than steel with superior edge retention and perfect enchantment acceptance. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mithral Quarterstaff|Mithral Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
