# Gelatinous Cube Residue Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Gelatinous Cube Residue (Creature Harvest). The semi-solid residue from a dissolved gelatinous cube. Items incorporating this material deal +1d6 acid damage. Armour coated with it dissolves non-magical projectiles on contact — arrows and bolts hitting the armour deal half damage. Bonus: +1d6 Acid.
