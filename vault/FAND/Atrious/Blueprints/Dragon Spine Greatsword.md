# Dragon Spine Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A greatsword incorporating a dragon spine as the spine of the blade. The vertebral segments create natural serrations. Deals elemental damage from the source dragon on crits.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
