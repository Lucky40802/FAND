# Age Reversal Treatment

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Uncommon*

A compound that reverses 500 years of physical aging on an object. Does not restore magical properties.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Age Reversal - 100 Years|Age Reversal - 100 Years]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
