# Shadowblade Wrap

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

A weapon wrap of veilcloth and shadowsteel applied to an existing blade. The blade becomes partially incorporeal, making it harder to track. Attacks with it in dim light or darkness have advantage on the first attack…

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
