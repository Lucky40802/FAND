# Night Hag Hair Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A talisman incorporating Night Hag Hair (Creature Harvest). Hair from a night hag, obtained through extraordinary means. Items incorporating night hag hair allow the bearer to invade the dreams of a sleeping creature once per night. The dreamer cannot tell the difference between the dream and reality. Bonus: Dream invasion.
