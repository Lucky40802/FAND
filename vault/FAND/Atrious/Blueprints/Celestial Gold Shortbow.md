# Celestial Gold Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

A compact bow. The Celestial Gold limbs glows with divine light and is catastrophic to fiends.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Celestial Gold Crossbow|Celestial Gold Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Celestial Gold Longbow|Celestial Gold Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Gold): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
