# Godsteel Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Rare*

A brand incorporating Godsteel (Metal). Metal touched and shaped by a deity directly. Cannot be crafted — only given. Weapons of Godsteel automatically hit (no attack roll needed) and deal maximum damage. Armour from it grants immunity to one damage type chosen at creation. Bonus: +5.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Godsteel): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
