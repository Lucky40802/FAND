# Magmaite Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Magmaite prod and stock. The construction is always hot enough to brand and amplifies fire enchantments.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Magmaite Longbow|Magmaite Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Magmaite Shortbow|Magmaite Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Magmaite): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
