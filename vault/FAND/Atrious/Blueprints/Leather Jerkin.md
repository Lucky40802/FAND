# Leather Jerkin

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Common*

A sleeveless vest of thick tanned leather. Covers the torso. Provides minimal but real protection against glancing cuts. The leather is oiled to keep it supple. Common working clothes in rough occupations.
