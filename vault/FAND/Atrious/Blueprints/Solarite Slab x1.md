# Solarite Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Very Rare*

A slab incorporating Solarite (Metal). A bright golden metal that only forms in locations of extreme concentrated sunlight over many centuries. Solarite weapons deal +2d4 radiant damage. Undead take maximum damage from solarite weapons. Fiends have disadvantage on attacks against a creature wearing solarite armour. Bonus: +2d4 Radiant.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Solarite): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
