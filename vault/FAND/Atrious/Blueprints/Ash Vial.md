# Ash Vial

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A sealed vial of cremated remains. Provides the holder a +2 bonus to Arcana checks related to the deceased individual's knowledge.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Grave Dirt Pouch|Grave Dirt Pouch]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soul Candle|Soul Candle]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
