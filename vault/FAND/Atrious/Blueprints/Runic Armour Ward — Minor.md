# Runic Armour Ward — Minor

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A protective rune sequence carved into armour. Grants +1 to AC while the runes are active. The runes must be retraced with a runecarving tool monthly or they fade.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Runesteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
