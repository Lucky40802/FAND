# Pocket Watch

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A mechanical watch accurate to within 2 minutes per day. Spring-wound, runs for 48 hours per winding. The mechanism is visible through a crystal face.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Lock Mechanism|Lock Mechanism]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spring Latch|Spring Latch]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
