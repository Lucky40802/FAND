# Glacial Iron Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Glacial Iron construction is always cold and slows targets on hit.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glacial Iron Greatsword|Glacial Iron Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glacial Iron Longsword|Glacial Iron Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
