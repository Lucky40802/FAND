# Gold Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Gold head channels divine energy but is too soft without reinforcement.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Mace|Gold Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
