# Gold Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A mechanical bow with a Gold prod and stock. The construction channels divine energy but is too soft without reinforcement.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Longbow|Gold Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Shortbow|Gold Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Gold): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
