# Graveyard Tea

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

Herbs gathered from graveyard soil brewed into a tea. The drinker can hear the most recent whispers of any corpse within 10ft for 10 minutes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ghost Mushroom Tincture|Ghost Mushroom Tincture]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
