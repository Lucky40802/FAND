# Obsidian Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Obsidian construction creates an edge sharp enough to part silk but requires careful use.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Obsidian Greatsword|Obsidian Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Obsidian Longsword|Obsidian Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Obsidian): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
