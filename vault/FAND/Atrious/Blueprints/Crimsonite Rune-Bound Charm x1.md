# Crimsonite Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Crimsonite (Gemstone & Crystal). A blood-red crystalline mineral that forms at sites of mass death. Absorbs life energy from nearby creatures. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic and the wielder heals for 1 HP per hit. Armour from it drains nearby undead of their animating energy. Bonus: +1d6 Necrotic.
