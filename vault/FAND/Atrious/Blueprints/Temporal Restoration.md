# Temporal Restoration

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Rare*

A ritual that restores an object to its state at a specific point in history. The restorer must have a historical record or direct knowledge of that moment.
