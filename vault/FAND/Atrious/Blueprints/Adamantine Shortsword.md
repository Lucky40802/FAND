# Adamantine Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Adamantine construction ignores object hardness and cannot be destroyed by normal means.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Adamantine Greatsword|Adamantine Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Adamantine Longsword|Adamantine Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 3, from Adamantine): Weapon: +2 damage. Armor: +2 Gear AC.
