# Darkvision Drops

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

Applied to the eyes. Grants darkvision 60ft for 4 hours. The world appears in shades of grey-blue during the effect. Combining these with actual darkvision causes no benefit — the two do not stack.
