# Rune of Freshness

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into the inside of a food storage container that slows spoilage. Food inside the container lasts 4 times as long as normal before going bad. Does not halt spoilage — it slows it.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of True North|Rune of True North]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silence Rune|Silence Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wall Spike Array|Wall Spike Array]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Weight Rune|Weight Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Window Bar Set|Window Bar Set]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
