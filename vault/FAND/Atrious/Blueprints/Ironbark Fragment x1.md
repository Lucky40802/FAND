# Ironbark Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Rare*

A fragment incorporating Ironbark (Metal). Bark from ancient oak trees in areas of concentrated natural magic. Harder than iron but flexible like wood. Ironbark weapons deal bludgeoning damage equal to a weapon one size category larger. Armour from it is as protective as steel but weighs 40% less. Bonus: +1.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ironbark): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
