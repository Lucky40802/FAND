# The Sanguine Veil

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A cloak and armour set treated with blood alchemy. When the wearer would take damage, 2 HP of that damage is instead distributed to all enemies within 15ft as necrotic.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
