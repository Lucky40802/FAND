# Sanguine Amplifier

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A ritual component that amplifies the power of blood magic. When included in a blood magic ritual, the effects of the ritual are doubled in range and duration. Single use. Consumed in the ritual.
