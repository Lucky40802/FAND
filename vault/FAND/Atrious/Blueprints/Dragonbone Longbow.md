# Dragonbone Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Legendary*

A 6-foot bow. The Dragonbone stave holds enchantments better than any metal and carries elemental resonance.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonbone Crossbow|Dragonbone Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonbone Shortbow|Dragonbone Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
