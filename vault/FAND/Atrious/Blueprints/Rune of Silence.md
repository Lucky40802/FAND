# Rune of Silence

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a stone that when placed in a room muffles sound. Sounds within 10ft of the stone cannot be heard outside that radius. The effect lasts 8 hours before the rune burns out.
