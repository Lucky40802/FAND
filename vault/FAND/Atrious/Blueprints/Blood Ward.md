# Blood Ward

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A ward painted in blood on a threshold. The ward alerts the painter when any creature whose blood was not used in the ward crosses the threshold. Range of alert: 100ft. Duration: 24 hours.
