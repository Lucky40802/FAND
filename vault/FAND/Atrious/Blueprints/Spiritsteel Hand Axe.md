# Spiritsteel Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Spiritsteel head strikes incorporeal creatures as solid matter. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Greataxe|Spiritsteel Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Spiritsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
