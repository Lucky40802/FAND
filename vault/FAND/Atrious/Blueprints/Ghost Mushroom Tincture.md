# Ghost Mushroom Tincture

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A tincture of ghost mushroom extract. When consumed, allows the drinker to see spirits and ghosts for 1 hour. The spirits are visible but not tangible — the drinker cannot interact with them beyond sight.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Graveyard Tea|Graveyard Tea]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
