# Mithral Tabar

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Tabar reforged with Mithral (Metal) worked into its construction. True silver — lighter than steel, harder than iron, and nearly impossible to enchant incorrectly. Mithral armour imposes no disadvantage on stealth. Mithral weapons never rust. The material of dwarven legend. Bonus: +3.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Mithral): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
