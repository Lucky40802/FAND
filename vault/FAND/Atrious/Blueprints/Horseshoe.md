# Horseshoe

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A U-shaped iron shoe nailed to a horse's hoof. Poorly shod horses go lame; well-shod horses last campaigns. The farrier's version of blacksmithing requires reading the animal as much as working the iron.
