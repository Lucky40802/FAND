# Shadow Dagger Pair

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Uncommon*

A matched pair of daggers of shadowsteel wrapped in veilcloth. In dim light, the blades are invisible. Attacks with them from concealment deal an additional 2d6 shadow damage on the first hit from hiding each round.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
