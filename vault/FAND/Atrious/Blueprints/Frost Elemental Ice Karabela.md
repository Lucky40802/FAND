# Frost Elemental Ice Karabela

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Karabela reforged with Frost Elemental Ice (Elemental) worked into its construction. Ice formed by water elementals that never melts — maintained by permanent elemental enchantment. Weapons deal +1d8 cold damage. On a hit, frost spreads — creatures hit have their movement speed reduced by 10ft for 1 round as ice forms on their joints. Bonus: +1d8 Cold.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Frost Elemental Ice): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
