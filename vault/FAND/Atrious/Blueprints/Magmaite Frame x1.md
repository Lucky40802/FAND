# Magmaite Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Very Rare*

A frame incorporating Magmaite (Metal). A metal formed deep within volcanoes in the Plane of Fire. Magmaite is always warm enough to brand skin on contact. When weapons made from it strike, they leave burning brands. Heat-based enchantments applied to magmaite weapons are 50% more effective. Bonus: +2 Fire.
