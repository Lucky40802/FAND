# Time's Dirk

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A Dirk that only functions properly because it channels a fragment of [[Time]] directly, cast in a core of Skymetal (+2). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system.

## Material Property
**Skyborne** (Potency 1, from Skymetal): +5 ft speed. At Potency 3+ you take no falling damage; at Potency 6+ you gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.
