# Tidecoral Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Rare*

A fetish incorporating Tidecoral (Creature Harvest). Coral grown in the deepest currents of the Plane of Water. Harder than stone, flexible enough to be worked, and constantly damp. Weapons made from tidecoral deal cold damage and can breathe underwater as if they were part of the sea. Bonus: +2 Cold.
