# Gold Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A 6-foot bow. The Gold stave channels divine energy but is too soft without reinforcement.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Crossbow|Gold Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Shortbow|Gold Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
