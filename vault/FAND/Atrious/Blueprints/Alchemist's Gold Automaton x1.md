# Alchemist's Gold Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Very Rare*

A automaton incorporating Alchemist's Gold (Metal). A gold-like compound created through alchemy. Indistinguishable from real gold by mundane means — only Detect Magic reveals the difference. Used by fraudsters and as a testing material. Stable indefinitely but reverts under extreme heat. Bonus: +0.
