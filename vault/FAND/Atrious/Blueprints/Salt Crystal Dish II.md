# Salt Crystal Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Very Rare*

A dish incorporating Salt Crystal (Common Material). Common salt, but sufficient concentration has spiritual significance. Line of salt crystals prevents spirits of CR 2 or lower from crossing. Applied to wounds, it causes an additional 1d4 damage but prevents infection. Bonus: Spirit ward.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Salt Crystal Dish x1|Salt Crystal Dish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
