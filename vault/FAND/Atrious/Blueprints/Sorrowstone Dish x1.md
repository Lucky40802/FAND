# Sorrowstone Dish x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Very Rare*

A dish incorporating Sorrowstone (Spirit & Soul). Dark blue-grey stone from locations of great tragedy. Weapons that draw blood cause the target to make WIS save DC 12 or be Saddened — a minor condition causing -1 to all rolls for 1 minute. Bonus: Fear induction. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sorrowstone Dish II|Sorrowstone Dish II]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
