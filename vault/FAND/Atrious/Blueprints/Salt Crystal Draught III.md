# Salt Crystal Draught III

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A draught incorporating Salt Crystal (Common Material). Common salt, but sufficient concentration has spiritual significance. Line of salt crystals prevents spirits of CR 2 or lower from crossing. Applied to wounds, it causes an additional 1d4 damage but prevents infection. Bonus: Spirit ward. A minor variation on a well-worn design.
