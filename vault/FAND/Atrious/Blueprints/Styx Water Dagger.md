# Styx Water Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Artifact*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Styx Water edge causes memory loss in struck targets who fail a WIS save. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 2, from Styx Water): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +2d6 cold to creatures in water.
