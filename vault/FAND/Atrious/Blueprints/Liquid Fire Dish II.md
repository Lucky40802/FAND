# Liquid Fire Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A dish incorporating Liquid Fire (Alchemical). A liquid that ignites on contact with air. Weapons can be briefly coated in it — for the next 3 hits, each deals +2d6 fire damage. The coating burns out after 3 hits. Applying it requires an action. Bonus: +2d6 Fire.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Liquid Fire Dish x1|Liquid Fire Dish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
