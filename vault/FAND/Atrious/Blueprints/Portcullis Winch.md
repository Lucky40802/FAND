# Portcullis Winch

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

A hand-crafted artifact, engineered for siege warfare using Timber Beam. Reliable enough to see real use in the field. A minor variation on a well-worn design.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Chevaux de Frise|Chevaux de Frise]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sturdy Timber Beam Engine|Sturdy Timber Beam Engine]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
