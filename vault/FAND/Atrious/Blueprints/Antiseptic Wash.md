# Antiseptic Wash

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A clear spirit-based wash that kills surface bacteria and prevents wound infection. Applied to wounds before binding. Not a healing agent — it prevents infection from making things worse. Standard military medical kit…
