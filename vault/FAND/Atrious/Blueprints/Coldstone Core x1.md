# Coldstone Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Rare*

A core incorporating Coldstone (Gemstone & Crystal). Stone from the deepest arctic caverns where the temperature never rises above absolute minimum. Perpetually cold to touch. Weapons deal +1d6 cold damage. Targets hit must CON save DC 13 or have their movement speed reduced by 10ft until the start of their next turn. Bonus: +1d6 Cold, Slow on hit.
