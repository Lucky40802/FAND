# Bloodfire Ruby Compass x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Rare*

Creatures killed by bloodfire ruby weapons cannot be raised as undead — their life force is consumed entirely. Bonus: +1d6 Fire and Necrotic.
