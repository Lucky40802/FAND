# Prismaite Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A focus incorporating Prismaite (Gemstone & Crystal). A crystal that cycles through all colours constantly. The damage type changes with the colour — fire when red, cold when blue, lightning when yellow, etc. Roll 1d6 on each hit: 1=Fire, 2=Cold, 3=Lightning, 4=Acid, 5=Force, 6=Radiant. Cannot predict or control the type. Bonus: Random elemental on each hit.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Prismaite): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
