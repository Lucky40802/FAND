# Soulwood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A carving incorporating Soulwood (Wood). Wood from trees in places where many people died peacefully. The grain sometimes forms the faces of the departed. Items made from soulwood allow the bearer to hear whispers from nearby spirits. Once per day, the bearer can ask a spirit in the area one yes/no question. The spirit answers truthfully. Bonus: Spirit communication.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Soulwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
