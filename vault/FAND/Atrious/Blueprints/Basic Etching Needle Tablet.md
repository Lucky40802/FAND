*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Uncommon*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Date Glyph|Date Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Journeyman's Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm|Journeyman's Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm]].
