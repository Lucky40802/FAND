# Gravestone Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A veil incorporating Gravestone (Spirit & Soul). Dark grey stone from ancient graveyards where hundreds or thousands died. Weapons deal +1d4 necrotic. Creatures struck by gravestone weapons have disadvantage on death saving throws for 1 minute. Bonus: +1d4 Necrotic. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Gravestone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
