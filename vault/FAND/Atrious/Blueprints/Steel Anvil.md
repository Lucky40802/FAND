# Steel Anvil

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A proper smith's anvil of steel with a flat face, horn, and hardy hole. Required for quality forging. Too heavy to steal conveniently.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Forged Steel Spurs|Forged Steel Spurs]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spring Steel Trap|Spring Steel Trap]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Manacles|Steel Manacles]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
