# Banded Iron Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

Overlapping iron bands riveted to a leather backing. Better coverage than chainmail, more flexible than plate. The rivets are countersunk so they don't catch on weapons. A solid mid-range armour choice.
