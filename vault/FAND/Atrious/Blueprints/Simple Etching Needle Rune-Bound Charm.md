# Simple Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A simple rune-bound charm built from etching needle, taught early in Sigilcraft training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Drying Glyph|Drying Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Float Glyph|Float Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Plain Etching Needle Tablet|Plain Etching Needle Tablet]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
