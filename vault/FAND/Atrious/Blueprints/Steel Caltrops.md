*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cart Wheel Rim|Cart Wheel Rim]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
