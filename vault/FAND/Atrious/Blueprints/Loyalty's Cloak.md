# Loyalty's Cloak

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Legendary*

This cloak only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Loyalty]] directly, bound into a core of Divination Eye (+2, wielder cannot be surprised, gains +1 to initiative). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**All-Seeing** (Potency 1, from Divination Eye): Darkvision 60 ft. At Potency 3+ see invisible; at Potency 6+ truesight 30 ft. Focus: +1 to divination spell DCs.
