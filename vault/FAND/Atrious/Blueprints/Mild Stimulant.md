# Mild Stimulant

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A small vial of sharp-smelling liquid. Eliminates one level of exhaustion for 4 hours. The exhaustion returns afterward with +1 additional level. Not for regular use. Exactly as dangerous as that sounds.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Antiseptic Wash|Antiseptic Wash]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Compress Potion|Cold Compress Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Endurance Tea|Endurance Tea]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Analgesic|Mild Analgesic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Scent Mask|Scent Mask]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Warmth Potion|Warmth Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Weak Paralytic|Weak Paralytic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wound Sealant|Wound Sealant]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
