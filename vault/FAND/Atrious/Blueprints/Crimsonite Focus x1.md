# Crimsonite Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A focus incorporating Crimsonite (Gemstone & Crystal). A blood-red crystalline mineral that forms at sites of mass death. Absorbs life energy from nearby creatures. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic and the wielder heals for 1 HP per hit. Armour from it drains nearby undead of their animating energy. Bonus: +1d6 Necrotic.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 2, from Crimsonite): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
