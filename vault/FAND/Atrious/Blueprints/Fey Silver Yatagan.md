# Fey Silver Yatagan

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Yatagan reforged with Fey Silver (Metal) worked into its construction. Silver refined in the Feywild over decades. Slightly iridescent, always slightly damp to touch. Weapons deal +2 to attack rolls against non-magical creatures. Armour from fey silver allows the bearer to see through illusions — they have advantage on saves against illusion magic. Bonus: +2 vs mundane creatures.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Fey Silver): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
