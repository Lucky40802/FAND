# Basic Healing Salve

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A tin of rendered fat mixed with common herbs — feverfew, yarrow, and a little calendula. Applied to wounds, it prevents infection and dulls pain. Heals nothing on its own but helps the body heal itself.
