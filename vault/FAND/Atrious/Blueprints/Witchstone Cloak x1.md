# Witchstone Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Rare*

A cloak incorporating Witchstone (Arcane Material). Dull green stone from locations where powerful curses have soaked into the earth. Items made from witchstone can store one curse — when a creature is struck by a weapon made from witchstone, they must save against the stored curse. Bonus: Curse application.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Witchstone): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
