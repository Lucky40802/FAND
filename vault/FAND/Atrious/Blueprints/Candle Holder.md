# Candle Holder

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A simple iron stand for a candle. The base is broad enough to prevent tipping. Blacksmiths make these between larger commissions to keep the fire hot and apprentices busy.
