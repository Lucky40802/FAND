# Rune of Seeking

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A rune carved into a weapon that allows it to track a specific named target. The weapon knows the direction of the target within 1 mile and gains +1 to attack rolls against that target specifically.
