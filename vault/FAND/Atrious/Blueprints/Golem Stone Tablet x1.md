# Golem Stone Tablet x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Rare*

A tablet incorporating Golem Stone (Arcane Material). The core stone from a destroyed golem. Still carries the animating energy. Items made from golem stone grant the bearer +2 to saves against effects that would Paralyse or Stun them. Armour from golem stone has a 20% chance of auto-repairing 1d6 damage it takes per round. Bonus: +2 to Construct saves.
