# The Crimson Reaper

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A longsword that drains blood from wounds. Each hit heals the wielder for the full damage dealt. On a kill, the blood empowers the next attack: +2d8 necrotic.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
