# Riding Saddle - Horse

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Common*

A standard saddle for a Medium or Large horse. Includes stirrups, girth, and crupper. The difference between riding and falling is often a good saddle.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Treat - Carnivore|Beast Treat - Carnivore]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Wound Wrap|Beast Wound Wrap]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Saddle — Basic|Saddle — Basic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Saddlebags — Large|Saddlebags — Large]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
