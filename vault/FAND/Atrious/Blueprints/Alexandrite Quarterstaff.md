# Alexandrite Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A 6-foot staff of Alexandrite. The material is unpredictable — the damage type changes randomly with each strike.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Alexandrite Spear|Alexandrite Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Alexandrite): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
