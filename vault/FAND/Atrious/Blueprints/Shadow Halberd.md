# Shadow Halberd

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A halberd of shadowsteel and voidmetal. Reach 10ft. Attacks are made at advantage when the wielder is in dim light.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidmetal): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
