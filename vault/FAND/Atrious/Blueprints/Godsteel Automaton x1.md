# Godsteel Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Very Rare*

A automaton incorporating Godsteel (Metal). Metal touched and shaped by a deity directly. Cannot be crafted — only given. Weapons of Godsteel automatically hit (no attack roll needed) and deal maximum damage. Armour from it grants immunity to one damage type chosen at creation. Bonus: +5.
