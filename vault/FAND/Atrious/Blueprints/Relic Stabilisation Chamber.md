# Relic Stabilisation Chamber

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Uncommon*

A hand-crafted artifact, restored from a recovered relic fragment using Fragment of the Original. Unremarkable to look at, but it does exactly what it's meant to. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Basic Fragment of the Original Relic|Basic Fragment of the Original Relic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
