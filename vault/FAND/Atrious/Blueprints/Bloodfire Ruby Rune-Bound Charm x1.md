# Bloodfire Ruby Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Bloodfire Ruby (Gemstone & Crystal). A deep red ruby that seems to have fire and blood swirling within it. Weapons deal +1d4 fire and +1d4 necrotic simultaneously. Creatures killed by bloodfire ruby weapons cannot be raised as undead — their life force is consumed entirely. Bonus: +1d6 Fire and Necrotic.
