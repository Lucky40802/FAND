# Hellforged Iron Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Hellforged Iron construction burns with hellfire and unnerves good-aligned opponents.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Greatsword|Hellforged Iron Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Longsword|Hellforged Iron Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Hellforged Iron): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
