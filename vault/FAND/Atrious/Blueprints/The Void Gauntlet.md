# The Void Gauntlet

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A single gauntlet of voidmetal. Unarmed strikes deal 1d6 shadow damage. Any creature struck must CON save DC 15 or be Blinded for 1 round.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidmetal): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
