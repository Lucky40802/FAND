# Voidsap Wood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Voidsap Wood (Wood). Wood from trees that grew near planar void rifts, absorbing void energy into their grain. The wood is slightly transparent and slightly cold. Weapons deal +1d4 force damage. Staves made from voidsap wood absorb 5 points of magical damage per round. Bonus: +1 Force.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidsap Wood): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
