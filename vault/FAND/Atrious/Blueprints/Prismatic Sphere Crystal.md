# Prismatic Sphere Crystal

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A crystal that, when shattered, releases all seven colours of the prismatic sphere simultaneously in a 20ft radius. Each colour applies its full effect. Impossible to survive without very specific resistances.
