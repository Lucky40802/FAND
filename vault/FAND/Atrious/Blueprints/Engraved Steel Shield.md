# Engraved Steel Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A heater shield of quality steel with engraved knotwork on the face. Functionally identical to a standard shield but the engraving work-hardens the surface slightly, and it looks considerably more impressive.
