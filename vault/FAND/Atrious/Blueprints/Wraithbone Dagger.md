# Wraithbone Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Wraithbone edge ignores non-magical armour and leaves unnaturally cold wounds. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Wraithbone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
