# Ghost Step Boots

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

Boots soled in wraithbone. The wearer makes no sound while moving and passes through difficult terrain as if it were clear ground.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Wraithbone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
