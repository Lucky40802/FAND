# Hellforged Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A heater shield of hellforged iron. The surface is always warm. Any creature that attacks the wielder with a melee weapon takes 1d6 fire damage from contact with the shield as their weapon grazes the surface.
