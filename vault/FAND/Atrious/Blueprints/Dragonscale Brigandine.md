# Dragonscale Brigandine

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A brigandine coat using dragon scales as the internal plates instead of iron. The scales are lighter, harder, and provide natural fire resistance. The colour of the scales determines the elemental affinity.
