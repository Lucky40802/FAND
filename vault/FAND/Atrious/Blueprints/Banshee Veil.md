# Banshee Veil

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A veil woven from the essence of a banshee. The wearer can unleash the banshee's wail once per day. All creatures within 30ft must CON save DC 18 or drop to 0 HP. The wearer is immune.
