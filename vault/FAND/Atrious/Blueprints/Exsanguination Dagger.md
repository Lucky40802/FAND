# Exsanguination Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A blade that extracts blood from wounds. On a critical hit the target must CON save DC 16 or be drained of 1d4 points of Constitution permanently until a Greater Restoration.
