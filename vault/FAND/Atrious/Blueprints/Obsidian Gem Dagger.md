# Obsidian Gem Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Obsidian Gem edge channels shadow and partially dematerialises wounds. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Obsidian Gem): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
