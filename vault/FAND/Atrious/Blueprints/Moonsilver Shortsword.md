# Moonsilver Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Moonsilver construction deals double damage to lycanthropes and creatures of the night.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonsilver Greatsword|Moonsilver Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonsilver Longsword|Moonsilver Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Moonsilver): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
