# Espada Ropera

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

The early rapier — a Spanish civilian sword designed for personal defence in the streets of Renaissance Europe. Longer and narrower than an arming sword, optimised for thrusting. The complex swept hilt protects the hand.
