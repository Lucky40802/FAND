# Ether Stone Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Ether Stone (Planar). Stone from the Border Ethereal, maintained in stable form in the material plane. Items made from ether stone allow the bearer to see into the Ethereal Plane as if using See Invisibility. Weapons can strike Ethereal creatures as if they were material. Bonus: Ethereal sight.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Ether Stone): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
