# Starthread Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Starthread head weighs almost nothing and phases through cover before striking. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Starthread Greataxe|Starthread Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Starthread): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
