# Dawnbrand, the Reforged

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

Reforged from the shards of a sword that once struck down a great and ancient evil in the age before this one. The blade bears the sun, moon, and seven stars etched along its length, and cannot be broken a second time. Its wielder is recognized on sight by anyone who remembers the old wars.
