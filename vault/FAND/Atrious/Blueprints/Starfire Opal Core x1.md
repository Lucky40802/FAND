# Starfire Opal Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Rare*

A core incorporating Starfire Opal (Gemstone & Crystal). An opal with a shifting inner fire that looks like a captured star. The damage type alternates between radiant and fire — radiant during the day, fire at night. Weapons deal +1d4 of the appropriate type. Armour from starfire opal provides resistance to whichever is currently active. Bonus: +1d4 Radiant or Fire.
