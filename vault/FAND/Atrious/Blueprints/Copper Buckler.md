# Copper Buckler

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A small fist-shield of Copper. is soft and dulls quickly but is cheap to replace.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials, AC) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Shield|Copper Shield]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
