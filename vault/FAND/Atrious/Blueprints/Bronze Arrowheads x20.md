# Bronze Arrowheads x20

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

Twenty bronze arrowheads of bodkin profile. Cast and then filed to a proper point. Fit standard arrow shafts. Bronze is softer than iron but adequate for hunting at close range.
