# Magmaite Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Magmaite tip is always hot enough to brand and amplifies fire enchantments. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Magmaite Quarterstaff|Magmaite Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Magmaite): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
