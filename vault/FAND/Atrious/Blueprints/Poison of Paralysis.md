# Poison of Paralysis

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A yellow liquid applied to a weapon. A creature struck must CON save DC 15 or be Paralyzed for one minute. Repeat save at end of each turn. Against paralyzed creatures, attacks from within 5ft automatically critical hit.
