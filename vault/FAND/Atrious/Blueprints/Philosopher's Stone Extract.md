# Philosopher's Stone Extract

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Legendary*

A drop of this liquid converts 1 pound of base metal to gold. Converts 1 pound of any material to mithral if the full vial is applied. The stone is consumed. Making another requires starting entirely over.
