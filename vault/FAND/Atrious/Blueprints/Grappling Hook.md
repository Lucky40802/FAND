# Grappling Hook

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

Four curved tines welded to a central ring with a rope attachment point. Thrown, it grabs ledges, windowsills, and walls.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Chain|Iron Chain]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Gate|Iron Gate]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
