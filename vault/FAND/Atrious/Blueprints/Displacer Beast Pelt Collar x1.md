# Displacer Beast Pelt Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A collar incorporating Displacer Beast Pelt (Creature Harvest). The pelt of a displacer beast. Armour made from it grants the bearer partial displacement — the first attack against the bearer each round has disadvantage. Attacks that miss hit a displacement after-image. Bonus: Displacement ward.
