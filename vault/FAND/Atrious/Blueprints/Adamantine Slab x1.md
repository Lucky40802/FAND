# Adamantine Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Rare*

A slab incorporating Adamantine (Metal). The hardest substance available without divine intervention. Adamantine weapons ignore object hardness entirely — they cut through iron doors, stone walls, and heavy armour as if through wood. Armour made from it is critical-hit proof. Bonus: +3.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 3, from Adamantine): Weapon: +2 damage. Armor: +2 Gear AC.
