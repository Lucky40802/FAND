# Soulbound Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

A shield reinforced with soulsteel. Once per day, the wielder can cause a spirit to manifest from the shield and intercept one attack, negating it entirely.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
