# Corrosive Salt Draught x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Very Rare*

A draught incorporating Corrosive Salt (Alchemical). A crystallised acid salt that is safe to handle with leather gloves. When introduced to moisture or metal, it activates into full corrosive acid. Weapons with corrosive salt inlays deal +1d6 acid damage against armoured targets specifically. Bonus: +1d6 Acid. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.
