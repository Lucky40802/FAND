# Shadow Mail

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Uncommon*

Chain mail of interlocked shadowsteel rings with veilcloth lining. AC 16. Makes no sound. The wearer moves in a permanent dim shadow even in full sunlight.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials, AC) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Scale Armour|Veilcloth Scale Armour]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
