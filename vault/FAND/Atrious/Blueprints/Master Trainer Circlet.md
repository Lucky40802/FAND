# Master Trainer Circlet

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

An unusual artifact, shaped for handling and bonding with beasts using Woven Sinew. Solid, dependable work — nothing about it is flashy, but it holds up. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Elder Beast Restraint|Elder Beast Restraint]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
