# Forged Steel Spurs

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A pair of rowel spurs of forged steel with a 6-point rowel wheel. The neck is angled to fit a standard cavalry boot. The straps are not included — those are leather. Used to signal and direct a war horse.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spring Steel Trap|Spring Steel Trap]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Anvil|Steel Anvil]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Manacles|Steel Manacles]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
