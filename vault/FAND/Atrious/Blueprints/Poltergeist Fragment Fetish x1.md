# Poltergeist Fragment Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Rare*

A fetish incorporating Poltergeist Fragment (Creature Harvest). Fragments of objects thrown repeatedly by poltergeists, infused with telekinetic energy. Weapons made from poltergeist fragments can be thrown regardless of their normal classification — the telekinetic energy guides them back on a miss. Bonus: Telekinetic strike.
