# Pneumatic Actuator

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Uncommon*

A specialized artifact, assembled from precision clockwork parts using Copper Wire. Built to be used, not admired. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clockwork Printing Press|Clockwork Printing Press]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Novice's Copper Wire Automaton|Novice's Copper Wire Automaton]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
