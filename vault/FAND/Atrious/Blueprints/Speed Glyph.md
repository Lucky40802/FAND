# Speed Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Uncommon*

Stepping on it increases speed by 30ft for 1 minute. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Speed Boost Glyph|Speed Boost Glyph]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
