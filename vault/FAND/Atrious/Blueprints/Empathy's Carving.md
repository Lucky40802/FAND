# Empathy's Carving

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Legendary*

This carving only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Empathy]] directly, bound into a core of Yew (+1). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Yew): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
