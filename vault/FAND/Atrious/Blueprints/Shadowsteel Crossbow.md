# Shadowsteel Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Shadowsteel prod and stock. The construction is silent and deals shadow damage, partially incorporealising wounds.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadowsteel Longbow|Shadowsteel Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadowsteel Shortbow|Shadowsteel Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
