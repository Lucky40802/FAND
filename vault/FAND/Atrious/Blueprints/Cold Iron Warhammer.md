# Cold Iron Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Cold Iron head disrupts fey enchantments on contact.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Iron Mace|Cold Iron Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
