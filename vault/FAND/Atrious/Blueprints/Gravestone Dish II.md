# Gravestone Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A dish incorporating Gravestone (Spirit & Soul). Dark grey stone from ancient graveyards where hundreds or thousands died. Weapons deal +1d4 necrotic. Creatures struck by gravestone weapons have disadvantage on death saving throws for 1 minute. Bonus: +1d4 Necrotic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gravestone Dish x1|Gravestone Dish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
