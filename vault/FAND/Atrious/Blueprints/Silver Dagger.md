# Silver Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Silver edge burns supernatural creatures that rely on regeneration. Easily concealed.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Main Gauche|Silver Main Gauche]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
