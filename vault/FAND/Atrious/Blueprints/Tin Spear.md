# Tin Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Tin tip flexes rather than snaps under lateral force. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tin Quarterstaff|Tin Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
