# Battle Bread

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

Dense travel bread prepared with warrior's intent. Eating a full loaf grants advantage on all STR checks for 4 hours.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Strength Tea|Strength Tea]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
