# Spiritsteel Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Spiritsteel construction strikes incorporeal creatures as solid matter.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Greatsword|Spiritsteel Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Longsword|Spiritsteel Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Spiritsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
