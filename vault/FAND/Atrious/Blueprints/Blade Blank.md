# Blade Blank

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A roughly shaped sword blank, forged to profile but not finished or edged. Sold to finishing smiths or swordgrinders.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Helmet Blank|Steel Helmet Blank]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
