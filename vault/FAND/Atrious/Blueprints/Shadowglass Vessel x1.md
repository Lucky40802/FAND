# Shadowglass Vessel x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Shadowglass (Planar). Glass formed in the Shadowfell from compressed shadow energy. Black and slightly translucent — looking through it shows the world in shades of grey. Weapons deal +1d6 shadow damage and extinguish non-magical light sources on hit. Bonus: +1d6 Shadow.
