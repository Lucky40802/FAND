# The Vowbrand

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

Reforged from the melted-down remains of a blade that broke its wielder's sworn oath. The steel runs deep red with black rippling patterns — a permanent stain from the smelting. Given only to those charged with upholding a vow of protection; it grows warm in the hand whenever that vow is at risk of being broken.
