# Plane Shift Calibrator

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Common*

A device that, when used before casting Plane Shift, reduces the arrival error radius from 500 miles to 50 miles. The device is calibrated to a specific plane and must be recalibrated for different destinations.
