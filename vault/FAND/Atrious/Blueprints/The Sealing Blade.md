# The Sealing Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

A sword said to seal darkness away rather than merely cut it. Forged by a goddess and tempered over centuries by the prayers of those who came after her. It cannot be lifted by anyone with a truly evil heart — the blade simply refuses them. Against creatures of pure malice, every strike lands true.
