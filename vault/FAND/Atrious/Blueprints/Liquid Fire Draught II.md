# Liquid Fire Draught II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A draught incorporating Liquid Fire (Alchemical). A liquid that ignites on contact with air. Weapons can be briefly coated in it — for the next 3 hits, each deals +2d6 fire damage. The coating burns out after 3 hits. Applying it requires an action. Bonus: +2d6 Fire.
