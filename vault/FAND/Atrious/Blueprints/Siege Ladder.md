# Siege Ladder

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

A 20-foot scaling ladder of oak with iron-shod feet for planting against stone walls. The rungs are iron rather than wood — they cannot be cut. Rated for 5 climbers simultaneously.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Siege Tower Section|Siege Tower Section]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
