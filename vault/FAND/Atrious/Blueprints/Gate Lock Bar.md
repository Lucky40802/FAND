# Gate Lock Bar

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

A heavy iron bar that drops across a gate to lock it. Prevents opening from outside by brute force below STR 22.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Arrow Slit Frame|Arrow Slit Frame]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Caltrop Spreader|Caltrop Spreader]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cauldron Bracket|Cauldron Bracket]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cauldron Over Gate|Cauldron Over Gate]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
