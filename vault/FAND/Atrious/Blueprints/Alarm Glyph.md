# Alarm Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph that sends a mental ping to the inscriber when triggered by a creature of a type specified during inscription. Range of mental alert: 1 mile. Single trigger — resets after 1 hour of inactivity.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Heat Glyph|Heat Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silence Glyph|Silence Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Strength Glyph|Strength Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Translation Rune|Translation Rune]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
