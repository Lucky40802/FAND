# Night-Eye Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

Applied to the eyes. Grants darkvision 120ft for 8 hours. During daylight the world appears flat and slightly dark but functional. The most effective non-magical darkvision enhancement available.
