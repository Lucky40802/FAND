# Rope and Grapple

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

Fifty feet of hempen rope with a four-tined iron grapple tied to one end. The grapple folds flat for transport and springs open when thrown. Standard adventuring equipment. The rope is the most used item in the kit.
