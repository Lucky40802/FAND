# Treant Bark Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Treant Bark (Wood). Bark shed by living treants. Treants shed bark every decade and often gift it to those who have helped them. Armour of treant bark provides excellent protection but is vulnerable to fire — fire damage against treant bark armour deals +1d6 additional damage. Bonus: Fire weakness ward.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Treant Bark): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
