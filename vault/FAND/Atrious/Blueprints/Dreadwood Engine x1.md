# Dreadwood Engine x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Very Rare*

A engine incorporating Dreadwood (Wood). Wood from trees at the edge of reality where the Far Realm presses close. The grain forms shapes that suggest faces or eyes if you look too long. Creatures hit by dreadwood weapons must WIS save DC 13 or be Frightened of the weapon's wielder until the end of their next turn. Bonus: Frightened on hit.
