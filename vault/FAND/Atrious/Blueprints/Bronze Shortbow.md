# Bronze Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A compact bow. The Bronze limbs holds a reasonable edge and rarely shatters.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bronze Crossbow|Bronze Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bronze Longbow|Bronze Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
