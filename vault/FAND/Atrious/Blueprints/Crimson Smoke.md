# Crimson Smoke

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A smoke bomb with blood as the carrier. When it detonates, all creatures in the cloud must make a CON save DC 12 or be Poisoned for 1 minute.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Smoke Bomb|Blood Smoke Bomb]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Ward Paint|Blood Ward Paint]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sanguine Mist Vial|Sanguine Mist Vial]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sanguine Tattoo Ink|Sanguine Tattoo Ink]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
