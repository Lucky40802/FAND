# Simple Copper Wire Automaton

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A simple automaton built from copper wire, taught early in Clockwork Engineering training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pivot Polisher|Pivot Polisher]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
