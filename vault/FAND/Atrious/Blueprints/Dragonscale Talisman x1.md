# Dragonscale Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A talisman incorporating Dragonscale (Dragon Material). The scales of a dragon, each one a natural armour plate. The elemental affinity depends on the dragon — red gives fire resistance, blue gives lightning resistance, and so on. Used in armour and as inlays on weapons. Bonus: +2.
