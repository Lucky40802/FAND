# Lawful Iron Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Rare*

A automaton incorporating Lawful Iron (Metal). Iron from Mechanus, shaped by the modrons over centuries. Perfectly uniform in composition — not a single impurity. Weapons deal +1 to attack and damage rolls against creatures of chaotic alignment. Armour from it cannot be broken by chaotic magic. Bonus: +1 vs chaotic creatures.
