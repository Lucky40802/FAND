# Acid Flask

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A flask of mild acid solution. Thrown as a ranged weapon. On a hit, deals 1d8 acid damage. Corrodes metal objects on contact, reducing their effectiveness if not cleaned within one minute.
