# Silver Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Silver tip burns supernatural creatures that rely on regeneration. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Quarterstaff|Silver Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Silver): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
