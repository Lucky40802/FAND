# Vitriol Draught III

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A draught incorporating Vitriol (Alchemical). Concentrated sulphuric acid stabilised in a crystalline carrier. The most potent acid compound available in mundane alchemy. Weapons coated in vitriol deal +1d8 acid damage. Armour corroded by vitriol loses 1 AC per hit (max -3) until repaired. Bonus: +1d8 Acid. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.
