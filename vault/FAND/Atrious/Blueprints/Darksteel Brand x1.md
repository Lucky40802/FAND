# Darksteel Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Darksteel (Metal). Steel worked in the Shadow Plane under conditions of absolute darkness. Darksteel blades are black and slightly warm. They deal necrotic damage and leave wounds that bleed shadow instead of blood. Bonus: +1.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Darksteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
