# Invisibility Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A clear liquid that becomes invisible in the vial when mixed correctly. Grants invisibility for 1 hour or until the drinker attacks or casts a spell. Unlike the spell, this effect does not require concentration.
