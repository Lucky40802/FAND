# Runesteel Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Legendary*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Runesteel construction means accepts more runes than standard steel and enchantments never decay.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Runesteel Longsword|Runesteel Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Runesteel Shortsword|Runesteel Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Runesteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
