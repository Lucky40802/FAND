# Isolation's Fragment

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Legendary*

This fragment only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Isolation]] directly, bound into a core of Lawful Iron (+1 vs chaotic creatures). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Lawful** (Potency 2, from Lawful Iron): Can't be disarmed or moved against your will. Once per day, bind a creature to a spoken promise; breaking it deals 2d6 psychic damage.
