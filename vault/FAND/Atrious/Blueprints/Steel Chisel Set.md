# Steel Chisel Set

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

Six steel chisels of different widths and profiles: flat, mortise, firmer, butt, paring, and gouge. The blades are hardened and tempered. The handles are fitted — not included, those are wood.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Chisel|Chisel]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
