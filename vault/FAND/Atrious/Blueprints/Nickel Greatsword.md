# Nickel Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Nickel construction means is corrosion-resistant and slightly magnetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Nickel Longsword|Nickel Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Nickel Shortsword|Nickel Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Nickel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
