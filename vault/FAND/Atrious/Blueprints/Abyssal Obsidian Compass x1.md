# Abyssal Obsidian Compass x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Very Rare*

A compass incorporating Abyssal Obsidian (Gemstone & Crystal). Obsidian formed in the Abyss under conditions of pure chaos and evil. Shards of it are constantly shifting in shape. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic. On a natural 20, a random chaotic effect occurs — the weapon's wielder rolls on the wild magic surge table. Bonus: +1d6 Necrotic, Chaos.
