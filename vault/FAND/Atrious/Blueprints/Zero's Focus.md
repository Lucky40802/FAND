# Zero's Focus

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Legendary*

This focus only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Zero]] directly, bound into a core of Coldstone (+1d6 Cold, Slow on hit). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Coldstone): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
