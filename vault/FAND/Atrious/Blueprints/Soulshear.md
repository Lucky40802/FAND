# Soulshear

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A spirit blade that is a literal extension of its wielder's own soul. In its sealed form it resembles an ordinary katana. Released by name, it reshapes itself according to the true nature of the soul that carries it — no two released forms are ever alike.
