# Starmetal Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Artifact*

A mechanical bow with a Starmetal prod and stock. The construction ignores magical damage resistance and holds enchantments permanently.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Starmetal Longbow|Starmetal Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Starmetal Shortbow|Starmetal Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Starmetal): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
