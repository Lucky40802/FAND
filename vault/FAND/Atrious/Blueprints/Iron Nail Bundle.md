# Iron Nail Bundle

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

Twenty iron nails for construction. Blacksmiths produce these by the thousand. Each nail is hand-cut and point-filed. Used in carpentry, shipbuilding, and occasionally as improvised caltrops.
