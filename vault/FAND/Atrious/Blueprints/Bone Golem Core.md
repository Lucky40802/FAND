# Bone Golem Core

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A soulsteel core that, when placed inside a constructed bone skeleton, animates it as a golem. The golem follows the animator's commands and has HP equal to twice the CR of the bones used.
