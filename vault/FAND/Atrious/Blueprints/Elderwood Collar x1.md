# Elderwood Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A collar incorporating Elderwood (Wood). Wood from elder trees that have lived over 1000 years. The wood has absorbed centuries of nature energy. Weapons deal +1d4 force damage. Bows made from elderwood have +60ft range. Once per day, a bow of elderwood can fire an arrow that splits mid-flight to hit two targets. Bonus: +2.
