# Soul Anchor

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A small iron spike inscribed with binding sigils, driven into the ground. Any spirit within 30ft of a soul anchor cannot move more than 30ft from the anchor. They can be spoken to, questioned, and dismissed but not…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ancestor Lantern|Ancestor Lantern]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
