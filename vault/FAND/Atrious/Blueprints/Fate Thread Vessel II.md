# Fate Thread Vessel II

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Fate Thread (Planar). Thread literally spun from the strands of fate. Items incorporating fate thread cannot be broken or destroyed by anything less than a deity. Once per long rest, the bearer can declare a roll they just made as the opposite result — a failure becomes a success or vice versa. Bonus: Fate manipulation.
