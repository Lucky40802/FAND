# Null Powder Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A dish incorporating Null Powder (Alchemical). A grey powder produced by grinding void nullstone. When applied to an object, it suppresses magical auras for 1 hour. Widely used by thieves to evade Detect Magic. Weapons coated in null powder deal non-magical damage regardless of what they're made of — useful against creatures that can only be har Bonus: Magic suppression.
