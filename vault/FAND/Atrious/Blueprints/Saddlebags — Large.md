# Saddlebags — Large

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Common*

Paired saddlebags for a Large mount, balanced and fitted to a standard saddle. Total capacity 100 pounds, 50 per side. The closures are double-buckled. Water-resistant but not waterproof.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Treat - Carnivore|Beast Treat - Carnivore]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Wound Wrap|Beast Wound Wrap]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Riding Saddle - Horse|Riding Saddle - Horse]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Saddle — Basic|Saddle — Basic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
