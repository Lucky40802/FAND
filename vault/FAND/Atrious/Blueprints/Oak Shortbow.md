# Oak Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A compact bow. The Oak limbs absorbs impact without splintering.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Oak Crossbow|Oak Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Oak Longbow|Oak Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
