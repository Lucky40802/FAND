# Corrosive Salt Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A dish incorporating Corrosive Salt (Alchemical). A crystallised acid salt that is safe to handle with leather gloves. When introduced to moisture or metal, it activates into full corrosive acid. Weapons with corrosive salt inlays deal +1d6 acid damage against armoured targets specifically. Bonus: +1d6 Acid.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Corrosive Salt Dish x1|Corrosive Salt Dish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
