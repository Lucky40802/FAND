# Night Vision Drops

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

Drops made from cave fish eye extract and darkbloom pollen. Applied to the eyes. Grant darkvision 30ft for 4 hours. The world appears silver-grey while active. Slight sensitivity to bright light during the duration.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Antidote to Common Poisons|Antidote to Common Poisons]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Burn Salve|Burn Salve]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cough Suppressant|Cough Suppressant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Emetic|Emetic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Fever Tea|Fever Tea]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Stomach Tonic|Iron Stomach Tonic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sleep Draught|Sleep Draught]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Smelling Salts|Smelling Salts]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
