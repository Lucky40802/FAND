# Reinforced Iron Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A kite shield of solid iron construction, banded at the rim. Heavier than wood-and-iron composite shields but significantly harder to cut through. Marks its carrier as serious.
