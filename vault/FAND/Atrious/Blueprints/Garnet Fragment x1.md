# Garnet Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Very Rare*

A fragment incorporating Garnet (Gemstone & Crystal). The gem of war and endurance. Deep red garnets channel force energy and combat conviction. Weapons set with garnets hit harder under pressure — the more wounds the wielder has taken, the more force the weapon delivers. Bonus: +2 Force.

**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Garnet): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
