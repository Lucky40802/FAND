# The Eternal Ward

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Very Rare*

A ward inscribed on every surface of a structure. The ward is permanent and self-maintaining — it draws power from the structure itself and never needs recharging. It prevents entry by all fiends, undead, and constructs.
