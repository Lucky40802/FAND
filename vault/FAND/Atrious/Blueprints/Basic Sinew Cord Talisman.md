# Basic Sinew Cord Talisman

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Common*

A basic talisman built from sinew cord, taught early in Dragon Carving training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Simple Sinew Cord Fetish|Simple Sinew Cord Fetish]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
