# Frost Crystal Arrow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Uncommon*

A broadhead arrowhead of glacial crystal. On hit deals 1d8+1d4 cold damage. On critical, the target is Restrained in ice until the end of their next turn.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
