# Iron Arrow Tips, 20

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

Twenty iron arrowheads in a leather pouch. Bodkin points for armour penetration, broadheads for unarmoured targets. A fletcher buys these and shafts them separately. A soldier carries extras of both.
