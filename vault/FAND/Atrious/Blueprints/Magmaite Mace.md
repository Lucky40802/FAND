# Magmaite Mace

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A flanged one-handed bludgeon. The Magmaite flanges is always hot enough to brand and amplifies fire enchantments.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Magmaite Warhammer|Magmaite Warhammer]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Magmaite): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
