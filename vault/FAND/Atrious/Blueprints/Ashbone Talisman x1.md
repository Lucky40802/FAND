# Ashbone Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A talisman incorporating Ashbone (Creature Harvest). The calcified ash-bone remains of powerful spellcasters. Used as a component in spellcasting foci and staves. Spells cast through items made with Ashbone have +1 to their save DC. The ashes remember what their original owner knew. Bonus: +1 Spell Save DC.
