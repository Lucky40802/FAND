# The Endless Edge

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Artifact*

A katana so long it is functionally a polearm in disguise — seven feet of single-edged blade meant to be wielded one-handed in movements that should be physically impossible for a blade of its length. Legend holds its last wielder never lost a duel, and never fought fair.
