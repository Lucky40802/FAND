# Ash Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Ash tip flexes rather than cracks under extreme stress. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ash Quarterstaff|Ash Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ash): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
