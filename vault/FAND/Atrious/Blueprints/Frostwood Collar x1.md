# Frostwood Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A collar incorporating Frostwood (Wood). Wood from trees in perpetually frozen environments. The grain is white and the wood itself is cold to touch always. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage. Creatures hit must CON save DC 12 or have their movement reduced by 5ft for 1 round. Bonus: +1d4 Cold.
