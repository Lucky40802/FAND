# Garnet Battle Focus

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Rare*

A garnet amulet that pulses faintly in combat. The wearer's attacks deal +1 damage for every 10 HP they are below their maximum, to a maximum of +5. Encourages fighting at full aggression regardless of personal injury.
