# Magmaite Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Magmaite edge is always hot enough to brand and amplifies fire enchantments. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Magmaite): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
