# Styx Water Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Rare*

A fragment incorporating Styx Water (Planar). Water from the River Styx, the boundary between life and death. Used to quench weapons during forging. Any creature struck by a Styx-quenched weapon must make a WIS save or forget the last minute of their life. Extremely dangerous to handle. Bonus: +Special.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 2, from Styx Water): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +2d6 cold to creatures in water.
