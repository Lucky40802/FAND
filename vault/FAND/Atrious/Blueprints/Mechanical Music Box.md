# Mechanical Music Box

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A music box with a 6-note comb and a 16-pin cylinder. Plays a fixed tune when wound. The tune is determined during crafting. Used as a gift, for entertainment, and as a signal device for those who know the tune.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clockwork Compass|Clockwork Compass]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
