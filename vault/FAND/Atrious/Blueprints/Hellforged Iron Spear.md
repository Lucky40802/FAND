# Hellforged Iron Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Hellforged Iron tip burns with hellfire and unnerves good-aligned opponents. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Quarterstaff|Hellforged Iron Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
