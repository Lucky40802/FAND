# Dragon Hatchling Collar

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Uncommon*

A collar of dragonscale and enchanted leather. Specifically designed for young dragons. The dragon obeys the tamer's commands and cannot grow past Huge while the collar is worn.
