# Starvine Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Very Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Starvine (Wood). A vine that grows on god-isles in the Astral Sea. Dries into a strong, flexible cord that always knows where true north is. Bows strung with starvine give +4 to navigation checks. Arrows fired from starvine-strung bows never miss due to wind. Bonus: Navigation +4.
