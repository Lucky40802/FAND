# Hardened Leather Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Uncommon*

Leather hardened by boiling in wax, then shaped and lacquered. Provides rigid protection over key areas while remaining lighter than metal armour. Popular with rogues and rangers who need mobility.
