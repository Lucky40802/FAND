# Veilcloth Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Veilcloth construction is invisible in darkness and grants advantage on opening strikes from concealment.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Hand Axe|Veilcloth Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
