# Ghost Food

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Uncommon*

Food prepared using the recipe of a deceased person. The cook recreates the experience of eating what the dead person made. Once per day a spirit within 60ft may be offered the food — they will stop to eat it.
