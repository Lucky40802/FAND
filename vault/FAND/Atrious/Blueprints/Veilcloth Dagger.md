# Veilcloth Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Veilcloth edge is invisible in darkness and grants advantage on opening strikes from concealment. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
