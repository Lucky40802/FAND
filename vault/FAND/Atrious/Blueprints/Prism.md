# Prism

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Common*

A triangular prism of clear crystal that splits white light into its component colours. Useful in magical colour-based applications.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Magnifier Array|Crystal Magnifier Array]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Orb — Light Storage|Crystal Orb — Light Storage]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Sound Bowl|Crystal Sound Bowl]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Water Purifier|Crystal Water Purifier]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
