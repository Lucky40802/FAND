# Rune of Warning

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A tripwire rune inscribed on a flat surface. When crossed by a creature of Small or larger size, it sends a mental alarm to the inscriber regardless of distance. The alarm is silent. Lasts 8 hours.
