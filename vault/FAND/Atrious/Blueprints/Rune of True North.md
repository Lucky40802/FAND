# Rune of True North

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a flat stone that always points to magnetic north when the stone is placed on a flat surface. More reliable than a compass in heavily magnetised areas. Cannot be fooled by lodestones.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Freshness|Rune of Freshness]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silence Rune|Silence Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wall Spike Array|Wall Spike Array]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Weight Rune|Weight Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Window Bar Set|Window Bar Set]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
