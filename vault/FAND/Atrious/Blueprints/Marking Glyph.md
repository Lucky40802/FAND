# Marking Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph that marks the passing of any creature through it invisibly. The inscriber can check the glyph later to see if it was triggered.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Alarm Rune|Alarm Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Door Glyph — Warning|Door Glyph — Warning]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hearth Glyph|Hearth Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Heat Rune|Heat Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Light Glyph|Light Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Light Rune — Persistent|Light Rune — Persistent]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Preservation Glyph|Preservation Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Dryness|Rune of Dryness]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wall Bracket|Wall Bracket]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Warning Glyph|Warning Glyph]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
