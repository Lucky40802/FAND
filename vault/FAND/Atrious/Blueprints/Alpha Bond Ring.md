# Alpha Bond Ring

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A ring worn by the tamer. While wearing it, they are treated as the alpha of any beast pack within 60ft. Beasts of CR 5 or lower automatically defer to them.
