# The Soul Cage - Greater

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

An advanced soul cage that holds up to 7 souls simultaneously. The holder can expend captured souls to restore up to 4d10 HP, cast Dominate Monster, or gain advantage on any one check.
