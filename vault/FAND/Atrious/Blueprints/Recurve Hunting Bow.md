# Recurve Hunting Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A more powerful version of the basic shortbow, with limbs that curve away from the archer when unstrung. The recurve design stores more energy and delivers more force. Standard tool of foresters and rangers.
