# Holding Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph inscribed onto a floor section. A creature that steps on the glyph must succeed a STR save DC 14 or be Restrained — their feet are held to the surface as if magnetised. Duration 1 minute. Triggers once then…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Fear Glyph|Fear Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Float Rune|Float Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Reveal Glyph|Reveal Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Memory|Rune of Memory]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Slow Poison|Rune of Slow Poison]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sharpness Rune|Sharpness Rune]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
