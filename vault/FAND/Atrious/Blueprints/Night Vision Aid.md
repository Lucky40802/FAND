# Night Vision Aid

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Common*

A specialized artifact, built for reading the stars and the space between them using Comet Dust. Unremarkable to look at, but it does exactly what it's meant to. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Simple Comet Dust Compass|Simple Comet Dust Compass]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
