# Mithral Chain Shirt

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A shirt of mithral rings reaching to the hip. Mithral chainmail weighs half what steel chainmail does and imposes no disadvantage on stealth. The rings are bright and never rust. Feels like wearing expensive cloth.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Mithral): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
