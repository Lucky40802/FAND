# Voidsap Wood Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Very Rare*

Weapons deal +1d4 force damage. Staves made from voidsap wood absorb 5 points of magical damage per round. Bonus: +1 Force.
