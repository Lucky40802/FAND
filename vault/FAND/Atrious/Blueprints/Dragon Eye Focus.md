# Dragon Eye Focus

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Uncommon*

A preserved dragon eye mounted in a bone frame, used as a spellcasting focus. The eye still partially sees — while using this focus, the caster can detect the presence of magical auras within 30ft as a bonus action.…
