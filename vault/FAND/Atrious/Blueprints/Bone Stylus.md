# Bone Stylus

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Common*

A writing stylus carved from a smaller dragon finger bone. Used for inscribing runes into wax or clay. Preserves detail better than metal.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragon Claw Pendant|Dragon Claw Pendant]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
