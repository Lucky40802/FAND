# Arcanite Tablet x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Very Rare*

A tablet incorporating Arcanite (Arcane Material). A shimmering blue-white substance that forms when magical energy is concentrated in one location for centuries. Raw magic in a solid state. All spells cast through arcanite items have +1 to attack rolls and save DCs. The substance slowly fades over 100 years as it releases its stored energy. Bonus: +1 to all spell rolls.
