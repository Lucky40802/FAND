# Thundercrystal Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Rare*

A core incorporating Thundercrystal (Gemstone & Crystal). Crystal formed by repeated lightning strikes at altitude. It hums audibly and crackles with stored energy. Weapons deal +1d4 thunder damage. On a critical hit, the thunder is so loud all creatures within 10ft must CON save DC 13 or be deafened for 1 round. Bonus: +1d4 Thunder.
