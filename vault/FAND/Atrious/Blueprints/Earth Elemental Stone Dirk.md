# Earth Elemental Stone Dirk

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Dirk reforged with Earth Elemental Stone (Elemental) worked into its construction. The stone core of a destroyed earth elemental. Items incorporating this stone grant tremorsense out to 30ft while in contact with the ground. Armour from it provides resistance to bludgeoning damage. Bonus: Tremorsense 30ft.

## Material Property
**Earthbound** (Potency 1, from Earth Elemental Stone): Tremorsense 5 ft while touching the ground. Weapon: +1d6 damage against objects and structures.
