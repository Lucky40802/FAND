# Veilcloth Vessel II

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Veilcloth (Planar). Cloth woven from shadow-stuff in the Shadowfell. Veilcloth is invisible in darkness and hard to see in dim light. Weapons wrapped in veilcloth gain shadow damage and advantage on the first attack from concealment. Bonus: +2 Shadow.
