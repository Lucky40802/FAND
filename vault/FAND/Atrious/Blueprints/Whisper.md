# Whisper

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A sword you hear before you see it — a faint whisper of steel against nothing, and then the wound is already there. Whisper is made of shadowsteel wrapped in veilcloth. The blade is invisible in any light below full…

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
