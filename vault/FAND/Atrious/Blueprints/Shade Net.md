# Shade Net

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

A large net of veilcloth. When thrown over a light source, it extinguishes it. Can also restrain creatures — those Restrained in it have disadvantage on all checks.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Night Shroud - Personal|Night Shroud - Personal]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Cloak Lining|Shadow Cloak Lining]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Net|Shadow Net]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
