# Ivory Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Uncommon*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Ivory blade holds carving detail and is structurally dense.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ivory Greatsword|Ivory Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ivory Shortsword|Ivory Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Ivory): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
