# Steel Cutlass

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

The iron cutlass's more refined cousin. Steel holds a finer edge, is lighter for the same strength, and doesn't rust as fast. The preferred sidearm of naval officers and privateers who can afford it.
