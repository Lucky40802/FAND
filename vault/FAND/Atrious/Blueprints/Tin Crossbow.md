# Tin Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A mechanical bow with a Tin prod and stock. The construction flexes rather than snaps under lateral force.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tin Longbow|Tin Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tin Shortbow|Tin Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Tin): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
