# Feywild Silver Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Feywild Silver edge changes colour with mood and can force shapeshifters back to true form. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Feywild Silver): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
