# Wild Compact

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A nature-magic contract between a tamer and the spirit of a beast type. The tamer can summon 1d6 beasts of that type per day — they arrive within 1 hour from anywhere on the plane.
