# Bloodiron Targe

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Bloodiron): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
