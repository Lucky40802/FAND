# Steel Mace Head

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A flanged steel mace head, unhandled. Six flanges of increasing height toward the rim. Drilled for a 1-inch haft. The flanges are forged integral to the body. Assembles with a haft of the crafter's choice.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
