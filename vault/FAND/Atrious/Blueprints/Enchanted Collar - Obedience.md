# Enchanted Collar - Obedience

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Uncommon*

A collar with a minor compulsion enchantment. The beast wearing it has advantage on all training-related Wisdom saving throws and cannot willingly harm its tamer.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Enchanted Lead - Compliance|Enchanted Lead - Compliance]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
