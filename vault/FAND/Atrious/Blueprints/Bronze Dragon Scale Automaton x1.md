# Bronze Dragon Scale Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Rare*

A automaton incorporating Bronze Dragon Scale (Metal). Scales from a bronze dragon. Armour provides resistance to lightning. Weapons deal +1d6 lightning. Once per day, strike the weapon against a surface to create a lightning bolt — 60ft line, 4d8 lightning, DEX save DC 15 for half. Bonus: +1d6 Lightning.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bronze Dragon Scale Engine x1|Bronze Dragon Scale Engine x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
