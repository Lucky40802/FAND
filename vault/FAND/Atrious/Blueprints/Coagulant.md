# Coagulant

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A liquid that, when applied to a wound, stops bleeding immediately. More effective than standard wound sealant — also begins the healing process, restoring 1 HP per application. Effective even on severe lacerations.
