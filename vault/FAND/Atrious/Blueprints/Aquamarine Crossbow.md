# Aquamarine Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A mechanical bow with a Aquamarine prod and stock. The construction channels wind energy and can redirect projectiles on hit.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Aquamarine Longbow|Aquamarine Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Aquamarine Shortbow|Aquamarine Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Aquamarine): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
