# Skymetal Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Rare*

A slab incorporating Skymetal (Metal). Metal that fell from the heavens. Lighter than steel, harder than iron. Skymetal weapons deal +1d6 radiant damage against creatures of Fiend or Undead type. Cannot be enchanted further — already saturated with cosmic energy. Bonus: +2.

## Material Property
**Skyborne** (Potency 1, from Skymetal): +5 ft speed. At Potency 3+ you take no falling damage; at Potency 6+ you gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.
