# Blood Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

Armour treated to harden on contact with blood. When the wearer is hit by a melee attack, their AC increases by 1 for 1 minute as the blood treatment hardens. Maximum +4.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Bloodstone): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
