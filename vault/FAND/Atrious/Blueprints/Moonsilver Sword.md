# Moonsilver Sword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A longsword of moonsilver. Deals radiant damage. Under a full moon, deals maximum damage automatically. Against lycanthropes, deals double damage and forces a WIS save DC 16 or immediate reversion to humanoid form.
