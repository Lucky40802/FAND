# Undine's Relic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Legendary*

This relic only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Undine]] directly, bound into a core of Evocation Crystal (+3 Fire/Lightning/Thunder (choose on craft)). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
