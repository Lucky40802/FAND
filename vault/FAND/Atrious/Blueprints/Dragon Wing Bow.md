# Dragon Wing Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Uncommon*

A bow made from the thin bones of a dragon wing. Lighter than ironwood and more flexible. Arrows fly faster and flatter.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
