# Bloodstone Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Bloodstone construction grows warm near open wounds and empowers blood magic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bloodstone Hand Axe|Bloodstone Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Bloodstone): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
