# Lich Dust Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Very Rare*

A fetish incorporating Lich Dust (Creature Harvest). The dust remaining from a destroyed lich's phylactery. Extremely rare. Items incorporating lich dust add +1 to all Necromancy spell save DCs and spell attack rolls. Undead created using spells cast through lich dust items have +1 to all their ability scores. Bonus: +1 to Necromancy spells.
