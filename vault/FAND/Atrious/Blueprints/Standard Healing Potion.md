# Standard Healing Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

The standard healing potion formula. A sweet-tasting red liquid that closes wounds and restores 2d4+2 HP over 1 minute after consumption. The ruby provides a consistent channel for the healing energy. Shelf life 1 year.
