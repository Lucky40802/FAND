# Chimera Barding

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

Modular barding designed for the irregular body shape of a chimera. Each section provides +2 AC to that section of the body independently.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonscale): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
