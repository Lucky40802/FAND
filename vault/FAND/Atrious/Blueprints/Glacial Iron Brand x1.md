# Glacial Iron Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Rare*

A brand incorporating Glacial Iron (Metal). Iron formed under centuries of glacial pressure at extreme cold. The metal never reaches room temperature — it is always cold to the touch. Weapons made from it deal cold damage and slow targets on hit. Bonus: +2 Cold.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
