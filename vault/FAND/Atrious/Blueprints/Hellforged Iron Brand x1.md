# Hellforged Iron Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Rare*

A brand incorporating Hellforged Iron (Metal). Iron worked in hellfire and quenched in brimstone. Always slightly warm. Deals fire damage. Creatures of good alignment take a -1 to attacks against the wielder while the weapon is drawn. Each weapon carries a minor binding contract. Bonus: +2 Fire.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Hellforged Iron): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
