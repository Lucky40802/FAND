# Feywild Approach Guide

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Uncommon*

A specialized artifact, built for reading the stars and the space between them using Comet Dust. Unremarkable to look at, but it does exactly what it's meant to. A minor variation on a well-worn design.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Approach Guide|Shadow Approach Guide]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sturdy Comet Dust Compass|Sturdy Comet Dust Compass]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
