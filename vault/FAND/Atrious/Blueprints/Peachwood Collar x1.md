# Peachwood Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A collar incorporating Peachwood (Wood). In certain traditions, peachwood is believed to ward off evil spirits. Items made from peachwood repel undead — undead creatures take 1d4 radiant damage when they touch peachwood items, and must save DC 12 or be unable to cross a threshold sealed with it. Bonus: Spirit ward.
