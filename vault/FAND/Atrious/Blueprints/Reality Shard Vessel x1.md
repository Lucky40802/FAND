# Reality Shard Vessel x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Reality Shard (Planar). A shard of crystallised reality from locations where the fabric of existence cracked. Items made from reality shards are partially unreal — they exist slightly outside of normal space and cannot be found by Detect Magic or similarly targeted by most spells. Bonus: Reality alteration. A minor variation on a well-worn design.
