# Potion of Growth

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A red-orange liquid that causes the drinker to double in size for 1 minute. Weapons and armour grow proportionally. Strength increases, speed is unchanged. The crash back to normal size is jarring.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Diminution|Potion of Diminution]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Speaking|Potion of Speaking]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
