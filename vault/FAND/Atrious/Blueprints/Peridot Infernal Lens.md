# Peridot Infernal Lens

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Uncommon*

A lens of peridot that shows the true nature of contracts and binding agreements. When viewing a written contract through this lens, any binding magic — including infernal contracts — becomes visible as glowing red text.
