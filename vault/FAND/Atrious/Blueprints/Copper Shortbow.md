# Copper Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A compact bow. The Copper limbs is soft and dulls quickly but is cheap to replace.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Crossbow|Copper Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Longbow|Copper Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Copper): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
