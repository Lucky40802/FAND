# Hide Armour for Beast

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Common*

Fitted armour for a beast companion, sized to Medium creatures. Provides AC 13 (natural armour equivalent). The chitin plates are riveted to leather backing and fitted around the legs and neck for movement.

## Material Property
**Hardened** (Potency 1, from Chitin): Armor or shield: +1 AC. Weapon: can't be broken, and ignores 1 Natural AC.
