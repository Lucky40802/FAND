# Void Iron Nodachi

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Nodachi reforged with Void Iron (Metal) worked into its construction. Iron smelted from matter compressed from the void between planes. Appears as extremely dark iron with faint purple sheen. Weapons deal force damage and pass through magical barriers as if they were open air. Bonus: +2.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Void Iron): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
