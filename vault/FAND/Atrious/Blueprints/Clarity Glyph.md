# Clarity Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph carved into a work surface. Any magical text placed on the surface becomes readable in any language while in contact.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clarity Glyph - Surface|Clarity Glyph - Surface]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clarity Glyph - Window|Clarity Glyph - Window]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
