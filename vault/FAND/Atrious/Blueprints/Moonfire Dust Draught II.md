# Moonfire Dust Draught II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Very Rare*

A draught incorporating Moonfire Dust (Alchemical). A fine silver dust produced by grinding moonstone through a specific alchemical process. Applied to a weapon, it glows in darkness and deals +1d4 radiant. Applied to armour, it makes the wearer glow — useful for visibility but terrible for stealth. Bonus: +1d4 Radiant.
