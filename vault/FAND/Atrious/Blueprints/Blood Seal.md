# Blood Seal

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A wax seal pressed with a drop of the signer's blood. The seal is magically binding — it cannot be replicated without the signer's actual blood. Used for contracts, wills, and royal documents that must be unforgeable. …

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Ink|Blood Ink]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
