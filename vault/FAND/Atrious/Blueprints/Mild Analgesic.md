# Mild Analgesic

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A clear liquid that numbs pain without impairing function. Allows a person to function normally despite significant injury. Does not heal. Has caused people to aggravate injuries by not feeling the warning pain.
