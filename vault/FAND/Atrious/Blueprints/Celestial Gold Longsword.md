# Celestial Gold Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Celestial Gold blade glows with divine light and is catastrophic to fiends.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Celestial Gold Greatsword|Celestial Gold Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Celestial Gold Shortsword|Celestial Gold Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Gold): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
