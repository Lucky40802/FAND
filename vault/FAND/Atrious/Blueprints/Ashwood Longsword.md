# Ashwood Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Ashwood blade burns without being consumed and deals fire damage.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ashwood Greatsword|Ashwood Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ashwood Shortsword|Ashwood Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ashwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
