# Hexite Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Rare*

A cloak incorporating Hexite (Arcane Material). A dull grey metal worked by hags using curses as part of the smelting process. Weapons of hexite apply a random minor curse on a critical hit — the target makes a CHA save DC 14 or suffers a minor curse for 1 week (hair falls out, voice changes pitch, objects fall from their hands, etc.). Bonus: Curse on critical hit.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Hexite): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
