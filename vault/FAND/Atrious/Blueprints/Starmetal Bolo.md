# Starmetal Bolo

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Bolo reforged with Starmetal (Metal) worked into its construction. Iron from the void between stars, carrying cosmic energy from its origin. Heavier than normal iron but holds enchantments permanently without decay. Strikes with starmetal weapons ignore magical damage resistance. Bonus: +3 Radiant.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Starmetal): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
