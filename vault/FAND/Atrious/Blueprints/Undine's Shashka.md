# Undine's Shashka

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A Shashka that only functions properly because it channels a fragment of [[Undine]] directly, cast in a core of Glacial Iron (+2 Cold). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
