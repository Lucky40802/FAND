# Cold Crystal Weapon Insert

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Uncommon*

A sapphire chip shaped to insert into a weapon. Once installed, the weapon deals +1d6 cold damage on hit. On a critical hit, the target must make a CON save or be slowed by 10ft until the start of their next turn.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Sapphire): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
