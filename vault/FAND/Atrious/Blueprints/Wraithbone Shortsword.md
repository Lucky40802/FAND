# Wraithbone Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Wraithbone construction ignores non-magical armour and leaves unnaturally cold wounds.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wraithbone Greatsword|Wraithbone Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wraithbone Longsword|Wraithbone Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Wraithbone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
