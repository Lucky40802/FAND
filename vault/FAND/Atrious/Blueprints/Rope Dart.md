# Rope Dart

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A weighted iron dart tied to ten feet of braided rope. Thrown to pierce, then yanked back. Requires considerable skill to use effectively but in trained hands becomes a terrifying reach weapon.
