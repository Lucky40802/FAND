# Ancestor Lantern

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soul Anchor|Soul Anchor]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
