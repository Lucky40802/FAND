# Adamantine Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

A mechanical bow with a Adamantine prod and stock. The construction ignores object hardness and cannot be destroyed by normal means.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Adamantine Longbow|Adamantine Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Adamantine Shortbow|Adamantine Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 3, from Adamantine): Weapon: +2 damage. Armor: +2 Gear AC.
