# Behemoth Hide Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A talisman incorporating Behemoth Hide (Creature Harvest). The shed outer skin of a behemoth creature. The hide is thicker than most armour even as raw material. Armour from behemoth hide provides bludgeoning resistance and doubles the bearer's HP for the purpose of being knocked unconscious by area effects. Bonus: Bludgeoning resistance.
