# Rune of Thunder

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

Inscribed on a weapon. On a hit, the rune releases a crack of thunder. Creatures within 10ft of the target must CON save DC 14 or be deafened for 1 round.
