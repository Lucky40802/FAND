# Assegai

*Spear — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A Assegai, a real-world spear design drawn from martial history. Forged in iron or steel by any competent smith once the pattern is known — the difficulty lies in learning the proportions and temper the design calls for, not the raw materials.
