# Nightwalker Boots

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

Boots soled in veilcloth with shadow obsidian studs. The wearer makes no sound and has advantage on Stealth checks in dim light.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadow Obsidian): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
