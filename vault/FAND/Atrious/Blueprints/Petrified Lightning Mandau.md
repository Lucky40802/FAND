# Petrified Lightning Mandau

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Mandau reforged with Petrified Lightning (Elemental) worked into its construction. Sand that has been fused into glass tubes by a lightning strike, then carefully extracted and worked. Called fulgurite by scholars. Weapons deal +2d6 lightning damage. Critical hits create a chain lightning effect hitting the nearest additional target for 1d6 lightning. Bonus: +2d6 Lightning.

## Material Property
**Petrifying** (Potency 3, from Petrified Lightning): Weapon: on a hit, Constitution save (DC 14) or slowed. A creature slowed twice is petrified until the end of its next turn.
