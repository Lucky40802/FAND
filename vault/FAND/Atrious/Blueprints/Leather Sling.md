# Leather Sling

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Common*

A strip of cured leather cut to cradle a stone or lead shot. No metal required, no forge needed. The simplest ranged weapon a person can carry. In the right hands, devastatingly accurate.
