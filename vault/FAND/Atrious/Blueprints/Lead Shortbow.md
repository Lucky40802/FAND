# Lead Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A compact bow. The Lead limbs absorbs magical residue and is extraordinarily heavy.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Lead Crossbow|Lead Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Lead Longbow|Lead Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
