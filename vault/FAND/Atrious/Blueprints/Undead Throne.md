# Undead Throne

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A throne of soulsteel and wraithbone. The occupant has total command of all undead within 1 mile as if they cast Dominate Monster on each. The command is immediate and requires no concentration.
