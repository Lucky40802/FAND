# Shadow Mantle

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

A mantle of veilcloth set with shadow obsidian chips along the shoulders. The mantle absorbs light around the wearer — a 5ft radius of magical darkness follows them in dim conditions. Does not affect bright light…

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadow Obsidian): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
