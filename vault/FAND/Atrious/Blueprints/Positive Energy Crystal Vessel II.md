# Positive Energy Crystal Vessel II

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A vessel incorporating Positive Energy Crystal (Planar). Crystal from the Positive Energy Plane. Constantly radiates healing energy — bearers recover 1 HP per minute. Weapons deal radiant damage. The crystal is dangerous to undead — undead creatures within 10ft of positive energy crystal items take 1d4 radiant per round. Bonus: Healing radiation.
