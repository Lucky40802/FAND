# Vital Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

Full plate treated in blood alchemy. The wearer regenerates 2 HP per round while below their HP maximum. This regeneration is suppressed for 1 round after taking radiant or fire damage.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
