# Copper Candlestick

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A single copper candlestick, plain and functional. The base is weighted for stability. Holds a standard taper. Turns green with age. The kind of object found in every tavern and home across Atrious.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Pot|Copper Pot]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
