# Wyrmling Bile Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Rare*

A brand incorporating Wyrmling Bile (Dragon Material). Bile extracted from dragon wyrmlings — a process that does not harm them as they produce surplus. The bile type matches the wyrmling species. Items coated in wyrmling bile deal +1 damage of the appropriate element. Wyrmlings take full damage from weapons made with their own species' bile. Bonus: +1 elemental.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Wyrmling Bile): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
