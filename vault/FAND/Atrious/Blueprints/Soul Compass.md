# Soul Compass

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

A compass that points toward the soul of a specific creature the binder has previously marked. Works across planes. The needle spins wildly if the target has died and their soul has departed the current plane.
