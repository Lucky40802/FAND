# Bloodfire Ruby Sigil x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Very Rare*

A sigil incorporating Bloodfire Ruby (Gemstone & Crystal). A deep red ruby that seems to have fire and blood swirling within it. Weapons deal +1d4 fire and +1d4 necrotic simultaneously. Creatures killed by bloodfire ruby weapons cannot be raised as undead — their life force is consumed entirely. Bonus: +1d6 Fire and Necrotic.
