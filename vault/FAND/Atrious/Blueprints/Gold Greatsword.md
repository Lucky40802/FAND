# Gold Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Gold construction means channels divine energy but is too soft without reinforcement.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Longsword|Gold Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Shortsword|Gold Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Gold): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
