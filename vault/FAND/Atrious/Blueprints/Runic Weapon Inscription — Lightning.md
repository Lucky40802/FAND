# Runic Weapon Inscription — Lightning

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A lightning rune sequence inscribed into a blade. The blade deals an additional 1d6 lightning damage on hit. On a critical hit, the lightning arcs to one adjacent creature for 1d6 lightning damage.
