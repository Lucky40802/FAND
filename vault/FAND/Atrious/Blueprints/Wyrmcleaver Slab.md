# Wyrmcleaver Slab

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A slab of iron shaped like a sword and far too heavy for any ordinary warrior. It was made for killing things many times larger than a person, and only someone of supernatural strength can swing it.
