# Rune of Door

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune inscribed on a wall that temporarily creates a door-shaped opening 3ft wide and 7ft tall for 10 minutes per day. The opening closes automatically when the time expires and is seamless from either side when closed.
