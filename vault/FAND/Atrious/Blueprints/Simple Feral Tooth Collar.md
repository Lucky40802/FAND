# Simple Feral Tooth Collar

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Uncommon*

A simple collar built from feral tooth, taught early in Beast Taming training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Elemental Beast Collar|Elemental Beast Collar]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Serpent Transport Tube|Serpent Transport Tube]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
