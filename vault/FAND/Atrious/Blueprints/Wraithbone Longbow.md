# Wraithbone Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Legendary*

A 6-foot bow. The Wraithbone stave ignores non-magical armour and leaves unnaturally cold wounds.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wraithbone Crossbow|Wraithbone Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wraithbone Shortbow|Wraithbone Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Wraithbone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
