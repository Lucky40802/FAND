# Dragonscale Belt

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Common*

A belt of dragon scales set in hardened leather. The scales provide the elemental resistance of the original dragon to the wearer's torso region. This is a narrow area but relevant — most attacks target the torso.
