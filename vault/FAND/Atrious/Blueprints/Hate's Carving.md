# Hate's Carving

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Legendary*

This carving only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Hate]] directly, bound into a core of Bloodwood (+1d4 Necrotic or Healing). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
