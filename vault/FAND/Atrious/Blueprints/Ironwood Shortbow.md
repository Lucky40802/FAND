# Ironwood Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A compact bow. The Ironwood limbs is as hard as iron while remaining lighter than steel.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ironwood Crossbow|Ironwood Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ironwood Longbow|Ironwood Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ironwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
