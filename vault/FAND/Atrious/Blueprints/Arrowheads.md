# Arrowheads

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

Broadhead arrowheads, a batch of 24. Cut and filed from plate then slit for the shaft. Each one identical or the archer will notice.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crossbow Prod|Crossbow Prod]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Porthole Cover|Porthole Cover]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
