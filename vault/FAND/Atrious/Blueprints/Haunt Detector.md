# Haunt Detector

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A pendulum of bone on an iron chain. Swings in the direction of the nearest undead or bound soul within 60ft. The swinging is slow for distant targets and rapid for close ones.
