# Rune of Accuracy

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A rune inscribed on a weapon or ammunition that improves its flight characteristics. The inscribed weapon or piece of ammunition gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls. This stacks with magical enhancement bonuses.
