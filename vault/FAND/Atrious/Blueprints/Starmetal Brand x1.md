# Starmetal Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Starmetal (Metal). Iron from the void between stars, carrying cosmic energy from its origin. Heavier than normal iron but holds enchantments permanently without decay. Strikes with starmetal weapons ignore magical damage resistance. Bonus: +3 Radiant.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Starmetal): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
