# Ashwood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Ashwood (Wood). Wood from trees that grow in volcanic regions near the Plane of Fire. The wood is charred black on the outside and blood-red inside. It burns without being consumed. Weapons made from ashwood deal fire damage and never catch fire from external sources. Bonus: +2 Fire.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ashwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
