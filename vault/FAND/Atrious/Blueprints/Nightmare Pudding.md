# Nightmare Pudding

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Uncommon*

A dessert that causes vivid nightmares when the eater sleeps. During the nightmare the eater can converse with their own fears given form. The eater gains one Inspiration but cannot benefit from a short rest.
