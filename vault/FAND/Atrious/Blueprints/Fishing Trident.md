# Fishing Trident

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A three-tined iron fork on a long haft, originally designed for spearing fish in shallow water. Heavier than a proper combat trident but functional as a weapon if you find yourself in trouble near the docks.
