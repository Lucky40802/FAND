# Ash Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A 6-foot staff of Ash. The material flexes rather than cracks under extreme stress.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ash Spear|Ash Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
