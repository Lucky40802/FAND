# The Iron Covenant

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A longsword of adamantine with platinum inlay runes along the blade. The runes are the Covenant — a binding oath of justice that the sword enforces. Against oath-breakers and the faithless, it deals +3d8 radiant damage.
