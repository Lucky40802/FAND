# Gold Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Gold head channels divine energy but is too soft without reinforcement. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gold Greataxe|Gold Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Gold): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
