# Ether Stone Vessel x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A vessel incorporating Ether Stone (Planar). Stone from the Border Ethereal, maintained in stable form in the material plane. Items made from ether stone allow the bearer to see into the Ethereal Plane as if using See Invisibility. Weapons can strike Ethereal creatures as if they were material. Bonus: Ethereal sight. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.
