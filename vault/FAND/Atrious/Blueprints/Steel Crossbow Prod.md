# Steel Crossbow Prod

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

The prod — the bow — of a heavy crossbow, forged from spring steel. The prod generates 500 pounds of draw weight. Requires a windlass or cranequin to span. Not a complete crossbow; the stock and mechanism are separate.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Crossbow|Steel Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Longbow|Steel Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Shortbow|Steel Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
