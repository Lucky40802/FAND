# Moonstone Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A focus incorporating Moonstone (Gemstone & Crystal). The gem of the moon and fey. Moonstone channels lunar and faerie energy. It glows faintly at night. Weapons set with moonstone deal radiant damage against fiends and undead. Bonus: +2 Radiant.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonstone Quarterstaff|Moonstone Quarterstaff]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonstone Spear|Moonstone Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Moonstone): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
