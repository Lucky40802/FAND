# Major Runic Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

Armour inscribed with a full protective rune sequence. Grants +2 to AC, resistance to one chosen damage type, and advantage on saving throws against magic while the rune sequence is active.
