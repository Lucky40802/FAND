# Cavalry Saber

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A slightly curved steel blade optimised for one-handed use from horseback. The curve aids the draw-cut when riding past a target. The guard is a simple D-ring to protect the hand during the charge.
