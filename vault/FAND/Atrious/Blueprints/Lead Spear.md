# Lead Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Lead tip absorbs magical residue and is extraordinarily heavy. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Lead Quarterstaff|Lead Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Lead): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
