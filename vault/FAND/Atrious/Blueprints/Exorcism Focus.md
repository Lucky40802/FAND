# Exorcism Focus

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A silver-tipped iron rod used in exorcism rituals. When used in an exorcism attempt against a possessed creature, the possessing entity must make a CHA save DC 12 or immediately be expelled regardless of its power.
