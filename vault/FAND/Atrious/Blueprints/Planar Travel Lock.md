# Planar Travel Lock

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Very Rare*

Prevents all planar travel in or out indefinitely. Requires 9th-level to dispel. The finish differs from smith to smith, though the core work is identical.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Planar Lock Glyph|Planar Lock Glyph]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
