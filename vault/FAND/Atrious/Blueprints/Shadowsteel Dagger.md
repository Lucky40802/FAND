# Shadowsteel Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Shadowsteel edge is silent and deals shadow damage, partially incorporealising wounds. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
