# Crystal Ball of Seeing

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Uncommon*

A fist-sized crystal sphere of exceptional clarity. Functions as a scrying device — a creature can use it to cast Scrying without spell slots once per day, using their spellcasting ability modifier for the save DC.
