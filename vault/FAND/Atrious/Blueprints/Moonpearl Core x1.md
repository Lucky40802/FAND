# Moonpearl Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Rare*

A core incorporating Moonpearl (Gemstone & Crystal). Pearls gathered from the deepest ocean on the night of a new moon. They glow faintly silver in darkness. Used in illusion-focused items. Moonpearl components add +1 to all illusion spell save DCs. The pearl shifts colour subtly based on the moon's phase. Bonus: +1 illusion spell DC.
