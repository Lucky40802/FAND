# Ratchet and Pawl

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A mechanism that allows motion in one direction only. Used in winches, gates, and weapon mechanisms. Cannot be reversed without releasing the pawl.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Trigger Assembly|Trigger Assembly]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
