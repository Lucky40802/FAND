# The Blade That Weeps

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A sword that absorbs the grief of everyone it kills. The blade makes no sound when it cuts — only a soft exhalation, like a last breath. After enough deaths, the accumulated sorrow begins to leak out in quiet moaning…
