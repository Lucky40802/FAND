# Necromancy Stone Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Rare*

A cloak incorporating Necromancy Stone (Arcane Material). A matte black stone with faint grey veining that pulses very slowly, like a heartbeat that never quite stops. Necromancy Stones form in places where vast numbers died and the necrotic energy had nowhere to go. They channel death itself — weapons set with them deal necrotic damage, and creatures kill Bonus: +3 Necrotic.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Necromancy Stone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
