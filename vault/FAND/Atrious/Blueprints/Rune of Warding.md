# Rune of Warding

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A single iron disc bearing the ward rune. When placed at a doorway or threshold, creatures of a chosen type (undead, fey, fiends) cannot cross it without a WIS save DC 10. The rune fades after one week.
