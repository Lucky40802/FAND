# Simple Brass Gear Automaton

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A simple automaton built from brass gear, taught early in Clockwork Engineering training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Jeweller's Loupe|Jeweller's Loupe]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pin Vice|Pin Vice]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
