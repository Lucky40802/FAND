# Moonpearl Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Rare*

A fragment incorporating Moonpearl (Gemstone & Crystal). Pearls gathered from the deepest ocean on the night of a new moon. They glow faintly silver in darkness. Used in illusion-focused items. Moonpearl components add +1 to all illusion spell save DCs. The pearl shifts colour subtly based on the moon's phase. Bonus: +1 illusion spell DC.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Moonpearl): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
