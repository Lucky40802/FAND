# Hellforged Iron Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Hellforged Iron blade burns with hellfire and unnerves good-aligned opponents.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Greatsword|Hellforged Iron Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Shortsword|Hellforged Iron Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Hellforged Iron): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
