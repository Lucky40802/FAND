# Sunfire Citrine Sigil x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Very Rare*

A sigil incorporating Sunfire Citrine (Gemstone & Crystal). A bright yellow-orange citrine that concentrates solar energy. In full sunlight, weapons deal +1d6 radiant. In darkness, weapons deal +1 radiant — reduced but not zero, as the stored sunlight releases slowly. Bonus: +1d6 Radiant.
