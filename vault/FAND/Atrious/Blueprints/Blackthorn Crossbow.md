# Blackthorn Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Uncommon*

A mechanical bow with a Blackthorn prod and stock. The construction has a natural affinity for boundary magic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blackthorn Longbow|Blackthorn Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blackthorn Shortbow|Blackthorn Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Blackthorn): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
