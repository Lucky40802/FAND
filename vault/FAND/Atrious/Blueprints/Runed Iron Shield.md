# Runed Iron Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

An iron shield with a single warding rune etched into the boss. The rune is functional, not decorative — it slightly dampens the force of magical attacks that strike the shield directly.
