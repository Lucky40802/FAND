# White Dragon Scale Karud

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Karud reforged with White Dragon Scale (Dragon Material) worked into its construction. Scales from a white dragon. Armour provides resistance to cold. Weapons deal +1d6 cold damage. Creatures struck must CON save DC 13 or be slowed as ice crystals form in their joints. Bonus: +1d6 Cold.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from White Dragon Scale): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
