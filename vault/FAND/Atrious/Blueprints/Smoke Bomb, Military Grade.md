# Smoke Bomb, Military Grade

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A larger and denser version of bottled smoke, designed for battlefield use. Covers a 30ft area. Burns for three minutes. The smoke is blue-grey and thick enough to completely obscure sight beyond five feet.
