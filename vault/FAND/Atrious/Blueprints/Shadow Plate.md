# Shadow Plate

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

Full plate of shadowsteel. AC 18. The wearer casts no shadow and makes no sound. Resistant to shadow damage. Once per day, enter shadow form — incorporeal — for 1 minute.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
