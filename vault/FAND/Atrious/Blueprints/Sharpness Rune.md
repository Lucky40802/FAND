# Sharpness Rune

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a blade that maintains its edge indefinitely. The blade never needs sharpening. Does not improve the quality of the edge beyond its current state — a poor blade with this rune remains a poor blade…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Fear Glyph|Fear Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Float Rune|Float Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Holding Glyph|Holding Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Reveal Glyph|Reveal Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Memory|Rune of Memory]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Slow Poison|Rune of Slow Poison]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
