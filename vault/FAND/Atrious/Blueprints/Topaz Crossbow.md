# Topaz Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A mechanical bow with a Topaz prod and stock. The construction builds static charge and discharges lightning on critical hits.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Topaz Longbow|Topaz Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Topaz Shortbow|Topaz Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Topaz): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
