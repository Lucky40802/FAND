# Wraithform Cloak

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A cloak that makes the wearer partially incorporeal. Physical attacks against the wearer have a 25% chance of passing through harmlessly. The wearer can move through walls of up to 1ft thickness once per short rest.
