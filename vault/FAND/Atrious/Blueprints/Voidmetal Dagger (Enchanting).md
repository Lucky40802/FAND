# Voidmetal Dagger (Enchanting)

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Voidmetal edge cuts through magical barriers as easily as physical matter. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidmetal): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
