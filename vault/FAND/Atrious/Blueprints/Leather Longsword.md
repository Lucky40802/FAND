# Leather Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Uncommon*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Leather blade is flexible and comfortable against skin.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Leather Greatsword|Leather Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Leather Shortsword|Leather Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Leather): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
