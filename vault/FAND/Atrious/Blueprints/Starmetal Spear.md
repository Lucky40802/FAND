# Starmetal Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Artifact*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Starmetal tip ignores magical damage resistance and holds enchantments permanently. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Starmetal Quarterstaff|Starmetal Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Starmetal): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
