*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Very Rare*

A draught incorporating Null Powder (Alchemical). A grey powder produced by grinding void nullstone. When applied to an object, it suppresses magical auras for 1 hour.
