# Manacite Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Manacite (Arcane Material). A crystal that forms in areas of intense magical activity. It absorbs ambient magical energy constantly. Once per day, touching a manacite object allows a spellcaster to recover one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower. The crystal dims after use and recharges over 24 hours. Bonus: +1 spell slot recovery.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Manacite): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
