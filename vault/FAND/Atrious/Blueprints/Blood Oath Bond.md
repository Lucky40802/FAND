# Blood Oath Bond

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A ritual that creates a blood-written contract. Either party who breaks the oath takes 2d6 psychic damage immediately.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Anti-Coagulant|Anti-Coagulant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Lamp|Blood Lamp]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Smelling Salts|Blood Smelling Salts]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
