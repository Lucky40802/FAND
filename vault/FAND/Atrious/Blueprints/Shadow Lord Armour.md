# Shadow Lord Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A well-tempered armor, woven from shadow-touched thread using Needle of Bone and Dim Silk. Made to a standard rather than to impress.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Dim Silk): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
