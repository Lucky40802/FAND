# Soulsteel Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Soulsteel blade bypasses incorporeal defences by striking the soul directly.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Greatsword|Soulsteel Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Shortsword|Soulsteel Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
