# Amethyst Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Amethyst construction means disrupts mental control and deals psychic damage on impact.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Amethyst Longsword|Amethyst Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Amethyst Shortsword|Amethyst Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Amethyst): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
