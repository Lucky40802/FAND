# Titanium Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Titanium blade is lighter than steel with equivalent strength.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Titanium Greatsword|Titanium Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Titanium Shortsword|Titanium Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Titanium): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
