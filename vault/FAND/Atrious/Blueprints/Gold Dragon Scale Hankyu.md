# Gold Dragon Scale Hankyu

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Hankyu reforged with Gold Dragon Scale (Metal) worked into its construction. Scales from a gold dragon. Armour provides resistance to fire and radiant. Weapons deal +1d6 radiant damage. Fiends and undead take maximum damage from weapons incorporating gold dragon scale. Bonus: +1d6 Radiant.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 3, from Gold Dragon Scale): Weapon: +3d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
