# Ironwood Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Very Rare*

A fragment incorporating Ironwood (Metal). A rare hardwood so dense it sinks in water. Ironwood is as hard as iron but lighter and more flexible. Weapons made from it are neither wood nor metal in any conventional sense. Bonus: +2.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ironwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
