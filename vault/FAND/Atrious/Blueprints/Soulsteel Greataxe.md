# Soulsteel Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Soulsteel construction bypasses incorporeal defences by striking the soul directly.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Hand Axe|Soulsteel Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
