*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Uncommon*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Emergency Beacon - Material Plane|Emergency Beacon - Material Plane]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Star Altitude Measurer|Star Altitude Measurer]].
