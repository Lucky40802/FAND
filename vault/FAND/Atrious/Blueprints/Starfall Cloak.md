# Starfall Cloak

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Rare*

A cloak woven from starthread with starmetal clasps. The wearer can use a bonus action to fold into the Astral Plane for up to one minute — they become invisible and incorporeal in the Material Plane but can observe it.
