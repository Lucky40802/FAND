# Soulsteel Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Soulsteel construction means bypasses incorporeal defences by striking the soul directly.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Longsword|Soulsteel Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Shortsword|Soulsteel Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
