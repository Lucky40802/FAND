# Banshee Wail Crystal Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A collar incorporating Banshee Wail Crystal (Creature Harvest). A crystal formed from a solidified banshee wail — this occurs only in extremely specific circumstances. Items incorporating it emit a keening sound when attacking. On a critical hit, all creatures within 30ft must save DC 14 or be Frightened for 1 minute. Bonus: Fear on crit.
