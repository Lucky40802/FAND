# Deepstone Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Deepstone construction is twice as dense as granite and delivers crushing force that bypasses hardness.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Deepstone Greatsword|Deepstone Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Deepstone Longsword|Deepstone Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Deepstone): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
