# The Meal of Immortality

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Legendary*

A single meal that, if fully consumed, permanently prevents the eater from dying of old age. Does not prevent death from other causes. Three dozen ingredients, each impossible to gather.
