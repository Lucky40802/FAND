# Iron Crowbar

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A prying bar of wrought iron, 24 inches long, with a curved end and a flat chisel end. Used for prying open crates, doors, and fingers that are gripping things they shouldn't. Doubles as a weapon in an emergency.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hammer Head|Hammer Head]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Cauldron|Iron Cauldron]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Cooking Grate|Iron Cooking Grate]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron File Set|Iron File Set]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tongs|Tongs]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
