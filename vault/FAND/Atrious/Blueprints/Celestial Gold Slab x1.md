# Celestial Gold Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Very Rare*

A slab incorporating Celestial Gold (Metal). Gold worked in the Upper Planes and infused with celestial conviction. The metal glows faintly with its own inner light. Weapons made from celestial gold deal radiant damage and are anathema to fiends — demons and devils have disadvantage on saving throws against them. Bonus: +3 Radiant.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Gold): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
