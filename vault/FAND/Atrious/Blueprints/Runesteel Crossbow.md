# Runesteel Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Runesteel prod and stock. The construction accepts more runes than standard steel and enchantments never decay.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Runesteel Longbow|Runesteel Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Runesteel Shortbow|Runesteel Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Runesteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
