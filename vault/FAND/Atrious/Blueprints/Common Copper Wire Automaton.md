# Common Copper Wire Automaton

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Rare*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clockwork War Wagon|Clockwork War Wagon]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
