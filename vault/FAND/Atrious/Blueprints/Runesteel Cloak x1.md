# Runesteel Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Runesteel (Arcane Material). Steel etched with permanent runes during the forging process rather than after. The runes are part of the metal's structure. Runesteel weapons can be enchanted with more runes than standard weapons and the enchantments never decay. Bonus: +2.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Runesteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
