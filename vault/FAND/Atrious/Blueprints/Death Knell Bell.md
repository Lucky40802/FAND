# Death Knell Bell

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

A small bell of soulsteel and silver. When rung over a dying creature, the creature stabilises automatically. When rung over a recently dead creature within 1 minute of death, they return to 1 HP. The bell has 3 charges.
