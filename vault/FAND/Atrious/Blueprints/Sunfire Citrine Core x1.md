# Sunfire Citrine Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Rare*

A core incorporating Sunfire Citrine (Gemstone & Crystal). A bright yellow-orange citrine that concentrates solar energy. In full sunlight, weapons deal +1d6 radiant. In darkness, weapons deal +1 radiant — reduced but not zero, as the stored sunlight releases slowly. Bonus: +1d6 Radiant.
