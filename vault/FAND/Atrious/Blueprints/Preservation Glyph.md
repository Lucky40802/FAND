# Preservation Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph inscribed onto a container that slows the deterioration of organic matter inside. Food lasts 4x as long. Potions do not expire. The glyph works passively — it requires no activation.

The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
