# Voidsap Wood Engine x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Very Rare*

A engine incorporating Voidsap Wood (Wood). Wood from trees that grew near planar void rifts, absorbing void energy into their grain. The wood is slightly transparent and slightly cold. Weapons deal +1d4 force damage. Staves made from voidsap wood absorb 5 points of magical damage per round. Bonus: +1 Force.
