# Rune of Light

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A carved rune that glows with soft light when activated by touch. Provides bright light 20ft and dim light 40ft. Lasts 24 hours. Used by miners, explorers, and anyone who needs reliable light without open flame.
