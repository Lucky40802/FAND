# Ether Stone Relic x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Very Rare*

A relic incorporating Ether Stone (Planar). Stone from the Border Ethereal, maintained in stable form in the material plane. Items made from ether stone allow the bearer to see into the Ethereal Plane as if using See Invisibility. Weapons can strike Ethereal creatures as if they were material. Bonus: Ethereal sight.
