# Blood Sovereign Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A greatsword running with blood alchemy fluid. On a hit the target bleeds 1d8 per round. Critical hits drain 1 CON permanently. The wielder heals for all bleed damage dealt.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Sovereign Blade|Blood Sovereign Blade]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
