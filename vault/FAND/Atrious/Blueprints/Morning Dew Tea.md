# Morning Dew Tea

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A small-batch potion, prepared through spirit cooking traditions using Binding Salt. A working example of the craft rather than a showpiece. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Apprentice Binding Salt Dish|Apprentice Binding Salt Dish]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
