# Potion of Etherealness

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A translucent grey liquid. On drinking, the user enters the Ethereal Plane for 1 minute. They can see and be seen from the Material Plane but cannot affect it.
