# Scaling Rope

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

A purpose-built artifact, engineered for siege warfare using Iron Fitting. Solid, dependable work — nothing about it is flashy, but it holds up. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Journeyman's Iron Fitting Engine|Journeyman's Iron Fitting Engine]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
