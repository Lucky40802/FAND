# Shadowsteel Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Shadowsteel blade is silent and deals shadow damage, partially incorporealising wounds.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadowsteel Greatsword|Shadowsteel Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadowsteel Shortsword|Shadowsteel Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
