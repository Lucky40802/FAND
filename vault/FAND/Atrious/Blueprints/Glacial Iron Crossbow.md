# Glacial Iron Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Glacial Iron prod and stock. The construction is always cold and slows targets on hit.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glacial Iron Longbow|Glacial Iron Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glacial Iron Shortbow|Glacial Iron Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
