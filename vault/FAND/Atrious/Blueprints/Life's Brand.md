# Life's Brand

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

This brand only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Life]] directly, bound into a core of Astral Silver (+2 to attack rolls vs outsiders). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Blinking** (Potency 1, from Astral Silver): Bonus action: teleport up to 30 ft, once per short rest (at will at Potency 5+).
