# Will-o-Wisp Essence Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A collar incorporating Will-o-Wisp Essence (Creature Harvest). The essence of a captured will-o-wisp, crystallised. Items incorporating this essence make a faint glow when a creature within 60ft is suffering — the light is brighter the more extreme the suffering. Used by healers and used by predators. Bonus: Torment detection.
