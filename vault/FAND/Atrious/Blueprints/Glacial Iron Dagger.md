# Glacial Iron Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Glacial Iron edge is always cold and slows targets on hit. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
