# Will-o-Wisp Essence Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Rare*

A fetish incorporating Will-o-Wisp Essence (Creature Harvest). The essence of a captured will-o-wisp, crystallised. Items incorporating this essence make a faint glow when a creature within 60ft is suffering — the light is brighter the more extreme the suffering. Used by healers and used by predators. Bonus: Torment detection.
