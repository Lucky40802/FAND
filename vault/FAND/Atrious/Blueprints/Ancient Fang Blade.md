# Ancient Fang Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A sword-length blade carved from the full fang of an ancient dragon. Deals the source dragon's elemental damage type on every hit, not just crits.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
