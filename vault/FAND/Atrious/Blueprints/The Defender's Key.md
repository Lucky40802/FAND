# The Defender's Key

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A Warden Key passed from master to student across generations — a weapon that is also a literal key to the locks between worlds. It unlocks hearts as easily as doors. In the hands of someone who has earned the title of Master, it radiates a light that steadies every ally nearby.
