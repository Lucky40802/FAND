# Styx Water Vessel x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A vessel incorporating Styx Water (Planar). Water from the River Styx, the boundary between life and death. Used to quench weapons during forging. Any creature struck by a Styx-quenched weapon must make a WIS save or forget the last minute of their life. Extremely dangerous to handle. Bonus: +Special.
