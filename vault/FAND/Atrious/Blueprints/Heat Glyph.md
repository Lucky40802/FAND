# Heat Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph that generates heat when activated. Temperature increases by 10 degrees F in a 10ft radius. Used for warming cold rooms, preventing pipes from freezing, and (at high density) as a trap component.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Alarm Glyph|Alarm Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silence Glyph|Silence Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Strength Glyph|Strength Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Translation Rune|Translation Rune]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
