# Fate Thread Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Fate Thread (Planar). Thread literally spun from the strands of fate. Items incorporating fate thread cannot be broken or destroyed by anything less than a deity. Once per long rest, the bearer can declare a roll they just made as the opposite result — a failure becomes a success or vice versa. Bonus: Fate manipulation.

## Material Property
**Fortunate** (Potency 3, from Fate Thread): 2 times per long rest, reroll one d20 you rolled.
