# Soulsteel Relic x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Rare*

A relic incorporating Soulsteel (Metal). A metal created by compressing soul energy into a physical medium. Grey and slightly translucent. Weapons made from soulsteel deal necrotic damage and can directly damage the soul rather than the body — effective against creatures that are incorporeal or immune to physical damage. Bonus: +2 Necrotic.
