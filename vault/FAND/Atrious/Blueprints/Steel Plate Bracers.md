# Steel Plate Bracers

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

Forearm guards of formed steel plate with leather straps. Three articulation points allow full wrist movement. Protects the forearm against weapon strikes and provides a hard surface for blocking.
