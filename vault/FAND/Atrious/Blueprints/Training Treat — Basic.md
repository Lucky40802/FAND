# Training Treat — Basic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Common*

Compressed animal fat and dried meat treats, made for training purposes. Used to reward positive behaviour in beasts during taming. One batch is 20 treats. Effective as a training reward for beasts of CR 2 or lower.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Collar - Basic|Beast Collar - Basic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Taming Collar — Small|Beast Taming Collar — Small]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Calming Herb Bundle|Calming Herb Bundle]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
