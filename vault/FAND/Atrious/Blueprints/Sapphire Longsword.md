# Sapphire Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Sapphire blade radiates deep cold and slows whatever it cuts.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sapphire Greatsword|Sapphire Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sapphire Shortsword|Sapphire Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Sapphire): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
