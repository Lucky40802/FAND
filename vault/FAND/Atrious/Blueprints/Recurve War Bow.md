# Recurve War Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Uncommon*

A recurve bow of sixty-pound draw weight, designed for warfare rather than hunting. The extra draw weight punches through light armour at range. Requires significant upper body strength to draw repeatedly.
