# Hellforged Iron Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Very Rare*

A frame incorporating Hellforged Iron (Metal). Iron worked in hellfire and quenched in brimstone. Always slightly warm. Deals fire damage. Creatures of good alignment take a -1 to attacks against the wielder while the weapon is drawn. Each weapon carries a minor binding contract. Bonus: +2 Fire.
