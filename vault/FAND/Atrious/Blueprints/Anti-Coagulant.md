# Anti-Coagulant

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

Prevents blood clotting when applied to a blade. Creatures bleed 1 HP per round for 10 strikes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Lamp|Blood Lamp]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Oath Bond|Blood Oath Bond]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Smelling Salts|Blood Smelling Salts]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
