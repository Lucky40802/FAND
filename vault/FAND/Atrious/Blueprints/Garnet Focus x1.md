# Garnet Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Rare*

A focus incorporating Garnet (Gemstone & Crystal). The gem of war and endurance. Deep red garnets channel force energy and combat conviction. Weapons set with garnets hit harder under pressure — the more wounds the wielder has taken, the more force the weapon delivers. Bonus: +2 Force.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Garnet): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
