# Ironwood Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Ironwood construction is as hard as iron while remaining lighter than steel.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ironwood Greatsword|Ironwood Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ironwood Longsword|Ironwood Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ironwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
