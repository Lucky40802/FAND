*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Drying Glyph|Drying Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Float Glyph|Float Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Simple Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm|Simple Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm]].
