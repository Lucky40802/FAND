# Rune of Growth

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a planting pot or garden marker. Plants grown near this rune grow at twice the normal rate and are resistant to disease. Does not make plants magical — just healthy and fast.
