# Prophet Crystal Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Very Rare*

A automaton incorporating Prophet Crystal (Arcane Material). A clear crystal with a constantly shifting interior pattern. Items made from it grant +2 to initiative — the bearer always acts with foreknowledge. Once per day, the bearer can ask a yes/no question of the GM and receive an honest answer.
