# Shadow Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A hand-forged axe, woven from shadow-touched thread using Dim Silk and Umbral Dust. Unremarkable to look at, but it does exactly what it's meant to.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Dim Silk): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
