# Chitin Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A 6-foot staff of Chitin. The material is naturally load-bearing and shaped by growth rather than craft.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Chitin Spear|Chitin Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Hardened** (Potency 1, from Chitin): Armor or shield: +1 AC. Weapon: can't be broken, and ignores 1 Natural AC.
