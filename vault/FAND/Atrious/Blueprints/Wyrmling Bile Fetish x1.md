# Wyrmling Bile Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Very Rare*

A fetish incorporating Wyrmling Bile (Dragon Material). Bile extracted from dragon wyrmlings — a process that does not harm them as they produce surplus. The bile type matches the wyrmling species. Items coated in wyrmling bile deal +1 damage of the appropriate element. Wyrmlings take full damage from weapons made with their own species' bile. Bonus: +1 elemental.
