# Crystal Armor Inserts — Protective

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] — Uncommon*

A set of crystal chips of diamond and garnet shaped to be worked into armour gaps. Installed properly, they grant +1 AC and resistance to one chosen energy type corresponding to the gem used.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Diamond): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
