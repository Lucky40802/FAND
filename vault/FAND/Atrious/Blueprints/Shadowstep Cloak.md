# Shadowstep Cloak

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Uncommon*

A full cloak of shadowsteel-woven veilcloth. Once per short rest, the wearer can teleport up to 30ft to any space they can see that is in dim light or darkness. The teleportation makes no sound and leaves no trace.
