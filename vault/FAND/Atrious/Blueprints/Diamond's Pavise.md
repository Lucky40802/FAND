# Diamond's Pavise

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A Pavise that only functions properly because it channels a fragment of [[Diamond]] directly, cast in a core of Astral Silver (+2 to attack rolls vs outsiders). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system.

## Material Property
**Blinking** (Potency 1, from Astral Silver): Bonus action: teleport up to 30 ft, once per short rest (at will at Potency 5+).
