# Lich Phylactery

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A container of soulsteel and wraithbone designed to hold a living soul indefinitely. The soul's body can be destroyed — it reformulates from the phylactery in 1d10 days. Extremely illegal. Very popular.
