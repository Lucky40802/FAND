# Wraith Gauntlets

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

Gauntlets of wraithbone. Melee attacks with these gauntlets deal necrotic damage and drain 1d6 HP from the target on a hit, restoring it to the wearer.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
