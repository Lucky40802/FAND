# Feywild Silver Engine x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Very Rare*

A engine incorporating Feywild Silver (Metal). Silver worked in the Feywild and touched by archfey magic. The metal changes colour slightly with the wielder's mood. Weapons made from it deal radiant damage, are immune to the effects of illusions, and can force shapeshifters to revert to their true form on a successful hit. Bonus: +2 Radiant.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Feywild Silver Frame x1|Feywild Silver Frame x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
