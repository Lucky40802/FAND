# Spin's Rune-Bound Charm

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Legendary*

This rune-bound charm only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Spin]] directly, bound into a core of Abjuration Ward Stone (+2, wielder gains +1 AC while weapon is drawn). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ignis's Relic|Ignis's Relic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
