# Steel Lance Head

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A lance head of heavy steel, 14 inches long with a diamond cross-section. The socket is 1.5 inches for a standard lance haft. Designed for the mounted charge — the point concentrates the entire force of horse and rider…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Quarterstaff|Steel Quarterstaff]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Spear|Steel Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
