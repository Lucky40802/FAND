# Steel Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Steel construction holds a fine edge through extended combat.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Greatsword|Steel Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Longsword|Steel Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
