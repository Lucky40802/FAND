# Amber Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A carving incorporating Amber (Common Material). Ancient fossilised tree resin. Creatures fully submerged in amber are perfectly preserved — suspended animation with no aging. Items made from amber are beautiful and valuable. Amber can sometimes preserve magical properties of items embedded within it. Bonus: Creature preservation.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Amber): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
