# Rage Brew

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Uncommon*

A fermented drink that induces a combat rage. Eater gains the effects of the Barbarian Rage feature for 1 hour. They cannot end it voluntarily.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Warriors Breakfast|Warriors Breakfast]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
