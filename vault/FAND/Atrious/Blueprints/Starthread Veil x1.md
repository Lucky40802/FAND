# Starthread Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A veil incorporating Starthread (Planar). Thread spun from solidified astral silver. Starthread armour weighs almost nothing. Weapons wrapped or strung with starthread deal radiant damage and briefly phase through cover before striking. Bonus: +2 Radiant.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Starthread): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
