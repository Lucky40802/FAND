# Wraithbone Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A collar incorporating Wraithbone (Creature Harvest). Bone harvested from powerful undead creatures that retains its supernatural properties after the creature's final death. Wraithbone weapons ignore non-magical armour entirely and deal necrotic damage. They are cold to the touch and make no sound. Bonus: +2 Necrotic.
