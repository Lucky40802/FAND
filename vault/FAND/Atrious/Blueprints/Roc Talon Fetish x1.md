# Roc Talon Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Very Rare*

A fetish incorporating Roc Talon (Creature Harvest). A talon shed by a Roc — each talon is the size of a longsword. Items made from Roc talons deal +2d6 piercing damage. Critical hits with Roc talon weapons knock the target prone (STR save DC 16) as the sheer force of impact is overwhelming. Bonus: +2d6 Piercing.
