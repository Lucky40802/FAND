# Adamantine Weapon Core

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

The core of an adamantine weapon — a blade or hammerhead of pure adamantine waiting for handle fitting and final shaping. The material ignores object hardness. The core is complete; a handle and finishing are required.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 3, from Adamantine): Weapon: +2 damage. Armor: +2 Gear AC.
