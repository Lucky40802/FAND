# Endurance Tonic

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A thick brown liquid with a sharp aftertaste. Removes one level of exhaustion and prevents gaining more exhaustion for 4 hours. The comedown is harsh — after 4 hours, gain two levels of exhaustion regardless of activity.
