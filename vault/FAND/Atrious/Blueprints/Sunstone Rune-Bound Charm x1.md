# Sunstone Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Sunstone (Gemstone & Crystal). A golden-orange crystal that stores sunlight energy. In darkness, sunstone weapons shed dim light in a 5ft radius. When striking undead, that light flares — maximum damage dice against undead targets. Bonus: +1d6 Radiant.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sunstone Core x1|Sunstone Core x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
