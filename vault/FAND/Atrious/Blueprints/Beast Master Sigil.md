# Beast Master Sigil

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Legendary*

A sigil carved from the fang of an apex predator and hung around the neck. The wearer is recognised as the ultimate alpha by all non-intelligent beasts. No check is required to command any beast of CR 10 or lower.
