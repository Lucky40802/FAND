# Healing Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph that releases a pulse of healing energy when triggered. Restores 2d8 HP to all living creatures within 10ft. Triggered by the inscription phrase or by a creature dropping to 0 HP within range. Single use.
