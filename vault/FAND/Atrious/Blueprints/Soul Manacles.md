# Soul Manacles

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

Manacles of soulsteel. When placed on a creature that then dies, the soul cannot leave — it is trapped in the body until the manacles are removed.
