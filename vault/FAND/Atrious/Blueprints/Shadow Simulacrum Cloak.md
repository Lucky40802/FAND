# Shadow Simulacrum Cloak

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A cloak that, when removed, becomes an illusory copy of the wearer that continues moving for 1 minute. The copy is fully real in appearance and is destroyed on taking any damage.
