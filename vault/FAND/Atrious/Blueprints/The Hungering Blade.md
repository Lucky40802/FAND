# The Hungering Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A living sword that feeds on the souls of its victims. It shifts and reshapes itself constantly, absorbing every weapon it defeats into its own form. An eye embedded in the guard never closes and never stops watching. Every wielder is consumed by it, eventually — the only question is how long they last first.
