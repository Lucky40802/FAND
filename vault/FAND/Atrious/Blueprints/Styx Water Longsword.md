# Styx Water Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Artifact*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Styx Water blade causes memory loss in struck targets who fail a WIS save.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Styx Water Greatsword|Styx Water Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Styx Water Shortsword|Styx Water Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 2, from Styx Water): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +2d6 cold to creatures in water.
