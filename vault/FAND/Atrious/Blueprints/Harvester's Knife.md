# Harvester's Knife

*Tool — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] (Scavenger) — Common*

A skinning and cutting knife. Advantage on checks to harvest materials from a creature.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bone Saw|Bone Saw]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gutting Hook|Gutting Hook]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pry Bar|Pry Bar]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
