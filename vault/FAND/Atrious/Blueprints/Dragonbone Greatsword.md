# Dragonbone Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Legendary*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Dragonbone construction means holds enchantments better than any metal and carries elemental resonance.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonbone Longsword|Dragonbone Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonbone Shortsword|Dragonbone Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
