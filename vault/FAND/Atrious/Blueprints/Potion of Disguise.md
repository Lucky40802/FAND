# Potion of Disguise

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A shimmering liquid. Drinking allows the user to alter their appearance as per the Disguise Self spell for 1 hour. The potion stores one specific appearance decided at brewing.
