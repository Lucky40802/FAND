# Wound Packing Paste

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A thick paste of clean fibre and coagulant herbs for packing deep wounds. Stops bleeding. Does not heal. Needs a healer after.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Breathing Aid|Breathing Aid]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
