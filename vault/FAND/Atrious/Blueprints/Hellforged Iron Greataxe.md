# Hellforged Iron Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Hellforged Iron construction burns with hellfire and unnerves good-aligned opponents.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Hand Axe|Hellforged Iron Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
