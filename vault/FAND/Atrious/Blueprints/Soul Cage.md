# Soul Cage

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A cage of soulsteel that traps a soul at the moment of death within 30ft. The caster must take their reaction. The soul can be interrogated — it must answer truthfully.
