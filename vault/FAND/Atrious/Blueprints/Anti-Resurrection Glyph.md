# Anti-Resurrection Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Rare*

A glyph inscribed on a burial site or corpse. Prevents any resurrection magic below 9th level from functioning on the marked body.
