# Celestial Marble Vessel x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Celestial Marble (Planar). Marble from Mount Celestia. Evil-aligned creatures cannot touch celestial marble without taking 1d6 radiant damage per round. Armour from it makes the bearer immune to charm effects from evil-aligned creatures. Used extensively in temples. Bonus: Evil repulsion.
