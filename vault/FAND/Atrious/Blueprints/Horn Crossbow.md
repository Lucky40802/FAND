# Horn Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Uncommon*

A mechanical bow with a Horn prod and stock. The construction is flexible and shock-absorbing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Horn Longbow|Horn Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Horn Shortbow|Horn Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Horn): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
