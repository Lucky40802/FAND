# Fire Resistance Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A warm orange liquid with a faint charred smell. Grants resistance to fire damage for 1 hour after consumption. Popular among smiths, explorers of volcanic regions, and anyone dealing with dragons.
