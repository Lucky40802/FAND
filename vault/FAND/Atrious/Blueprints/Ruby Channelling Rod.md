# Ruby Channelling Rod

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Common*

A rod of carved ruby, extremely difficult to produce without shattering the gem. Channels fire energy without the rod itself heating. A spellcaster using this rod as a focus deals +1 damage per die on fire spells.
