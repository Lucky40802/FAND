# Rune of the Patient Hunter

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

A rune inscribed on a hunting weapon. When the wielder spends a full turn readying an attack, the triggered attack automatically hits and deals maximum damage. The rune rewards patience over aggression.
