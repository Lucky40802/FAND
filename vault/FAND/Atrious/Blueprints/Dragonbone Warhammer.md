# Dragonbone Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Legendary*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Dragonbone head holds enchantments better than any metal and carries elemental resonance.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonbone Mace|Dragonbone Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
