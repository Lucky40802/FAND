# Iron Grate

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A grid of iron bars used in fireplaces, prison cells, or drainage. Each bar is hand-forged and the joints are fire-welded. Cutting through requires 30 minutes and an iron saw.
