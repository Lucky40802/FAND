# Iron Door Hinge

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A strap hinge of wrought iron, hand-forged. The strap is 8 inches. The knuckle is formed around a steel pin. Comes in pairs. Does not squeak if oiled regularly. Will squeak if not.
