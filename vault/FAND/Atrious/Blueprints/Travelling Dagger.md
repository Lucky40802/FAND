# Travelling Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A sturdy iron dagger with a broad fuller and a ring pommel. Designed for road travellers who need a general-purpose tool that can also defend them. The ring pommel can be used as a window-breaker.
