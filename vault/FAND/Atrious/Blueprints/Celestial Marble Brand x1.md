# Celestial Marble Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Celestial Marble (Planar). Marble from Mount Celestia. Evil-aligned creatures cannot touch celestial marble without taking 1d6 radiant damage per round. Armour from it makes the bearer immune to charm effects from evil-aligned creatures. Used extensively in temples. Bonus: Evil repulsion.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Marble): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
