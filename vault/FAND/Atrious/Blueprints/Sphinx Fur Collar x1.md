# Sphinx Fur Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A collar incorporating Sphinx Fur (Creature Harvest). Fur from a sphinx, given voluntarily — it cannot be taken by force. Items incorporating sphinx fur grant the bearer +1 to all saving throws. Once per day, the bearer can ask one question that is answered with perfect truth, as if by Legend Lore. Bonus: +1 to all saves.
