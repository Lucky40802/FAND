# Moonsilver Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Moonsilver edge deals double damage to lycanthropes and creatures of the night. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Moonsilver): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
