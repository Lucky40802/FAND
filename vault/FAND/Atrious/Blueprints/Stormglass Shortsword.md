# Stormglass Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Stormglass construction crackles with static charge and discharges lightning.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Stormglass Greatsword|Stormglass Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Stormglass Longsword|Stormglass Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Stormcharged** (Potency 3, from Stormglass): Weapon: +3d6 lightning or thunder damage. Armor: lightning resistance.
