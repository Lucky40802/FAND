# Steel Composite Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A composite bow with a steel core inside the wood-and-horn laminate. The steel core maintains the bow's shape in extreme heat or cold where standard composite bows warp. Built for soldiers, not hunters.
