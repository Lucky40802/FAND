# Sturdy Timber Beam Engine

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Uncommon*

A sturdy engine built from timber beam, taught early in Siege Engineering training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Chevaux de Frise|Chevaux de Frise]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Portcullis Winch|Portcullis Winch]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
