# Wraithbone Vial x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Very Rare*

A vial incorporating Wraithbone (Creature Harvest). Bone harvested from powerful undead creatures that retains its supernatural properties after the creature's final death. Wraithbone weapons ignore non-magical armour entirely and deal necrotic damage. They are cold to the touch and make no sound. Bonus: +2 Necrotic.
