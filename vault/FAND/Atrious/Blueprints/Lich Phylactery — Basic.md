# Lich Phylactery — Basic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A phylactery of soulsteel and adamantine that stores a soul. The soul can return to a prepared body within 10 days of the body's death. The prepared body must be the soul's original body or a newly created undead vessel.
