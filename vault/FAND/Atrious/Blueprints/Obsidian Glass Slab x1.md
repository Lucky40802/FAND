# Obsidian Glass Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Very Rare*

A slab incorporating Obsidian Glass (Gemstone & Crystal). Glass formed from rapid volcanic cooling. Razor sharp but brittle. Weapons of obsidian glass are sharper than steel but shatter on a roll of 1. Armour of it deflects but doesn't absorb. Bonus: +0.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Obsidian Glass): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
