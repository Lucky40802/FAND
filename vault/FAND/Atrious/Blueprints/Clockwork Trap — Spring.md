# Clockwork Trap — Spring

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A spring-loaded trap that triggers when a pressure plate is depressed. The spring releases a blade or spike arm dealing 2d6 piercing damage. DC 13 DEX save to avoid. Resets manually. Housed in a case 6 inches square. …

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clockwork Turnspit|Clockwork Turnspit]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mechanical Caltrops Deployer|Mechanical Caltrops Deployer]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
