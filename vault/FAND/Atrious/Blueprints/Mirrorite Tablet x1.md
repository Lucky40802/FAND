# Mirrorite Tablet x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Rare*

A tablet incorporating Mirrorite (Arcane Material). A silver metal that perfectly mirrors its surroundings. So reflective that divination magic finds nothing there — it shows only the surroundings. Armour from mirrorite has a 20% chance of reflecting a spell back at its caster. Weapons of mirrorite deal normal damage to targets that rely on sight. Bonus: Spell reflection chance.
