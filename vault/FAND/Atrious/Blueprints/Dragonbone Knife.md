# Dragonbone Knife

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A blade carved from the rib bone of a young dragon. Dragon bone is harder than most metals and lighter than iron. The edge holds indefinitely — it cannot be sharpened further but it never dulls.
