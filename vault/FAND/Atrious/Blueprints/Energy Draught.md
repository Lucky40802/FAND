# Energy Draught

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A bitter green liquid derived from stimulant herbs. Removes one level of exhaustion when consumed. Cannot remove more than one level per short rest. The crash after the stimulant wears off is real but manageable.
