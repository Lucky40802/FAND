# The Unheard Shot

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A shortbow of dragon bone with a shadowsteel riser and veilcloth wrapping. Arrows fired from The Unheard Shot make no sound. Not suppressed sound — no sound. The bow does not creak when drawn. The string does not hum…

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
