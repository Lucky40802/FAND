# Deepstone Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Deepstone blade is twice as dense as granite and delivers crushing force that bypasses hardness.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Deepstone Greatsword|Deepstone Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Deepstone Shortsword|Deepstone Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Deepstone): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.

See [[FAND/Atrious/Material Properties|Material Properties]].
