# Moonstone Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Moonstone tip glows at night and is especially effective against fiends and undead. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonstone Focus x1|Moonstone Focus x1]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonstone Quarterstaff|Moonstone Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Moonstone): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
