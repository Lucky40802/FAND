# Elderwood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Elderwood (Wood). Wood from elder trees that have lived over 1000 years. The wood has absorbed centuries of nature energy. Weapons deal +1d4 force damage. Bows made from elderwood have +60ft range. Once per day, a bow of elderwood can fire an arrow that splits mid-flight to hit two targets. Bonus: +2.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Elderwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
