# Rune of Mending

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A rune inscribed on a frequently used object. When the object breaks, the rune activates, restoring it to full structural integrity within one minute. The rune activates up to three times before being consumed.
