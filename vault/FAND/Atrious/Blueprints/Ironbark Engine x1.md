# Ironbark Engine x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Very Rare*

A engine incorporating Ironbark (Metal). Bark from ancient oak trees in areas of concentrated natural magic. Harder than iron but flexible like wood. Ironbark weapons deal bludgeoning damage equal to a weapon one size category larger. Armour from it is as protective as steel but weighs 40% less. Bonus: +1.
