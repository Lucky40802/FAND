# Paralysis Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph that, when triggered, forces all creatures within 15ft to make a CON save DC 14 or be Paralysed for 1 minute. They may repeat the save at the end of each turn. Resets after 1 hour.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Silence Zone|Rune of Silence Zone]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
