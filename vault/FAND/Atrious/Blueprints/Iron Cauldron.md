# Iron Cauldron

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A large cast iron cauldron, 3-gallon capacity, with three legs and a bail handle. Used for cooking, brewing, rendering, and — in some traditions — ritual purposes. Cast iron retains heat exceptionally well.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hammer Head|Hammer Head]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Cooking Grate|Iron Cooking Grate]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Crowbar|Iron Crowbar]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron File Set|Iron File Set]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tongs|Tongs]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
