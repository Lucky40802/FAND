# Catapult Arm

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

A replacement throwing arm for a standard catapult. Laminated oak with steel ferrules at the sling attachment point and the axle. A direct replacement for a broken arm — the existing frame is reused.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ballista Frame|Ballista Frame]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
