# Petrified Lightning Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Very Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Petrified Lightning (Elemental). Sand that has been fused into glass tubes by a lightning strike, then carefully extracted and worked. Called fulgurite by scholars. Weapons deal +2d6 lightning damage. Critical hits create a chain lightning effect hitting the nearest additional target for 1d6 lightning. Bonus: +2d6 Lightning.
