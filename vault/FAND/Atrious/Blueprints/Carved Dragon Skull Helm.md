# Carved Dragon Skull Helm

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A helm carved from the skull of a young dragon. The jaw serves as a visor. Intimidating. Provides immunity to the source dragon's breath weapon.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
