# Lust's Cloak

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Legendary*

This cloak only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Lust]] directly, bound into a core of Wild Magic Prismastone (+2 random (roll d6: 1-Fire, 2-Cold, 3-Lightning, 4-Acid, 5-Psychic, 6-Force), and wild magic surge on critical). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Chaotic** (Potency 3, from Wild Magic Prismastone): Weapon: +3d6 damage of a random type each hit. Once per long rest, trigger a wild magic surge on purpose.
