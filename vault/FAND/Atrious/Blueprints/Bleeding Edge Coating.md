# Bleeding Edge Coating

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A coating applied to a weapon. The coated weapon deals an additional 1d6 necrotic damage on the first hit against each target. The coating lasts for 10 hits before needing reapplication. Applied from a small vial.
