# Bronze Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Bronze construction holds a reasonable edge and rarely shatters.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bronze Hand Axe|Bronze Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
