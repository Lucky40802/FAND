# Blood Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A shortbow made from dragonbone with bloodstone inlaid along the limbs. Arrows fired from it carry blood-magic charge. Targets hit take an additional 1d6 necrotic damage and must make a CON save DC 13 or begin bleeding…

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
