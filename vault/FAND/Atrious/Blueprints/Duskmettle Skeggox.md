# Duskmettle Skeggox

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Skeggox reforged with Duskmettle (Metal) worked into its construction. Metal harvested from locations where day and night are perpetually balanced. Duskmettle weapons deal +1d4 radiant during daylight and +1d4 necrotic during darkness — automatically adapting to the current lighting. Bonus: +1.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Duskmettle): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
