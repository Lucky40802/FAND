# Titanium Pesh-kabz

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Pesh-kabz reforged with Titanium (Metal) worked into its construction. Exceptionally light and strong. A sword of titanium weighs half what steel weighs for the same size. Difficult to work — requires extreme temperatures and specialist technique. Bonus: +2.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Titanium): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
