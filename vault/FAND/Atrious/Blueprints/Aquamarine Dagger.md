# Aquamarine Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Aquamarine edge channels wind energy and can redirect projectiles on hit. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Aquamarine): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
