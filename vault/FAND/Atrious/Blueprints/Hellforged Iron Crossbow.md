# Hellforged Iron Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Hellforged Iron prod and stock. The construction burns with hellfire and unnerves good-aligned opponents.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Longbow|Hellforged Iron Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hellforged Iron Shortbow|Hellforged Iron Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 2, from Hellforged Iron): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
