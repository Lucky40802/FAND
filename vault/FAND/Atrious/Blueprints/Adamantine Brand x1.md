# Adamantine Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Rare*

A brand incorporating Adamantine (Metal). The hardest substance available without divine intervention. Adamantine weapons ignore object hardness entirely — they cut through iron doors, stone walls, and heavy armour as if through wood. Armour made from it is critical-hit proof. Bonus: +3.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 3, from Adamantine): Weapon: +2 damage. Armor: +2 Gear AC.
