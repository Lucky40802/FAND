# Life Ward Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Very Rare*

Next time bearer reaches 0 HP, glyph activates leaving them at 1 HP. Single use. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Death Ward Glyph|Death Ward Glyph]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
