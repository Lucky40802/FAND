# Steel Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Hand Axe|Steel Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
