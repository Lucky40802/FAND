# Obsidian Dust Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Obsidian Dust (Gemstone & Crystal). Finely ground obsidian powder. Not used in weapons directly — instead applied to surfaces or scattered as a tactical resource. Scattered as an action, creates a 10ft cloud that Blinds all creatures in it for 1 round. Bonus: Blinding on application.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Obsidian Dust Core x1|Obsidian Dust Core x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
