# Prophet Crystal Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Prophet Crystal (Arcane Material). A clear crystal with a constantly shifting interior pattern. Items made from it grant +2 to initiative — the bearer always acts with foreknowledge. Once per day, the bearer can ask a yes/no question of the GM and receive an honest answer. Bonus: +2 to initiative.

## Material Property
**All-Seeing** (Potency 2, from Prophet Crystal): Darkvision 60 ft. At Potency 3+ see invisible; at Potency 6+ truesight 30 ft. Focus: +1 to divination spell DCs.
