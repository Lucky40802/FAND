# Earth Elemental Stone Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Very Rare*

A core incorporating Earth Elemental Stone (Elemental). The stone core of a destroyed earth elemental. Items incorporating this stone grant tremorsense out to 30ft while in contact with the ground. Armour from it provides resistance to bludgeoning damage. Bonus: Tremorsense 30ft.
