# Copper Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Copper construction means is soft and dulls quickly but is cheap to replace.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Longsword|Copper Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Shortsword|Copper Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Copper): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
