*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials, AC) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ash Buckler|Ash Buckler]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
