# Endurance Tea

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

An herbal tea packet that, when brewed, allows the drinker to march or work for 4 additional hours without fatigue. Not magical — it uses stimulant herbs at a careful dose. Causes a crash afterward.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Antiseptic Wash|Antiseptic Wash]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Compress Potion|Cold Compress Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Analgesic|Mild Analgesic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Stimulant|Mild Stimulant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Scent Mask|Scent Mask]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Warmth Potion|Warmth Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Weak Paralytic|Weak Paralytic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wound Sealant|Wound Sealant]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
