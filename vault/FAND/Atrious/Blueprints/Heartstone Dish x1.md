# Heartstone Dish x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Very Rare*

A dish incorporating Heartstone (Spirit & Soul). A warm pinkish-red stone found at the centre of ancient forests. Items made from heartstone allow the bearer to regenerate 1 HP per minute while in natural outdoor settings. Bonus: Regeneration 1. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Heartstone Dish II|Heartstone Dish II]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
