# Dragonbone Bolo Knife

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Bolo Knife reforged with Dragonbone (Dragon Material) worked into its construction. Harder than most metals, lighter than iron. The internal structure of dragon bone creates natural reinforcing channels. Holds enchantments exceptionally well. Each bone carries residual elemental affinity from its dragon. Bonus: +3.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
