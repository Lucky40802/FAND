# Masterwork Hunting Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Uncommon*

A bow made from a carefully selected stave of yew, worked over months to balance draw weight and flex. Shoots farther and more accurately than standard bows. The string is twisted linen, waxed daily.
