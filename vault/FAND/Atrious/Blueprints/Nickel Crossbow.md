# Nickel Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A mechanical bow with a Nickel prod and stock. The construction is corrosion-resistant and slightly magnetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Nickel Longbow|Nickel Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Nickel Shortbow|Nickel Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
