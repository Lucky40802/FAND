# Spine Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Uncommon*

A spear with a shaft of ironwood and a tip of dragon spine. The spine tip transfers elemental energy from the dragon on every strike.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
