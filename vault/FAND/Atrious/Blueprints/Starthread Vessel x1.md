# Starthread Vessel x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Starthread (Planar). Thread spun from solidified astral silver. Starthread armour weighs almost nothing. Weapons wrapped or strung with starthread deal radiant damage and briefly phase through cover before striking. Bonus: +2 Radiant. The proportions shift slightly from maker to maker.
