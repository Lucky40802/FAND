# Copper Armlet

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A band of hammered copper worn around the upper arm. Provides no meaningful protection but carries cultural and status significance in many civilisations. The first metal jewellery an apprentice learns to make.
