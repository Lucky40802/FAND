# Frostwood Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Frostwood (Wood). Wood from trees in perpetually frozen environments. The grain is white and the wood itself is cold to touch always. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage. Creatures hit must CON save DC 12 or have their movement reduced by 5ft for 1 round. Bonus: +1d4 Cold.
