# Crystal Window Pane

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Common*

A flat pane of shaped crystal, 12x12 inches, 1/4 inch thick. Optically clear. Used where glass would be too fragile — high-security windows, alchemical lab dividers, and observation ports in smithing facilities.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Vial — Sealed|Crystal Vial — Sealed]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
