# Shadow Rope

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

50 feet of braided veilcloth rope. Nearly invisible in dim light. Stronger than hemp rope of equivalent thickness. Does not shine in light but becomes slightly visible in bright sunlight — a shadow-rope in full…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blackout Cloth|Blackout Cloth]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dusk Mittens|Dusk Mittens]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shade Gloves|Shade Gloves]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shade Scarf|Shade Scarf]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Pouch|Shadow Pouch]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Satchel|Shadow Satchel]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
