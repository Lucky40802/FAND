# Siege Bolt Magazine

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Uncommon*

A rotary magazine holding 20 heavy crossbow bolts. Mounts to siege crossbows. Reduces reload time to a free action.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clockwork Padlock - Timed|Clockwork Padlock - Timed]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
