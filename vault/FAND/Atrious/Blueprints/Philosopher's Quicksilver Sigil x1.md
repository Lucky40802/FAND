# Philosopher's Quicksilver Sigil x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Very Rare*

A sigil incorporating Philosopher's Quicksilver (Metal). Mercury stabilised through the philosopher's process. Does not evaporate, does not poison. A liquid metal that can be poured and shaped at will. Items made from it can change shape slowly — a Philosopher's Quicksilver dagger can become a shortsword over 1 minute. Bonus: +2.
