# Iron Cooking Grate

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A flat iron grate for a fire pit or hearth, 18 by 12 inches. The bars are 1/2 inch square in cross-section. Legs hold it 4 inches off the ground. Lasts decades if not left in the rain.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hammer Head|Hammer Head]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Cauldron|Iron Cauldron]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Crowbar|Iron Crowbar]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron File Set|Iron File Set]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tongs|Tongs]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
