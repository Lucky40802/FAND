# Silver Main Gauche

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Main Gauche reforged with Silver (Metal) worked into its construction. A soft metal that must be alloyed with iron to hold an edge. Silver disrupts the supernatural healing of lycanthropes and certain undead. Requires reapplication after heavy use. Bonus: +1.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Dagger|Silver Dagger]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Silver): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
