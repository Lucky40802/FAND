# Mithral Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

A 6-foot bow. The Mithral stave is lighter than steel with superior edge retention and perfect enchantment acceptance.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mithral Crossbow|Mithral Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mithral Shortbow|Mithral Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
