# Soulsteel Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Soulsteel tip bypasses incorporeal defences by striking the soul directly. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Quarterstaff|Soulsteel Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
