# Storm Elemental Spark Vajra-Mushti

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Vajra-Mushti reforged with Storm Elemental Spark (Elemental) worked into its construction. The crystallised spark of a storm elemental — stored in a charged crystal. Weapons deal +1d4 lightning and +1d4 thunder simultaneously. The double damage type makes it extremely effective against creatures with only one elemental resistance. Bonus: +1d6 Lightning and Thunder.

## Material Property
**Stormcharged** (Potency 1, from Storm Elemental Spark): Weapon: +1d6 lightning or thunder damage. Armor: lightning resistance.
