# Onyx Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Onyx construction channels necrotic energy and partially suppresses enemy healing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Onyx Hand Axe|Onyx Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Onyx): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
