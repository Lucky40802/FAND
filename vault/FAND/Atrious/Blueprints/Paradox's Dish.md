# Paradox's Dish

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Legendary*

This dish only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Paradox]] directly, bound into a core of Nature Hearthstone (+2 Poison or Bludgeoning (choose on craft), weapon is always warm in living forests). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
