# Cold Compress Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

Activated when the vial is cracked — two chemicals mix, producing a cold reaction. Drops to near-freezing instantly. Applied to burns, sprains, and heat stroke. Single use. Does not restore HP.
