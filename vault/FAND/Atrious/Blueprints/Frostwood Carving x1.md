# Frostwood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A carving incorporating Frostwood (Wood). Wood from trees in perpetually frozen environments. The grain is white and the wood itself is cold to touch always. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage. Creatures hit must CON save DC 12 or have their movement reduced by 5ft for 1 round. Bonus: +1d4 Cold.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Frostwood): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
