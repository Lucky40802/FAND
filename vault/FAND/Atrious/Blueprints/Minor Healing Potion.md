# Minor Healing Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

The standard healing potion. A slightly sweet red liquid that closes minor wounds and knits small cuts on contact with the bloodstream. Every adventurer carries at least one. Brewed in batches.
