# Wooden Training Staff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A simple hardwood staff used by monks and soldiers in training. Sanded smooth to prevent splinters. Not glamorous, but teaches the fundamentals of staff combat before a student earns a real weapon.
