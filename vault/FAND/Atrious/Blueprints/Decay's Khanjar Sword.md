# Decay's Khanjar Sword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A Khanjar Sword that only functions properly because it channels a fragment of [[Decay]] directly, cast in a core of Copper Dragon Scale (+1d6 Acid). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system.

**Corrosive** (Potency 1, from Copper Dragon Scale): Weapon: +1d6 acid damage, and a hit lowers the target's Gear AC by 1 until repaired. Armor: acid resistance.
