# Hankyu

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A Hankyu, a real-world bow design drawn from martial history. Forged in iron or steel by any competent smith once the pattern is known — the difficulty lies in learning the proportions and temper the design calls for, not the raw materials.
