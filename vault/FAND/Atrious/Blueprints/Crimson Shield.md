# Crimson Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A shield prepared in blood alchemy. When the wielder is hit, the shield absorbs 2 damage per hit and stores it. Once per day, release the stored damage as a necrotic burst.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Bloodstone): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
