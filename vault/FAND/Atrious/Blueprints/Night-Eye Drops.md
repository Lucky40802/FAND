# Night-Eye Drops

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

Drops made from the distilled eyes of cave fish and a small amount of darkbloom pollen. Applied to the eyes, they enhance low-light vision for four hours. The world looks slightly silver while active.
