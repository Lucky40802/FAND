# Night Wrapping

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

A roll of veilcloth for wrapping weapons. Wrapped items make no sound when moved.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Darkveil Wrap|Darkveil Wrap]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dim Cap|Dim Cap]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Bandage|Shadow Bandage]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Mask|Shadow Mask]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Patch|Shadow Patch]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Thread|Shadow Thread]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
