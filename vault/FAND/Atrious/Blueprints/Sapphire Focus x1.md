# Sapphire Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Rare*

A focus incorporating Sapphire (Gemstone & Crystal). The gem of water and ice. Sapphires channel cold and aquatic energy. Blue as deep water, they are cool to the touch and lower the ambient temperature when clustered. Bonus: +2 Cold.

**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Sapphire): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
