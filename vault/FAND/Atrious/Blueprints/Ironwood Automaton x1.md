# Ironwood Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Very Rare*

A automaton incorporating Ironwood (Metal). A rare hardwood so dense it sinks in water. Ironwood is as hard as iron but lighter and more flexible. Weapons made from it are neither wood nor metal in any conventional sense. Bonus: +2.
