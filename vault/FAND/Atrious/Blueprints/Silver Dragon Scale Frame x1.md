# Silver Dragon Scale Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Very Rare*

A frame incorporating Silver Dragon Scale (Metal). Scales from a silver dragon. Armour provides resistance to cold. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage. The weapon also has +1d4 radiant — silver dragon scales carry both elements. Bonus: +1d4 Cold.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Dragon Scale Engine x1|Silver Dragon Scale Engine x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
