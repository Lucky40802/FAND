# Life Drain Vial

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A vial prepared with stolen vitality. The target must make a CON save DC 13 or lose 2d6 max HP for 24 hours. That HP is transferred to the thrower.
