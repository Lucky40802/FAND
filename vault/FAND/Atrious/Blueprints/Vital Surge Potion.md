# Vital Surge Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A potion of distilled vitality. Grants 3d6 temporary HP and +2 to Strength for 1 minute. At the end, the drinker takes 1d6 necrotic damage as the borrowed vitality is returned.
