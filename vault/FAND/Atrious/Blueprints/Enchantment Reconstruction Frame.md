# Enchantment Reconstruction Frame

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Uncommon*

A hand-crafted artifact, restored from a recovered relic fragment using Fragment of the Original. Unremarkable to look at, but it does exactly what it's meant to. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Common Fragment of the Original Relic|Common Fragment of the Original Relic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Material Composition Analyser|Material Composition Analyser]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
