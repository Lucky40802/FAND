# Bloodfire Sword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A sword with bloodstone set into the crossguard. Each kill heals the wielder for 1d8 HP as the blood energy is absorbed by the stones. Wounds inflicted by this weapon bleed heavily — targets take 1d4 additional damage…
