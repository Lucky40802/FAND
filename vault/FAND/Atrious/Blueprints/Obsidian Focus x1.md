# Obsidian Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A focus incorporating Obsidian (Gemstone & Crystal). Volcanic glass that can be knapped to a single-molecule edge — sharper than any metal. Brittle under lateral force. The Aztecs used it to devastating effect. In magical contexts, obsidian disrupts illusions on contact. Bonus: +1.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Obsidian): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
