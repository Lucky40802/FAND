# Air Elemental Essence Chandrahas

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Chandrahas reforged with Air Elemental Essence (Elemental) worked into its construction. The crystallised essence of an air elemental, stable in solid form. Items incorporating this essence grant the bearer a slow fall effect — falling damage is halved. Armour from it grants a fly speed of 10ft (hover only). Bonus: Fly speed 10ft.

## Material Property
**Elemental Core** (Potency 1, from Air Elemental Essence): Pick one element when the item is made: weapon +1d6 of that type, or armor resistance to it.
