# Predator's Call

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Uncommon*

A horn carved from the tooth of an apex predator. When blown, all beasts of CR 2 or lower within 300ft must WIS save DC 15 or become Frightened for 1 minute.
