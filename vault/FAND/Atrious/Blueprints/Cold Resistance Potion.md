# Cold Resistance Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A cool blue liquid that feels cold even through the vial. Grants resistance to cold damage for 1 hour. Consumed before arctic expeditions, underwater dives, and encounters with frost giants.
