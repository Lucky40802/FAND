# Bronze Spearhead

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A leaf-shaped bronze spearhead fitted to an ash shaft. The cast construction is slightly less durable than a forged head but far cheaper to produce in quantity. Armies buy these by the crate.
