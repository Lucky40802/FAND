# Composite War Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Uncommon*

Built from layers of wood, horn, and sinew laminated together. The composite construction stores more energy than wood alone. Shorter than a longbow but more powerful. A nomad cavalry weapon made for the saddle.
