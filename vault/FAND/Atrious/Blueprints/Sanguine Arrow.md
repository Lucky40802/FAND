# Sanguine Arrow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

An arrow tip prepared in blood alchemy. On hit it enters the bloodstream and continues draining — 1d6 necrotic at the start of each of the target's turns for 3 rounds.
