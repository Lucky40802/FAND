# Vitriol Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A dish incorporating Vitriol (Alchemical). Concentrated sulphuric acid stabilised in a crystalline carrier. The most potent acid compound available in mundane alchemy. Weapons coated in vitriol deal +1d8 acid damage. Armour corroded by vitriol loses 1 AC per hit (max -3) until repaired. Bonus: +1d8 Acid.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Vitriol Dish x1|Vitriol Dish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
