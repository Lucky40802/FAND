# Shadow Essence Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A veil incorporating Shadow Essence (Planar). The concentrated essence of shadow, crystallised from the Shadowfell. Items made from shadow essence cast no shadow themselves and make no sound. Weapons deal +1d6 shadow damage. Armour from it makes the bearer invisible in magical darkness. Bonus: +1d6 Shadow. The finish differs from smith to smith, though the core work is identical.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadow Essence): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
