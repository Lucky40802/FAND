# Spirit Ward Paste

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A paste applied to a threshold that prevents spirits from crossing. Lower-powered spirits (CR 3 or below) cannot cross an inscribed threshold. More powerful spirits may attempt a WIS save DC 13 to pass. Duration 8 hours.
