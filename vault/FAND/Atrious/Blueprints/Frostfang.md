# Frostfang

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A bastard sword of pale grey steel with a wolf-head pommel carved from milk-white glass. One of only a handful of blades forged using a smelting technique lost to most smiths generations ago — the metal never dulls and holds a cold edge even in a forge fire.
