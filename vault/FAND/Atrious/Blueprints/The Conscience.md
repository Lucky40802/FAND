# The Conscience

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Very Rare*

A small knife with a blade that is always warm. The Conscience cannot be used to hurt an innocent — it simply will not cut them. Against the guilty — those who have knowingly caused suffering — it deals maximum damage…

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Starfire): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
