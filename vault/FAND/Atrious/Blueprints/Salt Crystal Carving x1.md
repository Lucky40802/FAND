# Salt Crystal Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Salt Crystal (Common Material). Common salt, but sufficient concentration has spiritual significance. Line of salt crystals prevents spirits of CR 2 or lower from crossing. Applied to wounds, it causes an additional 1d4 damage but prevents infection. Bonus: Spirit ward.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Salt Crystal): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
