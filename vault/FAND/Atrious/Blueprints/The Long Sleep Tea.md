# The Long Sleep Tea

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A tea that induces magical stasis. The drinker does not age, hunger, or require air for up to 100 years. They can specify a condition that awakens them.
