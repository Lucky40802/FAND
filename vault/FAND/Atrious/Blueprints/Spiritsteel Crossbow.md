# Spiritsteel Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Spiritsteel prod and stock. The construction strikes incorporeal creatures as solid matter.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Longbow|Spiritsteel Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Shortbow|Spiritsteel Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
