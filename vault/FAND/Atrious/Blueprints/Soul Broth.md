# Soul Broth

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A clear broth prepared from ingredients gathered at dawn. Non-magical. The ritual of preparation improves focus and grants advantage on WIS saving throws for 1 hour.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ancestor's Porridge|Ancestor's Porridge]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
