# Glacial Iron Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Very Rare*

A slab incorporating Glacial Iron (Metal). Iron formed under centuries of glacial pressure at extreme cold. The metal never reaches room temperature — it is always cold to the touch. Weapons made from it deal cold damage and slow targets on hit. Bonus: +2 Cold.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Glacial Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
