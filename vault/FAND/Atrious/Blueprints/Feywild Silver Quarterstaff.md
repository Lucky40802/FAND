# Feywild Silver Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A 6-foot staff of Feywild Silver. The material changes colour with mood and can force shapeshifters back to true form.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Feywild Silver Spear|Feywild Silver Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Feywild Silver): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
