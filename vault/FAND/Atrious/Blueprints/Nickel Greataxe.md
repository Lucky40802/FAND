# Nickel Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Nickel construction is corrosion-resistant and slightly magnetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Nickel Hand Axe|Nickel Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
