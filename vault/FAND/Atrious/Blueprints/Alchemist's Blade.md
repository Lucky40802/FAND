# Alchemist's Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A steel dagger with a hollow channel running through the blade, accessible via a pump in the grip. The channel can be filled with alchemical compounds. Squeezing the grip coats the blade. The mechanism is fragile.
