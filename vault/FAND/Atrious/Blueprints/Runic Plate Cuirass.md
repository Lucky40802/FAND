# Runic Plate Cuirass

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A steel plate cuirass with a full set of protective runes carved into the inner surface where they touch the wearer's skin. The runes are active only while worn. Requires a runesmith to re-activate after damage.
