# Fear Glyph

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A glyph that, when triggered, produces an aura of magical fear in a 15ft radius. Creatures in range make a WIS save DC 13 or are Frightened for 1 minute. Frightened creatures flee from the glyph. Reusable — resets…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Float Rune|Float Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Holding Glyph|Holding Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Reveal Glyph|Reveal Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Memory|Rune of Memory]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Slow Poison|Rune of Slow Poison]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sharpness Rune|Sharpness Rune]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
