# Runes's Slab

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Legendary*

This slab only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Runes]] directly, bound into a core of Silver Dragon Scale (+1d4 Cold). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Silver Dragon Scale): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
