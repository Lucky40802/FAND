# The Bound Furies

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

Two curved blades on chains, seared permanently into the wrists of whoever accepts them. The fire they carry is a punishment-fire, taken from a debt owed to the gods. They cannot be removed without a sacrifice equal to the one that bound them, and they cannot be refused once drawn.
