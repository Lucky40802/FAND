# Titanium Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Titanium tip is lighter than steel with equivalent strength. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Titanium Quarterstaff|Titanium Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Titanium): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
