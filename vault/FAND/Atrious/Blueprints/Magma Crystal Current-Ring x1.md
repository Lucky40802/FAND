# Magma Crystal Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Very Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Magma Crystal (Elemental). Crystal formed at the boundary of elemental fire and earth. Both fire and earth elemental energy is locked within. Weapons deal +1d6 fire. On a hit, the magma crystal pulses with force — dealing 1d4 additional bludgeoning from the shockwave. Bonus: +1d6 Fire and Bludgeoning.
