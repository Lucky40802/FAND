# Spiritsteel Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Spiritsteel blade strikes incorporeal creatures as solid matter.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Greatsword|Spiritsteel Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spiritsteel Shortsword|Spiritsteel Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Spiritsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
