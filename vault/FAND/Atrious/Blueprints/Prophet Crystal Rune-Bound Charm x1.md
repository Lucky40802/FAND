# Prophet Crystal Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Prophet Crystal (Arcane Material). A clear crystal with a constantly shifting interior pattern. Items made from it grant +2 to initiative — the bearer always acts with foreknowledge. Once per day, the bearer can ask a yes/no question of the GM and receive an honest answer. Bonus: +2 to initiative.
