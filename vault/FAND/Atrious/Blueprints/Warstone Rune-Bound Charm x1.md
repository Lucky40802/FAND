# Warstone Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Warstone (Gemstone & Crystal). Red crystal formed in locations of extreme sustained magical combat. Weapons made from warstone grant +2 to attack rolls. When the bearer drops below 50% HP, the stone flares — granting +1d6 to damage rolls for 1 minute. Bonus: +2 to attack rolls.
