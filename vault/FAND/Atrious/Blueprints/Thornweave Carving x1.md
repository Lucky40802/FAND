# Thornweave Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Thornweave (Wood). A woven material made from the thorns of fey plants. Thornweave armour and weapon wraps are light, flexible, and deliver piercing damage on contact. The thorns regrow if broken. Bonus: +1 Piercing.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Thornweave): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
