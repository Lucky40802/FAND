# Resonance Compound Dish II

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Very Rare*

A dish incorporating Resonance Compound (Alchemical). A crystallised compound that stores and releases sonic energy. Weapons with resonance compound inlays deal thunder damage on critical hits — the sound on impact is deafening. Creatures struck on a critical hit must CON save DC 14 or be deafened for 1 minute. Bonus: Thunder on crit.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Resonance Compound Dish x1|Resonance Compound Dish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
