# Ironwood Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A 6-foot staff of Ironwood. The material is as hard as iron while remaining lighter than steel.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ironwood Spear|Ironwood Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ironwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
