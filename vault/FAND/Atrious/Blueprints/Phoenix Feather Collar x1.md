# Phoenix Feather Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A collar incorporating Phoenix Feather (Creature Harvest). A feather voluntarily shed by a phoenix. The feather glows with warm light. Items incorporating phoenix feathers grant the bearer resistance to fire damage. Once — when the bearer would die, the phoenix feather activates, restoring them to 1 HP. Single use. Bonus: Resurrection ward.
