# Dragonscale Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Artifact*

A compact bow. The Dragonscale limbs provides elemental resistance matching the dragon it came from.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonscale Crossbow|Dragonscale Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonscale Longbow|Dragonscale Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonscale): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
