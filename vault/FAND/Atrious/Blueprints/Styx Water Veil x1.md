# Styx Water Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A veil incorporating Styx Water (Planar). Water from the River Styx, the boundary between life and death. Used to quench weapons during forging. Any creature struck by a Styx-quenched weapon must make a WIS save or forget the last minute of their life. Extremely dangerous to handle. Bonus: +Special.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 2, from Styx Water): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +2d6 cold to creatures in water.
