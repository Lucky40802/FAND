# Sorrowstone Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A veil incorporating Sorrowstone (Spirit & Soul). Dark blue-grey stone from locations of great tragedy. Weapons that draw blood cause the target to make WIS save DC 12 or be Saddened — a minor condition causing -1 to all rolls for 1 minute. Bonus: Fear induction.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Sorrowstone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
