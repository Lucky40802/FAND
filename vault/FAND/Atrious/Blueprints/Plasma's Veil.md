# Plasma's Veil

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Legendary*

This veil only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Plasma]] directly, bound into a core of Veilcloth (+2 Shadow). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
