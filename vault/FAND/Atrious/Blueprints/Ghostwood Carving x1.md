# Ghostwood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Ghostwood (Wood). Wood from trees rooted in both the material and Ethereal Planes simultaneously. Ghostwood weapons strike incorporeal creatures as if they were solid. Armour from it makes the bearer partially incorporeal — 15% chance for physical attacks to pass through harmlessly. Bonus: Strikes incorporeal.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Ghostwood): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
