# Titan Bone Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Rare*

A fetish incorporating Titan Bone (Creature Harvest). Bone from an ancient titan, the precursors to modern giants. The bones are the size of a greataxe even from a finger. Items made from titan bone grant +3 STR while they are being used. Weapons deal +1d8 bludgeoning on all hits from sheer mass. Bonus: +3 STR while using.
