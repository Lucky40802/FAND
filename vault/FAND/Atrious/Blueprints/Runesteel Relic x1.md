# Runesteel Relic x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Rare*

A relic incorporating Runesteel (Arcane Material). Steel etched with permanent runes during the forging process rather than after. The runes are part of the metal's structure. Runesteel weapons can be enchanted with more runes than standard weapons and the enchantments never decay. Bonus: +2.
