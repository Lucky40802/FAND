# Potion of Speaking

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A clear liquid with no smell. After drinking, the consumer can understand and speak any language for 1 hour. The ability is passive — it requires no conscious effort. Cannot be used to read unknown languages.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Diminution|Potion of Diminution]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Growth|Potion of Growth]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
