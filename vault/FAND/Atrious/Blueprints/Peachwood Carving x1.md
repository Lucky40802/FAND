# Peachwood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A carving incorporating Peachwood (Wood). In certain traditions, peachwood is believed to ward off evil spirits. Items made from peachwood repel undead — undead creatures take 1d4 radiant damage when they touch peachwood items, and must save DC 12 or be unable to cross a threshold sealed with it. Bonus: Spirit ward.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Peachwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
