# Petrification Resin Draught x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Very Rare*

A draught incorporating Petrification Resin (Alchemical). Resin extracted from basilisk eye glands and stabilised alchemically. Weapons coated in petrification resin apply a slow petrification — creatures hit must CON save DC 14 or have one limb temporarily stiffened (-2 to rolls involving that limb). Three failed saves causes full petrification. Bonus: Slow petrification. A minor variation on a well-worn design.
