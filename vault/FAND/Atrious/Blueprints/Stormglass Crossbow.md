# Stormglass Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Stormglass prod and stock. The construction crackles with static charge and discharges lightning.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Stormglass Longbow|Stormglass Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Stormglass Shortbow|Stormglass Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Stormcharged** (Potency 3, from Stormglass): Weapon: +3d6 lightning or thunder damage. Armor: lightning resistance.
