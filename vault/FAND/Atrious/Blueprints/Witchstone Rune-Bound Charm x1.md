# Witchstone Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Witchstone (Arcane Material). Dull green stone from locations where powerful curses have soaked into the earth. Items made from witchstone can store one curse — when a creature is struck by a weapon made from witchstone, they must save against the stored curse. Bonus: Curse application.
