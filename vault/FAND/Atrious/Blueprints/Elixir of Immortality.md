# Elixir of Immortality

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Legendary*

A single dose prevents death from old age for one century. Does not prevent death from violence, disease, or misfortune. Requires decades to brew. The primary market is kings.
