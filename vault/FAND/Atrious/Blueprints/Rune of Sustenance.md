# Rune of Sustenance

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune inscribed on the bottom of a food container. Food stored in a container bearing this rune does not spoil. Water remains fresh. The rune has no expiry — it functions until physically destroyed.
