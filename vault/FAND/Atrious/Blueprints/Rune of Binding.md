# Rune of Binding

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A rune sequence carved into manacles or rope that magically tightens when a creature attempts to escape. STR checks to escape have disadvantage. Magical escape attempts require the creature to succeed a DC 16 ability…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glyph of Holding|Glyph of Holding]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
