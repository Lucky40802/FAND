# Grave Dirt Pouch

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A pouch of earth from a graveyard. Undead within 30ft that do not have a master treat the holder as non-threatening unless attacked.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ash Vial|Ash Vial]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soul Candle|Soul Candle]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
