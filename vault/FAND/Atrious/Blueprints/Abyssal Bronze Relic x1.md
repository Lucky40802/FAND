# Abyssal Bronze Relic x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Rare*

A relic incorporating Abyssal Bronze (Metal). Bronze formed in the Abyss and corrupted by demonic energy. The metal weeps a thin acidic residue. Weapons made from it deal acid damage and cause wounds that resist magical healing for one minute. Bonus: +2 Acid.
