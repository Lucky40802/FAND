# Divine Feast

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Rare*

A full meal prepared according to divine specification. All who eat gain 2d10 temporary HP and resistance to one damage type for 24 hours.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/The Divine Broth|The Divine Broth]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
