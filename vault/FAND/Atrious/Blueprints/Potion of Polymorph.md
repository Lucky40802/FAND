# Potion of Polymorph

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A shimmering liquid that cycles through colours. Drinking forces the user to make a CON save DC 15 or transform into a random CR 1 or lower beast for 10 minutes. Pass and they choose the beast.
