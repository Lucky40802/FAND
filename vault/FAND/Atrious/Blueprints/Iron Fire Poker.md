# Iron Fire Poker

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A fire poker of wrought iron with a simple ring at the handle end. 24 inches long. The point is square and tapered. Used to manage burning logs without burning your hands.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Belt Buckle|Iron Belt Buckle]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Door Hinge|Iron Door Hinge]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Horseshoe Set|Iron Horseshoe Set]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pot Hook|Pot Hook]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tallow Candle Holder|Tallow Candle Holder]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
