# Cold Iron Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Cold Iron construction disrupts fey enchantments on contact.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Iron Greatsword|Cold Iron Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Iron Longsword|Cold Iron Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Freezing** (Potency 1, from Cold Iron): Weapon: +1d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
