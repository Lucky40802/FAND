# Harness — Draft

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Common*

A full harness for attaching a beast to a cart, plough, or sledge. Fits creatures of Large size. The traces are rated for 2,000 pounds of pull. The padding prevents sores. Buckled rather than tied for quick release.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Beast Lead — Medium|Beast Lead — Medium]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Hobbles|Hobbles]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Muzzle — Large Beast|Muzzle — Large Beast]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
