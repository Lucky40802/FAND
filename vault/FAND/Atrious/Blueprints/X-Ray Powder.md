# X-Ray Powder

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Common*

A specialized artifact, restored from a recovered relic fragment using Cleaning Solvent. Made to a standard rather than to impress. The finish differs from smith to smith, though the core work is identical.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Freeze-Dry Apparatus|Freeze-Dry Apparatus]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Simple Cleaning Solvent Relic|Simple Cleaning Solvent Relic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
