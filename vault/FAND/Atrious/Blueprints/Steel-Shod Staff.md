# Steel-Shod Staff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A quarterstaff of seasoned oak with steel-shod ends. The metal caps protect the staff from splitting and add significant striking weight to each end. Standard weapon for monks, shepherds, and travelling scholars.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
