# Loyalty's Fragment

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Legendary*

This fragment only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Loyalty]] directly, bound into a core of Topaz (+2 Lightning). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Topaz): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
