# Wyrmscale Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Wyrmscale (Dragon Material). Scales shed by ancient dragons over centuries, found in the deep places they sleep. The colour varies by dragon type. Wyrmscale armour provides resistance to the elemental damage type matching the source dragon. Multiple scales from different dragons can be combined but provide only partial resistan Bonus: Elemental resistance.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 3, from Wyrmscale): Weapon: ignores 6 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
