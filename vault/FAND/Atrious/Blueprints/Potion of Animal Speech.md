# Potion of Animal Speech

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A brown liquid that smells of hay. Grants the ability to speak with beasts for 1 hour. The beasts are not made more intelligent. Conversations are limited accordingly.
