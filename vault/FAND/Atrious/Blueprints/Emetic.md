# Emetic

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A vial of foul-tasting liquid that induces vomiting within 2 minutes. Used to expel ingested poisons before they are absorbed. Unpleasant but potentially life-saving.
