# Shadowglass Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A veil incorporating Shadowglass (Planar). Glass formed in the Shadowfell from compressed shadow energy. Black and slightly translucent — looking through it shows the world in shades of grey. Weapons deal +1d6 shadow damage and extinguish non-magical light sources on hit. Bonus: +1d6 Shadow.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowglass): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
