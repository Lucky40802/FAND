# Door Glyph — Warning

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Common*

A warning glyph inscribed onto a door or threshold. When triggered by contact, it produces a loud crack of thunder audible 100ft away. Does not prevent passage — only signals it. DC 12 to detect, DC 15 to disarm…
