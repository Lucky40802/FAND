# Devil's Focus

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Legendary*

This focus only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Devil]] directly, bound into a core of Shadow Obsidian (+3 Shadow, weapon is invisible in dim light or darkness). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadow Obsidian): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
