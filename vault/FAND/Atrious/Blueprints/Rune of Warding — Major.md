# Rune of Warding — Major

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A major warding rune carved into a threshold. Any creature of a type specified during carving cannot cross the threshold while the rune is active. The rune lasts 1 month and must be recharged with materials worth 50gp.
