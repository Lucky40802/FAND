*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A cloak of pure shadowsteel weave. The wearer can step into any shadow large enough to hold them and exit from any other shadow within 1 mile as a bonus action.
