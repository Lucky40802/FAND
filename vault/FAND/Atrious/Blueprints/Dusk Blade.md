# Dusk Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A greatsword of shadowsteel with a veilcloth-wrapped handle. The blade exists simultaneously in the material and shadow planes — it strikes incorporeal creatures as solid matter and can be used to cut shadows themselves.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidmetal): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
