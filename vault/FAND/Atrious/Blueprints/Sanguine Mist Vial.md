# Sanguine Mist Vial

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A vial releasing blood mist in a 10ft radius. All creatures in the mist can be tracked by the alchemist for 1 hour.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Smoke Bomb|Blood Smoke Bomb]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Ward Paint|Blood Ward Paint]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crimson Smoke|Crimson Smoke]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sanguine Tattoo Ink|Sanguine Tattoo Ink]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
