# Runegold Mongol Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Mongol Bow reforged with Runegold (Metal) worked into its construction. Gold that has been worked with runic inscription at every stage of smelting and casting. The runes are integral to the metal. Any enchantment applied to Runegold items costs half the normal materials and is 25% more powerful. The gold itself cannot be destroyed by any mundane means. Bonus: +2 to enchantment rolls.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Runegold): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
