# Spin's Self Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A Self Bow that only functions properly because it channels a fragment of [[Spin]] directly, cast in a core of Duskmettle (+1). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Duskmettle): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
