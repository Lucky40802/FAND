# Bloodstone Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Bloodstone construction grows warm near open wounds and empowers blood magic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bloodstone Greatsword|Bloodstone Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bloodstone Longsword|Bloodstone Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Bloodstone): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
