# Moonsilver Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Moonsilver head deals double damage to lycanthropes and creatures of the night. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonsilver Greataxe|Moonsilver Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Glamoured** (Potency 1, from Moonsilver): Once per long rest cast charm person or disguise self (also mislead at Potency 5+). +1 to Deception.
