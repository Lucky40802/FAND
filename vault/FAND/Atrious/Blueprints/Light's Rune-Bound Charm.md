# Light's Rune-Bound Charm

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Legendary*

This rune-bound charm only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Light]] directly, bound into a core of Shadow Obsidian (+3 Shadow, weapon is invisible in dim light or darkness). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
