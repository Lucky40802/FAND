# Enlarge Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A pink bubbly liquid. Doubles the drinker's size category for one minute. Their weapon attacks deal +1d4 additional damage from the increased force. Advantage on STR checks. They become Grappled by doorways.
