# Styx Water Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Artifact*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Styx Water tip causes memory loss in struck targets who fail a WIS save. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Styx Water Quarterstaff|Styx Water Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 2, from Styx Water): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +2d6 cold to creatures in water.
