*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mainspring Winder|Mainspring Winder]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
