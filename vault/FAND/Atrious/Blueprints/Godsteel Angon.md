# Godsteel Angon

*Spear — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Angon reforged with Godsteel (Metal) worked into its construction. Metal touched and shaped by a deity directly. Cannot be crafted — only given. Weapons of Godsteel automatically hit (no attack roll needed) and deal maximum damage. Armour from it grants immunity to one damage type chosen at creation. Bonus: +5.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Godsteel): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
