# Displacer Beast Pelt Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A talisman incorporating Displacer Beast Pelt (Creature Harvest). The pelt of a displacer beast. Armour made from it grants the bearer partial displacement — the first attack against the bearer each round has disadvantage. Attacks that miss hit a displacement after-image. Bonus: Displacement ward.
