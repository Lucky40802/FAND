# Iron Wall Anchor

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

An iron anchor driven into a stone wall and used to attach scaling ropes, siege ladders, and grapple lines. The anchor has a rated pull of 2,000 pounds. Installed in 10 minutes with a hammer.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Wedge Set|Iron Wedge Set]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
