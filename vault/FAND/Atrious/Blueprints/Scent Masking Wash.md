# Scent Masking Wash

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Common*

A wash rubbed onto a tamed beast to mask its scent during hunting or scouting. Replaces natural scent with forest floor smell. Duration 4 hours. Also masks the beast handler's scent when they remain close.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bird Jesses|Bird Jesses]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Eye Patch - Beast|Eye Patch - Beast]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Falconry Hood|Falconry Hood]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Muzzle|Muzzle]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Saddlebags|Saddlebags]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
