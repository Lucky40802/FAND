# Stone Repair Compound

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Common*

A compound of crushed stone in a mineral binder that cures hard and can be shaped before setting. Used to fill chips, cracks, and missing sections in stone objects. When cured and sanded, the repair is nearly invisible.
