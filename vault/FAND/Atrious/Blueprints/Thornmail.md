# Thornmail

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

Dragon scale armour woven through with thornweave. The exterior is covered in natural thorns that regrow when broken. Any creature that makes a melee attack against the wearer takes 1d6 piercing damage from the thorns.…

**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonscale): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
