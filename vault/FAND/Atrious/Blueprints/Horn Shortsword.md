# Horn Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Uncommon*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Horn construction is flexible and shock-absorbing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Horn Greatsword|Horn Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Horn Longsword|Horn Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Horn): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
