# Tracking Blood

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A vial of alchemically prepared blood. Applied to a surface, allows the preparer to smell the blood's owner within 300ft for 24 hours.
