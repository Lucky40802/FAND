# Prismatic Tourmaline Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Prismatic Tourmaline (Arcane Material). A tourmaline that cycles through all colours continuously. Weapons deal a random element of damage each hit — determined by a d6 roll. Armour from prismatic tourmaline provides 20% resistance to all elemental damage types simultaneously. Bonus: Random element.

**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Prismatic Tourmaline): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
