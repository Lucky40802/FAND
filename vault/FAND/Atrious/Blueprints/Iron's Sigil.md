# Iron's Sigil

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

This sigil only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Iron]] directly, bound into a core of Negative Energy Crystal (Death radiation). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
