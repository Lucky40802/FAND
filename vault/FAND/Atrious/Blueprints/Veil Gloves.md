# Veil Gloves

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

Gloves of veilcloth that leave no fingerprints, no traces, and no residual magical signatures. Wearing them during spellcasting removes the material component requirement for spells with non-consumed material components.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dim Veil|Dim Veil]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dusk Cowl|Dusk Cowl]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dusk Trousers|Dusk Trousers]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Bandolier|Shadow Bandolier]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Cloak - Basic|Shadow Cloak - Basic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Map|Shadow Map]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Quiver|Shadow Quiver]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silent Soles|Silent Soles]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
