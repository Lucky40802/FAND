# Iron Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Iron head is reliable and widely repairable but rusts without care.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Iron Mace|Iron Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
