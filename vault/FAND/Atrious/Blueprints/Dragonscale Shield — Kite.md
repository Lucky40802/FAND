# Dragonscale Shield — Kite

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Uncommon*

A kite shield with a face of dragon scales over a steel backing. The scales provide the elemental resistance of the dragon they came from. The steel backing means the shield will not break under ordinary combat.…
