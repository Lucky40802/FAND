# Voidwalker Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Legendary*

A hand-forged armor, woven from shadow-touched thread using Shadow-Touched Thread and Binding Dye. Made to a standard rather than to impress.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadow-Touched Thread): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
