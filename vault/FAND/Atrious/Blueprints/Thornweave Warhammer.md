# Thornweave Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Thornweave head delivers piercing damage on contact and regrows broken thorns.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Thornweave Mace|Thornweave Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Thornweave): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
