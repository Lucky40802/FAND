# Clockwork Door Lock

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A door lock with a time-delay mechanism. Set a time from 1 minute to 12 hours — the lock cannot be opened (even from the inside with a key) until the time elapses. Used for vaults, safe rooms, and time-sensitive storage.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mechanical Counter|Mechanical Counter]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mechanical Door Closer|Mechanical Door Closer]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
