# Rune of Slow Poison

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a cup or vessel that slows the absorption of any poison in the liquid by 4 hours. Does not neutralise — it delays. Used to give time to administer an antidote. Also used by assassins to control timing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Fear Glyph|Fear Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Float Rune|Float Rune]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Holding Glyph|Holding Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Reveal Glyph|Reveal Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Rune of Memory|Rune of Memory]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sharpness Rune|Sharpness Rune]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
