# Steel Scissors

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A pair of steel scissors with 4-inch blades and looped handles. The pivot is a steel rivet. The blades are hardened at the edge and softer at the back for flexibility. Used by tailors, barbers, and surgeons.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Draw Knife|Draw Knife]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Farrier's Rasp|Farrier's Rasp]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/File|File]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Saw|Steel Saw]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
