# Starmetal Needle

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Common*

A thin needle of starmetal. When balanced on a still water surface or hung on a thread, it always points toward the Astral Sea. In regions near active planar crossings, the needle vibrates perceptibly.
