# Potion of Water Breathing

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A blue-green liquid with a faintly marine smell. Allows the drinker to breathe underwater for 1 hour. Does not affect swimming speed. Works by temporarily restructuring the drinker's blood-gas exchange.
