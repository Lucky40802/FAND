# Folded Steel Tanto

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A short single-edged blade with a reinforced spine, folded twelve times during forging. The result is a blade with a very hard cutting edge and a tough, flexible spine. Does not break under heavy lateral force.
