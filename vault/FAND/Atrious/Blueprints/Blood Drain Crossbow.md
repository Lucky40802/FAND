# Blood Drain Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A crossbow that fires blood-drain bolts. On hit, target loses HP which is transferred to the archer. The archer heals for all damage dealt.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
