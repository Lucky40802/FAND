# Yew Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Uncommon*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Yew construction means stores tension energy and releases it explosively.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Yew Longsword|Yew Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Yew Shortsword|Yew Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Yew): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
