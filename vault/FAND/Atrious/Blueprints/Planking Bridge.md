# Planking Bridge

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

Standard planking for temporary bridge construction. Each plank 12ft by 2ft. Ten per bundle.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Abatis|Abatis]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moat Crossing Plank|Moat Crossing Plank]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
