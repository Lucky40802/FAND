# Ashbone Vial x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A vial incorporating Ashbone (Creature Harvest). The calcified ash-bone remains of powerful spellcasters. Used as a component in spellcasting foci and staves. Spells cast through items made with Ashbone have +1 to their save DC. The ashes remember what their original owner knew. Bonus: +1 Spell Save DC.
