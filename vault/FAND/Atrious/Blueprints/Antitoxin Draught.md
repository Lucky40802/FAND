# Antitoxin Draught

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A bitter liquid brewed from charcoal, activated clay minerals, and a handful of detoxifying herbs. Drink immediately after poisoning. Effectiveness decreases rapidly the longer you wait.
