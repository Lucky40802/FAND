# Adamantine Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Adamantine tip ignores object hardness and cannot be destroyed by normal means. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Adamantine Quarterstaff|Adamantine Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 3, from Adamantine): Weapon: +2 damage. Armor: +2 Gear AC.
