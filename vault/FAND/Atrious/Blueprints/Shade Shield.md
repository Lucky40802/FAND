# Shade Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Uncommon*

A shield of shadowsteel faced in veilcloth. Provides +2 AC. Once per day, the wielder can have the shield absorb a shadow, releasing it as a blinding burst affecting all creatures within 15ft.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
