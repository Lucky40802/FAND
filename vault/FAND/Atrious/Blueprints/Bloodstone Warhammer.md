# Bloodstone Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Bloodstone head grows warm near open wounds and empowers blood magic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bloodstone Mace|Bloodstone Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
