# Steel Riveted Hauberk

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A mail shirt of riveted steel rings reaching to mid-thigh. Each ring is closed with a steel rivet rather than just butted. Riveted mail is significantly harder to penetrate than butted mail. Heavy but worth the weight.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
