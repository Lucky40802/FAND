# Amethyst Shortbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A compact bow. The Amethyst limbs disrupts mental control and deals psychic damage on impact.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Amethyst Crossbow|Amethyst Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Amethyst Longbow|Amethyst Longbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Amethyst): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
