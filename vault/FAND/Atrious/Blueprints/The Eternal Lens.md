# The Eternal Lens

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Legendary*

A lens ground from diamond that shows not what is but what was, what is, and what might be — all simultaneously. The bearer must make a WIS save DC 20 each day or become Confused by the overlapping timelines.
