# Iron Scimitar

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A curved single-edged blade optimised for slashing from horseback. The curve brings the edge across a target in a draw-cut that maximises damage. Common in desert regions where cavalry dominates.
