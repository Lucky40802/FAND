# Plain Replacement Inlay Relic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Common*

A plain relic built from replacement inlay, taught early in Relic Restoration training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Magnification Hood|Magnification Hood]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Micro-Spatula Set|Micro-Spatula Set]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
