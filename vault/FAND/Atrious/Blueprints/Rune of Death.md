# Rune of Death

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Legendary*

A single rune inscribed on a blade. Any creature struck by the blade must make a CON save DC 20 or drop to 0 HP. Legendary creatures have advantage on the save. The rune can only be inscribed once per weapon, ever.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Void Nullstone): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
