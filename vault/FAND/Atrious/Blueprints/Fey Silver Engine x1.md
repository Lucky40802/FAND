# Fey Silver Engine x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Very Rare*

A engine incorporating Fey Silver (Metal). Silver refined in the Feywild over decades. Slightly iridescent, always slightly damp to touch. Weapons deal +2 to attack rolls against non-magical creatures. Armour from fey silver allows the bearer to see through illusions — they have advantage on saves against illusion magic. Bonus: +2 vs mundane creatures.
