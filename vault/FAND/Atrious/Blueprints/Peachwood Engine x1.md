# Peachwood Engine x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Rare*

A engine incorporating Peachwood (Wood). In certain traditions, peachwood is believed to ward off evil spirits. Items made from peachwood repel undead — undead creatures take 1d4 radiant damage when they touch peachwood items, and must save DC 12 or be unable to cross a threshold sealed with it. Bonus: Spirit ward.
