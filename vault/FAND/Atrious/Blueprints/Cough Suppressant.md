# Cough Suppressant

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A thick sweet syrup that suppresses coughing for 4 hours. Used by infiltrators who cannot afford to cough at the wrong moment. Also genuinely useful for the sick. Contains honey, thyme, and a mild sedative.
