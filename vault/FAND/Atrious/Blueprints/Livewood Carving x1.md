# Livewood Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A carving incorporating Livewood (Wood). Wood from a rare tree species that continues living after harvest. Items made from livewood slowly grow — cracks and chips heal at 1 HP per hour. Bows of livewood are living organisms and form bonds with their owners, improving in accuracy over time (+1 to attack rolls after 30 days of use). Bonus: Self-repair.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Livewood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
