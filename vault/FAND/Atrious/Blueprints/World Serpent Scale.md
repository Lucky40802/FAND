# World Serpent Scale

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Legendary*

A single scale from a serpent large enough to encircle the world. The scale is 3ft across. Made into armour it provides resistance to all elemental damage types simultaneously.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonscale): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
