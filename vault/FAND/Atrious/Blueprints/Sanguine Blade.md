# Sanguine Blade

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A dagger treated with blood alchemy. On a hit it enters the bloodstream — dealing 1d4 additional necrotic damage at the start of each of the target's turns until they use an action to staunch the wound.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crimson Dagger - Compact|Crimson Dagger - Compact]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sanguine Dagger|Sanguine Dagger]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Bloodstone): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
