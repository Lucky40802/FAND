# Foresee's Slab

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Legendary*

This slab only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Foresee]] directly, bound into a core of Alexandrite (+1d4 random). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.
