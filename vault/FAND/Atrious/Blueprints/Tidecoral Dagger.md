# Tidecoral Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Tidecoral edge is naturally damp and functional even at extreme ocean depths. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 1, from Tidecoral): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +1d6 cold to creatures in water.
