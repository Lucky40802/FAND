# Wraithbone Fetish x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Rare*

A fetish incorporating Wraithbone (Creature Harvest). Bone harvested from powerful undead creatures that retains its supernatural properties after the creature's final death. Wraithbone weapons ignore non-magical armour entirely and deal necrotic damage. They are cold to the touch and make no sound. Bonus: +2 Necrotic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wraithbone Talisman x1|Wraithbone Talisman x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
