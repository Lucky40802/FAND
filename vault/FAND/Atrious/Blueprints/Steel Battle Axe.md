# Steel Battle Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A double-bitted battle axe with two opposing blades on a 28-inch haft. The bits are forged from a single piece of steel with a socket for the haft. Both blades are symmetrical. A weapon as much as a tool.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
