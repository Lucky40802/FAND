# Negative Energy Crystal Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A veil incorporating Negative Energy Crystal (Planar). Crystal from the Negative Energy Plane. Constantly drains life energy from nearby creatures — bearers lose 1 HP per minute but gain necrotic damage. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic. Undead are healed by the energy rather than harmed. Bonus: Death radiation.

## Material Property
**Elemental Core** (Potency 1, from Negative Energy Crystal): Pick one element when the item is made: weapon +1d6 of that type, or armor resistance to it.
