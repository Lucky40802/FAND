# Sanguine Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

On a hit, deals 1d4 additional necrotic damage as compound enters bloodstream.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crimson Dagger - Compact|Crimson Dagger - Compact]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sanguine Blade|Sanguine Blade]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 1, from Bloodstone): Weapon: once per turn, regain 2 hit points when you hit.
