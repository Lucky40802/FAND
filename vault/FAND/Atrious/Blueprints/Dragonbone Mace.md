# Dragonbone Mace

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Legendary*

A flanged one-handed bludgeon. The Dragonbone flanges holds enchantments better than any metal and carries elemental resonance.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonbone Warhammer|Dragonbone Warhammer]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
