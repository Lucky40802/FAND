# Ashwood Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Ashwood construction burns without being consumed and deals fire damage.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ashwood Hand Axe|Ashwood Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ashwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
