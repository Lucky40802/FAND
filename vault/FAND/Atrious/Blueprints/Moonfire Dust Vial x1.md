# Moonfire Dust Vial x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Very Rare*

A vial incorporating Moonfire Dust (Alchemical). A fine silver dust produced by grinding moonstone through a specific alchemical process. Applied to a weapon, it glows in darkness and deals +1d4 radiant. Applied to armour, it makes the wearer glow — useful for visibility but terrible for stealth. Bonus: +1d4 Radiant.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Moonfire Dust Draught III|Moonfire Dust Draught III]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
