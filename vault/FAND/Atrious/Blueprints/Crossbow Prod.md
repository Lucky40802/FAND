# Crossbow Prod

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

The steel bow of a crossbow, tempered and shaped. The most technically demanding part of the weapon — too stiff and it won't span, too soft and it won't fire.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Arrowheads|Arrowheads]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Porthole Cover|Porthole Cover]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
