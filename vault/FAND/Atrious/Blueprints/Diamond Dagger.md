# Diamond Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Legendary*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Diamond edge channels divine light and makes the weapon glow faintly at all times. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Diamond): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
