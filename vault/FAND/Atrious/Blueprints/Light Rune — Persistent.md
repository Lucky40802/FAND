# Light Rune — Persistent

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune of light carved into a stone that makes it shed light as a torch. Duration 8 hours. Can be dimmed by covering it. Does not produce heat. The rune must be recarved after 30 uses.
