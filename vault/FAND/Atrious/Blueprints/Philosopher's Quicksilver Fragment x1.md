# Philosopher's Quicksilver Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Rare*

A fragment incorporating Philosopher's Quicksilver (Metal). Mercury stabilised through the philosopher's process. Does not evaporate, does not poison. A liquid metal that can be poured and shaped at will. Items made from it can change shape slowly — a Philosopher's Quicksilver dagger can become a shortsword over 1 minute. Bonus: +2.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Philosopher's Quicksilver): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
