# Shadowsteel Smallsword Court

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Smallsword Court reforged with Shadowsteel (Metal) worked into its construction. A dark metal formed in the Shadowfell. Shadowsteel is always cold to the touch and muffles the sound of its own movement. Weapons made from it are silent and deal shadow damage. They are also partially transparent — visible but not entirely real. Bonus: +2 Shadow.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
