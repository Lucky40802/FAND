# Runic Weapon Inscription — Fire

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A fire rune sequence inscribed into a blade during its final tempering. The blade deals an additional 1d6 fire damage on hit. The runes activate on contact rather than continuously — the blade is not hot to the touch…
