# Abyssal Obsidian Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Rare*

A focus incorporating Abyssal Obsidian (Gemstone & Crystal). Obsidian formed in the Abyss under conditions of pure chaos and evil. Shards of it are constantly shifting in shape. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic. On a natural 20, a random chaotic effect occurs — the weapon's wielder rolls on the wild magic surge table. Bonus: +1d6 Necrotic, Chaos.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 2, from Abyssal Obsidian): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
