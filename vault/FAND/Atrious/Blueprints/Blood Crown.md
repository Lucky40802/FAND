# Blood Crown

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Very Rare*

A crown of hardened blood and bloodstone. The wearer can sense the health of any creature within 60ft and can force any creature they can see to make a CON save DC 18 or lose 2d10 max HP.
