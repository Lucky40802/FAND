# Bloodiron Trident

*Spear — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Trident reforged with Bloodiron (Metal) worked into its construction. Iron that has absorbed so much spilled blood it has permanently changed. Dark red, slightly warm to touch. Weapons deal +1d4 necrotic. Against undead, deals radiant instead. Bonus: +1d4 Necrotic.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Bloodiron): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
