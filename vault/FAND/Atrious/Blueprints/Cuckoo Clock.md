# Cuckoo Clock

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A wall clock with a mechanical bird that emerges on the hour. Keeps time precisely. The bird mechanism adds no practical value and every client asks for it.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Battering Ram Handle|Battering Ram Handle]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mantlet Frame|Mantlet Frame]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pavise Stand|Pavise Stand]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Portable Mantlet|Portable Mantlet]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
