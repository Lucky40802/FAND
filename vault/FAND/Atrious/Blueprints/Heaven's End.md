# Heaven's End

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A longbow of starmetal strung with starthread. The arrows it fires appear as streaks of sunlight. Heaven's End deals radiant damage. Arrows fired from it have unlimited range — the attack roll has no range penalty.…

**Holy** (Potency 2, from Celestial Starfire): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
