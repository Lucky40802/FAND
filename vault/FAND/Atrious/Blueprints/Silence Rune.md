# Silence Rune

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Common*

A rune carved into a small iron disc. When pressed to a surface, that surface makes no sound when struck for 1 hour. Used by carpenters to work quietly, by infiltrators to move silently, and by parents of sleeping…

The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
