# Spiritsteel Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Spiritsteel (Metal). Steel that has been partially dissolved into the Astral Plane and reformed. Spiritsteel weapons can strike incorporeal creatures as if they were solid and deal force damage. They appear slightly transparent. Bonus: +2 Force.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Spiritsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
