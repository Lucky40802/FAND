# Armour of Runic Perfection

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

Full plate with 14 distinct runes covering every surface. Grants +2 AC above the armour's base, resistance to one damage type, advantage on saves against spells, and glows faintly blue.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 4, from All domain gems — one of each): Stores one spell of level 2 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +2 to spell attacks.
