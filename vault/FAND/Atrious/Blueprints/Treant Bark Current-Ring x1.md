# Treant Bark Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Treant Bark (Wood). Bark shed by living treants. Treants shed bark every decade and often gift it to those who have helped them. Armour of treant bark provides excellent protection but is vulnerable to fire — fire damage against treant bark armour deals +1d6 additional damage. Bonus: Fire weakness ward.
