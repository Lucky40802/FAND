# The Unbreaking

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

A shield that has never been broken. Not in the forge, not in the field. Its maker is unknown. The metal is unidentifiable by any alchemical test. It has been lost and recovered seventeen times. It always returns.
