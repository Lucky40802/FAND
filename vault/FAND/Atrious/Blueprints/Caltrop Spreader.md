# Caltrop Spreader

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

A device that, when dragged along the ground, drops iron caltrops at regular 1-foot intervals. Covers a 60-foot line per pass. More efficient than scattering caltrops by hand. The hopper holds 200 caltrops.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Arrow Slit Frame|Arrow Slit Frame]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cauldron Bracket|Cauldron Bracket]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cauldron Over Gate|Cauldron Over Gate]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gate Lock Bar|Gate Lock Bar]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
