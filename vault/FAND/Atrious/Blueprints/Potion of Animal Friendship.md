# Potion of Animal Friendship

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A green-tinted liquid with a grassy smell. Consumed by a person, it causes beasts of CR 1 or lower to regard the drinker as non-threatening for 1 hour. Does not grant control — only neutrality.
