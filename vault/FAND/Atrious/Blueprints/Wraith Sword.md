# Wraith Sword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A longsword of wrapped soulsteel. Strikes incorporeal creatures as solid. Crits drain 1d10 max HP from the target permanently until a Greater Restoration spell.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
