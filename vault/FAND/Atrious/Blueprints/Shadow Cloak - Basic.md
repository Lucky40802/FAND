# Shadow Cloak - Basic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

A full cloak of veilcloth. The wearer has advantage on Stealth checks and disadvantage on Perception checks against them in dim light or darkness.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dim Veil|Dim Veil]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dusk Cowl|Dusk Cowl]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dusk Trousers|Dusk Trousers]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Bandolier|Shadow Bandolier]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Map|Shadow Map]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Shadow Quiver|Shadow Quiver]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silent Soles|Silent Soles]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veil Gloves|Veil Gloves]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
