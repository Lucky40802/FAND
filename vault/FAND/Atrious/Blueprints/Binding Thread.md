# Binding Thread

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A length of soulsteel wire. Any knot tied in binding thread cannot be untied by anyone other than the person who tied it. Used to bind documents, seal pouches, and — in dark traditions — bind spirits to objects.
