# Potion of Diminution

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Uncommon*

A pale liquid that halves the drinker's size for 1 hour. Allows passage through small spaces. The drinker's Strength decreases proportionally. Useful for infiltration. Disorienting on return.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Growth|Potion of Growth]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Potion of Speaking|Potion of Speaking]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
