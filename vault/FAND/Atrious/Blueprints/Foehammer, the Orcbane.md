# Foehammer, the Orcbane

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

An elven war-hammer made in a hidden city during a lost age, before that city fell. Glows blue-white in the presence of orcs and goblins, bright enough to read by. Passed hand to hand through history, always finding its way to whoever most needs to hold a line.
