# Positive Energy Crystal Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Very Rare*

A veil incorporating Positive Energy Crystal (Planar). Crystal from the Positive Energy Plane. Constantly radiates healing energy — bearers recover 1 HP per minute. Weapons deal radiant damage. The crystal is dangerous to undead — undead creatures within 10ft of positive energy crystal items take 1d4 radiant per round. Bonus: Healing radiation. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

## Material Property
**Holy** (Potency 2, from Positive Energy Crystal): Weapon: +2d6 radiant damage, doubled against fiends and undead. Focus: +1 to healing.
