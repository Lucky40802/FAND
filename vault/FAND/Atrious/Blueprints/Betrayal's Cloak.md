# Betrayal's Cloak

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Legendary*

This cloak only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Betrayal]] directly, bound into a core of Prismatic Tourmaline (Random element). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Prismatic Tourmaline): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
