# Ballista Frame

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Siege Engineering) — Common*

The complete frame for a light ballista, excluding the prod and spanning mechanism. Made of seasoned oak with steel brackets. The elevation and traverse mechanisms are included. Assembles in 30 minutes with two workers.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Catapult Arm|Catapult Arm]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
