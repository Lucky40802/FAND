# Rune of Absolute Silence

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Very Rare*

A rune that when inscribed in a room and activated creates a permanent zone of magical silence within the room's boundaries. No sound enters or leaves. Communication within the room is possible but nothing is audible…
