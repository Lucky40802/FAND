# Mechanical Prosthetic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Uncommon*

A full replacement limb driven by clockwork. Provides full functionality. The hand can be detached and controlled via a cable system as a bonus action — useful for reaching around corners.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mechanical Arm - Articulated|Mechanical Arm - Articulated]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
