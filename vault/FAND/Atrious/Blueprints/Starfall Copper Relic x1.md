# Starfall Copper Relic x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Rare*

A relic incorporating Starfall Copper (Metal). Copper extracted from meteorites that fell through multiple planar layers before impact. Slightly blue-tinted. Weapons deal +1d6 radiant damage. Conducts magic more efficiently — spellcasting foci of starfall copper have +1 to spell attack rolls. Bonus: +2.
