# Bronze Dragon Scale Relic x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Very Rare*

A relic incorporating Bronze Dragon Scale (Metal). Scales from a bronze dragon. Armour provides resistance to lightning. Weapons deal +1d6 lightning. Once per day, strike the weapon against a surface to create a lightning bolt — 60ft line, 4d8 lightning, DEX save DC 15 for half. Bonus: +1d6 Lightning.
