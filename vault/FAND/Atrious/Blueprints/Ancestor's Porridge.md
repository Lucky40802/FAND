# Ancestor's Porridge

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A porridge recipe passed through a specific family. The eater gains temporary access to one skill proficiency that any of their direct ancestors possessed. Duration 1 hour.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soul Broth|Soul Broth]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
