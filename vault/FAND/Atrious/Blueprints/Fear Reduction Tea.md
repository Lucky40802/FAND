# Fear Reduction Tea

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

An herbal tea of courage herbs — lion's mane, valerian, and a pinch of cremated bone. Grants advantage on saves against Frightened for 8 hours. Does not eliminate fear — reduces the magical compulsion of it.
