# Wound Sealant

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A thick paste that seals surface wounds on contact. Stops bleeding immediately. Does not heal the wound — it seals it. Remove the sealant before properly treating the wound or it will heal incorrectly.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Antiseptic Wash|Antiseptic Wash]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Compress Potion|Cold Compress Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Endurance Tea|Endurance Tea]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Analgesic|Mild Analgesic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Stimulant|Mild Stimulant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Scent Mask|Scent Mask]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Warmth Potion|Warmth Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Weak Paralytic|Weak Paralytic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
