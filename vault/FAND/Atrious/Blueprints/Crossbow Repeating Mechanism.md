# Crossbow Repeating Mechanism

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A spanning and loading mechanism that allows a crossbow to fire and reload without the user's manual effort. Reduces firing to a bonus action.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cam Mechanism|Cam Mechanism]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Combination Lock|Combination Lock]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gear Train - Clock|Gear Train - Clock]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gear Train|Gear Train]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Portcullis Spike|Portcullis Spike]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Worm Gear Set|Worm Gear Set]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
