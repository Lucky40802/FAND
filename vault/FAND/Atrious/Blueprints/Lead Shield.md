# Lead Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A standard shield. The Lead face absorbs magical residue and is extraordinarily heavy.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials, AC) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Lead Buckler|Lead Buckler]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
