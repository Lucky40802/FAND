# Stormglass Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A 6-foot staff of Stormglass. The material crackles with static charge and discharges lightning.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Stormglass Spear|Stormglass Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
