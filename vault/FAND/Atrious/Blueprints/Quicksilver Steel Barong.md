# Quicksilver Steel Barong

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Barong reforged with Quicksilver Steel (Metal) worked into its construction. Steel alloyed with stabilised mercury through a dangerous alchemical process. The finished metal has a subtle fluid quality — it flexes rather than shattering. Weapons have a 50% chance to avoid breaking on a natural 1. Bonus: +1.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Quicksilver Steel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
