# Bone Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Uncommon*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Bone edge is lighter than metal and carries spiritual weight in certain traditions. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Bone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
