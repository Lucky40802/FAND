# Platinum Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A mechanical bow with a Platinum prod and stock. The construction conducts magical energy without loss.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Platinum Longbow|Platinum Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Platinum Shortbow|Platinum Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Platinum): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
