# Starvine Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Rare*

A carving incorporating Starvine (Wood). A vine that grows on god-isles in the Astral Sea. Dries into a strong, flexible cord that always knows where true north is. Bows strung with starvine give +4 to navigation checks. Arrows fired from starvine-strung bows never miss due to wind. Bonus: Navigation +4.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Starvine): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
