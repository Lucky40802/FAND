# Obsidian Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Obsidian tip creates an edge sharp enough to part silk but requires careful use. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Obsidian Quarterstaff|Obsidian Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Obsidian): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
