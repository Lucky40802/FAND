# Brionac - Lightning Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Legendary*

A lightning spear of divine origin — an interpretation of the Five Barriers of Brionac from Irish mythology adapted into fantasy. Five shafts of lightning converge into a single spear tip. The throw creates a shockwave.
