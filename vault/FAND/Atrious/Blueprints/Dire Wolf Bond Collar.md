# Dire Wolf Bond Collar

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Rare*

A collar that, when placed on a Dire Wolf, permanently bonds it to the tamer. The wolf gains +2 to all ability scores and acts on the tamer's turn.
