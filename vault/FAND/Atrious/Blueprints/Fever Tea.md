# Fever Tea

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A dried herb packet for brewing a medicinal tea. One packet brews one cup that reduces fever by 2 degrees. Contains willow bark, elderflower, and peppermint. Not a cure — supports the body's own recovery.
