# Glacial Diamond Fragment x1

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Void Smithing) — Very Rare*

A fragment incorporating Glacial Diamond (Gemstone & Crystal). A diamond formed under the pressure of glaciers in extreme northern conditions. Contains trapped ancient ice. Weapons deal +1d6 cold damage. Armour from glacial diamond provides immunity to natural cold and resistance to magical cold. Bonus: +1d6 Cold.

**Freezing** (Potency 2, from Glacial Diamond): Weapon: +2d6 cold damage, and a hit cuts the target's speed by 10 ft. Armor: cold resistance.
