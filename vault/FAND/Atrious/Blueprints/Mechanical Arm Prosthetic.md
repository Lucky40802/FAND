# Mechanical Arm Prosthetic

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Uncommon*

A functional mechanical replacement arm with 14 articulated joints and a grip capable of 40 pounds of force. Hand gestures can actuate the grip. The arm is fitted to the wearer's stump with a leather and buckle harness.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pneumatic Leg Prosthetic|Pneumatic Leg Prosthetic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
