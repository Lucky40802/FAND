# Oak Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Oak head absorbs impact without splintering. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Oak Greataxe|Oak Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Oak): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
