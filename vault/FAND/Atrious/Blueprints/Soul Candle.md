# Soul Candle

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Common*

A candle made from tallow rendered from bone marrow. When burned in the presence of a recently deceased creature, it keeps the spirit present for 10 minutes, allowing one question. The spirit answers truthfully but…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ash Vial|Ash Vial]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Grave Dirt Pouch|Grave Dirt Pouch]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
