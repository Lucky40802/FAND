# The Widow's Cleaver

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

An enormous cleaver-shaped blade with a hole in the upper corner of the cutting surface and a semi-circular notch at the base. Designed for a single purpose — the notch catches the neck, the hole lets the blade drink the iron from the blood it spills, slowly reforging itself stronger with every kill.
