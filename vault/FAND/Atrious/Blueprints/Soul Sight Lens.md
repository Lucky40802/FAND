# Soul Sight Lens

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

A lens ground from soulsteel and mounted in bone. Looking through it shows the soul of any living creature — its alignment, emotional state, and whether it is under any supernatural compulsion.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soul Resonance Extractor|Soul Resonance Extractor]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
