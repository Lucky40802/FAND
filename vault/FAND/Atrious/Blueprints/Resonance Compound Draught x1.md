# Resonance Compound Draught x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Very Rare*

A draught incorporating Resonance Compound (Alchemical). A crystallised compound that stores and releases sonic energy. Weapons with resonance compound inlays deal thunder damage on critical hits — the sound on impact is deafening. Creatures struck on a critical hit must CON save DC 14 or be deafened for 1 minute. Bonus: Thunder on crit. A common enough commission that most workshops keep the pattern on hand.
