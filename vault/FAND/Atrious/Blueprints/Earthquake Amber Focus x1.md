# Earthquake Amber Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A focus incorporating Earthquake Amber (Gemstone & Crystal). Ancient amber from regions prone to magical earthquakes. Items incorporating it vibrate subtly. Weapons deal normal damage but also release a concussive pulse — targets must STR save DC 12 or be knocked 5ft away from the wielder. Bonus: Knockback on hit.
