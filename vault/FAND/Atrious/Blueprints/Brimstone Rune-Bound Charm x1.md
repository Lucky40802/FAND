# Brimstone Rune-Bound Charm x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Rare*

A rune-bound charm incorporating Brimstone (Gemstone & Crystal). Natural sulphur crystal from volcanic or infernal environments. Weapons deal +1d4 fire damage and leave a lingering burning smell. On a kill, the body ignites — burning for 1 round and dealing 1d4 fire to adjacent creatures. Bonus: +1d4 Fire.
