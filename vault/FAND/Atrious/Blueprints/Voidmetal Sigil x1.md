# Voidmetal Sigil x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Very Rare*

A sigil incorporating Voidmetal (Metal). A metal that should not exist. Voidmetal appears black and slightly absorbs light. It cuts through magical barriers and force fields as easily as physical matter. Difficult to work — the metal itself resists being shaped. Bonus: +3 Force.
