# Arcanite Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Arcanite (Arcane Material). A shimmering blue-white substance that forms when magical energy is concentrated in one location for centuries. Raw magic in a solid state. All spells cast through arcanite items have +1 to attack rolls and save DCs. The substance slowly fades over 100 years as it releases its stored energy. Bonus: +1 to all spell rolls.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Arcanite): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
