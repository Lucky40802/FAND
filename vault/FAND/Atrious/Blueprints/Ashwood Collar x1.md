# Ashwood Collar x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Very Rare*

A collar incorporating Ashwood (Wood). Wood from trees that grow in volcanic regions near the Plane of Fire. The wood is charred black on the outside and blood-red inside. It burns without being consumed. Weapons made from ashwood deal fire damage and never catch fire from external sources. Bonus: +2 Fire.
