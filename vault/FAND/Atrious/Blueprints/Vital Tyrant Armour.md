# Vital Tyrant Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

Full plate blood alchemy armour. Heals 4 HP per round. Immune to necrotic. Any creature the wearer kills has their blood absorbed — restoring 2d10 HP.

**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
