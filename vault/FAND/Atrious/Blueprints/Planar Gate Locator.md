# Planar Gate Locator

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Uncommon*

A device that detects permanent planar gates within 10 miles. Shows direction and approximate type of gate. Does not detect temporary gates. Used by plane-walkers to find transit points without having prior knowledge…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Colour Pool Directory|Colour Pool Directory]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
