# Wraithbone Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Bone Carving) — Legendary*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Wraithbone head ignores non-magical armour and leaves unnaturally cold wounds. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wraithbone Greataxe|Wraithbone Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Necromantic** (Potency 1, from Wraithbone): Necromancy focus: +1 to necromancy spell attacks and save DCs. Undead you raise get 5 extra hit points.
