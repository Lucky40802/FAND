# Sounding Weight

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Common*

An unusual artifact, built for reading the stars and the space between them using Meridian Glass. The kind of piece a professional actually reaches for, not just something for a display case. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Basic Meridian Glass Compass|Basic Meridian Glass Compass]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
