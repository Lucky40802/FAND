# Fathomstone Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Very Rare*

A core incorporating Fathomstone (Gemstone & Crystal). Black crystal from the deepest ocean trenches. Items made from fathomstone allow the bearer to breathe water indefinitely. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage and slow targets (CON save DC 12 or -10ft speed for 1 round). Bonus: Waterbreathing.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Fathomstone Rune-Bound Charm x1|Fathomstone Rune-Bound Charm x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
