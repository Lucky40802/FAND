# Abyssal Obsidian Core x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Very Rare*

A core incorporating Abyssal Obsidian (Gemstone & Crystal). Obsidian formed in the Abyss under conditions of pure chaos and evil. Shards of it are constantly shifting in shape. Weapons deal +1d6 necrotic. On a natural 20, a random chaotic effect occurs — the weapon's wielder rolls on the wild magic surge table. Bonus: +1d6 Necrotic, Chaos.
