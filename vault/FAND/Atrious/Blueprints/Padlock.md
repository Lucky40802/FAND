# Padlock

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A hasp-and-shackle lock with a simple pin tumbler mechanism. Provides basic security against casual entry. A determined person with a pick can open it in under a minute. A determined person without one takes longer.
