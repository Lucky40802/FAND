# Veilcloth Spear

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A thrusting polearm 6 feet long. The Veilcloth tip is invisible in darkness and grants advantage on opening strikes from concealment. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Quarterstaff|Veilcloth Quarterstaff]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
