# Starfire Opal Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Very Rare*

A slab incorporating Starfire Opal (Gemstone & Crystal). An opal with a shifting inner fire that looks like a captured star. The damage type alternates between radiant and fire — radiant during the day, fire at night. Weapons deal +1d4 of the appropriate type. Armour from starfire opal provides resistance to whichever is currently active. Bonus: +1d4 Radiant or Fire.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 2, from Starfire Opal): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
