# Potion of Storm Giant Strength

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A blue-grey liquid with a smell of ozone. Sets the drinker's STR score to 29 for 1 hour. The storm giant is the strongest giant type — the strength this potion grants is near the ceiling of mortal physical capacity.
