# Starthread Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A short double-edged blade for close work. The Starthread edge weighs almost nothing and phases through cover before striking. Easily concealed.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Starthread): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
