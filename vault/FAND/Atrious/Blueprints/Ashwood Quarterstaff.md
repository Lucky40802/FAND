# Ashwood Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A 6-foot staff of Ashwood. The material burns without being consumed and deals fire damage.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Ashwood Spear|Ashwood Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Ashwood): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
