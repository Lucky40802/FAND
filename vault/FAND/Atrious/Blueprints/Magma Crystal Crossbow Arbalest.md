# Magma Crystal Crossbow Arbalest

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Crossbow Arbalest reforged with Magma Crystal (Elemental) worked into its construction. Crystal formed at the boundary of elemental fire and earth. Both fire and earth elemental energy is locked within. Weapons deal +1d6 fire. On a hit, the magma crystal pulses with force — dealing 1d4 additional bludgeoning from the shockwave. Bonus: +1d6 Fire and Bludgeoning.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Magma Crystal): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
