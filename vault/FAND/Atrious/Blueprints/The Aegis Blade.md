# The Aegis Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Celestial Metallurgy) — Artifact*

A crystalline sword of pure will, said to have been forged specifically to oppose a corrupting cursed blade somewhere out in the world. Whenever that curse stirs and grows strong enough to threaten the world again, this blade is said to awaken on its own, seeking a wielder.
