# Coagulant Patch

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

A patch soaked in blood alchemy coagulant. Applied to a wound it seals it and prevents bleeding for 24 hours.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tracking Scent|Tracking Scent]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
