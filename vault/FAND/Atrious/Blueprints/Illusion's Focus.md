# Illusion's Focus

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Legendary*

This focus only functions at its full power because it channels a fragment of [[Illusion]] directly, bound into a core of Garnet (+2 Force). Reserved for those who hold a genuine Primary connection to that Law, per the [[Class]] acquisition system. No substitute relic or lesser link will do.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Garnet): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
