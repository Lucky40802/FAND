# Pry Bar

*Tool — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] — Common*

Opens crates, coffins, and stuck doors. Advantage on Strength checks to force things open.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bone Saw|Bone Saw]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gutting Hook|Gutting Hook]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Harvester's Knife|Harvester's Knife]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
