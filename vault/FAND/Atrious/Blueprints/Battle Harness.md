# Battle Harness

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Uncommon*

A combat harness for a Large or Huge beast. The beast can take one additional action in combat that must be an Attack action.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Burrowing Beast Harness|Burrowing Beast Harness]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
