# Precision Scale

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A mechanical balance scale with precision counterweights accurate to 0.1 grams. Used by jewellers, alchemists, and apothecaries. The counterweights are calibrated against a standard weight included with the scale.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gear Tooth Counter|Gear Tooth Counter]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Spring Tension Gauge|Spring Tension Gauge]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
