# Portable Alchemy Kit

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Uncommon*

A compact kit for basic field alchemy — a small spirit lamp, three retorts, a mortar and pestle, measuring spoons, and a dozen empty vials. Not as capable as a full lab but usable in the field.
