# Weak Healing Draught

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A small vial of pale pink liquid — the weakest healing preparation available. Restores 1d4 HP. Tastes faintly sweet. Brewed from common yarrow and calendula in a water base. The shelf life is two weeks.
