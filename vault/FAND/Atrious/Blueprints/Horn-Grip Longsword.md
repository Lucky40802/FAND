# Horn-Grip Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Uncommon*

A standard longsword fitted with a dragon horn grip and a bone crossguard. The dragon components improve the weapon's affinity for enchantment.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonbone): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
