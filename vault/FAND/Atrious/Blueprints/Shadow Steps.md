# Shadow Steps

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Uncommon*

Boots with shadowsteel soles and veilcloth upper. The wearer leaves no footprints in natural terrain. Their footsteps make no sound on stone, wood, or earth. Tracking them requires magical means.
