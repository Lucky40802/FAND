# Veilcloth Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Veilcloth blade is invisible in darkness and grants advantage on opening strikes from concealment.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Greatsword|Veilcloth Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Shortsword|Veilcloth Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
