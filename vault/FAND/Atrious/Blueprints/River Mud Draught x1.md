# River Mud Draught x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Rare*

A draught incorporating River Mud (Common Material). Ordinary river mud. Applied to skin it provides camouflage (+2 Stealth in natural terrain) and masks scent (disadvantage on scent-based tracking for 4 hours until it dries). Essential wilderness survival material. Bonus: +0. Every crafter who makes one adds a small personal touch, though the function never changes.
