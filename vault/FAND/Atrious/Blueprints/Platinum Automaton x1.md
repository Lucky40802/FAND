# Platinum Automaton x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Rare*

A automaton incorporating Platinum (Metal). Rarer than gold, harder, and a better magical conductor. Conducts celestial energy without loss. Used for the highest-tier ritual weapons. Bonus: +2.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Platinum Frame x1|Platinum Frame x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
