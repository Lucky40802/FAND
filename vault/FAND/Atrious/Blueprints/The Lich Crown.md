# The Lich Crown

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A crown of soulsteel and onyx. The wearer is treated as undead for purposes of all spells and effects. They cannot be affected by Banishment, Turn Undead, or Divine Smite.
