# The Umbral Bow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A precisely built bow, woven from shadow-touched thread using Shadow-Touched Thread and Binding Dye. Made to a standard rather than to impress.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadow-Touched Thread): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
