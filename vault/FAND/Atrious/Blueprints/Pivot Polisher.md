# Pivot Polisher

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A specialized artifact, assembled from precision clockwork parts using Copper Wire. A working example of the craft rather than a showpiece. The finish differs from smith to smith, though the core work is identical.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Simple Copper Wire Automaton|Simple Copper Wire Automaton]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
