# Thornmetal Carving x1

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Very Rare*

A carving incorporating Thornmetal (Wood). Metal processed through an alchemical technique using thorn tree sap. The final product has microscopic thorn-like protrusions across its surface. Any creature that grapples or wrestles someone in thornmetal armour takes 1d6 piercing damage per round of the grapple. Weapons cause bleeding: 1d4 per r Bonus: Thorns on strike.
