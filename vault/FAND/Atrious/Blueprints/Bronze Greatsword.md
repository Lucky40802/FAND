# Bronze Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Uncommon*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Bronze construction means holds a reasonable edge and rarely shatters.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bronze Longsword|Bronze Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bronze Shortsword|Bronze Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Bronze): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
