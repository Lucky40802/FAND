# Necrotic Dagger

*Dagger — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Uncommon*

A dagger of soulsteel that deals additional necrotic damage. Wounds from this blade reduce the target's maximum HP by the necrotic damage dealt until a long rest.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
