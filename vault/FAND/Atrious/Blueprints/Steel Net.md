# Steel Net

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A weighted steel-mesh throwing net. On a ranged attack hit, the target is Restrained. Can be escaped with a STR check DC 12 or by dealing 10 slashing damage to the net. Used in gladiatorial combat and urban guard work.
