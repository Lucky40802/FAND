# Golem Stone Cloak x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A cloak incorporating Golem Stone (Arcane Material). The core stone from a destroyed golem. Still carries the animating energy. Items made from golem stone grant the bearer +2 to saves against effects that would Paralyse or Stun them. Armour from golem stone has a 20% chance of auto-repairing 1d6 damage it takes per round. Bonus: +2 to Construct saves.

## Material Property
**Earthbound** (Potency 1, from Golem Stone): Tremorsense 5 ft while touching the ground. Weapon: +1d6 damage against objects and structures.
