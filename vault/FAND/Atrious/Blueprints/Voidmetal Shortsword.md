# Voidmetal Shortsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A single-handed straight blade 18-22 inches long. Reliable in close quarters and easy to carry. The Voidmetal construction cuts through magical barriers as easily as physical matter.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Voidmetal Greatsword|Voidmetal Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Voidmetal Longsword|Voidmetal Longsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Nullifying** (Potency 3, from Voidmetal): Weapon: once per turn, a hit ends one spell of level 3 or lower on the target. Armor: advantage on saves against spells.
