# Magical Beast Collar

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] (Beast Taming) — Uncommon*

A collar of runesteel and moonstone for magical beasts of CR 5 or lower. The runes mark the beast as tamed and allow the tamer to communicate simple concepts telepathically with the beast. Range 300ft.
