# The Hemomancer's Throne

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Legendary*

A throne carved from hardened blood and bloodstone. Any creature sitting on the throne can control the blood of any living creature within 300ft. Targets must STR save DC 20 or be Restrained as their own blood hardens.
