# Chaos Matter Veil x1

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Rare*

A veil incorporating Chaos Matter (Planar). Matter from Limbo, stabilised through tremendous force of will. Weapons roll on a d6 wild effect table on each hit — effects range from extra damage of random type to temporary buffs for the wielder to the target being teleported 5ft. Bonus: Random effect on each hit. The finish differs from smith to smith, though the core work is identical.

## Material Property
**Chaotic** (Potency 3, from Chaos Matter): Weapon: +3d6 damage of a random type each hit. Once per long rest, trigger a wild magic surge on purpose.
