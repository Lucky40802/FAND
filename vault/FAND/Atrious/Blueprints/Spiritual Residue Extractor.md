# Spiritual Residue Extractor

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Relic Restoration) — Uncommon*

A purpose-built artifact, restored from a recovered relic fragment using Binding Resin. A working example of the craft rather than a showpiece. A regional variant exists in most workshops, though the differences are cosmetic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Apprentice Binding Resin Relic|Apprentice Binding Resin Relic]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
