# Mechanical Crossbow — Repeating

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A crossbow with a 10-bolt magazine and a lever-action reload mechanism. One pull of the lever ejects the empty case, advances the magazine, and spans the bow. Rate of fire: one bolt per round without the normal reload…
