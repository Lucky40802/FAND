# Wooden Round Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Common*

A round shield of laminated hardwood, iron-bossed in the centre. Lighter than iron shields, easier to carry on long marches. The wood grain is horizontal for maximum resistance to splitting.
