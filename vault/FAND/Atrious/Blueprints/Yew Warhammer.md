# Yew Warhammer

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Woodworking) — Uncommon*

A two-handed hammer with a square striking face. The Yew head stores tension energy and releases it explosively.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Yew Mace|Yew Mace]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Living** (Potency 1, from Yew): Druid and ranger focus: +1 to spell DCs. The item slowly regrows if damaged.
