# Veilcloth Greatsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A massive two-handed blade requiring full body mechanics. The Veilcloth construction means is invisible in darkness and grants advantage on opening strikes from concealment.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Longsword|Veilcloth Longsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Veilcloth Shortsword|Veilcloth Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
