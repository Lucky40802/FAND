# Soulsteel Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Rare*

A slab incorporating Soulsteel (Metal). A metal created by compressing soul energy into a physical medium. Grey and slightly translucent. Weapons made from soulsteel deal necrotic damage and can directly damage the soul rather than the body — effective against creatures that are incorporeal or immune to physical damage. Bonus: +2 Necrotic.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
