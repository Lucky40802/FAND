# Soulsteel Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Artifact*

A mechanical bow with a Soulsteel prod and stock. The construction bypasses incorporeal defences by striking the soul directly.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Longbow|Soulsteel Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Soulsteel Shortbow|Soulsteel Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Soulsteel): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
