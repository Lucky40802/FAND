# Traveller's Ration Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

Distilled nutrition in a small flask — a full day's calories in liquid form. Tastes faintly of oats and copper. Keeps for six months sealed. Used by soldiers on forced march who cannot stop to eat.
