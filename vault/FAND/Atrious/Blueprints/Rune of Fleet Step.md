# Rune of Fleet Step

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Uncommon*

A rune inscribed on a pair of boots or greaves. The wearer's speed increases by 10ft. Does not stack with other movement speed enchantments. The rune must be applied by a runesmith — it cannot be self-inscribed.
