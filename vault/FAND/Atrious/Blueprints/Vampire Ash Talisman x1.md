# Vampire Ash Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Very Rare*

A talisman incorporating Vampire Ash (Creature Harvest). The ash from a destroyed vampire. Items incorporating vampire ash deal 1 point of life drain on every hit — transferring 1 HP from the target to the bearer. This stacks with all other healing and cannot be blocked. Bonus: Life drain on hit.
