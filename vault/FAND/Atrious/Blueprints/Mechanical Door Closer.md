# Mechanical Door Closer

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A spring-mechanism that closes a door automatically after it is opened, at a controlled speed. The closing speed is adjustable via a hydraulic damper. Used in buildings where doors must remain closed but people move…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Clockwork Door Lock|Clockwork Door Lock]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mechanical Counter|Mechanical Counter]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
