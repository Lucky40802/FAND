# Shadowsteel Armour

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Legendary*

A suit of fitted armour. The Shadowsteel plates is silent and deals shadow damage, partially incorporealising wounds.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Shadowsteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
