# Clarity Soup

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A thin, clear soup that clears magical confusion and mental impairment. Consuming it ends the Charmed and Confused conditions immediately. Also provides advantage on INT saving throws for 1 hour.
