# Iron Stomach Tonic

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A brown liquid that makes the stomach resistant to spoiled food and minor toxins for 8 hours. Allows the drinker to consume questionable food and water without illness. Tastes worse than most of what it protects against.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Antidote to Common Poisons|Antidote to Common Poisons]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Burn Salve|Burn Salve]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cough Suppressant|Cough Suppressant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Emetic|Emetic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Fever Tea|Fever Tea]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Night Vision Drops|Night Vision Drops]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Sleep Draught|Sleep Draught]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Smelling Salts|Smelling Salts]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
