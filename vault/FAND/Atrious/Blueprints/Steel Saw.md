# Steel Saw

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A hand saw with a 20-inch steel blade and a wooden handle. The teeth are crosscut pattern for wood. The blade is tensioned by a wooden frame. Used by carpenters and coffin-makers alike.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Draw Knife|Draw Knife]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Farrier's Rasp|Farrier's Rasp]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/File|File]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Steel Scissors|Steel Scissors]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
