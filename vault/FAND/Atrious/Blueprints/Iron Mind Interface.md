# Iron Mind Interface

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Rare*

A headpiece that connects the wearer's mind directly to any clockwork device they have built. The devices respond to thought commands within 300ft. Setup requires 10 minutes per device.
