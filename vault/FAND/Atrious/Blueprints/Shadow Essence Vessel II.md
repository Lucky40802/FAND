# Shadow Essence Vessel II

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Rare*

A vessel incorporating Shadow Essence (Planar). The concentrated essence of shadow, crystallised from the Shadowfell. Items made from shadow essence cast no shadow themselves and make no sound. Weapons deal +1d6 shadow damage. Armour from it makes the bearer invisible in magical darkness. Bonus: +1d6 Shadow.
