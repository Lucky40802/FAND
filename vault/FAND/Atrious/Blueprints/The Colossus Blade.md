# The Colossus Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

An oversized single-edged blade as wide as a man's torso and as tall as most people, worn across the back on a strap thick enough to hold a horse. Passed down through a line of legendary mercenaries who believed a sword should weigh as much as the burden its wielder carries.
