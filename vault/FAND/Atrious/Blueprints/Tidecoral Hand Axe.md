# Tidecoral Hand Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A one-handed single-bit axe. The Tidecoral head is naturally damp and functional even at extreme ocean depths. Can be thrown.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tidecoral Greataxe|Tidecoral Greataxe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 1, from Tidecoral): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +1d6 cold to creatures in water.
