# Starthread Longbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Arcane Tailoring) — Very Rare*

A 6-foot bow. The Starthread stave weighs almost nothing and phases through cover before striking.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Starthread Crossbow|Starthread Crossbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Starthread Shortbow|Starthread Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Starthread): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
