# Mechanical Lock — Pin Tumbler

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Clockwork Engineering) — Common*

A four-pin tumbler lock with a steel barrel and iron case. Pick-resistant to DC 14. The key is unique — copies cannot be made without the original. Used for chests, doors, and any container worth protecting.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Gear Reduction Box|Gear Reduction Box]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
