# Lightning Resistance Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A crackling yellow liquid that occasionally discharges tiny sparks inside the vial. Grants resistance to lightning damage for 1 hour. Useful during storms and against creatures that channel electrical energy.
