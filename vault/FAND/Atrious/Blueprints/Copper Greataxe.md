# Copper Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Copper construction is soft and dulls quickly but is cheap to replace.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Hand Axe|Copper Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Copper): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
