# Mimic Adhesive Vial x1

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A vial incorporating Mimic Adhesive (Creature Harvest). The adhesive secreted by a slain mimic, preserved and crystallised. Weapons incorporating mimic adhesive cause targets to become stuck to the weapon — STR DC 14 to pull free. This does not prevent the target from acting but they cannot move away from the attacker until they succeed. Bonus: Grapple on contact.
