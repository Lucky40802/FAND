# Adamantine Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Rare*

A frame incorporating Adamantine (Metal). The hardest substance available without divine intervention. Adamantine weapons ignore object hardness entirely — they cut through iron doors, stone walls, and heavy armour as if through wood. Armour made from it is critical-hit proof. Bonus: +3.
