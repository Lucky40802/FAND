# Crystal Prism — Light Split

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Common*

A triangular crystal prism that splits white light into its component spectrum. Used for optical experiments, light art, and detecting certain magical auras that appear as specific colours in prismed light.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Ball — Decoration|Crystal Ball — Decoration]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Lens — Magnifying|Crystal Lens — Magnifying]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Crystal Lens|Crystal Lens]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Glass Bead|Glass Bead]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
