# Darksteel Dussack

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Rare*

A Dussack reforged with Darksteel (Metal) worked into its construction. Steel worked in the Shadow Plane under conditions of absolute darkness. Darksteel blades are black and slightly warm. They deal necrotic damage and leave wounds that bleed shadow instead of blood. Bonus: +1.

## Material Property
**Shadowed** (Potency 1, from Darksteel): +1 to Stealth. Once per rest, turn invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack. Weapon: +1d6 necrotic damage.
