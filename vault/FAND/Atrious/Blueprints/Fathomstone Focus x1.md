# Fathomstone Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Very Rare*

A focus incorporating Fathomstone (Gemstone & Crystal). Black crystal from the deepest ocean trenches. Items made from fathomstone allow the bearer to breathe water indefinitely. Weapons deal +1d4 cold damage and slow targets (CON save DC 12 or -10ft speed for 1 round). Bonus: Waterbreathing.

## Material Property
**Tidal** (Potency 1, from Fathomstone): Swim speed and water breathing. Weapons work normally underwater and deal +1d6 cold to creatures in water.
