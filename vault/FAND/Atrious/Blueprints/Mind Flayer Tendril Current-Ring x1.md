# Mind Flayer Tendril Current-Ring x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Tide Craft) — Very Rare*

A current-ring incorporating Mind Flayer Tendril (Creature Harvest). The facial tentacle of a slain mind flayer. Items incorporating mind flayer tendrils deal +1d4 psychic damage. On a critical hit the target must save against a mind blast (INT save DC 15) or be Stunned for 1 round. Bonus: +1d4 Psychic.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mind Flayer Tendril Fetish x1|Mind Flayer Tendril Fetish x1]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mind Flayer Tendril Talisman x1|Mind Flayer Tendril Talisman x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
