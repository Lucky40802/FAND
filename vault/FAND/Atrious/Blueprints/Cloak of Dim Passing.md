# Cloak of Dim Passing

*Armor — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring|Tailoring]] (Shadow Weaving) — Common*

A full cloak of veilcloth. In dim light or darkness, the wearer is heavily obscured — they appear as a shape of darkness rather than a person. Perception checks to see the wearer in dim light are at disadvantage.

## Material Property
**Supple** (Potency 1, from Veilcloth): Light or medium armor: +1 AC and no disadvantage on Stealth. Cloth: +1 to one Charisma skill.
