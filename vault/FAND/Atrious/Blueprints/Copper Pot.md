# Copper Pot

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Common*

A round-bottomed copper pot with a riveted handle. Holds two quarts. The copper conducts heat evenly. Tin-lined to prevent the metal from reacting with acidic foods. A kitchen essential.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Copper Candlestick|Copper Candlestick]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
