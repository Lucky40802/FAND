# Obsidian Gem Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Soul Binding) — Very Rare*

A 6-foot staff of Obsidian Gem. The material channels shadow and partially dematerialises wounds.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Obsidian Gem Spear|Obsidian Gem Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 1, from Obsidian Gem): Stores one spell of level 1 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +1 to spell attacks.
