# Magmaite Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Elemental Binding) — Legendary*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Magmaite construction is always hot enough to brand and amplifies fire enchantments.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Magmaite Hand Axe|Magmaite Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Magmaite): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
