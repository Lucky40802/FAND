# Journeyman's Rune Chalk Tablet

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Uncommon*

A journeyman's tablet built from rune chalk, taught early in Glyph Inscription training. Reliable, unremarkable, and easy to reproduce.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Compass Glyph|Compass Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Echo Glyph|Echo Glyph]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Repair Glyph|Repair Glyph]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
