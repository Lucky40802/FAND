# Dragonscale Longsword

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Artifact*

A hand-and-a-half blade of 35-40 inches. Versatile in one or two hands. The Dragonscale blade provides elemental resistance matching the dragon it came from.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonscale Greatsword|Dragonscale Greatsword]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Dragonscale Shortsword|Dragonscale Shortsword]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

## Material Property
**Piercing Edge** (Potency 1, from Dragonscale): Weapon: ignores 2 AC (see Armor Class) and crits on 19–20 (18–20 at Potency 6+).
