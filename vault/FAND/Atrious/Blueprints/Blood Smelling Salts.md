# Blood Smelling Salts

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Common*

Salts that, when crushed near a bleeding creature, immediately stabilise them without HP restoration.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Anti-Coagulant|Anti-Coagulant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Lamp|Blood Lamp]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blood Oath Bond|Blood Oath Bond]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
