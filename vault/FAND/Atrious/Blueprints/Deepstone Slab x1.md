# Deepstone Slab x1

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Stonecutting) — Rare*

A slab incorporating Deepstone (Gemstone & Crystal). A stone formed under immense pressure deep in the Plane of Earth. Deepstone is twice as dense as granite and effectively unbreakable. Bludgeoning weapons made from it deliver crushing force that bypasses hardness. Bonus: +2 Bludgeoning.

## Material Property
**Enduring** (Potency 1, from Deepstone): Can't be broken by nonmagical means. Shield: +1 AC. Weapon: +1 damage.
