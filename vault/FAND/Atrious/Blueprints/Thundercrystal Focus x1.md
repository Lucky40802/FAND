# Thundercrystal Focus x1

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Crystal Shaping) — Rare*

A focus incorporating Thundercrystal (Gemstone & Crystal). Crystal formed by repeated lightning strikes at altitude. It hums audibly and crackles with stored energy. Weapons deal +1d4 thunder damage. On a critical hit, the thunder is so loud all creatures within 10ft must CON save DC 13 or be deafened for 1 round. Bonus: +1d4 Thunder.

## Material Property
**Stormcharged** (Potency 1, from Thundercrystal): Weapon: +1d6 lightning or thunder damage. Armor: lightning resistance.
