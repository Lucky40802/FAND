# Runed Void Shield

*Shield — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Sigilcraft) — Legendary*

A shield inscribed with void runes on both faces. The front runes deflect. The back runes absorb. The combination means strikes against it lose force twice — once on contact, once on penetration attempt.
