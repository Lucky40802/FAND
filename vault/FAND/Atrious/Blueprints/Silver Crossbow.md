# Silver Crossbow

*Bow — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A mechanical bow with a Silver prod and stock. The construction burns supernatural creatures that rely on regeneration.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Longbow|Silver Longbow]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Silver Shortbow|Silver Shortbow]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
