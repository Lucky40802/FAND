# The Crimson Sage Staff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Rare*

A staff of oak set with bloodstones along its length. On a hit, heals wielder for full damage dealt. Once per day, cast Blood Drain — all creatures in 30ft take 3d8 necrotic and the wielder heals for total damage.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
