# Star Beacon - Permanent

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation|Navigation]] (Astral Navigation) — Rare*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Permanent Beacon Network|Permanent Beacon Network]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
