# Simple Etching Needle Tablet

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting|Enchanting]] (Glyph Inscription) — Uncommon*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Alert Glyph - Invisible|Alert Glyph - Invisible]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Novice's Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm|Novice's Etching Needle Rune-Bound Charm]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
