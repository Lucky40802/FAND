# Reality Shard Brand x1

*Hammer — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] (Infernal Forging) — Very Rare*

A brand incorporating Reality Shard (Planar). A shard of crystallised reality from locations where the fabric of existence cracked. Items made from reality shards are partially unreal — they exist slightly outside of normal space and cannot be found by Detect Magic or similarly targeted by most spells. Bonus: Reality alteration.

## Material Property
**Spell Vessel** (Potency 3, from Reality Shard): Stores one spell of level 2 or lower, recast once per long rest. Focus: +2 to spell attacks.
