# Truth Soup

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Cooking|Cooking]] (Spirit Cooking) — Common*

A soup that, when consumed, removes the ability to speak deliberate falsehoods for 1 hour. The consumer can be silent, can be evasive, and can refuse to answer. They simply cannot state things they know to be untrue.…

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Empathy Broth|Empathy Broth]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
