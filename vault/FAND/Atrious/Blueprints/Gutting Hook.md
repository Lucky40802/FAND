# Gutting Hook

*Tool — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] — Common*

Opens a carcass cleanly. Organs harvested with it stay usable twice as long.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Bone Saw|Bone Saw]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Harvester's Knife|Harvester's Knife]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Pry Bar|Pry Bar]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
