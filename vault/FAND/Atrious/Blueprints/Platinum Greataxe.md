# Platinum Greataxe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Legendary*

A massive two-handed axe with a broad bit. The Platinum construction conducts magical energy without loss.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Platinum Hand Axe|Platinum Hand Axe]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.

**Tempered** (Potency 1, from Platinum): Weapon: +1 damage. Armor: +1 Gear AC.
