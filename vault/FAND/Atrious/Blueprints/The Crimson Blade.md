# The Crimson Blade

*Sword — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Blood Alchemy) — Very Rare*

A longsword that draws blood from the air itself. Deals necrotic damage that is then healed by the wielder. Against targets that have taken 50+ HP of damage, it deals maximum damage automatically.

## Material Property
**Lifedrinking** (Potency 2, from Blood Magic Carnelian): Weapon: once per turn, regain 4 hit points when you hit.
