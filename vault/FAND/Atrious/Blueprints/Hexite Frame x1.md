# Hexite Frame x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering|Engineering]] (Golemancy) — Very Rare*

A frame incorporating Hexite (Arcane Material). A dull grey metal worked by hags using curses as part of the smelting process. Weapons of hexite apply a random minor curse on a critical hit — the target makes a CHA save DC 14 or suffers a minor curse for 1 week (hair falls out, voice changes pitch, objects fall from their hands, etc.). Bonus: Curse on critical hit.
