# Mithral Quarterstaff

*Staff — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Artifact*

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mithral Spear|Mithral Spear]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
