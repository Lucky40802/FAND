# Lich Dust Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A talisman incorporating Lich Dust (Creature Harvest). The dust remaining from a destroyed lich's phylactery. Extremely rare. Items incorporating lich dust add +1 to all Necromancy spell save DCs and spell attack rolls. Undead created using spells cast through lich dust items have +1 to all their ability scores. Bonus: +1 to Necromancy spells.
