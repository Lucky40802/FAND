# Warmth Potion

*Potion — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] (Potionmaking) — Common*

A red liquid that warms the body from inside. Grants resistance to cold environmental effects for 4 hours. Not magical — it accelerates the body's natural heat generation. Slight nausea if taken in warm conditions.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Antiseptic Wash|Antiseptic Wash]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Cold Compress Potion|Cold Compress Potion]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Endurance Tea|Endurance Tea]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Analgesic|Mild Analgesic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Mild Stimulant|Mild Stimulant]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Scent Mask|Scent Mask]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Weak Paralytic|Weak Paralytic]], [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Wound Sealant|Wound Sealant]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
