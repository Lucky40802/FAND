# Giant Spider Silk Talisman x1

*Artifact — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]] (Dragon Carving) — Rare*

A talisman incorporating Giant Spider Silk (Creature Harvest). Silk from giant spiders, carefully harvested and treated. Stronger than steel at the same weight. Weapons incorporating giant spider silk entangle on a hit — the target must succeed a STR save DC 12 or be Grappled by trailing silk. Bonus: Binding on hit.

**Stat variants:** shares exactly the same stats (level, damage, materials) with [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Giant Spider Silk Fetish x1|Giant Spider Silk Fetish x1]]. The difference is form and handling: pick whichever shape suits the wielder.
