# Salamander Scale Boarding Axe

*Axe — [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]] — Very Rare*

A Boarding Axe reforged with Salamander Scale (Elemental) worked into its construction. Scales from a fire salamander. Weapons deal +1d6 fire damage. Armour from salamander scale provides immunity to fire damage from fire elemental creatures specifically. The scales are warm to touch and maintain warmth in cold environments. Bonus: +1d6 Fire, immunity to own fire.

## Material Property
**Burning** (Potency 1, from Salamander Scale): Weapon: +1d6 fire damage. Armor: fire resistance (immunity at Potency 8+).
