# Alchemy

Potions, poisons, oils, elixirs, and the blood arts: anything brewed, distilled, or drawn from a living body.

**Works with:** Blood / Organ, plants, and supplies. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Alchemy.base|Alchemy Blueprints]] (563). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Potionmaking (283 Blueprints)
Create potions, toxins, alchemical oils, and mutagens.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv17 draught that lets the drinker breathe fire once before it wears off (Fire).
- A Lv12 tonic that sharpens the mind enough to resist one mental intrusion (Psychic).
- A Lv7 shimmering vial that stores a single low-level spell for later use (Arcane).

### Blood Alchemy (280 Blueprints)
Use blood rituals to empower forbidden creations.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv16 elixir that lets the drinker read a struck target's next move (Psychic).
- A Lv11 tonic that charges the blood with a single stored jolt of lightning (Lightning).
- A Lv6 draught that thickens the blood enough to slow bleeding to a crawl (Ice).

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Potionmaking | An alchemist's kit and clean water. |
| Blood Alchemy | Fresh blood from a willing donor or a kill no more than an hour old. Most temples frown on it. |
