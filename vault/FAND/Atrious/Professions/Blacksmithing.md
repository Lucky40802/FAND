# Blacksmithing

Working metal into weapons, armor, tools, and fittings, from village iron to the metals of Hell, Heaven, and the Void.

**Works with:** Ore / Metal. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Blacksmithing.base|Blacksmithing Blueprints]] (1215). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

Hell, Heaven, and Void metals need a **realm specialty**: a Blacksmith works them at disadvantage until they have finished one item of that metal under a teacher who has the specialty. The specialties are Infernal Forging (Hell), Celestial Metallurgy (Heaven), and Void Smithing (Void and Inner Void).

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Blacksmithing (860 Blueprints)
Forge swords, heavy armor, shields, and warsteel relics.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv16 mind-forged blade that unsettles whoever it cuts (Psychic).
- A Lv11 arcane-tempered breastplate that shrugs off a single spell each day (Arcane).
- A Lv6 venom-lined gauntlet that turns a bare-handed grapple toxic (Poison).

### Infernal Forging (111 Blueprints)
Forge with metals and materials pulled from the lower planes, at the cost of gear that always carries something of Hell in it.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv17 Hellforged Iron Armour (Armor, Fire).
- A Lv17 Abyssal Bronze Armour (Armor, Acid).
- A Lv17 Abyssal Bronze Crossbow (Bow, Piercing).

### Celestial Metallurgy (167 Blueprints)
Work metals blessed or born beyond the Mortal Realm — starmetal, celestial gold — into gear too rare for ordinary forges.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv20 The Unbreaking (Sword, Slashing).
- A Lv20 Celestial Gold Shortbow (Bow, Piercing).
- A Lv20 The Dawnbane Sword - Blade of Evil's Bane (Sword, Slashing).

### Void Smithing (77 Blueprints)
Forge with fragments and concepts pulled from the Void itself, producing weapons that don't behave like ordinary matter.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv20 Ea - Sword of Rupture (Sword, Slashing).
- A Lv18 Monado - Shulk's Blade (Sword, Slashing).

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | A forge and anvil. The default specialty every Blacksmith starts with. |
| Infernal Forging | Hell metal (Brimstone Iron, Infernium, and so on) and a forge hot enough for it: a Hell vent, a Realm Forge, or a fiend-fire forge. Items carry a trace of Hell; Heaven contractors distrust them. |
| Celestial Metallurgy | Heaven metal and consecrated tools. Items can't be used to harm a celestial without the wielder taking the same damage. |
| Void Smithing | Void or Inner Void metal and a forge that burns nothing: usually a Realm Forge connected to the Void. |
