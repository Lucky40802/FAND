# Enchanting

Putting power into things: runes, sigils, and glyphs; binding souls and elemental cores into items; and restoring broken relics.

**Works with:** Essence / Soul / Core, Crystal / Gem. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Enchanting.base|Enchanting Blueprints]] (1472). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Sigilcraft (292 Blueprints)
Bind runes into weapons, armor, and magical devices.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv18 rune-bound shield that can freeze an incoming weapon in place for a moment (Ice).
- A Lv13 rune-etched torch that never quite goes out (Fire).
- A Lv8 rune-carved circlet that quiets one intrusive thought per day (Psychic).

### Glyph Inscription (258 Blueprints)
Create scrolls, seals, wards, and glyph traps.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv20 grave-glyph that marks a target for a slow, creeping death (Necrotic).
- A Lv15 arcane sigil that stores a single spell for someone else to release later (Arcane).
- A Lv10 warning-glyph that releases a cloud of poison if disturbed (Poison).

### Soul Binding (322 Blueprints)
Seal spirits into relics and cursed equipment.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv20 bound vessel holding a captured spirit angry enough to blight whatever it touches (Necrotic).
- A Lv15 bound relic that releases its trapped spirit as a single bolt of lightning (Lightning).
- A Lv10 bound charm that keeps a captured spirit dormant and cold until called on (Ice).

### Elemental Binding (183 Blueprints)
Bind a raw element directly into a material rather than merely enchanting the finished item.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv17 Magmaite Armour (Armor, Fire).
- A Lv17 Ashwood Armour (Armor, Fire).
- A Lv17 Ashwood Crossbow (Bow, Piercing).

### Relic Restoration (417 Blueprints)
Repair divine relics and shattered artifacts.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv18 restored relic, once frozen mid-shatter, now able to freeze a strike solid (Ice).
- A Lv13 restored icon that sheds a faint, protective radiance (Radiant).
- A Lv8 restored urn that still carries a trace of whatever grave it was pulled from (Necrotic).

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Sigilcraft | Inks or chisels to carve sigils; works on gear that will be worn or wielded. |
| Glyph Inscription | Inks and a surface that won't be moved: walls, floors, doors, scrolls. |
| Soul Binding | A soul vessel (such as an Empty Vessel) and a soul that is willing or recently departed. Heaven contractors treat it as a crime. |
| Elemental Binding | An elemental core or essence to bind. |
| Relic Restoration | The broken relic itself, and a week of study before the first attempt. |
