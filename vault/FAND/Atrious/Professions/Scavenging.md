# Scavenging

Stripping corpses for crafting materials: skinning hides, pulling fangs and horns, draining blood and venom, cutting out organs, and bottling the essences of spirits and elementals. Every monster drop in the Bestiaries is taken with Scavenging. Scavengers can also refine organic materials (hide, bone, blood, essence) at disadvantage.

**Works with:** Organic materials from corpses: Hide / Cloth / Fiber, Bone / Fang / Horn / Scale, Blood / Organ, and Essence / Soul / Core. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Scavenging.base|Scavenging Blueprints]] (56). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Scavenger (19 Blueprints)
Strip usable materials from slain creatures and organic remains — the profession that feeds every other profession's material pool rather than finishing items itself.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv1 cured hide — common material, usable in most Armor and Artifact Blueprints calling for leather.
- A Lv5 refined sinew cord — a stronger substitute anywhere a Blueprint calls for rope or binding.
- A Lv9 drained venom sac — feeds [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] and [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)|Alchemy]] recipes calling for poison.
- A Lv14 preserved organ (heart, eye, or similar) from a genuinely dangerous creature — feeds high-tier Blueprints in [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]], [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving|Carving]], and similar professions that call for a named creature-part material.

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Scavenging | A corpse no more than a day old (a week if it was preserved). Parts from creatures of a higher realm than you've harvested before are taken at disadvantage the first time. |
