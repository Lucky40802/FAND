# Harvesting

Gathering what the land gives: farming, mining, foraging, and fishing, plus raising and taming living animals. Every ore, stone, crystal, and plant on the Gathering pages is gathered with Harvesting. (Parts taken from corpses are Scavenging.)

**Works with:** Any raw material; crafts crops, seed stock, and livestock. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Harvesting.base|Harvesting Blueprints]] (299). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Farming (31 Blueprints)
Grow the crops and raise the livestock that feed everyone else — the quiet profession most other trades depend on without noticing.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv1 hardy seed stock, resistant to poor soil and bad weather — a season's crop from it feeds a small settlement.
- A Lv6 irrigation channel design that measurably improves a farm's yield and reliability.
- A Lv11 preserved grain store, sealed against rot and pests for years rather than months.
- A Lv17 selectively-bred livestock line, valuable enough that neighboring farms pay well for breeding stock.

### Beast Taming (260 Blueprints)
Train creatures, mounts, and magical companions.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv19 storm-collar that lets a tamed beast call down a single bolt of lightning (Lightning).
- A Lv14 frostbound leash that lets a tamed beast shrug off one killing blow of cold (Ice).
- A Lv9 ember brand that keeps a tamed beast calm around open flame (Fire).

### Mining (6 Blueprints)
Digging ore, stone, and crystal out of the ground, from mortal mines to Law-veins in the Inner Realm.

### Foraging (2 Blueprints)
Gathering wild plants, herbs, fungi, and drifting materials, in forests, fey pockets, the Astral, and beyond.

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Farming | Land and a growing season. Crops from other realms need soil from that realm, or Godfall Soil. |
| Mining | A pick and a seam to work; deeper realms need gear made from their own materials (see Realm Travel). |
| Foraging | Knowing where to look: advantage on finding plants in a realm you've foraged before. |
| Beast Taming | A week of daily time with the animal before any check to tame or breed it. |
