# Carving

Shaping wood, stone, bone, horn, scale, crystal, and coral into bows, staffs, shields, foci, statues, and gem-set gear.

**Works with:** Wood / Plant, Stone, Bone / Fang / Horn / Scale, Crystal / Gem. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Carving.base|Carving Blueprints]] (1187). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Woodworking (183 Blueprints)
Shape wood — ironwood, ashwood, and rarer growths — into gear that trades metal's weight for a different kind of durability.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv13 Ironwood Hand Axe (Axe, Slashing).
- A Lv13 Ironwood Warhammer (Hammer, Bludgeoning).
- A Lv13 Ironwood Shortsword (Sword, Slashing).

### Stonecutting (76 Blueprints)
Shape raw stone into shields, tools, and fittings — the plainest and oldest of the crafting professions.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv2 Tallow Candle Shield (Shield, Bludgeoning).

### Bone Carving (94 Blueprints)
Carve the bones of the dead and the monstrous into weapons and armor that keep a trace of what they came from.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv17 Wraithbone Helm (Armor, Necrotic).
- A Lv17 Wraithbone Warhammer (Hammer, Bludgeoning).
- A Lv17 Wraithbone Buckler (Shield, Necrotic).

### Dragon Carving (275 Blueprints)
Use dragon parts to craft legendary artifacts.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv19 scale-carved talisman that briefly lends the wearer a dragon's arcane resilience (Arcane).
- A Lv14 fang-carved dagger coated in a poison drawn from the scale it was cut from (Poison).
- A Lv9 carved dragon-tooth charm that keeps a small flame lit no matter the wind (Fire).

### Crystal Shaping (481 Blueprints)
Manipulate mana crystals and arcane gems.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv16 black-crystal lens that shows a target's remaining life as a fading light (Necrotic).
- A Lv11 storm-crystal focus that stores one bolt of lightning to release later (Lightning).
- A Lv6 veined shard that weeps a slow poison when shattered (Poison).

### Tide Craft (78 Blueprints)
Work materials recovered from the sea and treated with its pressure and salt, favored for gear meant to survive the depths.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv16 Trident of the Sea King (Staff, Bludgeoning).
- A Lv13 Deepwater Trident (Staff, Bludgeoning).
- A Lv10 Seawater-Treated Leather Armour (Armor, Piercing).

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Woodworking | Carving tools and seasoned wood. |
| Stonecutting | Chisels and a quarry or workshop able to hold the stone. |
| Bone Carving | Fresh or properly cured bone; carving bone of a sentient creature is a crime in most lands. |
| Dragon Carving | Dragon or wyvern material. Dragons can smell their own kind's parts on you. |
| Crystal Shaping | A gem-cutting wheel and a steady hand: crafting checks are at disadvantage while moving or in combat. |
| Tide Craft | A workshop near the sea; coral and sea materials dry out and crack inland. |
