# Navigation

Maps, charts, and instruments for finding the way, in the Mortal Realm and across the Astral to other realms.

**Works with:** Maps and instruments. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Navigation.base|Navigation Blueprints]] (290). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Navigation (32 Blueprints)
Read terrain, chart routes, and make sure a party knows exactly where they are — a grounded, mundane skill, not a magical one.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv2 reliable compass, immune to the mundane sources of magnetic interference that throw off a cheaper one.
- A Lv7 detailed regional map, hand-surveyed rather than copied — grants advantage on checks to avoid getting lost in the mapped region.
- A Lv12 weatherproof map case that keeps its contents legible through rain, submersion, or rough handling.
- A Lv18 master survey chart of a genuinely remote or dangerous region, valuable enough to sell to scholars, guilds, or rival expeditions.

### Astral Navigation (258 Blueprints)
Create dimensional maps and planar devices.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv20 star-chart that lets the bearer navigate other minds as easily as open sky (Psychic).
- A Lv15 astrolabe that opens a short arcane shortcut between two known points (Arcane).
- A Lv10 frost-etched compass that always points toward the nearest safe harbor, even through a blizzard (Ice).

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Navigation | Instruments and a clear view of sky or landmarks. |
| Astral Navigation | Having crossed the Astral at least once, with someone who already has the specialty. |
