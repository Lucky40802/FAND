# Tailoring

Cloth, leather, hide, silk, and shadow-thread made into light armor, cloaks, robes, bags, and enchanted garments.

**Works with:** Hide / Cloth / Fiber. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Tailoring.base|Tailoring Blueprints]] (457). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Arcane Tailoring (197 Blueprints)
Weave arcane thread into cloth, cloaks, and light armor that carries magic without weighing it down.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv13 Chitin Hand Axe (Axe, Slashing).
- A Lv13 Starthread Warhammer (Hammer, Bludgeoning).
- A Lv13 Starthread Shortsword (Sword, Slashing).

### Shadow Weaving (260 Blueprints)
Craft stealth gear, illusion fabrics, and cursed cloaks.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv18 woven cloak that leaves a trail of poisoned shadow behind the wearer (Poison).
- A Lv13 woven veil that can flare with a single pulse of radiant light to blind pursuers (Radiant).
- A Lv8 woven mask that plants one unsettling image in a viewer's mind (Psychic).

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Arcane Tailoring | The ability to cast at least one cantrip, or a partner who can, to weave magic into the cloth. |
| Shadow Weaving | Work only in dim light or darkness; in bright light the thread unravels. |
