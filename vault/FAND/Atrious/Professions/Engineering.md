# Engineering

Mechanisms, constructs, siege weapons, and buildings: clockwork, golems, catapults, bridges, walls, and keeps.

**Works with:** Mechanism, plus Stone and Wood for structures. See Material Types in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**Blueprints:** [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints/Engineering.base|Engineering Blueprints]] (676). Each Blueprint's `specialty` field shows which branch of the craft it comes from.

## Specialties
Branches of the craft. A character with this profession can make items from any specialty; the DM may require a teacher before someone tries an unfamiliar one.

### Clockwork Engineering (277 Blueprints)
Build automatons and mechanical servants.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv17 furnace-heart automaton that vents a burst of flame when struck (Fire).
- A Lv12 grave-gear construct that drains a little vitality from whatever it strikes (Necrotic).
- A Lv7 spark-coil device that delivers a small shock to anyone who tampers with it (Lightning).

### Golemancy (77 Blueprints)
Build and maintain constructs — bodies of stone, metal, or clay animated to move and act on their own.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv10 Golem Repair Kit (Artifact).

### Siege Engineering (259 Blueprints)
Build siege engines and defensive structures.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv17 siege standard that flares with radiant light to rally troops at a glance (Radiant).
- A Lv12 siege ram tipped with something that saps the will of whatever it breaks through (Necrotic).
- A Lv7 arcane-braced catapult arm that resets itself after firing (Arcane).

### Building (37 Blueprints)
Raise structures people actually live and work in — houses, bridges, walls — as opposed to portable items or war machines.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv2 sturdy timber-frame cottage, built to actually survive a hard winter.
- A Lv7 reinforced bridge across a modest river, rated for laden wagon traffic.
- A Lv12 palisade wall around a small settlement, raised fast enough to matter against an actual threat.
- A Lv18 genuine keep or watchtower, the kind of project that takes a season and a crew, not a solo job.

### Masonry (26 Blueprints)
Cut, fit, and mortar stone into walls, foundations, and monuments — structural stonework, not stone items.

*Signature creations:*
- A Lv2 mortared stone wall section, sturdy enough to anchor a larger structure.
- A Lv7 carved foundation stone, cut precisely enough to bear real structural weight.
- A Lv12 load-bearing stone archway, freestanding without wooden supports.
- A Lv18 monument or statue base, the kind of work that outlives whoever commissioned it.

## Specialty rules
- You start a profession with **one specialty** of your choice.
- Crafting a Blueprint from a specialty you don't have is at **disadvantage**, unless its level is no more than half your profession level.
- **Gaining a specialty:** finish one item of it under a teacher who has it, or pick a new one free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Each specialty also has its own requirement:

| Specialty | Needs |
|---|---|
| Clockwork Engineering | Fine tools and springs or gears. |
| Golemancy | An animating core (Spark Core, Keeper Gear, or similar) for every construct. |
| Siege Engineering | A crew of at least four laborers for anything Large or bigger. |
| Building | A site, laborers, and a season for anything larger than a cottage. |
| Masonry | Quarried stone and laborers; structures take weeks, not days. |
