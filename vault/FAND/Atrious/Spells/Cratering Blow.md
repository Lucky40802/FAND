# Cratering Blow

*Original — Transmutation*

The ground within a chosen 20-foot cube erupts violently. Each creature there makes a Dexterity save, taking bludgeoning damage on a failure, and the area becomes difficult terrain.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d12 per slot level above 3rd
