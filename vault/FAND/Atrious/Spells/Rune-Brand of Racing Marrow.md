# Rune-Brand of Racing Marrow

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

A no-frills blood magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
