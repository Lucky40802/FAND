# Vile Blight

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A infernal working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
