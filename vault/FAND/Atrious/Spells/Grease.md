# Grease

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

Slick grease covers the ground in a 10-foot square; creatures there must succeed a Dexterity save or fall prone, and it's difficult terrain for the duration.

**Save:** Dexterity
