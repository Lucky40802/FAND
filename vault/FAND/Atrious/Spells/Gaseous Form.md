# Gaseous Form

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A willing creature you touch transforms, along with everything it's wearing and carrying, into a misty cloud for the duration; it gains resistance to nonmagical damage, can fit through small gaps, and has a limited flying speed.
