# Freedom of Movement

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Chronomancy (School)|Chronomancy]] in this vault*

A creature you touch ignores difficult terrain, and magic can't reduce its speed or cause it to be paralyzed or restrained; it can spend 5 feet of movement to automatically escape a nonmagical grapple.
