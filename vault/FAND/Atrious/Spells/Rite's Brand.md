# Rite's Brand

*Cantrip — Necromancy*

A necromancy staple, taught in every proper school and most improper ones. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 1d4 damage. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
