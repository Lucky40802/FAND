# Skill Empowerment

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You touch a creature and choose one skill it's proficient in. For the duration, that creature adds double its proficiency bonus to ability checks using that skill instead of its normal bonus.
