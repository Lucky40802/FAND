# Curse of Curtain

*Cantrip — Shadow Magic*

The kind of shadow magic working passed between friends rather than taught formally. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. It never scales, but it never needs to.
