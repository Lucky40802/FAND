# Reverse Gravity

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

Gravity reverses in a 50-foot-radius, 100-foot-tall cylinder; unsecured creatures and objects there fall upward and reach the top at the end of the first round unless something stops them, remaining there until the spell ends.

**Save:** Dexterity
