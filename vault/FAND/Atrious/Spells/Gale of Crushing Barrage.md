# Gale of Crushing Barrage

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A step up from the common evocation basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 15ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 6d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
