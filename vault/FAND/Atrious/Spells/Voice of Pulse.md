# Voice of Pulse

*Secondary-tier Chronomancy*

One of the more demanding entries in chronomancy's middle tier. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
