# Hallucinatory Terrain

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You make a natural terrain feature, such as a hill, tree, or stream, in a 150-foot cube look, sound, and smell like a different natural feature; physical interaction can reveal the illusion.
