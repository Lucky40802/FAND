# Chains of Faint Disguise

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A middling illusion technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
