# Curse of Lance

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

An ordinary psionics technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
