# Confusion

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Psionics (School)|Psionics]] in this vault*

Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere must succeed a Wisdom save or have its mind wracked with confusion, acting randomly each turn (rolling for wander, freeze, attack nearest, or act normally) for the duration.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** radius +5 feet per slot level above 4th
