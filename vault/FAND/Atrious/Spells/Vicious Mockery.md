# Vicious Mockery

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You unleash a string of insults laced with subtle magic at a creature you can hear. On a failed Wisdom save it takes psychic damage and has disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** scales to 2d4/3d4/4d4 at 5th/11th/17th level
