# Grave-touched Blight

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A plain necromancy spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
