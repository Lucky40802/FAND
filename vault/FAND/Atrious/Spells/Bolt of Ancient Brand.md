# Bolt of Ancient Brand

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. It bears down on everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Lightning damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
