# Mantle of Tender Care

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A working with real teeth, reserved for healing casters who've put in the hours. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
