# Bolt of Sodden Cinder

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A elemental spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. It bears down on everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Cold damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
