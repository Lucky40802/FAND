# Blur

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

Your body becomes blurred and shifting to onlookers for the duration; any creature attacking you has disadvantage on the attack roll, unless it doesn't rely on sight.
