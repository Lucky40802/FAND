# Clone

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You grow an inert duplicate of a living creature over the course of 120 days from the sealed material component; if the original dies, its soul transfers to the clone, provided the clone has had time to mature.
