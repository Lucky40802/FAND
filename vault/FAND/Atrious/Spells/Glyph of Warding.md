# Glyph of Warding

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Runic Magic (School)|Runic Magic]] in this vault*

You inscribe a glyph on a surface or object that triggers when a condition you set is met, unleashing either a stored spell of 3rd level or lower or an explosive burst of damage in a 20-foot-radius sphere.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 damage per slot level above 3rd
