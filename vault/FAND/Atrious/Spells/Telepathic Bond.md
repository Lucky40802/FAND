# Telepathic Bond

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

You forge a telepathic link among up to eight willing creatures, letting them communicate silently with each other over any distance, as long as they're on the same plane of existence, for the duration.
