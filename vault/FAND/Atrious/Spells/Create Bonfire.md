# Create Bonfire

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You create a 5-foot cube of fire at a point you can see. A creature in the space must make a Dexterity save, taking fire damage on a failure; you may re-center it as a bonus action on later turns.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
