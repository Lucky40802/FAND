# Mind Sliver

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Psionics (School)|Psionics]] in this vault*

You drive a disorienting spike of psychic energy into a creature's mind. On a failed Intelligence save it takes psychic damage and subtracts 1d4 from the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn.

**Save:** Intelligence

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
