# Gust of Wind

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A line of strong wind blasts out from you for the duration; creatures in the line must succeed a Strength save or be pushed 15 feet away, and the wind extinguishes small flames and disperses gas/vapor.

**Save:** Strength
