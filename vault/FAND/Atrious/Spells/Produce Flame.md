# Produce Flame

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A flame flickers in your palm for the duration, shedding light and doing no harm to you. You can also hurl it as a ranged spell attack for fire damage.

**At Higher Levels:** scales to 2d8/3d8/4d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
