# Ward of Veil

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A standard-issue abjuration working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
