# Feeblemind

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

You unleash a mental assault against a creature; on a failed Intelligence save, in addition to the psychic damage, its Intelligence and Charisma scores become 1, incapable of casting spells or understanding language, until healed by greater restoration or similar.

**Save:** Intelligence
