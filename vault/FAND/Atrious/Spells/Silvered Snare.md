# Silvered Snare

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

One of the more forgettable entries in abjuration, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
