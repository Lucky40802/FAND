# Bolt of Anchored Passage

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

One of the more demanding entries in conjuration's middle tier. It strikes everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
