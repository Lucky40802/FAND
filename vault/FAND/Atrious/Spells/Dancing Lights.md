# Dancing Lights

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

You create up to four torch-sized lights in range, shaped as glowing orbs, flames, or humanoid shapes. As a bonus action you move them anywhere within range, up to 60 feet from each other.
