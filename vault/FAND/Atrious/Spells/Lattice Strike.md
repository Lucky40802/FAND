# Lattice Strike

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

A simple abjuration trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. It strikes a single target, dealing 2d6 Force damage. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
