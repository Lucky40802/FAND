# Shroud of Bastion

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A middling abjuration technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
