# Grove Restoration

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A standard-issue nature magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. Up to six creatures of the caster's choice in the area regain 1d4 + the caster's spellcasting modifier hit points. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
