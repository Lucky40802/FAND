# Mantle of Faded Span

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A plain chronomancy spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
