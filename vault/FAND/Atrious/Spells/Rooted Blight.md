# Rooted Blight

*Cantrip — Nature Magic*

A simple nature magic trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
