# Blazing Lance

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

One of the more demanding entries in evocation's middle tier. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Thunder damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
