# Spider Climb

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A willing creature you touch gains the ability to move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and upside down along ceilings, without needing to make a check, and gains a climbing speed equal to its walking speed.
