# Prism Barrier

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A more demanding evocation working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A shimmering barrier surrounds up to 4 creatures of the caster's choice, granting each 16 temporary hit points. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
