# Steadfast Snare

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it becomes Frightened of the caster until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
