# Bind of Abyss

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A step up from the common void magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
