# Curse of Toll

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

One of the more forgettable entries in necromancy, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
