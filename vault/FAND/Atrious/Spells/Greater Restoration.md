# Greater Restoration

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You touch a creature and end one of the following afflicting it: one exhaustion level, a charm or petrification effect, a curse (including attunement to a cursed item), or reduced maximum hit points or an ability score.
