# Shroud of Sanctum

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
