# Aura of Vitality

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

Healing energy radiates from you; as a bonus action on your turns you can cause a creature in the aura to regain 2d6 hit points.
