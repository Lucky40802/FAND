# Whisper of Restorative Salve

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

An ordinary healing technique, unremarkable but dependable. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
