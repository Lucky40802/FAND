# Uncontrolled Wrench

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

The kind of wild magic working passed between friends rather than taught formally. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Fire damage instead of stopping short. It never scales, but it never needs to.
