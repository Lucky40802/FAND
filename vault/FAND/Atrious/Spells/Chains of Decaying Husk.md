# Chains of Decaying Husk

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
