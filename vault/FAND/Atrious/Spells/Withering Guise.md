# Withering Guise

*Cantrip — Illusion*

A illusion staple, taught in every proper school and most improper ones. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
