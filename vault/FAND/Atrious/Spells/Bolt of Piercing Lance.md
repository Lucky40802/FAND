# Bolt of Piercing Lance

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A more demanding evocation working, taught only once the basics are mastered. It strikes everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Thunder damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
