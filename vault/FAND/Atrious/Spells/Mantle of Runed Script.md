# Mantle of Runed Script

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
