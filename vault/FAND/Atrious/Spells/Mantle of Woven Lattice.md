# Mantle of Woven Lattice

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

The most basic working taught in abjuration — every apprentice learns it in their first month. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
