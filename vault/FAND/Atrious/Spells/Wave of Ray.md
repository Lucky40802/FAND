# Wave of Ray

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A evocation technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15ft in a direction of the caster's choice, falling Prone if it hits a solid surface. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 7d6 Thunder damage instead of stopping short. Not something a caster reaches for casually. A handful of minor phrasings exist for this working, all functionally identical.
