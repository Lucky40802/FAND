# Chains of Living Canopy

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A plain nature magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
