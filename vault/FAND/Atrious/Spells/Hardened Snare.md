# Hardened Snare

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A step up from the common abjuration basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
