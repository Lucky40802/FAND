# Feign Death

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You touch a willing creature to put it into a cataleptic state indistinguishable from death to all outward inspection, including magical means short of true resurrection-tier effects; it gains resistance to all damage while in this state.
