# Spirit Guardians

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Spectral guardians flit around you in a 15-foot radius for the duration; enemy creatures there have their speed halved, and a creature that enters or starts its turn there takes damage on a failed Wisdom save, half on a success.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 3rd
