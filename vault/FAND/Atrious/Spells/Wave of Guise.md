# Wave of Guise

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

One of the more forgettable entries in transmutation, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
