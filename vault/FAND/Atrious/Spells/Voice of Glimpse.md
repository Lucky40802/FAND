# Voice of Glimpse

*Secondary-tier Divination*

One of the more demanding entries in divination's middle tier. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it. The exact ritual varies slightly from caster to caster, though the effect never does.
