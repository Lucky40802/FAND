# Silence

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

No sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose; creatures inside are deafened, and verbal spellcasting is impossible there.
