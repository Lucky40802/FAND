# Color Spray

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

A dazzling array of flashing, colored light springs from your hand; creatures in the cone are blinded until the end of your next turn, starting with those with the lowest current hit points, up to a total of 6d10 hit points of creatures.

**At Higher Levels:** total hit points affected increases by 2d10 per slot level above 1st
