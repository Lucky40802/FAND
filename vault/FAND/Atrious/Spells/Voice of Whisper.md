# Voice of Whisper

*Secondary-tier Divination*

A divination working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
