# Seraph's Purification

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

A more demanding celestial working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A willing creature within range is cured of one disease or one condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned) and regains 5d6 hit points. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
