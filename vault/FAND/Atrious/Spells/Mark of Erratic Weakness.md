# Mark of Erratic Weakness

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

A rite so basic to wild magic that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
