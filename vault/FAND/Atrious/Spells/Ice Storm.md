# Ice Storm

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A hail of rock-hard ice pounds down in a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-tall cylinder; creatures there take bludgeoning and cold damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success, and the ground becomes difficult terrain.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 cold damage per slot level above 4th
