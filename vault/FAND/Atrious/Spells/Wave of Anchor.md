# Wave of Anchor

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A conjuration technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 15ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 6d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
