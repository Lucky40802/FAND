# Whisper of Hidden Glimpse

*Secondary-tier Divination*

An intermediate divination working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
