# Ashen Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A no-frills elemental working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Thunder damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
