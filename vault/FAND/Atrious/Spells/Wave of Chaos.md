# Wave of Chaos

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Lightning damage instead of stopping short. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
