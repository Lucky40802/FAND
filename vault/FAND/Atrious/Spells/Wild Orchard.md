# Wild Orchard

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A nature magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. Plants within a 30-foot radius bloom and bear fruit. Up to 8 creatures who eat a fruit within the next hour regain 4d4 hit points and are cured of one poison. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
