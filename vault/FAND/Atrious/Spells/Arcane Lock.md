# Arcane Lock

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You touch a closeable object, magically locking it; only you or creatures you designate can open it, and it resists knock and similar effects.
