# Gale of Frozen Stone

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A no-frills elemental working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Acid damage instead of stopping short. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
