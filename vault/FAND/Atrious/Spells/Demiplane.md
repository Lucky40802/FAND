# Demiplane

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Shadow Magic (School)|Shadow Magic]] in this vault*

You create a shadowy door on a solid surface within range, leading to a 30-foot cubic extradimensional room that's mostly featureless unless you've created it before; the door disappears at the end of the duration, trapping anyone left inside.
