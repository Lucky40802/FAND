# Mark of Frozen Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A plain elemental spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
