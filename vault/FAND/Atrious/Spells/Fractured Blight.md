# Fractured Blight

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

One of the more forgettable entries in psionics, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
