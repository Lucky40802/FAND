# Withering Grasp

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A common, easily-taught necromancy working. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
