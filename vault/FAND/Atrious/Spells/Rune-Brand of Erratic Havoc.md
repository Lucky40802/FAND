# Rune-Brand of Erratic Havoc

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

A wild magic basic that survives in every curriculum precisely because it's so cheap to teach. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
