# Cloudkill

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

A 20-foot-radius sphere of poisonous, yellow-green fog spreads out, killing lesser creatures automatically and dealing poison damage to others on a failed Constitution save, half on a success; you can move it 10 feet each round.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 5th
