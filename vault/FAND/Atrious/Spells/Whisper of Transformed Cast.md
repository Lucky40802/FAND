# Whisper of Transformed Cast

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A transmutation spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
