# Ward of Etching

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A step up from the common runic magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
