# Ward of Chaos

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A standard-issue wild magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
