# Bolt of Withering Wither

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

One of the more demanding entries in necromancy's middle tier. It sweeps across everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
