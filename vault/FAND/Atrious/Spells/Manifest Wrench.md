# Manifest Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

A plain conjuration spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
