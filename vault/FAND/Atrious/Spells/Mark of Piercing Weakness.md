# Mark of Piercing Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

One of the more forgettable entries in psionics, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
