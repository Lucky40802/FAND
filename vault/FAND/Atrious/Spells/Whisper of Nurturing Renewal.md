# Whisper of Nurturing Renewal

*Cantrip — Healing*

A rite so basic to healing that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster and one willing creature exchange a single word silently. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
