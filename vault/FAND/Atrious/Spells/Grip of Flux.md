# Grip of Flux

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
