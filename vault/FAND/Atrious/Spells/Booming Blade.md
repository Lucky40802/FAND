# Booming Blade

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You make a melee weapon attack against one creature; on a hit it takes normal weapon damage plus thunder damage, and if it willingly moves before the start of your next turn it takes additional thunder damage as a peal of thunder erupts from it.

**At Higher Levels:** extra 1d8 at 5th/11th/17th character level, plus another 1d8 if target moves
