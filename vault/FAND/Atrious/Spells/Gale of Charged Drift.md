# Gale of Charged Drift

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

An ordinary elemental technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Lightning damage instead of stopping short. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
