# Mark of Weightless Weakness

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A more demanding void magic working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it. Regional variants exist, though none differ mechanically from this version.
