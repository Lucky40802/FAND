# Withering Discharge

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
