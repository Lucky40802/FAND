# Grip of Glamour

*Cantrip — Illusion*

One of the first things any illusion practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
