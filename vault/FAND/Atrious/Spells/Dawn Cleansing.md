# Dawn Cleansing

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A common, easily-taught celestial working. A willing creature within range is cured of one disease or one condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned) and regains 2d6 hit points. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
