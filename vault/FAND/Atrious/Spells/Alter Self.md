# Alter Self

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You assume a new form, choosing one option for the duration: aquatic adaptation (gills and swimming speed), a changed appearance, or natural weapons (unarmed strikes dealing 1d6 damage of a chosen type).
