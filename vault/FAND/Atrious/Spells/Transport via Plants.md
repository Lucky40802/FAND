# Transport via Plants

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You create a temporary teleportation circle linking a Large or larger plant you can see to a plant of the same species you're familiar with, allowing creatures to step from one to the other.
