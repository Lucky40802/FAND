# Water Walk

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Up to ten willing creatures you can see can move across any liquid surface, such as water, acid, mud, or lava, as if it were solid ground, for the duration.
