# Bind of Veil

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

A step up from the common shadow magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
