# Mark of Umbral Weakness

*Cantrip — Shadow Magic*

Considered too minor to charge for — most shadow magic teachers throw this in for free. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. No spell slot required — cast it as often as the moment calls for.
