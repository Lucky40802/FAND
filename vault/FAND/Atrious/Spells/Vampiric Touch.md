# Vampiric Touch

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

Your hand glows with dark power; a melee spell attack against a creature deals necrotic damage on a hit, and you regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt, for the duration whenever you repeat the attack.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 3rd
