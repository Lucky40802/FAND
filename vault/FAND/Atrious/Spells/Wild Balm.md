# Wild Balm

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A nature magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. Up to six creatures of the caster's choice in the area regain 5d4 + the caster's spellcasting modifier hit points. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
