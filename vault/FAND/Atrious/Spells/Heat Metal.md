# Heat Metal

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You cause a manufactured metal object to glow red-hot; a creature touching or wearing it takes fire damage and must drop it or take disadvantage on attacks/checks unless it succeeds a Constitution save.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 2nd
