# Contagion

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

Your touch (melee spell attack) infects a creature with a magical disease of your choice on a hit; three failed Constitution saves over successive days bring on the disease's full effects, while three successes end the threat.

**Save:** Constitution
