# Sight of Flesh

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

A simple transmutation trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. No damage, no save required — the working creates a small light or sound at a point the caster can see. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
