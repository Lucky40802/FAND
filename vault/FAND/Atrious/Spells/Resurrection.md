# Resurrection

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You touch a creature that died no more than a century ago, returning it to life fully healed, with any missing body parts restored, though it doesn't restore any missing organs necessary for life if the creature is undead.
