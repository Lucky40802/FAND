# Silent Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Divination*

An ordinary divination technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
