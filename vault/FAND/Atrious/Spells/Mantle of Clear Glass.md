# Mantle of Clear Glass

*Secondary-tier Divination*

A divination technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
