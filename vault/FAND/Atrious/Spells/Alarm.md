# Alarm

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You set an alarm on a 20-foot cube; when a Tiny or larger creature you didn't designate enters the area, you're alerted, audibly or mentally depending on the version chosen.
