# Withering Duplicate

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A step up from the common illusion basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
