# Curse's Brand

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

One of the more forgettable entries in necromancy, but a reliable one. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 1d6 damage. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
