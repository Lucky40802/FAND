# Altered Wrench

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

An intermediate transmutation working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15ft in a direction of the caster's choice, falling Prone if it hits a solid surface. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 5d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
