# Silent Image

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

You create a purely visual illusion of an object, creature, or phenomenon within a 15-foot cube; a creature investigating it can discern it's an illusion with an Investigation check against your spell save DC.
