# Mossy Bloom

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A nature magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. Plants within a 30-foot radius bloom and bear fruit. Up to 4 creatures who eat a fruit within the next hour regain 2d4 hit points and are cured of one poison. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
