# Heal

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A creature you can see regains 70 hit points and is cured of blindness, deafness, and any disease afflicting it. This spell has no effect on constructs or undead.

**At Higher Levels:** +10 hp healing per slot level above 6th
