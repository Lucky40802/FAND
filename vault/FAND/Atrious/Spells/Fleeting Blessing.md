# Fleeting Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
