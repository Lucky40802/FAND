# Tide of Alteration

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

A transmutation staple, taught in every proper school and most improper ones. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
