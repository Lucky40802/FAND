# Bind of Marrow

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A no-frills necromancy working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
