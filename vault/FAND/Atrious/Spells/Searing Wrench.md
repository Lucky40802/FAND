# Searing Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A no-frills evocation working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Thunder damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
