# Shatter

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A sudden loud, ringing noise damages creatures in a 10-foot-radius sphere on a failed Constitution save, half on a success; nonmagical objects that aren't worn or carried also take damage.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 2nd
