# Velocity Unbound

[[Plasma]] — Sorcerer, Aberrant Mind

A line of superheated velocity streaks out 90 feet, striking every creature in its path. A Dexterity save halves the damage; there is no damage type resistance available against it, since it isn't truly fire, lightning, or any ordinary element.
