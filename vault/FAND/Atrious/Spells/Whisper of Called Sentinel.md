# Whisper of Called Sentinel

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

A standard-issue conjuration working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
