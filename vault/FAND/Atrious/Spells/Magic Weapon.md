# Magic Weapon

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A nonmagical weapon you touch becomes magical, granting a +1 bonus to its attack and damage rolls for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** +2 bonus at 4th-level slot, +3 bonus at 6th-level slot
