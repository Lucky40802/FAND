# Sight of Body

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A more demanding transmutation working, taught only once the basics are mastered. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
