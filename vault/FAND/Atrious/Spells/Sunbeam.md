# Sunbeam

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

A beam of brilliant light flashes out in a 5-foot-wide, 60-foot-long line; creatures there take radiant damage and are blinded until your next turn on a failed Constitution save, half damage and no blindness on a success. You can use your action on later turns to create the beam again.

**Save:** Constitution
