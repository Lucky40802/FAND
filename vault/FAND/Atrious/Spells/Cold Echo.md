# Cold Echo

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A standard-issue shadow magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. No damage, no save required — the working reveals whether a creature or object within range is magical. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
