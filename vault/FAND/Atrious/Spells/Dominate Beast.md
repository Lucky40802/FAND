# Dominate Beast

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You attempt to beguile a beast you can see; on a failed Wisdom save it's charmed and you can telepathically command its actions for the duration.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** duration extends to 10 minutes/1 hour/8 hours at 5th/6th/7th-level slots
