# Darkness

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Shadow Magic (School)|Shadow Magic]] in this vault*

Magical darkness spreads from a point you choose in a 15-foot radius, spreading around corners; nonmagical light can't illuminate it, and creatures without darkvision that see into it are effectively blinded.
