# Faded Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
