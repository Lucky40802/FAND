# Bind of Bone

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
