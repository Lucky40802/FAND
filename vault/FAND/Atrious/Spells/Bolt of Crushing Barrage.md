# Bolt of Crushing Barrage

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

One of the more demanding entries in evocation's middle tier. It strikes everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Fire damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
