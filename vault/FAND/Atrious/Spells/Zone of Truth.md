# Zone of Truth

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

Creatures within a 15-foot-radius sphere that fail a Charisma save can't speak a deliberate lie while inside the zone, though they can avoid answering or speak misleading truths.

**Save:** Charisma
