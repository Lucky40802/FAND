# Door Step

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A conjuration spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster and up to 6 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 180 feet. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
