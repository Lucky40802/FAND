# Speak with Plants

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You imbue plants in range with limited sentience and animation, letting you converse with them for information about the area, and command them to grasp, entangle, or part for creatures for the duration.
