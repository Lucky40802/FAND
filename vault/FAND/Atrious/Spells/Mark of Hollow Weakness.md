# Mark of Hollow Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A middling illusion technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
