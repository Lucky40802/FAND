# Mantle of Bound Rune

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for runic magic casters who've put in the hours. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
