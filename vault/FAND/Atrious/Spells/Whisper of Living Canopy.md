# Whisper of Living Canopy

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A workaday nature magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster read any language for 1 minute. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
