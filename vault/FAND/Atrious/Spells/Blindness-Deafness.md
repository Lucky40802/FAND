# Blindness/Deafness

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You blind or deafen a creature you choose; it can repeat the Constitution save at the end of each of its turns to end the effect.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 2nd
