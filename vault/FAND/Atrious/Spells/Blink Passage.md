# Blink Passage

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

An ordinary conjuration technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster and up to 1 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 30 feet. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
