# Mage Hand

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

A spectral, floating hand appears at a point you choose within range and can manipulate objects, open unlocked doors, or carry up to 10 pounds as you direct it with your action.
