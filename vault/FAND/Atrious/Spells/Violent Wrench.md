# Violent Wrench

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A step up from the common evocation basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15ft in a direction of the caster's choice, falling Prone if it hits a solid surface. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 6d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
