# Carved Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A workaday runic magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
