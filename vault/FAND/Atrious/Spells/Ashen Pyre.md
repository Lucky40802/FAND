# Ashen Pyre

[[Fire]] — Sorcerer (Ignis bloodline)

A burst of consuming flame erupts in a 15-foot radius. Each creature in the area makes a Dexterity save, taking full damage on a fail and half on a success. Flammable objects not worn or carried ignite.
