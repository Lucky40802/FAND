# Mark of Phantom Weakness

*Cantrip — Illusion*

A rite so basic to illusion that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
