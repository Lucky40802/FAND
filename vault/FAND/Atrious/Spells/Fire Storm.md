# Fire Storm

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A wall of roaring flame fills up to ten contiguous 10-foot cubes you choose within range; creatures there take fire damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success, and unattended flammable objects ignite.

**Save:** Dexterity
