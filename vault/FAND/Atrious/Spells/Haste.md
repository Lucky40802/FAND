# Haste

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Chronomancy (School)|Chronomancy]] in this vault*

A willing creature's speed doubles, it gains a +2 bonus to AC, has advantage on Dexterity saves, and gains an extra action limited to Attack, Dash, Disengage, Hide, or object use, for the duration; when the spell ends the target is incapacitated for a turn.
