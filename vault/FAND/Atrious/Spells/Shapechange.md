# Shapechange

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You transform into a different creature whose challenge rating is equal to your level or lower, keeping your own mind and personality while gaining the new form's physical statistics and abilities; you can revert or shift again as a bonus action.
