# Lash of Decay

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A working with real teeth, reserved for necromancy casters who've put in the hours. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
