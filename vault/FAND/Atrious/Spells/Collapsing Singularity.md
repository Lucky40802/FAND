# Collapsing Singularity

[[Singularity]] — Monk, Way of the Astral Self

A point of collapsed space pulls every creature in a 15-foot radius 10 feet toward its center, then bursts. Creatures that collide with each other or a solid surface take the damage; a Dexterity save halves it.
