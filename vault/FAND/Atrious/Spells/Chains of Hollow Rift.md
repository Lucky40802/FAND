# Chains of Hollow Rift

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
