# Fly

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A creature you touch gains a flying speed of 60 feet for the duration; if the spell ends while it's aloft, it falls unless it can stop the fall.

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 3rd
