# Withering Flux

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

One of the more forgettable entries in wild magic, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
