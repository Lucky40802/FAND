# Light

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

You touch an object no larger than 10 feet in any dimension, causing it to shed bright light in a 20-foot radius. An unwilling creature holding the object can negate the effect with a successful save.

**Save:** Dexterity
