# Control Weather

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You take control of the weather within 5 miles for the duration, changing precipitation, temperature, and wind incrementally each hour toward conditions of your choosing.
