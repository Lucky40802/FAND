# Whisper of Ticking Turn

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A no-frills chronomancy working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
