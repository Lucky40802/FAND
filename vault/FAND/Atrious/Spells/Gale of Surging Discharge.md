# Gale of Surging Discharge

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A step up from the common wild magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15ft in a direction of the caster's choice, falling Prone if it hits a solid surface. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 5d6 Poison damage instead of stopping short. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
