# Mantle of Steady Solace

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A working with real teeth, reserved for healing casters who've put in the hours. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
