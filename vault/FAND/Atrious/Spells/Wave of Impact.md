# Wave of Impact

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A standard-issue evocation working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Thunder damage instead of stopping short. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
