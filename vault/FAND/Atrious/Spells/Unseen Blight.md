# Unseen Blight

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A no-frills psionics working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
