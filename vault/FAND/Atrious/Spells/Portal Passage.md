# Portal Passage

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A more demanding conjuration working, taught only once the basics are mastered. The caster and up to 5 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 150 feet. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
