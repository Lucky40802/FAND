# Tide of Frame

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

An ordinary transmutation technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
