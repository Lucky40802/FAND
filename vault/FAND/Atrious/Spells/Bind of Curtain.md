# Bind of Curtain

*Cantrip — Shadow Magic*

Widely regarded as the easiest working in shadow magic. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
