# Fleeting Snare

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A standard-issue illusion working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
