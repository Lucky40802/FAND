# Chains of Boundless Breach

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A no-frills void magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
