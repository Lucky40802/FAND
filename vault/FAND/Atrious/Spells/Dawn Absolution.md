# Dawn Absolution

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

A more demanding celestial working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A willing creature within range is cured of one disease or one condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned) and regains 6d6 hit points. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
