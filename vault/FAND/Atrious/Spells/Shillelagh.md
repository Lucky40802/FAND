# Shillelagh

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

The wood of a club or quarterstaff you touch glows; for the duration you can use your spellcasting ability instead of Strength for its attack and damage rolls, and its damage die becomes a d8, with the weapon counting as magical.
