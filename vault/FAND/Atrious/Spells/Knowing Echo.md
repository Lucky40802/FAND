# Knowing Echo

*Cantrip — Divination*

The most basic working taught in divination — every apprentice learns it in their first month. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster and one willing creature exchange a single word silently. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
