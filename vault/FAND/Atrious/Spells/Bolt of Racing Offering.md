# Bolt of Racing Offering

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. It presses against everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Piercing damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
