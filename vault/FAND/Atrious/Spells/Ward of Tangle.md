# Ward of Tangle

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

An intermediate wild magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably. The gestures involved vary by teacher, but the result is always the same.
