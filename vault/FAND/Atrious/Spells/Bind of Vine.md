# Bind of Vine

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A more demanding nature magic working, taught only once the basics are mastered. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
