# Aura of Life

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

Life-preserving energy surrounds you; allies in the aura have resistance to necrotic damage and can't have their hit point maximum reduced, and a living creature there that drops to 0 hp instead drops to 1 (once per creature).
