# Enemies Abound

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Psionics (School)|Psionics]] in this vault*

You strike at the mind of a creature, forcing an Intelligence save. On a failure it can't tell friend from foe, treating the closest creature to it as an enemy each round, friend or otherwise.

**Save:** Intelligence
