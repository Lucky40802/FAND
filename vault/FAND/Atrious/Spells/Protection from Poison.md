# Protection from Poison

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You touch a creature, neutralizing one poison affecting it and granting resistance to poison damage and advantage on saves against being poisoned for the duration.
