# Chains of Echoing Surge

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A psionics working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
