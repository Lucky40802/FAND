# Sleep

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

This spell sends creatures into a magical slumber, affecting up to 5d8 hit points of creatures, starting with the one with the lowest current hp; unconscious creatures don't wake for the duration unless damaged or shaken.

**At Higher Levels:** total hit points affected increases by 2d8 per slot level above 1st
