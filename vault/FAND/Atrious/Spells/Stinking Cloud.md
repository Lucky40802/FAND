# Stinking Cloud

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

A 20-foot-radius sphere of yellow, nauseating gas spreads; each creature there must succeed a Constitution save or waste its turn retching and reeling, unable to act, for as long as it remains and the spell lasts.

**Save:** Constitution
