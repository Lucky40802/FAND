# Animal Shapes

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You transform up to one willing creature per 10 feet of range into a beast form with a challenge rating of 4 or lower; each transformed creature can revert as a bonus action, and the spell ends early for any creature reduced to 0 hp.
