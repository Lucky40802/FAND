# Astral Projection

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You and up to eight willing creatures project your astral bodies onto the Astral Plane, leaving your physical bodies behind in a state of suspended animation; the spell ends for a creature if its silver cord is severed, killing it.
