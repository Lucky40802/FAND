# Lightning Lure

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A whip of lightning lashes out at one creature within range. The target must succeed on a Strength save or take lightning damage and be pulled up to 10 feet toward you.

**Save:** Strength

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 at 5th/11th/17th character level
