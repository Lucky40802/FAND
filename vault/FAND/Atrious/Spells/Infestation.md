# Infestation

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You cause a cloud of mites, fleas, and other biting insects to appear briefly on a creature. On a failed Constitution save it takes poison damage and moves 5 feet in a random direction if it can.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
