# Circle of Power

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Divine energy radiates from you; friendly creatures in the aura have advantage on saving throws against spells, and take no damage if a spell would normally deal half damage on a successful save.
