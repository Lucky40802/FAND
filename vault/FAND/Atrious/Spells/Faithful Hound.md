# Faithful Hound

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

An invisible watchdog appears at a point you choose, barking loudly if a creature you didn't designate comes within 30 feet, and biting any intruder that comes within 5 feet.
