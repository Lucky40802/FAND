# Wave of Tumult

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A middling wild magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
