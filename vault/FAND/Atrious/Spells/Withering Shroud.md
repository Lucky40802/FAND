# Withering Shroud

*Cantrip — Shadow Magic*

The most basic working taught in shadow magic — every apprentice learns it in their first month. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
