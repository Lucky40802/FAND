# Curse of Scar

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
