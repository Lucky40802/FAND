# Rune-Brand of Wicked Bargain

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

A infernal technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. Requires 10 minutes to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 2d8 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
