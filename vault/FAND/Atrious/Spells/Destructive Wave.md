# Destructive Wave

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

You strike the ground, unleashing a wave of divine energy; creatures around you take thunder plus radiant or necrotic damage (your choice) and are knocked prone on a failed Constitution save, or half damage and no prone on a success.

**Save:** Constitution
