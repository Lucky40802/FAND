# Mantle of Woven Guard

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
