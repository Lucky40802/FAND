# Simulacrum

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

You shape a snow or ice duplicate of a creature you touch, which has half the original's hit points and is friendly to you and your allies, obeying your commands as if it were you.
