# Whisper of Mending Grace

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A healing technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Not something a caster reaches for casually. The gestures involved vary by teacher, but the result is always the same.
