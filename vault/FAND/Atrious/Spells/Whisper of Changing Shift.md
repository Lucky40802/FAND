# Whisper of Changing Shift

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A workaday transmutation technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster read any language for 1 minute. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
