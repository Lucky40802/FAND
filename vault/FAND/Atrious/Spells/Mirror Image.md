# Mirror Image

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

Three illusory duplicates of yourself appear in your space; whenever a creature targets you with an attack, it instead hits a duplicate unless the attacker can tell them apart, destroying that duplicate.
