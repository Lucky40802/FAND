# Chains of Unreal Veil

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A illusion working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
