# Hollow Weightlessness

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A willing creature becomes weightless for 3 rounds: it can float, fly at its walking speed, and pass through nonmagical objects. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
