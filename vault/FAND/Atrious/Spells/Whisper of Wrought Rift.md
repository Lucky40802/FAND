# Whisper of Wrought Rift

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A conjuration working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit. The gestures involved vary by teacher, but the result is always the same.
