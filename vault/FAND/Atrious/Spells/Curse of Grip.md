# Curse of Grip

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

A step up from the common psionics basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained. The gestures involved vary by teacher, but the result is always the same.
