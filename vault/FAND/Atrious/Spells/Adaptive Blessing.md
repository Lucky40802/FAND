# Adaptive Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A middling transmutation technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
