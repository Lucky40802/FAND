# Lash of Dark

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for void magic casters who've put in the hours. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
