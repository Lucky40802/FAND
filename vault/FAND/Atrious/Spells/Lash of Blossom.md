# Lash of Blossom

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for nature magic casters who've put in the hours. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Piercing damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
