# Rune-Brand of Ancient Seal

*Cantrip — Runic Magic*

The kind of runic magic working passed between friends rather than taught formally. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. It never scales, but it never needs to.
