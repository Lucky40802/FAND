# Sight of Vision

*Cantrip — Divination*

A divination basic that survives in every curriculum precisely because it's so cheap to teach. No damage, no save required — the working creates a small light or sound at a point the caster can see. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
