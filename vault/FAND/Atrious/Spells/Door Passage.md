# Door Passage

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

One of the more forgettable entries in conjuration, but a reliable one. The caster and up to 3 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 90 feet. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
