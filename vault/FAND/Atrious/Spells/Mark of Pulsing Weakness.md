# Mark of Pulsing Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
