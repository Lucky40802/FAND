# Catapult

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Choose a loose, nonmagical object between 1 and 5 pounds within range and hurl it at a point you can see. Creatures within 5 feet of that point must succeed on a Dexterity save or take bludgeoning damage.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 1st
