# Darkvision

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You touch a willing creature to grant it the ability to see in the dark, gaining darkvision out to 60 feet for the duration.
