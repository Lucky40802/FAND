# Lingering Blight

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A workaday chronomancy technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
