# 🎶 School of Resonance
*The Bard's Exclusive School of Magic*

> *"Not all spells are cast with words of power. Some are sung into being — quietly, carefully, in the spaces between heartbeats."*
> — Mistress Vael, College of the Singing Wound

[[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Spell Index|Spell Index]] · [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Bard|Bard]]

---

## Overview

The School of Resonance is a unique form of magic accessible **only to Bards**. Unlike conventional spellcasting, Resonance magic requires the caster to perform a specific **Verbal Chant** (their *Resonance Phrase*) before or during casting. In exchange for this limitation, Resonance spells are:

- **Subtle** — they register as no school to Detect Magic
- **Stacking** — effects can linger and compound with other Bard abilities
- **Non-expending** — some Resonance cantrips do not consume Bardic Inspiration dice

However, Resonance spells have **reduced potency** compared to equivalent spells from other schools. They trade raw power for flexibility and stealth.

---

## How Resonance Works

### The Chant Requirement

Every Resonance spell requires the caster to speak, hum, or sing their **Resonance Phrase** — a personal verbal sequence that varies by bard. This phrase:
- Must be audible to the caster (does not need to be heard by others at low volume)
- Takes the form of **V (Verbal)** component but is *always required*, even for spells that would normally allow S-only casting
- Can be incorporated into performance, speech, or background music without drawing attention — DC 12 Deception or Performance check

> **If Silenced:** A Bard under the Silence spell or equivalent cannot cast any Resonance magic. This is Resonance's primary weakness.

### Resonance Phrases

Each Bard develops their own unique phrase. Work with your DM to establish yours. Examples:

| Style | Example Phrase |
|-------|----------------|
| Ancient Tongue | *"Vas en kural, mii sorath"* |
| Melodic | Three descending notes, hummed softly |
| Rhythmic | A specific drum pattern tapped on the chest |
| Spoken | *"Still the world, bend the veil"* |
| Breath-pattern | An inhale-hold-exhale rhythm |

## Resonance Spells by Level

### Cantrips

#### Lingering Note
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** 60ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Components:** V, S
- **Effect:** You weave a barely audible tone into the air. One creature of your choice within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer **disadvantage on Concentration checks** for the duration. Creatures that cannot hear are immune. This registers as no school of magic.
- *Lower potency than Hold Person but costs no spell slot at Cantrip rank.*

#### Echoing Whisper
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** 30ft
- **Duration:** Until dispelled or triggered
- **Components:** V
- **Effect:** You implant a short phrase (up to 10 words) into a surface, object, or doorway. The phrase plays back aloud — in your voice — when a creature you specify passes within 5ft. Only you can perceive the implanted phrase before it triggers; it is otherwise invisible to Detect Magic.
- *Useful for messaging, alarms, or ambushes.*

#### Fading Chord
- **Casting Time:** 1 Bonus Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Components:** V
- **Effect:** Until the start of your next turn, your voice and any music you produce cannot be traced to a source — listeners cannot determine where the sound originates. You gain advantage on Performance and Deception checks that involve misdirection through sound.

### Level 1

#### Sympathetic Resonance
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** Touch
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
- **Components:** V, S
- **Damage:** None
- **Effect:** You establish a resonant link between two willing creatures (one of which may be yourself). For the duration, each creature is aware of the emotional state of the other — not thoughts, only feelings. Additionally, once per round as a free action, you may spend 1 Bardic Inspiration die to transfer up to 5 hit points from one linked creature to the other (no action required from the recipient).
- *A subtle support tool. The healing is minor but costs no action.*

#### Dissonant Hum
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** 30ft cone
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Components:** V
- **Damage:** 1d6 Psychic
- **Save:** Constitution, half damage
- **Effect:** You emit a short dissonant burst. Creatures in the cone take **1d6 Psychic damage** and must succeed on a CON save or be **Distracted** until the end of their next turn (disadvantage on the next attack roll or ability check they make).
- *Scales: +1d6 per spell slot above 1st. Max 5d6 at 5th slot.*
- *Weaker than Vicious Mockery but affects all creatures in cone.*

#### Borrowed Tempo
- **Casting Time:** 1 Reaction (Chant required; triggered when an ally rolls Initiative)
- **Range:** 60ft
- **Duration:** 1 minute
- **Components:** V
- **Effect:** When a creature within range rolls Initiative, you may use your Reaction to sing a fragment of tempo into them. They gain a +2 bonus to their Initiative roll. Additionally, on their first turn in combat, they may take one additional 5ft movement without triggering opportunity attacks.
- *A subtle combat opener — gives allies a slight positional and Initiative edge.*

---

### Level 2

#### Harmonic Anchor
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** 60ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Components:** V, S
- **Effect:** You fix a creature's position in harmonic space. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be **Resonance Locked** — it cannot be forcibly moved (pushed, pulled, teleported) for the duration, though it can still move willingly. While locked, it also has resistance to Thunder damage.
- *Lower potency than Hold Person — the creature can still act freely. But the lock is hard to break externally.*

#### Woven Lullaby
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** 30ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Components:** V
- **Save:** Wisdom
- **Effect:** You weave a thread of drowsiness into the ambient sound around one creature. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have its speed reduced by 10ft and suffer a **-2 penalty to Perception checks** for the duration. On a failed save by 5 or more, the creature is also **Incapacitated** for 1 round before recovering.
- *Compared to Sleep — this affects one creature of any HP, but the effect is softer.*

#### Sound Mirror
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
- **Components:** V, S
- **Effect:** You create a magical duplicate of your own voice that sounds from a location you can see within 60ft. You can use a Bonus Action on subsequent turns to move the duplicate up to 30ft. The duplicate can speak, whisper, or sing at any volume you choose. Detect Magic reveals nothing unusual — this spell produces no magical signature.
- *Useful for misdirection, communication, and performance.*

---

### Level 3

#### Resonant Fracture
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** 60ft
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Components:** V, S
- **Damage:** 3d8 Thunder
- **Save:** Constitution, half damage
- **Effect:** You strike a single creature with a focused resonant pulse. On a hit, the target takes **3d8 Thunder damage** and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, any Concentration spell they are maintaining immediately ends.
- *Scales: +2d8 per slot level above 3rd.*
- *The Concentration-break is the real weapon here. Weaker raw damage than Thunderwave, but targeted and devastating against casters.*

#### Layered Cadence
- **Casting Time:** 1 Minute (Chant required; this is a ritual)
- **Range:** 30ft radius
- **Duration:** 8 hours
- **Components:** V, S, M *(a tuning fork or instrument string worth at least 5gp)*
- **Effect:** You spend one minute establishing a resonant frequency in a location. For the duration, all creatures you designate within the area gain a +1 bonus to saving throws and cannot be surprised. You are aware (as a vague sense) whenever a new creature enters the area, even while asleep.
- *A camp-protection ritual. Weak bonus, but no action cost after setup.*

#### Countermelody
- **Casting Time:** 1 Reaction (Chant required; triggered when you see a spell being cast within 60ft)
- **Range:** 60ft
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Components:** V
- **Effect:** When a creature within range casts a spell, you may use your Reaction to weave a countermelody. The caster must succeed on a **Charisma saving throw** (DC = your spell save DC) or their spell is cast at **disadvantage** — if the spell requires an attack roll, the roll is made with disadvantage; if it requires targets to save, they save with advantage this turn.
- *Compared to Counterspell — this doesn't cancel the spell, but it significantly weakens it. No spell slot required.*

---

### Level 4

#### Dissonant Veil
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** Self (20ft radius)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
- **Components:** V
- **Effect:** You fill the area around you with subtle dissonant harmonics. Hostile creatures within the area have disadvantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing. Additionally, spells cast within the area that have a verbal component must succeed on a DC 13 Concentration check or fail, as the dissonance interrupts spellcasting.
- *An area denial tool — particularly brutal in enclosed spaces. Weaker than Silence because it doesn't fully prevent casting.*

#### Resonance Echo
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required)
- **Range:** Touch
- **Duration:** 24 hours
- **Components:** V, S
- **Effect:** You touch a willing creature and bind a faint echo of a spell to their aura. Choose one Bard Resonance cantrip you know. For the duration, the target creature can cast that cantrip once — as if they were a Bard — without needing to know the Resonance Phrase. They use your spell save DC. After casting it, the echo fades.
- *A gift spell. Lets you lend a cantrip to an ally for a day.*

---

### Level 5

#### The Grand Chord
- **Casting Time:** 1 Action (Chant required; this is your Resonance Phrase spoken in full at maximum volume)
- **Range:** 90ft
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Components:** V, S
- **Effect:** You strike a resonant chord that shakes reality. All creatures of your choice within 90ft must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they are **Resonance Bound** for the duration — their emotions are flattened, their will suppressed. A Resonance Bound creature:
  - Cannot cast spells that require Verbal components
  - Has disadvantage on Charisma checks and saving throws
  - Takes **2d6 Psychic damage** at the start of each of their turns
  - May repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns

  On a success, the creature takes **2d6 Psychic damage** only.
- *This is Resonance's "big spell" — broadly effective but requires concentration and the open chant gives it away completely.*

## Design Philosophy

Resonance magic is intentionally **subtle and support-oriented**. It punishes silence and rewards positioning and preparation. A Bard specialising in Resonance is:

- A **battlefield manipulator** who weakens enemies rather than destroying them
- A **camp utility specialist** — setup rituals, communication tools, protective harmonics
- **Dependent on their voice** — their greatest vulnerability

When Silenced, a Resonance Bard loses *all* of this school. Plan accordingly.

## College Synergies

Resonance spells synergise with:
- **College of the Iron Drum** — Resonance damage spells gain a Thunder rider
- **College of the Silent Bell** — Resonance spells can be cast silently, breaking the Silence limitation once per long rest
- **College of the Broken Lute** — Resonance cantrips cost no Bardic Inspiration when cast at emotional peak

*(These three Colleges are not yet written up as full subclasses in this vault — noted here as hooks for a future subclass pass.)*
