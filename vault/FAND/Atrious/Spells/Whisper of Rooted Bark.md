# Whisper of Rooted Bark

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A nature magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
