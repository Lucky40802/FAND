# Ward of Blessing

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

A celestial technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
