# Mark of Withering Weakness

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
