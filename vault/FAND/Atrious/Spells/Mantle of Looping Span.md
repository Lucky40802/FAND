# Mantle of Looping Span

*Cantrip — Chronomancy*

A chronomancy fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
