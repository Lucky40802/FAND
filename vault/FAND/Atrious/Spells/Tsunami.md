# Tsunami

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

A wall of water up to 300 feet long, 300 feet high, and 50 feet thick rises and then crashes forward over several rounds, dealing bludgeoning damage on a failed Strength save, half on a success, and sweeping away creatures and structures in its path.

**Save:** Strength
