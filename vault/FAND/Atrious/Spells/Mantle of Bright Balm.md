# Mantle of Bright Balm

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A healing working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit. Some casters add a personal flourish to the casting, though it changes nothing about the effect.
