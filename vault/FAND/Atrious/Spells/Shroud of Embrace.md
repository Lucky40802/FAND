# Shroud of Embrace

*Cantrip — Healing*

The most basic working taught in healing — every apprentice learns it in their first month. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
