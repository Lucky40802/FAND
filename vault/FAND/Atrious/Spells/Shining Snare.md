# Shining Snare

*Cantrip — Celestial*

The entry point into celestial for most casters. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it can't move more than 5ft on its next turn. Rarely impressive, but never fails when it's actually needed.
