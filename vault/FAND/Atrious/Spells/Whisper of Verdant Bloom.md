# Whisper of Verdant Bloom

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A no-frills nature magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
