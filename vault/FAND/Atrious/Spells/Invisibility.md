# Invisibility

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

A creature you touch becomes invisible until the spell ends or the target attacks or casts a spell, at which point it ends for that creature.

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 2nd
