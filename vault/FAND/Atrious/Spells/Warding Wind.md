# Warding Wind

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A wind rages around you, making the area difficult terrain for others, muffling sound, extinguishing unprotected flames, and imposing disadvantage on ranged weapon attacks made into or out of the area.
