# Vigilant Blessing

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

The most basic working taught in abjuration — every apprentice learns it in their first month. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next Perception check. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
