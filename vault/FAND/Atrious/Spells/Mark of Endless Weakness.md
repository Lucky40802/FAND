# Mark of Endless Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A no-frills void magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
