# Armor of Agathys

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Frost covers you as protective magic wraps you in icy force, granting 5 temporary hit points. If a creature hits you with a melee attack while any of those hit points remain, it takes cold damage.

**At Higher Levels:** +5 temp HP and +5 retaliation damage per slot level above 1st
