# Lash of Bargain

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

A working with real teeth, reserved for infernal casters who've put in the hours. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
