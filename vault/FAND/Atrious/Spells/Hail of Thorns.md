# Hail of Thorns

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

The next ranged weapon attack you make before the spell ends causes thorny vines to burst from the target on a hit, showering all creatures within 5 feet with thorns.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 per slot level above 1st
