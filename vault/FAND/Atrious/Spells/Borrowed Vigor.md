# Borrowed Vigor

*Original — Necromancy*

You sacrifice some of your own health to mend another. You take necrotic damage equal to twice a rolled amount, and a creature you can see within range regains that many hit points.

**At Higher Levels:** +2d8 healing per slot level above 3rd
