# Wicked Blight

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

One of the more forgettable entries in infernal, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
