# Grip of Pallor

*Cantrip — Necromancy*

A simple necromancy trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
