# Meld into Stone

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You step into a stone object or surface large enough to fully contain your body, merging with and becoming part of the stone for the duration.
