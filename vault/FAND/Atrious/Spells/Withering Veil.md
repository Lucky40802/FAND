# Withering Veil

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A no-frills illusion working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
