# Animate Dead

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You target a corpse or skeleton, animating it as a zombie or skeleton under your control; it obeys your commands and remains animate until destroyed, though it fights on its own if you give no orders.

**At Higher Levels:** animate 2 additional corpses/skeletons per slot level above 3rd
