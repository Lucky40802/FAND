# Glyph of Rune

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A plain runic magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
