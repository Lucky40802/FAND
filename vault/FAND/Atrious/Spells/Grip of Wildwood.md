# Grip of Wildwood

*Cantrip — Nature Magic*

The most basic working taught in nature magic — every apprentice learns it in their first month. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
