# Feather Fall

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Choose up to five falling creatures within range; their rate of falling slows to 60 feet per round, and they take no damage from the fall if it ends before the spell does.
