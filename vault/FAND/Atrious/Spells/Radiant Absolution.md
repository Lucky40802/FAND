# Radiant Absolution

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A no-frills celestial working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A willing creature within range is cured of one disease or one condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned) and regains 1d6 hit points. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
