# Teleportation Circle

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You draw a 10-foot-diameter circle that opens a portal linked to a permanent teleportation circle you know, letting anyone step through to teleport to the other circle's location.
