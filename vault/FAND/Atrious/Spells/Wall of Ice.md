# Wall of Ice

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You create a wall of ice up to ten contiguous 10-foot-square panels; creatures adjacent when it appears take cold damage on a failed Dexterity save, and the wall's collapse or breach can pelt nearby creatures with damaging ice fragments.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +2d6 wall damage and +1d6 ice-fragment damage per slot level above 6th
