# Stone Rune of Shelter

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A workaday runic magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster inscribes a rune on a willing creature. Once in the next hour, when that creature would take damage, the damage is reduced by 10. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
