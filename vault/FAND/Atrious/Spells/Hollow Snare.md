# Hollow Snare

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

An intermediate illusion working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
