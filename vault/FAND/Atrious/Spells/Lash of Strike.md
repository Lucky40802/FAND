# Lash of Strike

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A evocation working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. It lashes out at everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
