# Splinter Volley

*Original — Void Magic*

Immediately after casting a spell of 1st level or higher, a shard of leftover energy streaks toward a creature within range as a bonus action, making a spell attack that deals force damage on a hit.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
