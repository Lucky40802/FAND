# Charged Crag

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A elemental spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. It grips at everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Fire damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
