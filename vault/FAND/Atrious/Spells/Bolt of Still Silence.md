# Bolt of Still Silence

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. It grips at everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
