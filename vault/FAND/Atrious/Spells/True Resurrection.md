# True Resurrection

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

You touch a creature that died no more than 200 years ago, or speak its name if you have no remains, and it's restored to life with a full new body, fully healed and free of any conditions caused by its death.
