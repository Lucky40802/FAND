# Withering Ichor

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in blood magic's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
