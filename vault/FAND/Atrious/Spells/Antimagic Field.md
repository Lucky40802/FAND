# Antimagic Field

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

A 10-foot-radius sphere of antimagic surrounds you, suppressing spells, magic items, and most other magical effects within it or that try to enter it, and giving spell attacks and effects no impact inside the field.
