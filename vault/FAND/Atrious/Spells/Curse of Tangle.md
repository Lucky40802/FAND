# Curse of Tangle

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
