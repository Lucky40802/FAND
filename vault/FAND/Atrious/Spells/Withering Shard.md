# Withering Shard

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A elemental working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
