# Geas

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You place a magical command on a creature to carry out some service or refrain from an action, as you decide, on a failed Wisdom save; disobeying causes psychic damage each day and disadvantage on ability checks and saves.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** duration extends to 1 year/permanent at 7th/9th-level slots
