# Bind of Branch

*Cantrip — Nature Magic*

A nature magic exercise more than a spell, the sort drilled into muscle memory early. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
