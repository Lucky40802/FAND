# Whisper of Echoing Surge

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A psionics working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
