# Etherealness

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You step into the Border Ethereal, able to see and hear the plane you left (dimly, and out to 60 feet) while remaining generally unable to affect or be affected by creatures on it, for the duration.
