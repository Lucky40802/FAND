# Mantle of Lingering Echo

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A no-frills chronomancy working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
