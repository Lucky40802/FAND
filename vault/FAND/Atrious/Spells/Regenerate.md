# Regenerate

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A creature you touch regains 4d6+15 hit points immediately and then regains 1 hit point at the start of each of its turns for the duration, and severed body parts (if the creature is still alive) regrow after a few minutes of contact.
