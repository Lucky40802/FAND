# Coin Wild Boon

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster rolls a d6 for a willing creature within range: on 1–2 it regains 3d8 hit points; on 3–4 it gains 15 temporary hit points; on 5–6 it has advantage on all d20 rolls for 1 minute. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
