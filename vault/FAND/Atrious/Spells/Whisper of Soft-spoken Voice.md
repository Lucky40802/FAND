# Whisper of Soft-spoken Voice

*Cantrip — Enchantment*

A enchantment basic that survives in every curriculum precisely because it's so cheap to teach. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
