# Budding Snare

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A no-frills nature magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
