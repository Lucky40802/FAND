# Gale of Pulled Gate

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

A workaday conjuration technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
