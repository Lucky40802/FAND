# Create Undead

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You animate up to three corpses as ghouls (or as ghasts/mummies if you're of sufficiently high level), which serve you until they're destroyed or you die.

**At Higher Levels:** animate 2 more corpses per slot level above 6th
