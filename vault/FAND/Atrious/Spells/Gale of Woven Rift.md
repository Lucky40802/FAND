# Gale of Woven Rift

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

A no-frills conjuration working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
