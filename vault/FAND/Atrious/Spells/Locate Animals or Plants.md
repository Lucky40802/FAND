# Locate Animals or Plants

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You describe or name a specific kind of beast or plant; you learn the direction and distance to the closest such creature or plant within 5 miles, if any are present.
