# Mark of Resonant Weakness

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

A psionics technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually. Regional variants exist, though none differ mechanically from this version.
