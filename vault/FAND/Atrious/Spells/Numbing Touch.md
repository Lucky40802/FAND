# Numbing Touch

*Original — Elemental*

Numbing frost lances into a creature. On a failed Constitution save it takes cold damage and has disadvantage on its next weapon attack roll before the end of its next turn.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
