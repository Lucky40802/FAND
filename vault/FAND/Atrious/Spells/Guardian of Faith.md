# Guardian of Faith

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

A Large spectral guardian appears and floats nearby; a creature hostile to you that moves within 10 feet of it for the first time on a turn must succeed a Dexterity save or take radiant damage, until the guardian has dealt 60 total damage.

**Save:** Dexterity
