# Withering Chain

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

An ordinary infernal technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
