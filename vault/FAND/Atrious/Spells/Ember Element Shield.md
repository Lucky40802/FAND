# Ember Element Shield

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

One of the more demanding entries in elemental's middle tier. A willing creature gains resistance to fire, cold, lightning, and thunder damage for 1 hour, and immunity to one of them of the caster's choice. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
