# Withering Bolt

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
