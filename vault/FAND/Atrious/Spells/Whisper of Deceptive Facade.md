# Whisper of Deceptive Facade

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A no-frills illusion working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
