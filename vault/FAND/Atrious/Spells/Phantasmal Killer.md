# Phantasmal Killer

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

You manifest an illusion of the target's deepest fears, visible only to it; on a failed Wisdom save it's frightened and takes psychic damage each of its turns until it succeeds on a repeated save.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 per slot level above 4th
