# Find Steed

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You summon a spirit that assumes the form of a loyal, intelligent steed, giving you a mount able to serve you in combat as long as it remains near you.
