# Hunter's Mark

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

You mark a creature as your quarry; your weapon attacks against it deal an extra 1d6 damage, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) or (Survival) checks to find it.

**At Higher Levels:** cast at 3rd/5th level for 8 hour/24 hour duration
