# Verdant Bloom

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A workaday nature magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. Plants within a 30-foot radius bloom and bear fruit. Up to 4 creatures who eat a fruit within the next hour regain 2d4 hit points and are cured of one poison. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
