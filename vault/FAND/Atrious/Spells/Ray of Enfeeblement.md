# Ray of Enfeeblement

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

A black beam of enervating energy lances toward a creature (ranged spell attack); on a hit it deals only half damage with Strength-based weapon attacks until the spell ends, and can repeat the save each of its turns.

**Save:** Constitution
