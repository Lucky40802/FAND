# Shield

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

An invisible barrier of force appears and protects you when you're hit by an attack or targeted by magic missile; you gain +5 AC until the start of your next turn, and magic missile automatically fails against you.
