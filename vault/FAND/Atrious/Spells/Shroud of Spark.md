# Shroud of Spark

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
