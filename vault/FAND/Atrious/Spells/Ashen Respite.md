# Ashen Respite

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. A dying creature within range is stabilized and regains 6d6 hit points. Undead the caster controls within range regain the same amount. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
