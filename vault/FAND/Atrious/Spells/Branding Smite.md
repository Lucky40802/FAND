# Branding Smite

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

The next time you hit with a weapon attack before the spell ends, the weapon deals extra radiant damage and the target becomes visible, shedding dim light, even if invisible.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 2nd
