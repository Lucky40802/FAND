# Wild Blight

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A workaday nature magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
