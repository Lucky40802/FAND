# Mantle of Bound Aegis

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A working with real teeth, reserved for abjuration casters who've put in the hours. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
