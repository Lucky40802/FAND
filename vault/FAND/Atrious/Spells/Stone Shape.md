# Stone Shape

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You touch a Medium or smaller stone object or a section of stone no more than 5 feet in any dimension, reshaping it into any form you choose within reason.
