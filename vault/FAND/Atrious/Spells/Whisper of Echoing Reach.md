# Whisper of Echoing Reach

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

An ordinary psionics technique, unremarkable but dependable. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
