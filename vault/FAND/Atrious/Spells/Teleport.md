# Teleport

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You instantly transport yourself and up to eight willing creatures joined in a circle to a destination you're familiar with; the more familiar you are with the destination, the lower the risk of a mishap, similarity, or off-target arrival.
