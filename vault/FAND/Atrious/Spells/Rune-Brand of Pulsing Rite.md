# Rune-Brand of Pulsing Rite

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

A workaday blood magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
