# Mark of Hallowed Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A workaday celestial technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
