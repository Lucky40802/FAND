# Pulsing Blight

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

An intermediate blood magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
