# Shroud of Seal

*Cantrip — Runic Magic*

A runic magic staple, taught in every proper school and most improper ones. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next Perception check. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
