# Furious Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A workaday evocation technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Lightning damage instead of stopping short. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
