# Silver Bastion

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. A shimmering barrier surrounds up to 4 creatures of the caster's choice, granting each 16 temporary hit points. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
