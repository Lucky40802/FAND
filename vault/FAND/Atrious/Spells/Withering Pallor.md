# Withering Pallor

*Cantrip — Necromancy*

One of the first things any necromancy practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
