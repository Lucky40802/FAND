# Chains of Cold Husk

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

An ordinary necromancy technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
