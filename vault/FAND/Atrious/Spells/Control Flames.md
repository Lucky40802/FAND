# Control Flames

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You choose a nonmagical flame you can see within range and cause one minor effect: shape it, expand or reduce it, snuff it out, or cause it to briefly change color and flare.
