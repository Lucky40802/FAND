# Glyph of Script

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A common, easily-taught runic magic working. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
