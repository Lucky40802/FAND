# Gale of Wild Flux

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A plain wild magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Fire damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
