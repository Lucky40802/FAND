# Ward of Salve

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

One of the more forgettable entries in healing, but a reliable one. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
