# Mark of Sodden Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

One of the more forgettable entries in elemental, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
