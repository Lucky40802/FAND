# Revivify

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You touch a creature that died within the last minute, returning it to life with 1 hit point; the spell can't restore missing body parts or reverse permanent conditions like old age.
