# Antilife Shell

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Infernal (School)|Infernal]] in this vault*

A shimmering barrier surrounds you for the duration, preventing most living creatures other than you from passing through it, though it doesn't stop you from moving through it yourself.
