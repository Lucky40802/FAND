# Gale of Twisted Form

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

One of the first things any transmutation practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
