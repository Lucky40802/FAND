# Brand of Snare

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A infernal working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 1d6 damage. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
