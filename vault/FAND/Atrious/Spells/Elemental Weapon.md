# Elemental Weapon

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A nonmagical weapon you touch becomes magical, gaining a +1 bonus to attack rolls and dealing an extra 1d4 damage of a chosen elemental type on a hit, for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** +2 attack/damage bonus and +2d4 elemental damage at 5th/7th-level slots
