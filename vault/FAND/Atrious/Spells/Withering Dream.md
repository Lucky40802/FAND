# Withering Dream

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A illusion technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually. A handful of minor phrasings exist for this working, all functionally identical.
