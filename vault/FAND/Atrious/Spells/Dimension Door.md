# Dimension Door

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You teleport yourself, and optionally one willing creature within 5 feet, to a spot you can visualize within range; arriving in an occupied space deals damage to you and shunts you to the nearest open space.
