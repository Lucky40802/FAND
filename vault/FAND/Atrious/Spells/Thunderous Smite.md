# Thunderous Smite

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

The next time you hit with a weapon attack before the spell ends, your weapon rings with thunder that is audible within 300 feet, dealing extra thunder damage and pushing the target 10 feet away and knocking it prone if it fails a Strength save.

**Save:** Strength
