# Whisper of Fleeting Facade

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A illusion spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
