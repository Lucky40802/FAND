# Dawn Recovery

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

A celestial spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. Up to six creatures of the caster's choice in the area regain 4d4 + the caster's spellcasting modifier hit points. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
