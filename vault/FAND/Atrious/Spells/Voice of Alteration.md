# Voice of Alteration

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A middling transmutation technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
