# Burning Hands

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A thin sheet of flame shoots from your outstretched fingertips; each creature in the cone takes fire damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success, and flammable objects not worn or carried ignite.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 1st
