# Curse of Gust

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A elemental working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
