# Mark of Charmed Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Enchantment*

An ordinary enchantment technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
