# Renewing Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A standard-issue healing working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
