# Infernal Vigor

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A plain infernal spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A willing creature gains 15 temporary hit points and advantage on Strength checks for 1 minute. When the spell ends, it takes 1d6 fire damage. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
