# Whisper of Phantom Veil

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A plain illusion spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
