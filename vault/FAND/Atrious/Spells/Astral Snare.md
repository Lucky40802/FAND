# Astral Snare

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A celestial working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
