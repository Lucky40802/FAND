# Shroud of Inscription

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

One of the more forgettable entries in runic magic, but a reliable one. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
