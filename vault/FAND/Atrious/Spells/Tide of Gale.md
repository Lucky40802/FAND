# Tide of Gale

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A elemental technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15ft in a direction of the caster's choice, falling Prone if it hits a solid surface. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 5d6 Fire damage instead of stopping short. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
