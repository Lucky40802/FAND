# Living Wrench

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A transmutation working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 15ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 5d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
