# Rune-Brand of Scorching Fang

*Cantrip — Infernal*

Considered too minor to charge for — most infernal teachers throw this in for free. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. No spell slot required — cast it as often as the moment calls for.
