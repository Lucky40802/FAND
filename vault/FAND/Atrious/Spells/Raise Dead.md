# Raise Dead

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You return a creature that has been dead no longer than 10 days to life, curing conditions caused by its death (though not other lingering harm), leaving it with 1 hit point.
