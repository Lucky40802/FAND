# Sudden Blight

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A psionics working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
