# Shroud of Light

*Cantrip — Healing*

A healing basic that survives in every curriculum precisely because it's so cheap to teach. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next Perception check. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
