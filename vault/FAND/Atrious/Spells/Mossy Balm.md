# Mossy Balm

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

An intermediate nature magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. Up to six creatures of the caster's choice in the area regain 4d4 + the caster's spellcasting modifier hit points. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
