# Mantle of Restorative Mercy

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A no-frills healing working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
