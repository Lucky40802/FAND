# Witch Bolt

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A lance of lightning strikes a creature (ranged spell attack) and forms a sustained arc between you; on your subsequent turns you can deal automatic 1d12 lightning damage as an action while the target stays within range.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d12 to the initial hit per slot level above 1st
