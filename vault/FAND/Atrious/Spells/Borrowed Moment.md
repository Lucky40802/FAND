# Borrowed Moment

[[Time]] — Wizard, Chronurgy Magic

Time hiccups around the target for an instant, letting it act as though the triggering attack hadn't happened yet — it may use its reaction to move up to 5 feet, potentially avoiding the hit entirely at the DM's discretion.
