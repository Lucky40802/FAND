# Ward of Balm

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A more demanding healing working, taught only once the basics are mastered. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
