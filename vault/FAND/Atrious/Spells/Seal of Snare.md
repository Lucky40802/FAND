# Seal of Snare

*Cantrip — Infernal*

Widely regarded as the easiest working in infernal. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
