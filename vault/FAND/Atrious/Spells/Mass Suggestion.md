# Mass Suggestion

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You suggest a reasonable-sounding course of action to up to twelve creatures that can hear and understand you; on a failed Wisdom save each pursues the suggestion until it's completed or the duration ends.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** duration extends to 10 days/30 days/1 year/permanent at 7th/8th/9th-level slots
