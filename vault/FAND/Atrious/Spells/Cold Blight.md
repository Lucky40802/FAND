# Cold Blight

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A middling necromancy technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
