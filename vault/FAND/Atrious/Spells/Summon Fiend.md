# Summon Fiend

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Infernal (School)|Infernal]] in this vault*

You call forth a fiendish spirit that takes a form of your choosing — Demonic, Devilish, or Yugoloth — fighting alongside you and obeying your commands for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** the summoned spirit gains more hit points and a stronger attack per slot level above 6th
