# Heaven's Renewal

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A celestial working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A willing creature within range regains 3d8 + the caster's spellcasting modifier hit points. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
