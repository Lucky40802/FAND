# Mark of Wicked Weakness

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

An intermediate infernal working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
