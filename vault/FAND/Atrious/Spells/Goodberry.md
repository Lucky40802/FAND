# Goodberry

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Up to ten berries appear in your hand; a creature can eat one as an action to regain 1 hit point and gain enough nourishment to sustain it for a day. Unused berries lose potency after 24 hours.
