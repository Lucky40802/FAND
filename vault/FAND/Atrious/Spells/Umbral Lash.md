# Umbral Lash

[[Dark]] — Sorcerer, Shadow Magic

A whip of solid shadow lashes out at a target the caster can see in dim light or darkness. If the target is reduced to 0 hit points by this damage, it is briefly consumed by shadow instead of falling normally.
