# Mantle of Resolute Sanctum

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A standard-issue abjuration working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
