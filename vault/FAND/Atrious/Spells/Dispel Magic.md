# Dispel Magic

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You choose one creature, object, or magical effect within range; any spell of 3rd level or lower on the target ends, and for higher-level spells you make an ability check to end them.

**At Higher Levels:** automatically ends effects of spells of level equal to the slot used or lower
