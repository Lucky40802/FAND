# Banishing Smite

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Void Magic (School)|Void Magic]] in this vault*

The next time you hit with a weapon attack before the spell ends, the weapon deals extra force damage; if this brings the target to 50 hit points or fewer, it's banished to a harmless demiplane until the spell ends.
