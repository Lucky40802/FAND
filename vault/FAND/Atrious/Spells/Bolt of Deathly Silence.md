# Bolt of Deathly Silence

*Cantrip — Necromancy*

The most basic working taught in necromancy — every apprentice learns it in their first month. It presses against a single target, dealing 2d6 Necrotic damage. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
