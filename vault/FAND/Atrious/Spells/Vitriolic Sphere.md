# Vitriolic Sphere

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You point at a location within range and a glowing green orb streaks there, exploding into a 20-foot-radius sphere of acid. Creatures in the area take acid damage on a failed Dexterity save, and take half that again at the end of their next turn from lingering acid.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +2d4 per slot level above 4th
