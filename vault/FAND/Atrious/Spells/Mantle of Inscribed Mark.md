# Mantle of Inscribed Mark

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
