# Iron Ward-Rune

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

An ordinary runic magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster inscribes a rune on a willing creature. Once in the next hour, when that creature would take damage, the damage is reduced by 5. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
