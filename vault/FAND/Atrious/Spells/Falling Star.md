# Falling Star

[[Star]] — Cleric, Light Domain

A mote of raw starlight streaks down onto a point the caster can see, exploding in a 10-foot radius. Creatures in the area make a Dexterity save for half damage; on a failed save they're also blinded until the end of their next turn.
