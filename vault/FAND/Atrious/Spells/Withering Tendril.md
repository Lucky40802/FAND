# Withering Tendril

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do. The exact ritual varies slightly from caster to caster, though the effect never does.
