# Blight

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

Necromantic energy withers a creature, dealing necrotic damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success; plant creatures and magical plants take maximum damage automatically.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 4th
