# Gale of Malleable Skin

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A middling transmutation technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
