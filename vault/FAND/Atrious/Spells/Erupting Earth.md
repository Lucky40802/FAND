# Erupting Earth

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Choose a point within range; the ground erupts in a 20-foot cube. Each creature there must make a Dexterity save, taking bludgeoning damage on a failure, and the area becomes difficult terrain.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d12 per slot level above 3rd
