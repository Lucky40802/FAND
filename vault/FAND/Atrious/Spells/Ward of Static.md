# Ward of Static

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

One of the first things any wild magic practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
