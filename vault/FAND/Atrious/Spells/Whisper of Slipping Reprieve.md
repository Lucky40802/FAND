# Whisper of Slipping Reprieve

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. No damage, no save required — the working reveals whether a creature or object within range is magical. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
