# Ward of Light

*Cantrip — Healing*

A simple healing trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
