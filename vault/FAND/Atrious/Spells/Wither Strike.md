# Wither Strike

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A step up from the common necromancy basics, and it shows in the effort required. It lashes out at everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
