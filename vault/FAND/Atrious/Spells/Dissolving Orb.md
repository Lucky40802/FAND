# Dissolving Orb

*Original — Elemental*

A glowing green orb streaks to a point you choose and bursts into a 20-foot-radius sphere of acid. Creatures there take acid damage on a failed Dexterity save, and half that again at the end of their next turn from lingering acid.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +2d4 per slot level above 4th
