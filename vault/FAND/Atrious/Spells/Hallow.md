# Hallow

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

You imbue a location up to a 60-foot radius with sanctity, warding it against celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead entering or acting freely there, and imbue it with one additional magical effect of your choice, such as courage, silence, or protection from a chosen energy type.
