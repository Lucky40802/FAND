# Grip of Guard

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it becomes Frightened of the caster until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
