# Explosive Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A middling evocation technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Lightning damage instead of stopping short. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
