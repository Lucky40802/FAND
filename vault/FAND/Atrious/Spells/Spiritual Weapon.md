# Spiritual Weapon

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A spectral weapon of your choosing appears and attacks a creature within 5 feet of it (spell attack roll); as a bonus action on later turns you can move it 20 feet and attack again.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per two slot levels above 2nd
