# Gale of Warped Change

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A common, easily-taught transmutation working. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
