# Mantle of Glowing Circle

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
