# Whisper of Overwhelming Grip

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

One of the more forgettable entries in psionics, but a reliable one. No damage, no save required — the working reveals whether a creature or object within range is magical. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
