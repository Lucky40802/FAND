# Eyebite

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

For the duration, on each of your turns you can target one creature you can see; on a failed Wisdom save it becomes asleep, panicked and fleeing, or sickened with disadvantage on attacks and ability checks, until the spell ends or it takes damage.

**Save:** Wisdom
