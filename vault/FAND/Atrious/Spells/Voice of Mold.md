# Voice of Mold

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

The most basic working taught in transmutation — every apprentice learns it in their first month. No damage, no save required — the working creates a small light or sound at a point the caster can see. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
