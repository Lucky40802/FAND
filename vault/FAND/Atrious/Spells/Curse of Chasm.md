# Curse of Chasm

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A plain void magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
