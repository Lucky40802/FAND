# Lash of Root

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for nature magic casters who've put in the hours. It cuts into everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Bludgeoning damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
