# Harm

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

A violent wave of virulent magic sweeps through a creature, dealing necrotic damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success; the target's hit point maximum is reduced by the necrotic damage taken until it receives magical healing.

**Save:** Constitution
