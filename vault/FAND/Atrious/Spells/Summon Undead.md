# Summon Undead

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You call forth an undead spirit that takes a form of your choosing — Ghostly, Putrid, or Skeletal — fighting alongside you and obeying your commands for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** the summoned spirit gains more hit points and a stronger attack per slot level above 3rd
