# Thunderwave

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A wave of thunderous force sweeps out from you; each creature in the area takes thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away on a failed Constitution save, or half damage and no push on a success. Unsecured objects are also pushed and loose windows/doors shatter.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 1st
