# Withering Pressure

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A plain psionics spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
