# Mantle of Ascendant Star

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

A step up from the common celestial basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained. Some casters add a personal flourish to the casting, though it changes nothing about the effect.
