# Stone Barrier

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

One of the more forgettable entries in abjuration, but a reliable one. A shimmering barrier surrounds up to 3 creatures of the caster's choice, granting each 12 temporary hit points. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
