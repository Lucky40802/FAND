# Grip of Wave

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

One of the more forgettable entries in psionics, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
