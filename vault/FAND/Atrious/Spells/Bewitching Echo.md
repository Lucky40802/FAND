# Bewitching Echo

*Cantrip — Enchantment*

One of the first things any enchantment practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. No damage, no save required — the working creates a small light or sound at a point the caster can see. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
