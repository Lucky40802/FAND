# Wave of Gust

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

One of the more forgettable entries in elemental, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Thunder damage instead of stopping short. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
