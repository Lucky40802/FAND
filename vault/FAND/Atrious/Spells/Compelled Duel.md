# Compelled Duel

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You attempt to compel a creature to attack only you; on a failed Wisdom save it has disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you and must make a save to move more than 30 feet from you.

**Save:** Wisdom
