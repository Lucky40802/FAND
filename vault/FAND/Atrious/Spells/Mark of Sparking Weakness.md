# Mark of Sparking Weakness

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

An intermediate elemental working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
