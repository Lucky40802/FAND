# Curse of Ward

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
