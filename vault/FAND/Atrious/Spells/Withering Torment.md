# Withering Torment

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A workaday infernal technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
