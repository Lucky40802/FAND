# Damning Sigil

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A standard-issue infernal working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
