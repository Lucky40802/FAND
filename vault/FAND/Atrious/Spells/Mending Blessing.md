# Mending Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A healing working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
