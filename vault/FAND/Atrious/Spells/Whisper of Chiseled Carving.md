# Whisper of Chiseled Carving

*Cantrip — Runic Magic*

One of the first things any runic magic practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
