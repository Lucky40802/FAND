# Chains of Silvered Circle

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
