# Cold Snare

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

An intermediate necromancy working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
