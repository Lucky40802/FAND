# Summoner's Step

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

One of the more forgettable entries in conjuration, but a reliable one. The caster and up to 3 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 90 feet. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
