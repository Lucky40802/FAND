# Chains of Binding Charm

*Tertiary-tier Enchantment*

One of the more forgettable entries in enchantment, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
