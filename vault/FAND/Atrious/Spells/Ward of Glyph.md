# Ward of Glyph

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

An ordinary runic magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
