# Curse of Silence

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A plain necromancy spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
