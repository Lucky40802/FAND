# Grave Last Breath

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A dying creature within range is stabilized and regains 2d6 hit points. Undead the caster controls within range regain the same amount. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
