# Animal Friendship

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You convince a beast that you mean it no harm; on a failed Wisdom save it is charmed by you for the duration or until you or a companion harms it.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 1st
