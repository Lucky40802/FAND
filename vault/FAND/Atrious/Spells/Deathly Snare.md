# Deathly Snare

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
