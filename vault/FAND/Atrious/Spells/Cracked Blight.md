# Cracked Blight

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A working with real teeth, reserved for elemental casters who've put in the hours. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
