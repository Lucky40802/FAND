# Steadfast Blessing

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

The entry point into abjuration for most casters. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Rarely impressive, but never fails when it's actually needed.
