# Charred Blight

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A no-frills infernal working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
