# Withering Burst

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
