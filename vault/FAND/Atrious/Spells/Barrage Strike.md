# Barrage Strike

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

An intermediate evocation working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It lashes out at everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Fire damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
