# Ray of Frost

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A frigid beam streaks toward a creature (ranged spell attack); on a hit it takes cold damage and its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**At Higher Levels:** scales to 2d8/3d8/4d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
