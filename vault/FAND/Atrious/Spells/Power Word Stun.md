# Power Word Stun

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You speak a word of power at a creature you can see; if it has 150 hit points or fewer, it's instantly stunned with no saving throw, and can attempt a Constitution save at the end of each of its turns to end the effect.
