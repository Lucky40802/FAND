# Lash of Detonation

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A evocation spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Fire damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
