# Random Blight

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A no-frills wild magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
