# Cordon of Arrows

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You plant up to four pieces of ammunition in the ground; when a creature you didn't designate comes within 30 feet, an arrow fires at it, dealing piercing damage on a failed Dexterity save.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1 arrow per slot level above 2nd
