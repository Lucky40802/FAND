# Animate Objects

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Up to ten nonmagical objects of your choice within range animate and become your loyal minions for the duration, fighting as you direct with your bonus action.

**At Higher Levels:** animate 2 more objects per slot level above 5th
