# Antipathy/Sympathy

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You imbue an object or location with a magical aura that repels (antipathy) or attracts (sympathy) creatures of a kind you specify; affected creatures that fail a Wisdom save are compelled to avoid or approach the target for the duration.

**Save:** Wisdom
