# Bound Echo

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
