# Ward of Second

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

One of the more forgettable entries in chronomancy, but a reliable one. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
