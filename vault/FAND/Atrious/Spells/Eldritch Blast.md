# Eldritch Blast

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Void Magic (School)|Void Magic]] in this vault*

A beam of crackling energy streaks toward a creature in range (ranged spell attack), dealing force damage on a hit. At higher levels you fire more beams simultaneously, each targeting one creature or the same one.

**At Higher Levels:** gains an additional beam at 5th/11th/17th level
