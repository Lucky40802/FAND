# Uncontrolled Snare

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A workaday wild magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
