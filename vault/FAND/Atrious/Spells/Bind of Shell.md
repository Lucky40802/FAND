# Bind of Shell

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A workaday abjuration technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
