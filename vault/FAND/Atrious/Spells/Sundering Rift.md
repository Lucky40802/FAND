# Sundering Rift

[[Dimension]] — Warlock, The Great Old One

Tears open a brief fold of extradimensional space beneath a target. It must succeed on a Strength save or be pulled through and ejected in a random location within 1 mile, taking 3d6 force damage from the crossing.
