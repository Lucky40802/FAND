# Curse of Hollow

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A plain shadow magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
