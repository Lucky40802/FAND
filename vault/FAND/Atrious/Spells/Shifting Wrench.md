# Shifting Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A workaday transmutation technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
