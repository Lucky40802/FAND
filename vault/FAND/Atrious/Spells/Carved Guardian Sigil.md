# Carved Guardian Sigil

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster inscribes a rune on a willing creature. Once in the next hour, when that creature would take damage, the damage is reduced by 25. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
