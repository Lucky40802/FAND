# Hunger of Hadar

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A 20-foot-radius sphere of void-black darkness and grasping, clawing tendrils appears; creatures inside are blinded, and any creature that starts its turn there takes cold damage and is affected by an additional random horror effect.
