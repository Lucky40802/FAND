# Mental Blight

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A middling psionics technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
