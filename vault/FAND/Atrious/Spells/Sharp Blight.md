# Sharp Blight

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

A step up from the common psionics basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
