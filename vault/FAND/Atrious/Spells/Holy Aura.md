# Holy Aura

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Divine light flares around you; friendly creatures in the radius gain advantage on saving throws, attackers against them have disadvantage, and when a hostile creature hits one of them, it can be blinded until the spell ends.
