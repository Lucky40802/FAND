# Grip of Tendril

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

A step up from the common shadow magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
