# Ashen Blight

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A more demanding necromancy working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
