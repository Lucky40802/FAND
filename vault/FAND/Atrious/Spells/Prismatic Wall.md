# Prismatic Wall

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

A wall of shimmering, multicolored light up to 90 feet long and 30 feet high appears, made of seven layers, each a different color with its own damaging or blocking property; passing through destroys layers in order, and the wall blocks teleportation and most divination through it.

**Save:** Dexterity or Constitution (varies by layer)
