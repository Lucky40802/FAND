# Spell Index

Three layers live side by side in each school below (each is one Obsidian Base / database view, filterable by everything including the `tags:` tier marker):

1. **Original content** — hand-written or generated from my own word banks and templates, not sourced from the Atrious archive's generated volumes. Tagged `cantrip`/`tertiary`/`secondary`/`primary` by tier.
2. **Real D&D 5e spells (SRD)** — the official open-licensed spell list, remapped from D&D's 8 official schools into whichever of this vault's 20 schools actually fits the spell's theme. Marked `tier: "SRD"` and tagged `srd`. Two sub-batches:
   - **SRD 5.1 core** (350 spells) — the 2014-era open list: Fireball, Magic Missile, Cure Wounds, and the rest.
   - **SRD 5.2 additions** (35 spells) — the batch WotC folded into the open license in 2024, including former Xanathar's/Tasha's-exclusive favorites (Booming Blade, Absorb Elements, the Summon X spells, etc.).
3. **DLC-inspired originals** (25 spells) — mechanical concepts drawn from well-known non-SRD supplement spells (Xanathar's, Tasha's, Fizban's), but with original names, flavor, and reworded effects rather than WotC's actual text — the same treatment used for the vault-wide named-spell cleanup. Marked `tier: "Original"`, tagged by tier like the rest of the original content. These are *not* SRD-licensed content since nothing verbatim was copied; they're original spells that happen to share a mechanical idea with a real one.

> **Attribution:** This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.1 and 5.2 ("SRD") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document, licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode). Descriptions are paraphrased, not verbatim, and spells are reclassified into this vault's own school system. The DLC-inspired originals (category 3 above) are separate, original works — no WotC text or names were copied for those.

**Pyramid structure per school (original content only):** Cantrip (unlimited-use, no slot, ~15 per school) → Tertiary (common, levels 1-3, ~40-60 per school) → Secondary (levels 4-6, ~25 per school) → Primary (rare, levels 7-9, ~10 per school, each tied to one specific named Rune and requiring a genuine Primary-tier acquisition per [[Class]]).
