# Locate Creature

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

You sense the direction to a familiar creature or a specific kind of creature within 1,000 feet, provided it isn't behind running water or lead, and the direction updates as you move.
