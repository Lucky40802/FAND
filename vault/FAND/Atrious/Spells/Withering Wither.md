# Withering Wither

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

One of the more demanding entries in necromancy's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
