# Mantle of Revealed Vision

*Secondary-tier Divination*

A divination technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
