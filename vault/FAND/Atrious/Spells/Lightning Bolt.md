# Lightning Bolt

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A stroke of lightning forming a 100-foot line, 5 feet wide, blasts out from you; each creature in the line takes lightning damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success, and flammable objects there ignite.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 3rd
