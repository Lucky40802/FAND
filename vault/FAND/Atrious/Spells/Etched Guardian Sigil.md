# Etched Guardian Sigil

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

One of the more forgettable entries in runic magic, but a reliable one. The caster inscribes a rune on a willing creature. Once in the next hour, when that creature would take damage, the damage is reduced by 15. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
