# Whisper of Flourishing Bloom

*Secondary-tier Nature Magic*

A step up from the common nature magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained. Some casters add a personal flourish to the casting, though it changes nothing about the effect.
