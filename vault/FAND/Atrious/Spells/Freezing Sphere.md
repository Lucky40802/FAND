# Freezing Sphere

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A frozen orb flies to a point you choose and explodes into a 60-foot-radius sphere of freezing cold; creatures there take cold damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success, and any body of water in the area can freeze over.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 6th
