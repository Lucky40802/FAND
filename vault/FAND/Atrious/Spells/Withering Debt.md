# Withering Debt

*Cantrip — Infernal*

A simple infernal trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
