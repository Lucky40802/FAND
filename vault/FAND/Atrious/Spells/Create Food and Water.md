# Create Food and Water

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You create 45 pounds of food and 30 gallons of water on the ground within range, enough to sustain 15 humanoids or 5 steeds for a day.
