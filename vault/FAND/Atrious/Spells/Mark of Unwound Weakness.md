# Mark of Unwound Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

An ordinary chronomancy technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
