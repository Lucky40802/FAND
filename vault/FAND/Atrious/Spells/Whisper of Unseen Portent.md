# Whisper of Unseen Portent

*Tertiary-tier Divination*

A standard-issue divination working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
