# Ward of Shell

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A plain abjuration spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
