# Hallowed Snare

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A celestial working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
