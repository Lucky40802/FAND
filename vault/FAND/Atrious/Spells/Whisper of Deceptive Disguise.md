# Whisper of Deceptive Disguise

*Cantrip — Illusion*

A illusion exercise more than a spell, the sort drilled into muscle memory early. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
