# Mantle of Looping Interval

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
