# Mark of Drifting Weakness

*Cantrip — Chronomancy*

A chronomancy staple, taught in every proper school and most improper ones. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
