# Arcane Gate

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You create a pair of linked circular portals up to 10 feet apart, 5 feet in diameter; anything entering one portal instantly exits the other, facing the chosen direction.
