# Whisper of Hollow Shade

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A more demanding illusion working, taught only once the basics are mastered. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
