# Rooted Snare

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A standard-issue nature magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
