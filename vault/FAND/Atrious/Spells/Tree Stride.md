# Tree Stride

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You gain the ability to enter a living tree within 5 feet and step out of a second living tree of the same species within 500 feet that you've seen before, using up to one such transit per turn for the duration.
