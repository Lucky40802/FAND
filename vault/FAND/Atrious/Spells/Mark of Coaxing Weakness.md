# Mark of Coaxing Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Enchantment*

A workaday enchantment technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
