# Mantle of Cleansing Solace

*Secondary-tier Healing*

One of the more demanding entries in healing's middle tier. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
