# Levitate

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

One creature or object of your choice rises vertically up to 20 feet and remains suspended there for the duration; an unwilling creature can negate the effect with a successful Constitution save.

**Save:** Constitution
