# Command

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You speak a one-word command (such as drop, flee, halt, grovel, or approach) at a creature you can see; on a failed Wisdom save it obeys on its next turn.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** affect one additional creature per slot level above 1st
