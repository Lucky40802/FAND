# Curse of Husk

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A step up from the common necromancy basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
