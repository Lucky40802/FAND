# Ward of Question

*Cantrip — Divination*

A rite so basic to divination that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
