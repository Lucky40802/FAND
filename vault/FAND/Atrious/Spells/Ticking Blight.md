# Ticking Blight

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
