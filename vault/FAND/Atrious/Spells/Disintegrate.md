# Disintegrate

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Void Magic (School)|Void Magic]] in this vault*

A thin green ray hits a target on a failed Dexterity save, taking force damage; if this reduces it to 0 hit points, its body is disintegrated into fine gray dust, leaving only equipment behind.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +3d6 per slot level above 6th
