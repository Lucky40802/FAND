# Curse of Dark

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A common, easily-taught shadow magic working. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
