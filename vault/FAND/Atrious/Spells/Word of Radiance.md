# Word of Radiance

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Divine energy radiates from you. Each creature of your choice within 5 feet that you can see must succeed on a Constitution save or take radiant damage.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
