# Sleet Storm

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

Freezing rain and sleet fall in a 40-foot-radius, 20-foot-tall cylinder, heavily obscuring the area, extinguishing open flames, and making the ground difficult terrain that can cause creatures to fall prone.

**Save:** Dexterity
