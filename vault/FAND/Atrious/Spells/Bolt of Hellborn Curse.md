# Bolt of Hellborn Curse

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

One of the more demanding entries in infernal's middle tier. It sweeps across everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
