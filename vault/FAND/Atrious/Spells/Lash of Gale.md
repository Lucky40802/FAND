# Lash of Gale

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

One of the more demanding entries in elemental's middle tier. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Cold damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
