# Sunburst

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Brilliant sunlight flashes in a 60-foot-radius sphere; creatures there take radiant damage and are blinded for a minute on a failed Constitution save, half damage and no blindness on a success, and the light dispels magical darkness in the area.

**Save:** Constitution
