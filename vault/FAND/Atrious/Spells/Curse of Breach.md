# Curse of Breach

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in void magic's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it. Regional variants exist, though none differ mechanically from this version.
