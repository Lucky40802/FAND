# Wind Walk

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

Up to ten willing creatures transform into a gaseous form for the duration, immune to nonmagical damage, with a flying speed of 300 feet; they can revert to normal form as a bonus action.
