# Red Blood Gift

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A more demanding blood magic working, taught only once the basics are mastered. The caster spends 6d4 of their own hit points to restore twice that many hit points to a willing creature they touch. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
