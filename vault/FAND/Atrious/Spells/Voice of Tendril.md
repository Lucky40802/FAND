# Voice of Tendril

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A middling shadow magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster read any language for 1 minute. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
