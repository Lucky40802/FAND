# Private Sanctum

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You make an area up to a 100-foot cube warded against scrying and other means of observation, choosing among effects that block sound, sight, teleportation, and astral/ethereal travel into it.

**At Higher Levels:** cast at 9th level to make it permanent
