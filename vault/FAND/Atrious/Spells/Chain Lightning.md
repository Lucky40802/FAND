# Chain Lightning

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You launch a bolt of lightning toward a target, which then arcs to up to three other creatures within 30 feet of the first; each takes lightning damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 6th
