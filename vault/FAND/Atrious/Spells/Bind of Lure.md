# Bind of Lure

*Secondary-tier Enchantment*

A enchantment spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
