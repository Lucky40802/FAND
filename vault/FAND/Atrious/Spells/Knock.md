# Knock

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You choose a lock, mundane or magical, within range; it unlocks, or a stuck/barred door or lid frees itself, with a loud knock audible from 300 feet.
