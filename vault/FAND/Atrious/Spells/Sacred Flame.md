# Sacred Flame

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Flame-like radiance descends on a creature you can see, which takes radiant damage on a failed Dexterity save; the target gains no benefit from cover for this save.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** scales to 2d8/3d8/4d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
