# Absorbing Ward

*Original — Abjuration*

Cast the instant you take acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage: you halve that damage against yourself, and your next melee attack before the end of your next turn deals an extra 1d6 of that same damage type.

**At Higher Levels:** extra 1d6 to the following melee attack per slot level above 1st
