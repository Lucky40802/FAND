# Ward of Remedy

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A healing spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do. The exact ritual varies slightly from caster to caster, though the effect never does.
