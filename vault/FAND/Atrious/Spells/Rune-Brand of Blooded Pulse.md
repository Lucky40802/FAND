# Rune-Brand of Blooded Pulse

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. Requires 10 minutes to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 2d8 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
