# Mark of Untamed Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

An ordinary wild magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
