# Bind of Shroud

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
