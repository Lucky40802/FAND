# Sunder Wall

*Original — Void Magic*

A thin green ray lances toward a creature or object. On a failed Dexterity save, it takes massive force damage; if reduced to 0 hit points this way, it disintegrates into fine dust that can only be restored by powerful magic.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +3d6 per slot level above 6th
