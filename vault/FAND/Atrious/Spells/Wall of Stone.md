# Wall of Stone

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A nonmagical wall of solid stone springs into existence, up to ten contiguous 10-foot-square panels, which can be shaped into battlements, ramps, or arches within range.
