# Wind Wall

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A wall of strong, gusting wind rises in a line up to 50 feet long; it deflects small nonmagical projectiles, halves the damage from and disperses gas or fog, and can knock down flying creatures that fail a Strength save.

**Save:** Strength
