# Heaven's Purification

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

An ordinary celestial technique, unremarkable but dependable. A willing creature within range is cured of one disease or one condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned) and regains 1d6 hit points. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
