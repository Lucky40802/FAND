# Withering Wing

*Cantrip — Celestial*

One of the first things any celestial practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
