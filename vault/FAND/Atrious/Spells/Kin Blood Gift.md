# Kin Blood Gift

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A step up from the common blood magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster spends 6d4 of their own hit points to restore twice that many hit points to a willing creature they touch. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
