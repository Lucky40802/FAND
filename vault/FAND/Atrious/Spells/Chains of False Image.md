# Chains of False Image

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A workaday illusion technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
