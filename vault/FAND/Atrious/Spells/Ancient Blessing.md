# Ancient Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A no-frills runic magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
