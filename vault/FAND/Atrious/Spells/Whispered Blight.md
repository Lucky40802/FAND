# Whispered Blight

*Cantrip — Enchantment*

The most basic working taught in enchantment — every apprentice learns it in their first month. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
