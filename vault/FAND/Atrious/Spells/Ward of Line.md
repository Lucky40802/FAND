# Ward of Line

*Cantrip — Runic Magic*

A runic magic exercise more than a spell, the sort drilled into muscle memory early. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
