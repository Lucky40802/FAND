# Sight of Truth

*Secondary-tier Divination*

A divination spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
