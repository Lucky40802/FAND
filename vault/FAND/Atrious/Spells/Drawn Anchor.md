# Drawn Anchor

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A more demanding conjuration working, taught only once the basics are mastered. It presses against everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
