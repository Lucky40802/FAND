# Bind of Tangle

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
