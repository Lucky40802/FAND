# Bind of Wall

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

A abjuration fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. It never scales, but it never needs to.
