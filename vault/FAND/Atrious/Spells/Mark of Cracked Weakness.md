# Mark of Cracked Weakness

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A working with real teeth, reserved for elemental casters who've put in the hours. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
