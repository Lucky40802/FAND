# Curse of Rot

*Cantrip — Necromancy*

One of the first things any necromancy practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
