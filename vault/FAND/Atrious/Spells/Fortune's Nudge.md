# Fortune's Nudge

*Original — Enchantment*

Cast when a creature you can see succeeds on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw: force it to reroll, using the lower result. You may then grant advantage on the next attack roll, check, or save made by a different creature you choose before the end of your next turn.
