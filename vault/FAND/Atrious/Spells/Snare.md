# Snare

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You create a magical, near-invisible tripwire trap in a space you touch. A creature that triggers it must succeed on a Dexterity save or be hoisted into the air, hanging upside down.

**Save:** Dexterity
