# Withering Chorus

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A plain celestial spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
