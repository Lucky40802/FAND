# Remove Curse

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You touch a creature and end all curses affecting it, or if the touched object is a cursed magic item, the curse remains but you break its attunement to it.
