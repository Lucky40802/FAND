# Fog Cloud

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

You create a 20-foot-radius sphere of fog that heavily obscures its area for the duration, or until dispersed by a moderate wind.

**At Higher Levels:** radius increases by 20 feet per slot level above 1st
