# Arcane Sword

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A sword-shaped plane of shimmering force appears and hovers near a creature of your choice; as a bonus action each turn you can move it up to 20 feet and make a melee spell attack against a creature within 5 feet of it.
