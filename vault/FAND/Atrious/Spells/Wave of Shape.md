# Wave of Shape

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A standard-issue transmutation working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
