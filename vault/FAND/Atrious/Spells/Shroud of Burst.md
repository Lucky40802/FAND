# Shroud of Burst

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A common, easily-taught wild magic working. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
