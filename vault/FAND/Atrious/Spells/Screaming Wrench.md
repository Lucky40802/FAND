# Screaming Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A plain evocation spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Lightning damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
