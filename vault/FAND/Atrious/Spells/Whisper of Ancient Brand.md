# Whisper of Ancient Brand

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A middling runic magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. No damage, no save required — the working reveals whether a creature or object within range is magical. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
