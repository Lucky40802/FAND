# Whisper of Vigilant Threshold

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A middling abjuration technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
