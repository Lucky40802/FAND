# Irresistible Dance

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

A creature you can see is compelled to dance a foolish jig; it has disadvantage on Dexterity saves and attack rolls against it have advantage, while its own attack rolls have disadvantage, until it succeeds a Wisdom save at the end of one of its turns.

**Save:** Wisdom
