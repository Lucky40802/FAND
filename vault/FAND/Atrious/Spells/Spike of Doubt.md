# Spike of Doubt

*Original — Psionics*

A disorienting spike of psychic energy drives into a creature's mind. On a failed Intelligence save it takes psychic damage and subtracts 1d4 from the next save it makes before the end of your next turn.

**Save:** Intelligence

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
