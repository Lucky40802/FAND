# Fluxing Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A plain transmutation spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
