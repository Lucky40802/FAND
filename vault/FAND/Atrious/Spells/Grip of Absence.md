# Grip of Absence

*Cantrip — Void Magic*

A void magic fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
