# Tide of Vessel

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A working with real teeth, reserved for transmutation casters who've put in the hours. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 15ft in a direction of the caster's choice, falling Prone if it hits a solid surface. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 7d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
