# Wild Harvest

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A plain nature magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. Plants within a 30-foot radius bloom and bear fruit. Up to 2 creatures who eat a fruit within the next hour regain 1d4 hit points and are cured of one poison. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
