# Mantle of Tender Recovery

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A plain healing spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
