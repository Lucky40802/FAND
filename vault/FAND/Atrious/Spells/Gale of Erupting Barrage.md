# Gale of Erupting Barrage

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A evocation working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Cold damage instead of stopping short. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
