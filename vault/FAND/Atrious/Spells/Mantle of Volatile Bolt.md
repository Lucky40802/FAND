# Mantle of Volatile Bolt

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A middling wild magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
