# Mark of Ashen Weakness

*Cantrip — Necromancy*

Considered too minor to charge for — most necromancy teachers throw this in for free. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. No spell slot required — cast it as often as the moment calls for.
