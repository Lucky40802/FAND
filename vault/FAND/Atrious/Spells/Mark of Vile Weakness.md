# Mark of Vile Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A no-frills infernal working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
