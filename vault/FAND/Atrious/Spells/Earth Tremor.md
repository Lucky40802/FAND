# Earth Tremor

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

You cause a tremor in the ground within range. Each creature other than you in that area must make a Strength save, taking bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure.

**Save:** Strength

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 1st
