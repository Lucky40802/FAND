# Mark of Nightbound Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
