# Bind of Gap

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
