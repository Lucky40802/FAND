# Roaring Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A evocation working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Cold damage instead of stopping short. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
