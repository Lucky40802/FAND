# Grasping Vine

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

A grasping vine sprouts and pulls a creature within range 20 feet toward you, or as close as terrain allows, on a failed Dexterity save.

**Save:** Dexterity
