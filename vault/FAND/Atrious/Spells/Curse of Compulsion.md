# Curse of Compulsion

*Secondary-tier Enchantment*

A enchantment technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
