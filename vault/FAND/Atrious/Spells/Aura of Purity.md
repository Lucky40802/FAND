# Aura of Purity

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

Purifying energy surrounds you; allies in the aura can't become diseased, have resistance to poison damage, and have advantage on saves against being charmed, frightened, or poisoned.
