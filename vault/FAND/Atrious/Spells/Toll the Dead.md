# Toll the Dead

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You point at a creature and a sound like a tolling bell rings. On a failed Wisdom save it takes necrotic damage, larger if it's already missing hit points.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** scales up by one damage die at 5th/11th/17th level
