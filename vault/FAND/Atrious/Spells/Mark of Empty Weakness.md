# Mark of Empty Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
