# Power Word Heal

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A creature you touch regains all of its hit points, and is freed from being charmed, frightened, paralyzed, or stunned; if it's prone it can stand as part of this spell without spending movement.
