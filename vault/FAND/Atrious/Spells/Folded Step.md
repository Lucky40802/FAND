# Folded Step

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

One of the more demanding entries in conjuration's middle tier. The caster and up to 4 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 120 feet. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
