# Instant Summons

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You enchant an object of 10 pounds or less that isn't held or carried by anyone else; at any later time, speaking the command word teleports the item into your hand from wherever it is.
