# Chill Touch

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

A ghostly skeletal hand strikes a creature in range (ranged spell attack). On a hit it takes necrotic damage and can't regain hit points until your next turn starts; if the target is undead it also has disadvantage on attacks against you until then.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
