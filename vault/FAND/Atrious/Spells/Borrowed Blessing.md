# Borrowed Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A no-frills chronomancy working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
