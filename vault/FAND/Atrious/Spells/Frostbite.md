# Frostbite

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Numbing frost lances toward a creature. On a failed Constitution save it takes cold damage and has disadvantage on its next weapon attack roll before the end of its next turn.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
