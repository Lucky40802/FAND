# Bind of Shade

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A working with real teeth, reserved for illusion casters who've put in the hours. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it becomes Frightened of the caster until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
