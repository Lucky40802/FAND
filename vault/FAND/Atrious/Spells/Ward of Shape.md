# Ward of Shape

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

An ordinary transmutation technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
