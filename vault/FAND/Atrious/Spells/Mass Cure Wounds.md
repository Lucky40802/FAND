# Mass Cure Wounds

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

Up to six creatures of your choice in a 30-foot-radius sphere regain hit points equal to 3d8 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 healing per slot level above 5th
