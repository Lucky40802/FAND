# Absolute Blight

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A standard-issue void magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
