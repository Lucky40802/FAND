# Mantle of Girded Barrier

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A plain abjuration spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
