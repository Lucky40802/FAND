# Charred Sigil

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A plain infernal spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
