# Untamed Echo

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A nature magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
