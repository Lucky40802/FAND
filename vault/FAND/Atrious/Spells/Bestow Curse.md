# Bestow Curse

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You touch a creature, which must succeed a Wisdom save or be cursed for the duration with an effect of your choice: disadvantage on ability checks/attacks with a chosen ability, disadvantage on saves or attacks against you, forced Wisdom saves each turn or waste its action, or your attacks deal an extra 1d8 necrotic damage to it.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** if the necrotic-damage option is chosen, +1d8 per slot level above 3rd
