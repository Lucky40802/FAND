# Grip of Aegis

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A more demanding abjuration working, taught only once the basics are mastered. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
