# Void Float

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A no-frills void magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A willing creature becomes weightless for 1 rounds: it can float, fly at its walking speed, and pass through nonmagical objects. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
