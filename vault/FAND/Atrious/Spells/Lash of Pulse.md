# Lash of Pulse

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

An intermediate blood magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It cuts into everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
