# Marked Marking

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for blood magic casters who've put in the hours. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 2d6 damage. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
