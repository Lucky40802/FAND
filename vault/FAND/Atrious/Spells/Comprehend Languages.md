# Comprehend Languages

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

For the duration you understand the literal meaning of any spoken language you hear and any written language you touch, though you don't gain the ability to speak unknown languages.
