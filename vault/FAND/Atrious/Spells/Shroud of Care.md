# Shroud of Care

*Cantrip — Healing*

One of the first things any healing practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next Perception check. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
