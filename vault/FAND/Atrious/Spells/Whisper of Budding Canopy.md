# Whisper of Budding Canopy

*Cantrip — Nature Magic*

Widely regarded as the easiest working in nature magic. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
