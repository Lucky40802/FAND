# Voice of Seal

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in runic magic's middle tier. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
