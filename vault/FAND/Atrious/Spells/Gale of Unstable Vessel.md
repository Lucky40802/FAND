# Gale of Unstable Vessel

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

The entry point into transmutation for most casters. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Rarely impressive, but never fails when it's actually needed.
