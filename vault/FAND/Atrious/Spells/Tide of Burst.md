# Tide of Burst

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A common, easily-taught evocation working. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Cold damage instead of stopping short. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
