# Forbiddance

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

You ward an area of up to 40,000 square feet against teleportation and planar travel into it, and can bar entry to a specified creature type, which takes damage each time it enters.
