# Awaken

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You touch a beast or plant with an Intelligence of 3 or less, granting it Intelligence 10 and the ability to speak a language you know; it becomes friendly toward you unless treated poorly.
