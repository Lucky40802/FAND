# Mantle of Sacred Ray

*Cantrip — Celestial*

A celestial fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
