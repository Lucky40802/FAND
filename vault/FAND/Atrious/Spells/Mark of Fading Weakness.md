# Mark of Fading Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A plain shadow magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
