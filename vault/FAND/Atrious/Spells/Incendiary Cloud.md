# Incendiary Cloud

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A 20-foot-radius sphere of roiling smoke shot through with white-hot embers appears; each creature there takes fire damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success, and you can move the cloud 60 feet each round as a bonus action.

**Save:** Dexterity
