# Shroud of Pulse

*Secondary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
