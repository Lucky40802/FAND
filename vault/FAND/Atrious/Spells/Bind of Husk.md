# Bind of Husk

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it becomes Frightened of the caster until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
