# Imprisonment

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You bind a creature you can see into an inescapable prison, choosing among burial, chaining, hedged confinement, minuscule containment, or magical slumber; on a failed Wisdom save the creature is imprisoned until the spell is dispelled or the hidden component destroyed.

**Save:** Wisdom
