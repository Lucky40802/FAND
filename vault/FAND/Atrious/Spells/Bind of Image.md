# Bind of Image

*Cantrip — Illusion*

The entry point into illusion for most casters. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it can't move more than 5ft on its next turn. Rarely impressive, but never fails when it's actually needed.
