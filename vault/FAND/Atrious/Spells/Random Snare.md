# Random Snare

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A step up from the common wild magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
