# Bind of Thicket

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A middling nature magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
