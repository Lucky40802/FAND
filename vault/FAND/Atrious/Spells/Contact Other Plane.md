# Contact Other Plane

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

You mentally contact a demigod, spirit, or other extraplanar entity to ask up to five questions; on a failed Intelligence save you take psychic damage and are stunned, and answers may be cryptic, misleading, or unintelligible.

**Save:** Intelligence
