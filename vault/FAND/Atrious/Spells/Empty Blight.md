# Empty Blight

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A no-frills void magic working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
