# Borrowed Blight

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A middling chronomancy technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
