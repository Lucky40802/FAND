# Seal of Scar

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in blood magic's middle tier. Requires 10 minutes to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 2d8 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
