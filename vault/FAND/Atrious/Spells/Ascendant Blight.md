# Ascendant Blight

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A no-frills celestial working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
