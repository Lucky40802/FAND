# Dream

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

You or a willing companion enters the dreams of a creature you both know, delivering a message or, if you choose to appear as a nightmare instead, dealing psychic damage and imposing exhaustion.
