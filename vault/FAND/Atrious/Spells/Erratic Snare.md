# Erratic Snare

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

A wild magic fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it can't move more than 5ft on its next turn. It never scales, but it never needs to.
