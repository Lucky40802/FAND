# Voice of Echo

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

An intermediate psionics working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
