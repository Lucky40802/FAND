# Mark of Slipping Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A standard-issue chronomancy working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
