# Mantle of Restorative Recovery

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A middling healing technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
