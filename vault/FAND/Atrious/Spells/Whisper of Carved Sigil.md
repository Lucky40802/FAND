# Whisper of Carved Sigil

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A plain runic magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. No damage, no save required — the working reveals whether a creature or object within range is magical. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
