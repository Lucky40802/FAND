# Nondetection

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

For the duration, you hide a target creature, object, or location from divination magic; it can't be targeted by scrying or similar detection effects.
