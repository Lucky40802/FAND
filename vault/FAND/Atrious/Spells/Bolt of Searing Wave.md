# Bolt of Searing Wave

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A more demanding evocation working, taught only once the basics are mastered. It bears down on everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
