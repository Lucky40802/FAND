# Whisper of Living Skin

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A transmutation spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do. Regional variants exist, though none differ mechanically from this version.
