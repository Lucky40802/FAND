# Chains of Fractured Shove

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

An ordinary psionics technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
