# Power Word Kill

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You speak a word of power at a creature you can see; if it has 100 hit points or fewer, it dies instantly with no saving throw.
