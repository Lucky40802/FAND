# Faerie Fire

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

Objects and creatures in a 20-foot cube are outlined in light on a failed Dexterity save; they can't benefit from being invisible, and any attack roll against an outlined creature has advantage.

**Save:** Dexterity
