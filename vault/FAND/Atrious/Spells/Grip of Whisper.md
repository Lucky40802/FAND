# Grip of Whisper

*Tertiary-tier Enchantment*

A enchantment working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
