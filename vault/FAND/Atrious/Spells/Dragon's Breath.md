# Dragon's Breath

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You touch a willing creature and choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison. Once before the spell ends, the target can use an action to exhale energy of that type in a 15-foot cone, each creature there making a Dexterity save.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 2nd
