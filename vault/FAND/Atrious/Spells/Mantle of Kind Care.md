# Mantle of Kind Care

*Secondary-tier Healing*

One of the more demanding entries in healing's middle tier. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it. Regional variants exist, though none differ mechanically from this version.
