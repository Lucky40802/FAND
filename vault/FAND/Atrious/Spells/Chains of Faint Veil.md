# Chains of Faint Veil

*Cantrip — Illusion*

A illusion basic that survives in every curriculum precisely because it's so cheap to teach. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
