# Uncontrolled Sigil

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

A wild magic staple, taught in every proper school and most improper ones. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
