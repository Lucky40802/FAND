# Chains of Vigilant Screen

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A no-frills abjuration working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
