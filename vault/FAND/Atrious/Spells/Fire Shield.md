# Fire Shield

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Wispy flames wreathe your body, shedding light and granting resistance to cold or fire damage (your choice); any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes fire or cold damage in return.
