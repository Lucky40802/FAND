# Grip of Chorus

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

An ordinary celestial technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
