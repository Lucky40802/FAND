# Sight of Sigil

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A plain runic magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
