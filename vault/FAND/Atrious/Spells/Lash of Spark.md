# Lash of Spark

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Poison damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
