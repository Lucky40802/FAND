# Swift Quiver

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You touch a quiver, which endlessly reproduces nonmagical ammunition of the type it contains; when you take the Attack action you can make two additional attacks with a weapon that uses that ammunition, as a bonus action.
