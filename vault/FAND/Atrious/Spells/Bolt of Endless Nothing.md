# Bolt of Endless Nothing

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

An intermediate void magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It strikes everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
