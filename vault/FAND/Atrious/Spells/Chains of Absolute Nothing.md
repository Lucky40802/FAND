# Chains of Absolute Nothing

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A standard-issue void magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
