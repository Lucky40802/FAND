# Mark of Unseen Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

An ordinary psionics technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
