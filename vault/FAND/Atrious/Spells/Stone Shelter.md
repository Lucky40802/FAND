# Stone Shelter

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A workaday abjuration technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A willing creature within range gains 5 temporary hit points and resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 hour. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
