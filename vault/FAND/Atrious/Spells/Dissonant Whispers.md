# Dissonant Whispers

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You whisper a discordant melody only one creature can hear; on a failed Wisdom save it takes psychic damage and must use its reaction to move away from you if it can.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 1st
