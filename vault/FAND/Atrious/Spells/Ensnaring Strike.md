# Ensnaring Strike

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

The next weapon attack you make before the spell ends causes grasping vines to erupt from the target on a hit, restraining it unless it succeeds on a Strength save.

**Save:** Strength

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 1st
