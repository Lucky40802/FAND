# Voice of Guise

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

One of the more demanding entries in illusion's middle tier. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
