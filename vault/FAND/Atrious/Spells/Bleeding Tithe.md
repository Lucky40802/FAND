# Bleeding Tithe

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 1d6 damage. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
