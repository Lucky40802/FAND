# Grip of Veil

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

An ordinary illusion technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
