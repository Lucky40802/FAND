# Wrathful Smite

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

The next time you hit with a weapon attack before the spell ends, your weapon crackles with dark power, dealing extra psychic damage and frightening the target unless it succeeds a Wisdom save.

**Save:** Wisdom
