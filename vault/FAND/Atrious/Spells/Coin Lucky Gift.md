# Coin Lucky Gift

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in wild magic's middle tier. The caster rolls a d6 for a willing creature within range: on 1–2 it regains 4d8 hit points; on 3–4 it gains 20 temporary hit points; on 5–6 it has advantage on all d20 rolls for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
