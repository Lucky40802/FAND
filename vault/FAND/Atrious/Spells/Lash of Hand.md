# Lash of Hand

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

An intermediate necromancy working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It cuts into everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Necrotic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
