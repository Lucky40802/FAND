# Portal Step

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

A middling conjuration technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster and up to 2 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 60 feet. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
