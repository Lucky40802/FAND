# Plant Growth

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You choose either to overgrow a 100-foot-radius area with vines and undergrowth, making it difficult terrain for the next hour, or to enrich a half-mile-radius circle of land, doubling the yield of plants grown there over the next year.
