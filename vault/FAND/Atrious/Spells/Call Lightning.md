# Call Lightning

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A storm cloud forms overhead; on each of your turns you can call down a bolt of lightning to strike a point you choose within range, dealing lightning damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 per slot level above 3rd
