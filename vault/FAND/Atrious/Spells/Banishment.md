# Banishment

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You attempt to send a creature to a harmless demiplane on a failed Charisma save; if the target is native to another plane, this banishment becomes permanent if the spell lasts its full duration.

**Save:** Charisma

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 4th
