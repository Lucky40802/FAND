# Wave of Crag

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A standard-issue elemental working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Acid damage instead of stopping short. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
