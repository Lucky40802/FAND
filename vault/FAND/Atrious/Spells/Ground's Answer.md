# Ground's Answer

*Original — Evocation*

You cause the ground around you to buck and heave. Each creature other than you in the area makes a Strength save, taking bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure.

**Save:** Strength

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 1st
