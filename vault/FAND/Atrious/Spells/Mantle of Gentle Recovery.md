# Mantle of Gentle Recovery

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

An ordinary healing technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
