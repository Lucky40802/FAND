# Nurturing Blessing

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A healing spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
