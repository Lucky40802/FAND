# Sealed Etching

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in runic magic's middle tier. It presses against everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Lightning damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
