# Seal of Ward

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. Requires 10 minutes to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 2d8 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
