# Bolt of Untamed Frenzy

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A more demanding wild magic working, taught only once the basics are mastered. It presses against everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Psychic damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
