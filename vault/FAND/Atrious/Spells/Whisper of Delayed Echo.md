# Whisper of Delayed Echo

*Cantrip — Chronomancy*

Considered too minor to charge for — most chronomancy teachers throw this in for free. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster and one willing creature exchange a single word silently. No spell slot required — cast it as often as the moment calls for.
