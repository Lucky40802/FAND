# Curse of Shadow

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

A more demanding shadow magic working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
