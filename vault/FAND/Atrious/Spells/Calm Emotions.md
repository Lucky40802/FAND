# Calm Emotions

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Psionics (School)|Psionics]] in this vault*

You attempt to suppress strong emotion in a group of up to 6 creatures in a 20-foot-radius sphere, either quelling hostility (ending combat aggression) or suppressing a chosen charm/fear effect.

**Save:** Charisma
