# Earthquake

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A violent tremor rips through a 100-foot-radius area, knocking creatures prone on a failed Dexterity save, opening fissures that can swallow creatures, and potentially collapsing structures within the radius.

**Save:** Dexterity
