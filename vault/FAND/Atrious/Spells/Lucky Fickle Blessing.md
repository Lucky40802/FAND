# Lucky Fickle Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A common, easily-taught wild magic working. The caster rolls a d6 for a willing creature within range: on 1–2 it regains 1d8 hit points; on 3–4 it gains 5 temporary hit points; on 5–6 it has advantage on all d20 rolls for 1 minute. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
