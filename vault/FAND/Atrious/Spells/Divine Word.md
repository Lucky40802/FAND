# Divine Word

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

You utter a word of divine power; creatures that fail a Charisma save are deafened, blinded, stunned, or (if reduced to 50 hp or fewer) killed outright, depending on their current hit points.

**Save:** Charisma
