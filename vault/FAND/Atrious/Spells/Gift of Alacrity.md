# Gift of Alacrity

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

You bless a creature you touch with helpful supernatural instincts. Once before the spell ends, the target can add 1d8 to a single initiative roll it makes.
