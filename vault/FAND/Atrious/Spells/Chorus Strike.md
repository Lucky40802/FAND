# Chorus Strike

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

One of the more demanding entries in celestial's middle tier. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Radiant damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
