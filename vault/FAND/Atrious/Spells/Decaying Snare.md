# Decaying Snare

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

One of the more demanding entries in necromancy's middle tier. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
