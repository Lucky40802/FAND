# Murky Blight

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in shadow magic's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it. Some casters add a personal flourish to the casting, though it changes nothing about the effect.
