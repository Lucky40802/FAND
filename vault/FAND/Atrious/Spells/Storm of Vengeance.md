# Storm of Vengeance

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A storm cloud spreads to a 360-foot radius overhead; each round it unleashes a different escalating effect on those beneath it, from acidic rain and deafening thunder to lightning bolts, hailstones, and gale-force winds.

**Save:** Constitution
