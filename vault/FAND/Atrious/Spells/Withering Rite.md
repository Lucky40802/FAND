# Withering Rite

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A workaday necromancy technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
