# Curse of Root

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A plain nature magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
