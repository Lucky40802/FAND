# Mantle of Sealed Circle

*Tertiary-tier Runic Magic*

A workaday runic magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
