# Ashen Frost

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A more demanding elemental working, taught only once the basics are mastered. It strikes everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Fire damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
