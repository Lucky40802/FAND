# Silver Ward

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. A willing creature within range gains 25 temporary hit points and resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 hour. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
