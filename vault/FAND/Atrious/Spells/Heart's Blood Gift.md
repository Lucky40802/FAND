# Heart's Blood Gift

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in blood magic's middle tier. The caster spends 5d4 of their own hit points to restore twice that many hit points to a willing creature they touch. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
