# Veiled Snare

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A working with real teeth, reserved for illusion casters who've put in the hours. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
