# Unstable Blessing

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for wild magic casters who've put in the hours. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
