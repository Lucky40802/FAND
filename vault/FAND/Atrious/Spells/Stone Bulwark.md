# Stone Bulwark

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A no-frills abjuration working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A willing creature within range gains 10 temporary hit points and resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 hour. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
