# Cloud of Daggers

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You fill the air with spinning daggers in a 5-foot cube; any creature that enters the area or starts its turn there takes slashing damage automatically.

**At Higher Levels:** +2d4 per slot level above 2nd
