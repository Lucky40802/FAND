# Ward of Breath

*Secondary-tier Healing*

An intermediate healing working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably. The exact ritual varies slightly from caster to caster, though the effect never does.
