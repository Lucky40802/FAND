# Steadfast Bulwark

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

An intermediate abjuration working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. A willing creature within range gains 20 temporary hit points and resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 hour. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
