# Chains of Layered Threshold

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A working with real teeth, reserved for abjuration casters who've put in the hours. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
