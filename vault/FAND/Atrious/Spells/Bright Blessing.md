# Bright Blessing

*Secondary-tier Healing*

A healing working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
