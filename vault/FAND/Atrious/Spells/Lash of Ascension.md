# Lash of Ascension

*Cantrip — Celestial*

A simple celestial trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. It lashes out at a single target, dealing 2d6 Radiant damage. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
