# Searing Smite

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

The next time you hit with a weapon attack before the spell ends, the weapon flares with fire, dealing extra fire damage and igniting the target unless it succeeds a Constitution save at the end of its turns.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 1st
