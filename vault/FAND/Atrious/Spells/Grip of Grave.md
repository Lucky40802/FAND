# Grip of Grave

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A workaday necromancy technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Intelligence saving throw, it falls Prone. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
