# Greater Invisibility

*SRD — Illusion (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Illusion (School)|Illusion]] in this vault*

A creature you touch becomes invisible for the duration, remaining so even while attacking or casting spells, unlike lesser invisibility.
