# Wall of Fire

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You create a wall of fire on a surface in range, either a straight line up to 60 feet long or a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter, dealing fire damage to creatures that end their turn within 10 feet of the side you designate, or that pass through it.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 4th
