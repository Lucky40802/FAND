# Shroud of Mercy

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A workaday healing technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
