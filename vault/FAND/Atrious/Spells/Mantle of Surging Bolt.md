# Mantle of Surging Bolt

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A middling wild magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
