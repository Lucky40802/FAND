# Prismatic Spray

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Eight rays of shimmering, multicolored light flash out; each creature in the cone is struck by a randomly determined ray, ranging from elemental damage to being blinded, restrained in force, banished, or turned to stone.

**Save:** Dexterity
