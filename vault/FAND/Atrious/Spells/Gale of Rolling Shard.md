# Gale of Rolling Shard

*Secondary-tier Elemental*

A step up from the common elemental basics, and it shows in the effort required. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 15ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 5d6 Fire damage instead of stopping short. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
