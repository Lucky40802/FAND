# Flesh to Stone

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A creature you can see begins to turn to stone on a failed Constitution save, becoming restrained; three cumulative failed saves at the end of its turns fully petrify it, while three successes end the effect.

**Save:** Constitution
