# Gale of Scorched Drift

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A plain elemental spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Acid damage instead of stopping short. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
