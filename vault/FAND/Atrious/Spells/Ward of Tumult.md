# Ward of Tumult

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A plain wild magic spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
