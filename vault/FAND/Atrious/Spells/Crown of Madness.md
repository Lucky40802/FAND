# Crown of Madness

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

A humanoid's eyes glaze with madness on a failed Wisdom save; on each of its turns it must attack the nearest creature you designate as a bonus action, or attack of its choice if none is reachable.

**Save:** Wisdom
