# Anchored Wrench

*Cantrip — Conjuration*

A rite so basic to conjuration that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
