# Whisper of Dim Grasp

*Secondary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
