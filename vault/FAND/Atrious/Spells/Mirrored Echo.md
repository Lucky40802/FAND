# Mirrored Echo

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A illusion spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
