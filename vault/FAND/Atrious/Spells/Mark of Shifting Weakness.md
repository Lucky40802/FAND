# Mark of Shifting Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A illusion working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
