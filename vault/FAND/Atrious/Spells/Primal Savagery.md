# Primal Savagery

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Your teeth or fingernails sharpen briefly into a natural weapon coated in acid. Make a melee spell attack against one creature within reach; on a hit it takes acid damage.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 at 5th/11th/17th level
