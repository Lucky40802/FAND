# Bind of Hand

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

One of the more forgettable entries in necromancy, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
