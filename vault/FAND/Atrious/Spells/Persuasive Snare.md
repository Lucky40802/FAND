# Persuasive Snare

*Cantrip — Enchantment*

Considered too minor to charge for — most enchantment teachers throw this in for free. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it can't move more than 5ft on its next turn. No spell slot required — cast it as often as the moment calls for.
