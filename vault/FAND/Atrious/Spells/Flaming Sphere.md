# Flaming Sphere

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A 5-foot-diameter sphere of fire appears and rolls where you direct it as a bonus action, dealing fire damage to creatures it touches on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 2nd
