# Cone of Cold

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

A blast of cold air erupts from your hands in a 60-foot cone; creatures there take cold damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success, and can be reduced to 0 speed if they take enough damage.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 5th
