# Chains of Cloaked Silence

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
