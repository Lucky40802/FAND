# Time Stop

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Chronomancy (School)|Chronomancy]] in this vault*

Time freezes for everyone but you; you take 1d4+1 turns in a row, during which you can act and move freely, though the spell ends early if you affect another creature or an object it's wearing/carrying, or move more than 1,000 feet from where you cast it.
