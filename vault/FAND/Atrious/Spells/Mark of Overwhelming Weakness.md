# Mark of Overwhelming Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A psionics working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
