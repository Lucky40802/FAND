# Endless Snare

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

One of the more forgettable entries in void magic, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
