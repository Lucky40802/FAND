# Curse of Bond

*Secondary-tier Enchantment*

A enchantment technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
