# Curse of Veil

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
