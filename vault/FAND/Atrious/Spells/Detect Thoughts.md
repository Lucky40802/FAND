# Detect Thoughts

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

For the duration you can read the surface thoughts of one creature within 30 feet at a time; a target aware of magic can resist with a Wisdom save, and you can probe deeper at the risk of stunning it.

**Save:** Wisdom
