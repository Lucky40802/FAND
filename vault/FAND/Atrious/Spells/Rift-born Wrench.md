# Rift-born Wrench

*Cantrip — Conjuration*

Widely regarded as the easiest working in conjuration. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
