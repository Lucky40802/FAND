# Speak with Dead

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You grant a corpse the semblance of life and speech; you can ask it up to five questions, which it answers to the best of its knowledge, though it may be evasive if hostile toward you in life.
