# Dispel Evil and Good

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

Aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead have disadvantage on attacks against you for the duration; you can also use your action to break a charm effect, force a shapechanger to revert, or attempt to force one such creature back to its home plane.

**Save:** Charisma
