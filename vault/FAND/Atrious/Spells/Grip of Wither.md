# Grip of Wither

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it becomes Frightened of the caster until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
