# Forcecage

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

An immobile, invisible cage or barred cell of magical force springs up, up to 20 feet across; creatures inside that fail a Charisma save can't leave by any means short of a wish spell or plane shift, though the cage doesn't restrict actions or attacks within it.

**Save:** Charisma
