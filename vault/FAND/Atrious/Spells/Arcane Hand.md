# Arcane Hand

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

You conjure a Large hand of shimmering force that you can direct each turn to punch a creature, forcefully grapple it, push it, or interpose itself as a shield.

**Save:** Strength or Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** the hand's damage and AC/HP improve slightly at higher slot levels
