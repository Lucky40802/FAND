# Blade Barrier

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A wall of whirling, razor-sharp blades appears, up to 100 feet long, 20 feet high, and 5 feet thick; creatures that pass through or start their turn inside it take slashing damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success.

**Save:** Dexterity
