# Thunderclap

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You create a burst of thunderous sound audible 100 feet away. Each creature within 5 feet of you other than yourself must succeed on a Constitution save or take thunder damage.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
