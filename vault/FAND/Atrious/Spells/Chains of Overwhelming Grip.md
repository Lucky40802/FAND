# Chains of Overwhelming Grip

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

A psionics spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't cast spells with a Verbal component until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
