# Absolute Snare

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
