# Control Water

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You choose an area of water within range and control it in one of four ways: flood the surrounding land, part it to create a trench, redirect its flow, or create a whirlpool.
