# Whisper of Unseen Glass

*Tertiary-tier Divination*

One of the more forgettable entries in divination, but a reliable one. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
