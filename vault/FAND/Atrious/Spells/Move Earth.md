# Move Earth

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You choose an area of terrain no larger than 40 feet on a side and reshape dirt, sand, or clay there, digging trenches, raising hills, or filling holes, over the course of the spell's duration.
