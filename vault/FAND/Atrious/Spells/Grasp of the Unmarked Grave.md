# Grasp of the Unmarked Grave

*Original — Necromancy*

You call forth an undead spirit in a form of your choosing — Ghostly, Putrid, or Skeletal — that fights beside you and obeys your commands for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** the spirit gains more hit points and a stronger attack per slot level above 3rd
