# Resilient Sphere

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

A creature or object of your choice is encased in a shimmering, weightless sphere of force for the duration on a failed Dexterity save; nothing can pass through it, and it's immune to all damage from outside.

**Save:** Dexterity
