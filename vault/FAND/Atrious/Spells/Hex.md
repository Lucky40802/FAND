# Hex

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You curse a creature; your attacks against it deal an extra 1d6 necrotic damage, and you have disadvantage on one ability check type of your choice while it lives. You can move the curse to a new target if the original drops to 0 hp.

**At Higher Levels:** cast at 3rd/5th level for 8 hour/24 hour duration
