# Chains of Insistent Whisper

*Tertiary-tier Enchantment*

A common, easily-taught enchantment working. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
