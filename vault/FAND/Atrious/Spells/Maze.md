# Maze

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You banish a creature you can see into a labyrinthine demiplane; it must use its action to make a DC 20 Intelligence check to find its way through the maze, escaping if it succeeds or when the spell ends.
