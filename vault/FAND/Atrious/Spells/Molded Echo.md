# Molded Echo

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

One of the more demanding entries in transmutation's middle tier. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
