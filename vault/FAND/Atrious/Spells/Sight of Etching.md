# Sight of Etching

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
