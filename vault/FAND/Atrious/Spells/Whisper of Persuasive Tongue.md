# Whisper of Persuasive Tongue

*Secondary-tier Enchantment*

A enchantment working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
