# Binding Blight

*Tertiary-tier Enchantment*

A enchantment working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
