# Mark of Fractured Weakness

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

A psionics technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
