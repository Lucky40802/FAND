# Mantle of Anchored Lattice

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

An intermediate abjuration working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 10 temporary hit points and advantage on its next attack roll. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
