# Summon Fey

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You call forth a fey spirit that takes a form of your choosing — Fury or Mirthful — fighting alongside you and obeying your commands for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** the summoned spirit gains more hit points and a stronger attack per slot level above 3rd
