# Bind of Mirage

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A standard-issue illusion working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
