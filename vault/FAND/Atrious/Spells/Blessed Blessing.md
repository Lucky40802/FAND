# Blessed Blessing

*Secondary-tier Healing*

An intermediate healing working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
