# Whisper of Knowing Vision

*Cantrip — Divination*

A simple divination trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster and one willing creature exchange a single word silently. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
