# Mass Healing Word

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

You choose up to six creatures you can see within range; each regains hit points equal to 1d4 plus your spellcasting ability modifier.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d4 healing per slot level above 3rd
