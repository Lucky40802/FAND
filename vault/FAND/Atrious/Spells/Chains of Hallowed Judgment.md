# Chains of Hallowed Judgment

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

A standard-issue celestial working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
