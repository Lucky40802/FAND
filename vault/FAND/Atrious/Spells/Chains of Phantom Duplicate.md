# Chains of Phantom Duplicate

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

An intermediate illusion working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it becomes Incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
