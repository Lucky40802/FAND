# Layered Snare

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

A abjuration exercise more than a spell, the sort drilled into muscle memory early. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it can't move more than 5ft on its next turn. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
