# Mark of Decaying Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

An ordinary necromancy technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
