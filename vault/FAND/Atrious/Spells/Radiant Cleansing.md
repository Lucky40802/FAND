# Radiant Cleansing

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

One of the more demanding entries in celestial's middle tier. A willing creature within range is cured of one disease or one condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned) and regains 6d6 hit points. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
