# Mantle of Rewound Cycle

*Cantrip — Chronomancy*

Widely regarded as the easiest working in chronomancy. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next Perception check. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
