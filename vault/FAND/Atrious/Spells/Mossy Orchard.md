# Mossy Orchard

*Tertiary-tier Nature Magic*

A standard-issue nature magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. Plants within a 30-foot radius bloom and bear fruit. Up to 6 creatures who eat a fruit within the next hour regain 3d4 hit points and are cured of one poison. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
