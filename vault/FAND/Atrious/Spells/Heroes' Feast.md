# Heroes' Feast

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

You conjure a great feast for up to twelve creatures; those who partake are cured of poison and disease, immune to poison and being frightened for 24 hours, gain advantage on Wisdom saves, and gain 2d10 extra maximum hit points.
