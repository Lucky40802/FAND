# Curse of Torrent

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

An ordinary elemental technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
