# Rune-Brand of Marked Marrow

*Cantrip — Blood Magic*

A rite so basic to blood magic that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
