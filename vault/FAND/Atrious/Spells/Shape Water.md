# Shape Water

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You choose an area of water within range and cause one minor effect: change its shape, color it, freeze it, or cause it to gently move.
