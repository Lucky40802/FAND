# Brand of Bone

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A step up from the common necromancy basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 2d6 damage. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
