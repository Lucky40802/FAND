# Reincarnate

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A creature that has been dead no longer than 10 days is restored to life in a new adult humanoid body of a race you determine randomly, remembering its former life and identity.
