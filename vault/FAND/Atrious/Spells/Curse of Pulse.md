# Curse of Pulse

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

A standard-issue blood magic working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
