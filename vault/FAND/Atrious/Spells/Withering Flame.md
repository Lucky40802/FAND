# Withering Flame

*Cantrip — Elemental*

One of the first things any elemental practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
