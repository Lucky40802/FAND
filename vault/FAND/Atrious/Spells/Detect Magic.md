# Detect Magic

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Divination (School)|Divination]] in this vault*

For the duration you sense the presence of magic within 30 feet, and can use your action to see a faint aura around any visible magical item or creature and learn its school of magic if any.
