# Whisper of Steadfast Aegis

*Cantrip — Abjuration*

Widely regarded as the easiest working in abjuration. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster and one willing creature exchange a single word silently. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
