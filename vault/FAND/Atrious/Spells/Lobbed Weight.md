# Lobbed Weight

*Original — Transmutation*

You hurl a loose, nonmagical object of 1-5 pounds at a point you can see. Creatures within 5 feet of that point make a Dexterity save, taking bludgeoning damage on a failure.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 per slot level above 1st
