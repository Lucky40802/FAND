# Anchored Echo

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A step up from the common abjuration basics, and it shows in the effort required. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
