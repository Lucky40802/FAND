# Mark of Formless Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A workaday void magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
