# Charm Person

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Psionics (School)|Psionics]] in this vault*

You attempt to charm a humanoid you can see; on a failed Wisdom save it is charmed by you, regarding you as a friendly acquaintance while the spell lasts.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** target one additional creature per slot level above 1st
