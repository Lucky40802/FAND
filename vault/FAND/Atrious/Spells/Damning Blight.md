# Damning Blight

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

One of the more forgettable entries in infernal, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
