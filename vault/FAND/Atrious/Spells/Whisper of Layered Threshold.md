# Whisper of Layered Threshold

*Secondary-tier Abjuration*

A more demanding abjuration working, taught only once the basics are mastered. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
