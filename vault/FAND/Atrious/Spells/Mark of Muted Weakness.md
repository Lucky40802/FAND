# Mark of Muted Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A shadow magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
