# Hideous Laughter

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

A creature succumbs to uncontrollable giggling on a failed Wisdom save, falling prone and becoming incapacitated for the duration; it can repeat the save when it takes damage or someone uses an action to shake it out of it.

**Save:** Wisdom
