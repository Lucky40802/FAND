# Creeping Blight

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A workaday shadow magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
