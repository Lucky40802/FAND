# Rope Trick

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

A rope you touch rises to hang in midair; at the top an invisible entrance leads to an extradimensional space where up to eight Medium creatures can rest, hidden and safe, for the duration.
