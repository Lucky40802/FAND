# Spell Slots

How many spell slots a character has at each level, split by caster type. Follows the standard 5e chassis, same as every class in this vault (see each [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Class]] page).

The key thing to note: **pure magic classes get noticeably more at level 1 than everyone else** — a Full Caster starts with 3 first-level slots at character level 1 (bumped up from the standard 5e value of 2, a deliberate house rule for this vault), while a Half Caster gets *zero* spell slots until level 2. That gap is intentional and carries through this vault — being *only* a magic class is the tradeoff that earns the faster progression.

### Exception: Artificer
[[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Artificer|Artificer]] is technically a Half Caster but rounds its caster level *up* instead of down, so unlike Paladin/Ranger it gets 2 first-level slots starting at character level 1 — the only Half Caster that keeps pace with Full Casters at the very start.

## Pact Magic (unique)
[[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Warlock|Warlock]]

Not on the same track as the tables above. Warlock slots are always cast at the Warlock's highest available slot level, there are far fewer of them, and — unlike every other caster — they recover on a **short rest**, not a long rest.

## Non-Casters
[[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Barbarian|Barbarian]], [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Fighter|Fighter]], [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Monk|Monk]], [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Rogue|Rogue]]

No spell slots from the base class. Two subclasses graft a Third Caster progression on top of these (Eldritch Knight for Fighter, Arcane Trickster for Rogue) — those follow yet another table, sitting between Half Caster and no casting at all, and only reach 4th-level slots by character level 20.
