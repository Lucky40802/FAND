# Ever-Reaching Chains

*Original — Elemental*

Ghostly chains lash out from you toward a creature within range. On a failed Strength save it takes lightning damage and is pulled up to 10 feet toward you.

**Save:** Strength

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
