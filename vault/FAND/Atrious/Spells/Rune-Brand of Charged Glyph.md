# Rune-Brand of Charged Glyph

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

An intermediate runic magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. Requires 10 minutes to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 2d8 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
