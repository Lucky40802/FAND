# Rune-Brand of Marked Bond

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

An ordinary blood magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
