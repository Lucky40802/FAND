# Pale Reprieve

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A more demanding necromancy working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A dying creature within range is stabilized and regains 5d6 hit points. Undead the caster controls within range regain the same amount. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
