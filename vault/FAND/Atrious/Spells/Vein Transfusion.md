# Vein Transfusion

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster spends 4d4 of their own hit points to restore twice that many hit points to a willing creature they touch. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
