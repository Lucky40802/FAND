# Shattering Impact

*Cantrip — Evocation*

A simple evocation trick, common enough that even half-trained hedge casters can manage it. It washes over a single target, dealing 2d6 Thunder damage. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
