# Mark of Damning Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A workaday infernal technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
