# Bolt of Warded Symbol

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. It strikes everything in a wide area, dealing 6d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
