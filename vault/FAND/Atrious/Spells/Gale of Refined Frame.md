# Gale of Refined Frame

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

A transmutation basic that survives in every curriculum precisely because it's so cheap to teach. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 5ft directly away from the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
