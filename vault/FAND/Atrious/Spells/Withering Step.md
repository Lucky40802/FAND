# Withering Step

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

A middling shadow magic technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
