# Bleeding Ichor

*Secondary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 2d6 damage. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
