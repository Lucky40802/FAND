# Mark of Uncontrolled Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A workaday wild magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
