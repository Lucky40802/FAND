# Mass Heal

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A flood of healing energy distributes up to 700 hit points among any number of creatures you can see within range, curing them of blindness, deafness, and any disease.
