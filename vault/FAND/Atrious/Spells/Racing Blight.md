# Racing Blight

*Cantrip — Blood Magic*

One of the first things any blood magic practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
