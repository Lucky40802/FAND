# Ray of Sickness

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

A ray of sickly green energy lashes out at a creature (ranged spell attack); on a hit it takes poison damage and is poisoned until the end of your next turn.
