# Wish

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

The most powerful spell a mortal can cast: you can duplicate any spell of 8th level or lower without needing its components, or create one of several other extraordinary effects, though attempting anything beyond simple duplication risks stress that can weaken or kill you and may cost you the ability to cast wish again.
