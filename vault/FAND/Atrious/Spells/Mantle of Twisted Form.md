# Mantle of Twisted Form

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

A transmutation fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. It never scales, but it never needs to.
