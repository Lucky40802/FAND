# Slow

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Chronomancy (School)|Chronomancy]] in this vault*

You alter time around up to six creatures in a 40-foot cube; on a failed Wisdom save each is hindered for the duration: halved speed, -2 to AC and Dexterity saves, no reactions, and only a single action or bonus action per turn.

**Save:** Wisdom
