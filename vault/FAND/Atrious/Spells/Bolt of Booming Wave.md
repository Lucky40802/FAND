# Bolt of Booming Wave

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

An intermediate evocation working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It grips at everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Fire damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
