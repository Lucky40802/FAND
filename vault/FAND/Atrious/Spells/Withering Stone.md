# Withering Stone

*Tertiary-tier Elemental*

A middling elemental technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
