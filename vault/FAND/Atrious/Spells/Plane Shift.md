# Plane Shift

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You and up to eight willing creatures joined by hand are transported to a different plane of existence; an unwilling target can negate the effect on itself with a successful Charisma save.

**Save:** Charisma
