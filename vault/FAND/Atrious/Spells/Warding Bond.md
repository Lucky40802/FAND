# Warding Bond

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You touch a willing creature to link your fates for the duration; it gains +1 AC and saving throws and resistance to all damage, while you take on an equal share of any damage it would suffer.
