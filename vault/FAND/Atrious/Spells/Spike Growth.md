# Spike Growth

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

The ground in a 20-foot radius sprouts hard, sharp spikes and thorns; the area becomes difficult terrain, and each creature that moves there takes piercing damage for every 5 feet traveled.
