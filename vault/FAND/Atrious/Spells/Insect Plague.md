# Insect Plague

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A 20-foot-radius sphere of biting, stinging locusts fills the air around a point you choose; creatures there take piercing damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success, and the area is lightly obscured.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 per slot level above 5th
