# Chains of Fleeting Facade

*Secondary-tier Illusion*

A illusion working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
