# Lash of Charm

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

An intermediate runic magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It washes over everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Lightning damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
