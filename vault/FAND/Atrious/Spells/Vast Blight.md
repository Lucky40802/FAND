# Vast Blight

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
