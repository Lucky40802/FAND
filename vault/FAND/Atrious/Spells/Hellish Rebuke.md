# Hellish Rebuke

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Infernal (School)|Infernal]] in this vault*

When you're hit by an attack from a creature within range, you can use your reaction to wreathe it in hellish flame; it takes fire damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 per slot level above 1st
