# Sight of Vessel

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A conjuration working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. No damage, no save required — the working creates a private pocket of silence in a 15ft radius for 1 minute. Fewer practitioners bother to learn it, since it demands real focus to cast reliably.
