# Summon Beast

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You call forth a spirit that assumes a bestial form of your choice — Air, Land, or Water — fighting alongside you and obeying your commands for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** the summoned spirit gains more hit points and a stronger attack per slot level above 2nd
