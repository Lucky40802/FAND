# Devil's Bargain

[[Devil]] — Warlock, The Fiend

The caster offers a devil's trade: the target is healed immediately, but takes 2d6 necrotic damage at the start of its next turn as the cost comes due. A willing target may refuse the drawback, in which case the healing is only half as much.
