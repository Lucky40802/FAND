# Stone Ward

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

A abjuration working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A willing creature within range gains 5 temporary hit points and resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 hour. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
