# Dominate Monster

*SRD — Enchantment (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Enchantment (School)|Enchantment]] in this vault*

You attempt to beguile any creature you can see; on a failed Wisdom save it's charmed and you can telepathically command its actions for the duration.

**Save:** Wisdom

**At Higher Levels:** duration extends to 8 hours/24 hours at 9th-level slot
