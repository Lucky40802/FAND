# Sealed Blight

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

An ordinary abjuration technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
