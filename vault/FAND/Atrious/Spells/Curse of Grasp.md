# Curse of Grasp

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

One of the more demanding entries in infernal's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
