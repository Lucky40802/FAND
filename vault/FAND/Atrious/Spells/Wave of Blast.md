# Wave of Blast

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

An ordinary evocation technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Lightning damage instead of stopping short. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
