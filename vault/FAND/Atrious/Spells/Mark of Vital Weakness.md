# Mark of Vital Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Blood Magic*

A blood magic working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
