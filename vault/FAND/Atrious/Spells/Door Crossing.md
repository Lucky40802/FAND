# Door Crossing

*Secondary-tier Conjuration*

A conjuration spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster and up to 4 willing creatures within range teleport to an unoccupied spot the caster can see within 120 feet. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
