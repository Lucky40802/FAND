# Acid Splash

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You hurl a bubble of acid at one or two creatures within 5 feet of each other in range. Each target makes a Dexterity save, taking acid damage on a failure.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 at 5th/11th/17th level
