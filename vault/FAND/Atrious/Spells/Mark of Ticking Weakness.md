# Mark of Ticking Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A common, easily-taught chronomancy working. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Unremarkable in every way except how often it actually gets used.
