# Chiseled Sigil

*Cantrip — Runic Magic*

The most basic working taught in runic magic — every apprentice learns it in their first month. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Draws almost nothing from the caster, which is exactly why it never falls out of use.
