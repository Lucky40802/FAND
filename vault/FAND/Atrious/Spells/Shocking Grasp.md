# Shocking Grasp

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Lightning springs from your hand to a creature you touch (melee spell attack, with advantage if the target wears metal armor). On a hit it takes lightning damage and can't take reactions until its next turn.

**At Higher Levels:** scales to 2d8/3d8/4d8 at 5th/11th/17th level
