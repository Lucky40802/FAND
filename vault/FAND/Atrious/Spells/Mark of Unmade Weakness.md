# Mark of Unmade Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
