# Consuming Nothing

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A working with real teeth, reserved for void magic casters who've put in the hours. It cuts into everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
