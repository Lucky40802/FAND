# Hand's Brand

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 2d6 damage. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
