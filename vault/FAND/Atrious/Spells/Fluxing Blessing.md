# Fluxing Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A workaday transmutation technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting 5 temporary hit points. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
