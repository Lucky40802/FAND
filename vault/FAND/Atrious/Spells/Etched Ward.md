# Etched Ward

[[Runes]] — Wizard, [[Atrious/Subclasses/Rune Sage|Rune Sage]]

Inscribes a temporary rune onto a willing creature or object, granting a +1 bonus to one type of saving throw of the caster's choice for the duration. Only one such rune can be active on a target at a time.
