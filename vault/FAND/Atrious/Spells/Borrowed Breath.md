# Borrowed Breath

*Original — Transmutation*

You touch a willing creature and choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison. Once before the spell ends, the target can exhale energy of that type in a 15-foot cone as an action, forcing a Dexterity save from each creature caught in it.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 2nd
