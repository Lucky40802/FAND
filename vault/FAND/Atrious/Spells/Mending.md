# Mending

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You mend a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a torn cloak or a cracked chain link, leaving no trace of the former damage.
