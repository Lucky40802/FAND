# Boundless Snare

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

A void magic technique that separates a serious student from a hobbyist. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it becomes Frightened of the caster until the end of its next turn. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
