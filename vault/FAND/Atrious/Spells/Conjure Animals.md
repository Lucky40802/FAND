# Conjure Animals

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You summon fey spirits that take the form of beasts of your choice appearing in unoccupied spaces you can see, fighting as allies under your command until the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels:** summon more or higher-CR beasts at higher slot levels
