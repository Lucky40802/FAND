# Bound Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

A conjuration working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 3d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
