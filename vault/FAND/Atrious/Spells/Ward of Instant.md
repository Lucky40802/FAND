# Ward of Instant

*Secondary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not something a caster reaches for casually. The gestures involved vary by teacher, but the result is always the same.
