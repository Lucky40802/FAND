# Chains of Silent Whisper

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

An ordinary shadow magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it falls Prone. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
