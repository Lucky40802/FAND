# Daylight

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Shadow Magic (School)|Shadow Magic]] in this vault*

A 60-foot-radius sphere of bright sunlight-like light spreads from a point you choose; it dispels magical darkness created by spells of 3rd level or lower in the area.
