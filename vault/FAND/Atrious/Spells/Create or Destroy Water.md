# Create or Destroy Water

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You create or destroy up to 10 gallons of water, or purify/fog a similar volume within a 30-foot cube.

**At Higher Levels:** affects 10 more gallons, or a 40-foot cube of fog, per slot level above 1st
