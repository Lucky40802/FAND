# Seal of Contract

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A middling infernal technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Taught early enough that almost every caster of this school knows it.
