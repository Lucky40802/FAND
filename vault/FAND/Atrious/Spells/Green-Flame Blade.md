# Green-Flame Blade

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

You make a melee weapon attack against one creature; on a hit it takes normal weapon damage, and green fire leaps to a second creature within 5 feet, dealing fire damage to it as well.

**At Higher Levels:** extra 1d8 at 5th/11th/17th character level
