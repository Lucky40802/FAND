# Whispering Blight

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

An ordinary shadow magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
