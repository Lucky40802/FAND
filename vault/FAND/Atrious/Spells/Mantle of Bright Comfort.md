# Mantle of Bright Comfort

*Cantrip — Healing*

One of the first things any healing practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next Perception check. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
