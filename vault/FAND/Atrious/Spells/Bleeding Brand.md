# Bleeding Brand

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A no-frills infernal working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 1d6 damage. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
