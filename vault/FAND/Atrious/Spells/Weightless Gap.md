# Weightless Gap

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

An intermediate void magic working — not hard to learn, but hard to cast well. It presses against everything in a wide area, dealing 5d6 Force damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
