# Changing Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A no-frills transmutation working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
