# Uncertain Blight

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A workaday illusion technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Charisma saving throw, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
