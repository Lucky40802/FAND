# Erupting Flare

*Secondary-tier Evocation*

A evocation working that punishes a distracted caster more than the basics ever would. It presses against everything in a wide area, dealing 7d6 Thunder damage on a failed Dexterity saving throw. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
