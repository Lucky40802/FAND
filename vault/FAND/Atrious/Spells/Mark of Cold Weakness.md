# Mark of Cold Weakness

*Secondary-tier Necromancy*

A necromancy technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
