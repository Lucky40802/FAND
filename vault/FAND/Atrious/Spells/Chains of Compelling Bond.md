# Chains of Compelling Bond

*Secondary-tier Enchantment*

A more demanding enchantment working, taught only once the basics are mastered. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
