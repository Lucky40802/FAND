# Bind of Chasm

*Tertiary-tier Void Magic*

An ordinary void magic technique, unremarkable but dependable. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
