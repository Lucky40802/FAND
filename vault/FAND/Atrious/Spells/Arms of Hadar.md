# Arms of Hadar

*SRD — Conjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Conjuration (School)|Conjuration]] in this vault*

You invoke tendrils of dark energy around yourself; each creature in range takes necrotic damage and can't take reactions until its next turn on a failed save, or half damage on a success.

**Save:** Strength

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 per slot level above 1st
