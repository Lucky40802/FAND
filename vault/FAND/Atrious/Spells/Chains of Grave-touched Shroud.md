# Chains of Grave-touched Shroud

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A no-frills necromancy working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
