# Bright Bastion

*Tertiary-tier Evocation*

A plain evocation spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A shimmering barrier surrounds up to 1 creatures of the caster's choice, granting each 4 temporary hit points. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
