# Flame Strike

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

A column of divine fire roars down in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-tall cylinder; creatures there take fire and radiant damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success.

**Save:** Dexterity

**At Higher Levels:** +1d6 to either damage type per slot level above 5th
