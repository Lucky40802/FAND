# Whisper of Borrowed Interval

*Secondary-tier Chronomancy*

A more demanding chronomancy working, taught only once the basics are mastered. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it. A handful of minor phrasings exist for this working, all functionally identical.
