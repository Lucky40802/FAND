# Seal of Chaos

*Tertiary-tier Wild Magic*

A workaday wild magic technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
