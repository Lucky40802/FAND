# Mantle of Cleansing Touch

*Cantrip — Healing*

Considered too minor to charge for — most healing teachers throw this in for free. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +1 bonus to its next attack roll or saving throw. No spell slot required — cast it as often as the moment calls for.
