# Adaptive Wrench

*Tertiary-tier Transmutation*

A transmutation working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 10ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 4d6 Bludgeoning damage instead of stopping short. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
