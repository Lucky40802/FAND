# Runed Echo

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A step up from the common runic magic basics, and it shows in the effort required. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
