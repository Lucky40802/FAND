# Mark of Rotting Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Necromancy*

A middling necromancy technique, taught somewhere between the basics and real mastery. A single target within range must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
