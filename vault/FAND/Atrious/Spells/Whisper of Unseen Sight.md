# Whisper of Unseen Sight

*Cantrip — Divination*

One of the first things any divination practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. Costs no real effort to cast and can be repeated endlessly.
