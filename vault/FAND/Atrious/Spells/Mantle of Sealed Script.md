# Mantle of Sealed Script

*Secondary-tier Runic Magic*

A runic magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not something a caster reaches for casually.
