# Chains of Uncontrolled Whim

*Cantrip — Wild Magic*

A wild magic fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
