# Polymorph

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Nature Magic (School)|Nature Magic]] in this vault*

You transform a creature you can see into a beast form of challenge rating equal to or less than its own, or lower, on a failed Wisdom save; it keeps its own mind but assumes the new form's statistics.

**Save:** Wisdom
