# Living Blessing

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A step up from the common transmutation basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained. A handful of minor phrasings exist for this working, all functionally identical.
