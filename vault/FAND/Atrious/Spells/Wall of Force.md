# Wall of Force

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Evocation (School)|Evocation]] in this vault*

An invisible wall of force springs into existence, up to ten contiguous 10-foot-square panels; nothing can physically pass through it, and it's immune to virtually all damage and most spells.
