# Glyph of Curse

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

One of the more demanding entries in infernal's middle tier. Requires 10 minutes to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 2d8 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
