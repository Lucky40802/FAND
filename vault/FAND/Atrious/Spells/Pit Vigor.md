# Pit Vigor

*Secondary-tier Infernal*

A more demanding infernal working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A willing creature gains 30 temporary hit points and advantage on Strength checks for 1 minute. When the spell ends, it takes 1d6 fire damage. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
