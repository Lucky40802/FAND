# Warden's Ward

*Tertiary-tier Abjuration*

An ordinary abjuration technique, unremarkable but dependable. A willing creature within range gains 5 temporary hit points and resistance to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 hour. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
