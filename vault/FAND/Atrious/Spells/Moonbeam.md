# Moonbeam

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

A silvery beam of pale light shines in a 5-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder that you can move as an action; creatures there take radiant damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success, and shapechangers revert to their true form.

**Save:** Constitution

**At Higher Levels:** +1d10 per slot level above 2nd
