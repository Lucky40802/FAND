# Fickle Blight

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in wild magic's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the end of its next turn. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
