# Withering Gloom

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

One of the more forgettable entries in shadow magic, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
