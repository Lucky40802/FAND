# Seal of Line

*Cantrip — Runic Magic*

The entry point into runic magic for most casters. Requires 1 minute to inscribe a seal onto a weapon, shield, or other handheld object. The next time that item is used to make an attack, it deals an extra 1d4 damage of the caster's choice, then the seal burns away. Only one seal can be active on a given item at a time. Rarely impressive, but never fails when it's actually needed.
