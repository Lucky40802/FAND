# Fleeting Blight

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A workaday chronomancy technique, the sort taught by rote rather than insight. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
