# Renewing Echo

*Cantrip — Healing*

A healing fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
