# Finger of Death

*SRD — Necromancy (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Necromancy (School)|Necromancy]] in this vault*

You send a killing surge of negative energy at a creature, dealing necrotic damage on a failed Constitution save, half on a success; a humanoid slain by this spell rises at the start of your next turn as a zombie permanently under your command.

**Save:** Constitution
