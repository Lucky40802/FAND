# Immolation

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Flames wreathe one creature you can see. On a failed Dexterity save it takes fire damage and burns for half that damage at the start of each of its turns until the fire is put out.
