# Quiet Blessing

*Tertiary-tier Divination*

A standard-issue divination working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
