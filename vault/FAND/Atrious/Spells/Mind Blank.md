# Mind Blank

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

A willing creature you touch becomes immune to psychic damage, to having its thoughts read or its emotions/memories manipulated, and to most divination magic, for the duration.
