# Halo Renewal

*Secondary-tier Celestial*

A more demanding celestial working, taught only once the basics are mastered. Up to six creatures of the caster's choice in the area regain 5d4 + the caster's spellcasting modifier hit points. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
