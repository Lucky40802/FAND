# Meteor Swarm

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Elemental (School)|Elemental]] in this vault*

Four fiery orbs streak down to points you choose, each exploding in a 40-foot-radius sphere; creatures in an area take fire and bludgeoning damage on a failed Dexterity save, half on a success (overlapping spheres don't double damage).

**Save:** Dexterity
