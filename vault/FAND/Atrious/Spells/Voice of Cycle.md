# Voice of Cycle

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

A chronomancy working most casters learn out of necessity rather than interest. No damage, no save required — the working opens quiet two-way communication with one creature the caster knows for 1 minute. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
