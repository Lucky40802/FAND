# Mark of Boundless Weakness

*Secondary-tier Void Magic*

One of the more demanding entries in void magic's middle tier. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
