# Withering Fracture

*Cantrip — Psionics*

A psionics fundamental, drilled into students before anything else. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Unglamorous, but the sort of thing a caster ends up using constantly.
