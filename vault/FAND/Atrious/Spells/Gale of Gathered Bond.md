# Gale of Gathered Bond

*Tertiary-tier Conjuration*

An ordinary conjuration technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10ft in a direction of the caster's choice. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 2d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
