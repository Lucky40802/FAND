# Arcane Eye

*SRD — Divination (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Shadow Magic (School)|Shadow Magic]] in this vault*

You create an invisible, magical eye that hovers in place and can fly up to 30 feet per round in any direction; you see through it as if you were there, in darkness or light.
