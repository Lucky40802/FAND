# Blink

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

At the end of each of your turns for the duration, roll a d20; on an 11 or higher you vanish into the Ethereal Plane until the start of your next turn, or you can choose to end the spell on a later such roll.
