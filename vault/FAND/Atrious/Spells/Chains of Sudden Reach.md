# Chains of Sudden Reach

*Secondary-tier Psionics*

One of the more demanding entries in psionics's middle tier. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it becomes Restrained until the end of its next turn. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
