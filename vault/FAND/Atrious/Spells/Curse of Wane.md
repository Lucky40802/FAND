# Curse of Wane

*Tertiary-tier Chronomancy*

One of the more forgettable entries in chronomancy, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
