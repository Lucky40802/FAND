# Mark of Scorching Weakness

*Cantrip — Infernal*

A rite so basic to infernal that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
