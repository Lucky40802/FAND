# Gale of Molded Structure

*Secondary-tier Transmutation*

A working with real teeth, reserved for transmutation casters who've put in the hours. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 15ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 7d6 Force damage instead of stopping short. Demanding enough that it's usually cast with intent, not out of habit.
