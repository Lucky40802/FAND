# Withering Reflection

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

An ordinary illusion technique, unremarkable but dependable. A single target within range must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer a -2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
