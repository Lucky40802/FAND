# Withering Cut

*Cantrip — Blood Magic*

One of the first things any blood magic practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next attack roll. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
