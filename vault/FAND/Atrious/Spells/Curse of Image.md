# Curse of Image

*Tertiary-tier Illusion*

A plain illusion spell, favored for being easy to learn rather than being strong. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
