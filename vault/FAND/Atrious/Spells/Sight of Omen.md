# Sight of Omen

*Secondary-tier Divination*

A more demanding divination working, taught only once the basics are mastered. No damage, no save required — the working lets the caster see through the eyes of a familiar animal within a mile for 1 minute. Costs real concentration, which is exactly why fewer casters bother mastering it.
