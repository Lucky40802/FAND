# Cure Wounds

*SRD — Evocation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Healing (School)|Healing]] in this vault*

A creature you touch regains hit points equal to 1d8 plus your spellcasting ability modifier. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.

**At Higher Levels:** +1d8 healing per slot level above 1st
