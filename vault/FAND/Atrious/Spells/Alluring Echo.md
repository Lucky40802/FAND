# Alluring Echo

*Cantrip — Enchantment*

One of the first things any enchantment practitioner is shown, usually within their first week of study. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. Simple enough to cast while distracted, which is usually how it gets used.
