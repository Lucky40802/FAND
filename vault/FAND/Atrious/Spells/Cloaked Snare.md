# Cloaked Snare

*Cantrip — Shadow Magic*

A rite so basic to shadow magic that forgetting it is treated as a sign of a poor education. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw, it can't move more than 5ft on its next turn. Reliable enough that most casters stop thinking about it entirely.
