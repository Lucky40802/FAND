# Righteous Snare

*Tertiary-tier Celestial*

One of the more forgettable entries in celestial, but a reliable one. The caster fixes on a single target within range. Unless it succeeds on a Dexterity saving throw, it can't take reactions until the end of its next turn. Not the kind of thing anyone brags about casting, but it earns its place.
