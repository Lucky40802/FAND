# Absorb Elements

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

Cast in response to taking acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage, you halve that damage against yourself, and your next melee attack before the end of your next turn deals an extra 1d6 of that same damage type.

**At Higher Levels:** extra 1d6 to the following melee attack per slot level above 1st
