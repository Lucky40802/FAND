# Magic Circle

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

You create a 10-foot-radius, 20-foot-tall cylinder of magical energy that bars a chosen creature type (celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, or undead) from crossing it, and imposes disadvantage on their attacks against creatures inside if you choose.

**At Higher Levels:** duration extends by 1 hour per slot level above 3rd
