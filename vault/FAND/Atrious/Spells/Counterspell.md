# Counterspell

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Abjuration (School)|Abjuration]] in this vault*

You attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell; if it's casting a spell of 3rd level or lower, the spell fails, and against higher-level spells you make an ability check to determine success.

**At Higher Levels:** automatically succeeds against spells of level equal to the slot used minus 1 or lower; otherwise an ability check
