# Mind Turned Against Itself

*Original — Psionics*

You strike at a creature's mind, forcing an Intelligence save. On a failure it can no longer distinguish friend from foe, and treats the nearest creature to it as an enemy each round regardless of who that is.

**Save:** Intelligence
