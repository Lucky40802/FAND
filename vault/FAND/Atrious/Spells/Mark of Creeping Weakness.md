# Mark of Creeping Weakness

*Tertiary-tier Shadow Magic*

One of the more forgettable entries in shadow magic, but a reliable one. A single target within range must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Nothing about the working is subtle, but it does its job.
