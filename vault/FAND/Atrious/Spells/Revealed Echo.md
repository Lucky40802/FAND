# Revealed Echo

*Secondary-tier Divination*

A divination spell reserved for casters past their earliest training. No damage, no save required — the working grants the caster knowledge of the exact distance and direction to a named person or object they've met before. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
