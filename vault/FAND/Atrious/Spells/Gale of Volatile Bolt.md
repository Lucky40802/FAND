# Gale of Volatile Bolt

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A wild magic technique that asks more of the caster than the basics do. A single target within range must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled 15ft toward the caster. If the movement forces it into a wall or other solid obstacle, it takes 6d6 Psychic damage instead of stopping short. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained.
