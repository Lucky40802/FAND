# Slipping Blessing

*Secondary-tier Chronomancy*

A step up from the common chronomancy basics, and it shows in the effort required. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute. A working that separates the practiced from the merely trained. The gestures involved vary by teacher, but the result is always the same.
