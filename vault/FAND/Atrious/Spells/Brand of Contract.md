# Brand of Contract

*Tertiary-tier Infernal*

A no-frills infernal working, the kind every hedge caster keeps in reserve. The caster marks a single target within range. For the duration, the first attack that lands against the marked target each turn deals an extra 1d6 damage. Widely known among practitioners of this school.
