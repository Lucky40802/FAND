# Veil of False Sight

[[Illusion]] — Wizard, School of Illusion

The area is overlaid with a false image of the caster's choosing. Physical interaction reveals the illusion instantly; a successful INT (Investigation) check against the caster's spell save DC reveals it from a distance.
