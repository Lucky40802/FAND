# Planar Binding

*SRD — Abjuration (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Celestial (School)|Celestial]] in this vault*

You attempt to bind a celestial, elemental, fey, or fiend that you can see to your service for the duration, on a failed Charisma save.

**Save:** Charisma

**At Higher Levels:** duration extends to 10/30/180 days or 1 year at 6th/7th/8th/9th-level slots
