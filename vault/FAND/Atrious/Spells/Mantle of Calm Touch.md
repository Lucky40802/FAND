# Mantle of Calm Touch

*Tertiary-tier Healing*

A healing working most journeymen pick up early and never stop using. The caster touches a willing creature (which may be themselves), granting a +2 bonus to AC until the start of the caster's next turn. A workhorse rather than a showpiece.
