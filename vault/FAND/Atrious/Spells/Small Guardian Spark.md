# Small Guardian Spark

*Original — Void Magic*

You animate a small construct guardian that follows your commands, can make a melee spell attack dealing force damage, and can interpose itself to redirect an attack made against you within 5 feet toward itself instead.

**At Higher Levels:** the guardian's hit points and attack scale with slot level
