# Unstable Blight

*Secondary-tier Wild Magic*

A more demanding wild magic working, taught only once the basics are mastered. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer vulnerability to one damage type of the caster's choice for 1 minute. Not every caster keeps this one ready — it costs more attention than most basics do.
