# Warped Echo

*Cantrip — Transmutation*

The most basic working taught in transmutation — every apprentice learns it in their first month. No damage, no save required — the working reveals invisible creatures or objects within range as a faint outline for 1 round. The kind of working nobody remembers learning, only knowing.
