# Enhance Ability

*SRD — Transmutation (official) — filed under [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Transmutation (School)|Transmutation]] in this vault*

You touch a creature and grant it advantage on ability checks using one chosen ability score for the duration, plus a related minor benefit (such as advantage on Strength checks to push/lift, or being unaffected by low-oxygen conditions).

**At Higher Levels:** +1 target per slot level above 2nd
