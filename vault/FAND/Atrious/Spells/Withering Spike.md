# Withering Spike

*Tertiary-tier Psionics*

A standard-issue psionics working — nothing about it stands out, and that's the point. A single target within range must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or suffer disadvantage on its next saving throw. Common enough to be found in nearly every caster's repertoire.
