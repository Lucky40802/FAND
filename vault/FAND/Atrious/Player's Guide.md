# Player's Guide

A plain-language tour of what makes FAND different from standard D&D. It covers the rules you'll use at the table, and each section links to the full rules page.

## 1. The world in one paragraph
The gods of FAND are copies. The original gods died, and their power broke apart into **Runes**, the Laws of the world (Fire, Time, Love, Death...). Today's gods are built from stolen Runes, and they grow stronger by stealing more or by getting mortals to follow their Law. That's why gods want you. See [[Runes]] and the [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]].

## 2. Your class and Runes
Your class works like normal D&D 5e. If your class is tied to a Rune, owning a piece of the dead god or spirit behind that Rune makes you stronger:
- **Tertiary:** anything themed on the Rune. Raises power only.
- **Secondary:** a relic once removed from the source. Unlocks a unique ability.
- **Primary:** a piece of the source itself. Unlocks a stronger unique ability.

See [[Class]].

## 3. Armor Class works differently
- Your AC **starts at 0**. It comes from Constitution, natural traits, armor, shields, and magic.
- AC doesn't make attacks miss. It **reduces damage** after a hit: AC 10 blocks 25%, AC 30 blocks half, and AC 90 blocks three quarters. It never reaches 100%, and every hit does at least 1 damage.
- Headshots are critical hits: ×1.5 damage before AC.
- Armor looted from enemies is Damaged (half AC) until it's repaired.

See [[FAND/Atrious/Armor Class|Armor Class]].

## 4. Materials have grades
Every material comes from a **realm** and has a **grade from 1 to 10** in it, like `Hell 7`. Realms rank from weakest to strongest: Human, Mysterious, Hell/Heaven, Chaos, Abyss, Inner Realm, Outer Realm, Void, Inner Void, Primordial. Better realm and grade means stronger gear. Every material also has a **property**: fire materials burn, mimic parts shapeshift, undead remains make necromancy foci.

See [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]], [[FAND/Atrious/Material Properties|Material Properties]], and the [[FAND/Atrious/Materials/Materials.base|Materials database]].

## 5. Getting materials
- **Hunt and scavenge:** monsters carry materials of their realm and grade. Stripping a corpse for hide, bone, blood, organs, and essences uses the **Scavenging** profession. See the Bestiaries in the [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]].
- **Harvest:** ore, stone, crystals, and plants are gathered with the **Harvesting** profession, listed on each realm's Gathering page.
- **Buy:** prices depend on grade (Steel is 20 gp, a Seraph Feather 4,000 gp). See [[FAND/Atrious/Economy|Economy]].
- **Refine:** three of one grade make one of the next grade. See [[FAND/Atrious/Material Refining|Material Refining]].

## 6. Professions and crafting
There are **10 professions**:

| Profession | Makes or does |
|---|---|
| Blacksmithing | Metal weapons and armor |
| Carving | Wood, stone, bone, and crystal gear |
| Tailoring | Cloth and leather gear |
| Alchemy | Potions and poisons |
| Cooking | Meals that buff |
| Enchanting | Runes, glyphs, and bound souls |
| Engineering | Machines, constructs, and buildings |
| Harvesting | Gathers ore and plants, raises animals |
| Scavenging | Strips corpses for parts |
| Navigation | Maps and instruments |

Each has **specialties**. You start with one and gain more as you level; crafting outside them is at disadvantage. See the [[FAND/Atrious/Profession Quick Reference|Profession Quick Reference]] and [[FAND/Atrious/Master Professions|Master Professions]].

You can also design your own unique items:
1. Pick a form (sword, cloak, potion...). The form decides which profession makes it.
2. Pick a **primary** material (sets its power) and up to two **secondary** materials (each adds its property).
3. Make a design check, then craft it.

Your profession level goes up as you craft. See [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]], [[FAND/Atrious/Crafting Rules|Crafting Rules]], and [[FAND/Atrious/Profession Progression|Profession Progression]].

## 7. Contracts with gods
A god can offer you a **contract**: its power in exchange for your service. Ranks run from Minor up to Apostle, then the five **Fingers**, the god's personal Hand. Higher ranks give spells, Natural AC, and rare materials, but come with duties, rivals, and a price for breaking faith. Gods who hold the same Rune are enemies, and so are their contractors.

See [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|God Contracts]] and the [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]].

## 8. Travelling between realms
Other realms push back on visitors. Each realm has a **Pressure** (Mysterious 1, Hell 2... Primordial 9). Your **Attunement** comes from your level, your contract rank, or gear made from that realm's materials. If Pressure is higher, you suffer strain (disadvantage, then exhaustion). Getting there takes thin places, the Astral, gates, rifts, or a god's invitation.

See [[FAND/Atrious/Realm Travel|Realm Travel]] and the [[FAND/Atrious/Realm Map|Realm Map]].

## Quick reference
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How much damage does my armor stop? | AC ÷ (AC + 30) |
| How strong is a material? | Realm tier and grade → Material Rank → Item Level |
| How strong is its property? | Potency: Item Level ÷ 5, +1 at grade 9, +2 at grade 10 |
| What does it cost? | Realm base × grade (Human 5 gp, Mysterious 50, Hell/Heaven 500...) |
| Can I survive in that realm? | If your Attunement is at least its Pressure |
