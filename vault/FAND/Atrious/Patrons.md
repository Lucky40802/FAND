# Patrons

Warlock patron flavor options, grouped by archetype. Most real ones here are the same entities already documented in [[FAND/Atrious/Gods Index|Gods]] and [[FAND/Atrious/Demons Index|Demons]] — Fiend Patrons are the Archdevils, Great Old One Patrons are the same adapted Lovecraftian entities. A few additional real figures show up only here:

## Archfey Patrons
- Titania - Summer Queen: Queen of the Seelie Court, ruler of summer and beauty.
- Oberon - The Green Lord: King of the Seelie Court alongside Titania.
- Baba Yaga - The Iron-teethed Witch: An ancient witch of Slavic folklore adapted into D&D lore, living in a hut that walks on chicken legs.

## Undying Patrons
- Nemoth - The Undying King: Once a mortal lich who ascended to godhood, this setting's most iconic villain-deity.
- Nyxmourne - Mistress of souls and fate: a deity of death and fate who guards against anyone cheating death permanently.

## Celestial Patrons
- Sunhallow - God of dawn and renewal: among the most widely worshipped good gods in this setting.

The rest of the Archfey/Undying/Celestial/Genie groups, and all of the 'Homebrew Patrons' group, are original or unverified additions rather than established mythology or published D&D lore.

*(Nemoth, Nyxmourne, and Sunhallow were renamed from their original D&D-derived versions to remove trademark risk from the vault, consistent with every other renamed entity from the source archive.)*
