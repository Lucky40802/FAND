# Profession Progression

How characters gain levels in a crafting profession (see [[FAND/Atrious/Master Professions|Master Professions]]). Profession level is separate from character level. It runs from 1 to 20, and each profession's own page lists its crafting bonus at each level.

## Learning a profession
- **Starting:** a character begins at level 1 in a new profession by spending a week with a teacher (someone at level 5 or higher in it) or by studying one of its Blueprints for a month alone.
- **How many:** any number, but only the **two highest** professions earn full Profession XP. Every other profession earns half.

## Specialties
Each of the 10 professions has **specialties**, the narrower crafts it absorbed (see [[FAND/Atrious/Master Professions|Master Professions]]). Every Blueprint has a `specialty` field.
- You start a profession with one specialty of your choice.
- Crafting outside your specialties is at disadvantage, unless the Blueprint's level is no more than half your profession level.
- You gain a specialty by finishing one item of it under a teacher who has it, or free at profession levels 5, 10, 15, and 20.
- Some specialties have extra requirements (Blood Alchemy needs fresh blood, Void Smithing needs a Void forge, and so on); each profession's page lists them.

## Profession XP
Profession XP comes from doing the work:

| Activity | XP |
|---|---|
| Craft an item (success) | 10 × the item's level |
| Craft an item (natural 20) | Double |
| Craft an item 5 or more levels below your profession level | Half |
| Failed craft | 2 × the item's level |
| Design a [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints\|Custom Blueprint]] (success) | 20 × the item's level |
| Reverse-engineer a Blueprint (success) | 15 × the item's level |
| [[FAND/Atrious/Material Refining\|Refine]] a material | 5 × the result's grade × realm tier |
| Gather a material (with the listed profession) | The material's grade × realm tier |
| Work with a material from a higher realm than you've worked before | +100, once per realm |

## Levels
To reach the next level, you need **100 × the next level** in Profession XP, counted since your last level-up.

| Level | XP to reach it | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 200 | 200 |
| 5 | 500 | 1,400 |
| 10 | 1,000 | 5,400 |
| 15 | 1,500 | 11,900 |
| 20 | 2,000 | 20,900 |

Worked example: a level 4 Blacksmith needs 500 XP for level 5. Forging five level-10 swords (100 XP each) gets them there.

## Teachers
- A teacher at least 5 levels above you halves the XP needed for your next level. It takes a week together and costs the teacher's day rate (see [[FAND/Atrious/Economy|Economy]]: 10 gp × the teacher's level per day).
- A **Finger** or **Apostle** of a god whose Runes match the profession (for example, a Hephaestus contractor for Blacksmithing) can teach past level 15, where ordinary teachers become rare.

## Milestones
| Level | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 5 | Journeyman: may take apprentices and teach levels 1–4 |
| 10 | Can design Custom Blueprints for Rare items without disadvantage |
| 15 | Master: recognized by guilds; temples of matching gods may offer a contract |
| 20 | Grandmaster: can work Abyss-grade materials and above at a Realm Forge (see [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints\|Custom Blueprints]]) |
