# God Contracts

A **contract** is a formal agreement between a mortal and a [[God]]. The god lends a share of its power. In return, the mortal spreads the god's Law, does its work, and fights its wars.

Gods need contracts. Every god is a replica powered by Runes. It only grows stronger by stealing power from other gods or by getting more mortals to follow its Law (see [[Runes]]). Contractors are how a god reaches into the mortal world to do both.

Contracts are different from two existing mechanics:
- **[[Pacts]]** are loans from devils, paid back later.
- **[[Worship]]** is devotion without terms.

A contract is a **rank in a god's service**, with clear duties and a clear reward.

## The ten ranks (weakest to strongest)

| # | Rank | Holders per god | Power granted | Natural AC | Can grant contracts up to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Minor** | Unlimited | One cantrip from the god's Law; +1 to one skill tied to the god | +1 | — |
| 2 | **Normal** | Unlimited | One 1st-level spell of the god's Law, once per long rest; the god can send you dreams | +2 | — |
| 3 | **Major** | Hundreds | The god's spells up to 2nd level; you can sense other contractors of your god | +3 | — |
| 4 | **Senior** | Dozens | Spells up to 3rd level; resistance to one damage type tied to the god | +4 | Minor |
| 5 | **Apostle** | 12 | Spells up to 5th level; a **Secondary-tier** link to the god's Rune, which unlocks one unique ability (see [[Class]]) | +6 | Normal |
| 6 | **5th Finger** | 1 | Spells up to 6th level; a **Primary-tier** fragment of the god's Rune, with its own unique ability | +8 | Major |
| 7 | **4th Finger** | 1 | Spells up to 7th level; immune to being charmed or frightened by other gods' contractors | +10 | Senior |
| 8 | **3rd Finger** | 1 | Spells up to 8th level; once per day, use one of the god's Rune spells without holding the Rune | +12 | Senior |
| 9 | **2nd Finger** | 1 | Spells up to 9th level; can **create new spells** from the god's Law, which normally only those close to godhood can do | +14 | Apostle |
| 10 | **1st Finger** | 1 | Acts as the god's avatar: speaks with its voice and can channel its power directly; everything above | +16 | Apostle |

### How the table works
- **Spells.** Contract spells are extra spells from the god's Law. You can cast them even if your class can't. They use your class's spell slots, or once per long rest each if you have no slots.
- **Natural AC.** The god's power toughens the contractor. This counts as **Natural AC** under [[FAND/Atrious/Armor Class|Armor Class]], so it can't be looted. It replaces the previous rank's bonus rather than stacking with it.
- **The Hand.** Every god has exactly **five Fingers**, from the 5th Finger up to the 1st. Together they're called the god's **Hand**. Each position has one holder at a time. A god's Hand is the most dangerous group of mortals it controls.
- **Granting contracts.** From Senior upward, a contractor can sign lower-rank contracts on the god's behalf. That's how a god's reach spreads through a region.

## Duties
Every contract has duties. They grow with the rank:

| Ranks | Duties |
|---|---|
| Minor, Normal | Obey the god's core rule (its Law); a small task or offering now and then |
| Major, Senior | Spread worship (bring new followers); take missions from the god's higher contractors |
| Apostle | Lead a region or order for the god; answer any summons |
| Fingers | Act as the god's weapon in wars with other gods; guard the god's Runes; carry out the god's bets on mortal wars and successions |

Each god's specific wants are listed in the [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]].

## Rewards
Besides spells and Natural AC, gods pay in **materials** and **craft**. Every god in the [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]] has three signature materials (Normal, Senior, and Apostle) and a unique Finger relic.

| Rank | Reward |
|---|---|
| Minor | Buy Human-grade materials at the god's temples at a discount |
| Normal | The god's temples sell its **Normal signature material** to you |
| Major | One free **Normal signature material** per completed task; temple crafters work for you at half cost |
| Senior | One **Senior signature material** each time you rise in rank or finish a major task; access to one existing Blueprint that fits the god's theme |
| Apostle | One **Apostle signature material** on taking the seat; the god's home realm counts as a Realm Forge for you (see [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints\|Custom Blueprints]]) |
| 5th–2nd Finger | A personal relic of your own design, crafted by the god's forges from its Apostle signature material; Potency counts as 2 higher than normal |
| 1st Finger | The god's **Finger relic** itself |

Signature materials are what the god gives freely. Contractors can still gather or buy any other material.

## Rising in rank
To rise, a contractor must **complete a task the god sets** and meet the rank's requirement:

| Moving to | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Normal, Major | Serve faithfully and finish the god's tasks |
| Senior | Bring the god a meaningful number of new followers, or win a notable victory in its name |
| Apostle | An Apostle seat must be empty (by death, betrayal, or promotion), and the god must choose you |
| Any Finger | The position must be empty, **or you defeat its current holder** |

Gods allow Fingers to be taken by force. A Finger who can be killed wasn't strong enough to hold the position.

## One god at a time
- **Minor and Normal** contracts can be held with several gods at once, like hired work.
- **Major and above** are exclusive: one god only. Taking a second contract at Major or higher breaks the first (see below).
- Contractors of rival gods are fair game for each other. The higher your rank, the more other gods' contractors will want you dead.

## Breaking a contract

| Rank | What happens |
|---|---|
| Minor, Normal | All contract power is lost immediately |
| Major, Senior | Power is lost, and the god places a curse: a DM-chosen disadvantage until you atone or the god dies |
| Apostle | Power and Natural AC are lost, and the god sends its other contractors to hunt you |
| Fingers | The Rune fragment tears itself out. Make a Constitution save (DC 10 + 2 × rank number). On a failure you drop to 0 hit points. The god's whole Hand hunts you |

## When a god dies
Gods are killed and usurped all the time. When a god dies:
- All its contracts become void. Contractors lose contract power at the start of their next long rest.
- The god's power breaks into **Runes**, as every dead god's does.
- Its Fingers are closest to that power. A Finger who reaches the god's remains first may claim a Primary piece (see [[Class]]) before rivals or the killer take it. This is how a 1st Finger can take the first step toward becoming a god.
