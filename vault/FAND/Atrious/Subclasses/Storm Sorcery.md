# Storm Sorcery

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Storm]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Sorcery tied to wind, thunder, and lightning.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wind Speaker | Speak, read, and write Primordial. |
| 1 | Tempestuous Magic | After casting a 1st-level or higher spell, use a bonus action to fly 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks. |
| 6 | Heart of the Storm | Resist lightning and thunder; your lightning/thunder spells damage creatures near you. |
| 6 | Storm Guide | Control rain and wind near you. |
| 14 | Storm's Fury | Reaction when hit by a melee attack: deal lightning damage and possibly push the attacker. |
| 18 | Wind Soul | Immune to lightning and thunder; gain a flying speed and share it with allies. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A mobile, control-focused sorcerer.
