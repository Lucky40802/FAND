# Oath of the Ancients

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Paladins sworn to preserve life, light, and joy in the world.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Nature's Wrath | Restrain a creature within 10 ft with spectral vines unless it passes a Strength or Dexterity save. |
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Turn the Faithless | Turn fey and fiends within 30 ft; also reveals shapechangers. |
| 7 | Aura of Warding | You and allies within 10 ft have resistance to spell damage. |
| 15 | Undying Sentinel | When reduced to 0 hit points, drop to 1 instead once per long rest; you stop aging. |
| 20 | Elder Champion | For 1 minute: regain hit points each turn, cast paladin spells as bonus actions, and enemies near you struggle against your spells. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A durable, spell-resistant protector.
