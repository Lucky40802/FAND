# College of Valor

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Battle-poets who inspire allies from the front lines.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Bonus Proficiencies | Medium armor, shields, and martial weapons. |
| 3 | Combat Inspiration | A creature with your Bardic Inspiration die can add it to a damage roll or to its AC against one attack. |
| 6 | Extra Attack | Attack twice when you take the Attack action. |
| 14 | Battle Magic | After casting a bard spell with your action, make one weapon attack as a bonus action. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A martial gish-bard.
