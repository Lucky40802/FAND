# Blood Titan

## Overview
Blood Titan expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Barbarian class.

## Theme
A barbarian who grows and hardens by spending their own blood.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Blood Armor | While raging, damage resistance fueled by lost hit points. |
| 6 | Titan's Grip | Advantage on grapple and shove attempts while raging. |
| 10 | Crimson Growth | Size increases by one category while raging. |
| 14 | Undying Rage | Once per long rest, drop to 1 hit point instead of 0 while raging. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
