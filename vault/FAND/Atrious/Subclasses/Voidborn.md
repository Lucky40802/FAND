# Voidborn

## Overview
Voidborn expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Sorcerer class.

## Theme
A sorcerer born already touched by the Void rather than by any bloodline.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Empty Skin | Resistance to force damage. |
| 6 | Unmake | A spell attack ignores a small amount of resistance. |
| 10 | Step Outside | A short teleport, once per rest. |
| 14 | Silence of the Void | Briefly negate one spell being cast nearby. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
