# Iron's Patron

## Overview
Iron's Patron is an Otherworldly Patron for the [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Warlock|Warlock]] class — the patron being [[Iron]] himself, sealed away rather than dead, closer to an Undying patron than a Fiend or Great Old One.

## Theme
Unlocked by acquisition rather than level, following the [[Class]] system's Primary/Secondary/Tertiary tiers. No confirmed warlock of this patron exists in the current age.

## Acquisition Tiers
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Iron]] himself, were he ever freed or killed rather than merely sealed in the spirit realm. Unique ability unknown, since none has ever been acquired.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Steel]], Iron's own evolved descendant. Lets the warlock reshape a weapon's edge mid-fight, trading it between a duller, tougher form and a sharper, more brittle one at will.
- **Tertiary** — anything else forged from or touched by [[Iron]]: ordinary tools, common ore. Raises power only, no unique ability.
