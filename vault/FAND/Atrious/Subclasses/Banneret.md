# Banneret

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Knights who inspire and rally allies. (Printed in SCAG under a setting-specific name; later reprinted as Banneret.)

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Rallying Cry | When you use Second Wind, nearby allies also regain hit points. |
| 7 | Royal Envoy | Persuasion proficiency with doubled proficiency bonus. |
| 10 | Inspiring Surge | When you Action Surge, an ally can make an attack as a reaction. |
| 15 | Bulwark | When you use Indomitable, an ally can reroll a failed save too. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A leader-style fighter.
