# Aberrant Mind

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Plasma]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Sorcery from a psionic taint — telepathy and mind magic.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Psionic Spells | Gain a set of mind-focused spells. |
| 1 | Telepathic Speech | Create a telepathic link with a nearby creature for a while. |
| 6 | Psionic Sorcery | Cast psionic spells using sorcery points, without verbal or somatic components. |
| 6 | Psychic Defenses | Resist psychic damage; advantage against charm and fear. |
| 14 | Revelation in Flesh | Spend sorcery points to reshape your body for flight, swimming, or squeezing. |
| 18 | Warping Implosion | Teleport and pull nearby creatures toward your original position, damaging them. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A telepathic sorcerer with a strong teleport-crush capstone.
