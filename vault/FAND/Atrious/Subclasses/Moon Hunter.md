# Moon Hunter

## Overview
Moon Hunter expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Ranger class.

## Theme
A ranger who tracks best under moonlight, close kin to old werewolf-hunter lore.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature        | Effect                                                              |
| ----- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3     | Moonlit Step   | Bonus to stealth and perception at night.                           |
| 6     | Silver Arrow   | Once per rest, one attack deals extra damage to shapechangers.      |
| 10    | Lunar Focus    | Advantage on attacks against a marked target while under moonlight. |
| 14    | Full Moon Fury | Briefly gain an extra attack each round.                            |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
