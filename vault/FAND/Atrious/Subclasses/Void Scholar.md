# Void Scholar

## Overview
Void Scholar expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Wizard class.

## Theme
A wizard who studies the Void as an academic subject rather than a power to wield lightly.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Empty Study | Advantage on checks to identify Void-related magic. |
| 6 | Null Ward | Once per rest, gain resistance to force damage. |
| 10 | Fold Space | A short teleport usable once per rest without a spell slot. |
| 14 | Unravel | Briefly suppress a magical effect on a target. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
