# Oath of Vengeance

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Paladins sworn to punish those who have committed grievous sins.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Abjure Enemy | Frighten a creature within 60 ft and drop its speed to 0 if it fails a Wisdom save. |
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Vow of Enmity | Gain advantage on attack rolls against one creature for 1 minute. |
| 7 | Relentless Avenger | After hitting with an opportunity attack, move half your speed without provoking. |
| 15 | Soul of Vengeance | Reaction: attack a creature under your Vow of Enmity when it attacks. |
| 20 | Avenging Angel | Grow wings, fly, and radiate an aura that frightens enemies. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A single-target hunter paladin.
