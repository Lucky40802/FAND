# Void Knight

## Overview
Void Knight expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Fighter class.

## Theme
A fighter touched by the Void rather than trained by any order.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Void Step | A short teleport, once per rest. |
| 6 | Null Strike | Once per rest, one attack ignores resistance. |
| 10 | Unmaking Guard | Briefly gain resistance to all damage types at once. |
| 14 | Collapse the Line | Open a small rift that pulls enemies toward the knight. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
