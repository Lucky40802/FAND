# Twilight Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Clerics who guard the night — protectors against darkness and despair.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonus Proficiencies | Martial weapons and heavy armor. |
| 1 | Eyes of Night | 300 ft darkvision, shareable with nearby allies. |
| 1 | Vigilant Blessing | Give a creature advantage on its next initiative roll. |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Twilight Sanctuary | A 30 ft sphere of twilight for 1 minute; creatures inside gain temporary hit points or shake off charm and fear at the end of their turns. |
| 6 | Steps of Night | In dim light or darkness, sprout wings and fly for a minute (limited uses). |
| 8 | Divine Strike | Once per turn, weapon hits deal extra 1d8 radiant damage (2d8 at 14th). |
| 17 | Twilight Shroud | You and allies within the sanctuary have half cover. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A vigil-keeping support cleric with strong area protection.
