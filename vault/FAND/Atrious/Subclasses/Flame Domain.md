# Flame Domain

## Overview
Flame Domain expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Cleric class.

## Theme
A cleric who channels fire rather than a single domain of a living god.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Kindling Touch | Melee spell attacks add minor fire damage. |
| 6 | Ember Ward | Resistance to fire for self and one ally. |
| 10 | Wildfire | A spreading area of fire damage over several turns. |
| 14 | Phoenix Breath | A burst centered on self that heals and burns at once. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
