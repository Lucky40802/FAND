# Way of Echoes

## Overview
Way of Echoes expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Monk class.

## Theme
A monk who leaves an afterimage of every motion.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Echo Step | Leave a brief afterimage that can absorb one attack. |
| 6 | Doubled Strike | Occasionally land an extra unarmed strike as an echo. |
| 10 | Lingering Form | The echo can make one attack of its own, once per rest. |
| 14 | Chorus | Briefly create several echoes for a flurry of extra strikes. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
