# Path of the Storm Herald

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Lightning]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
A barbarian whose rage summons a storm aura — this vault leans on the Sea variant.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Storm Aura | While raging, bonus action to create an aura around you. Sea: lightning damage to a creature of your choice in the aura, with a Dexterity save for half. |
| 6 | Storm Soul | Sea: resistance to lightning damage, breathe underwater, and gain a swimming speed. |
| 10 | Shielding Storm | Allies in your aura gain the resistances your storm soul grants. |
| 14 | Raging Storm | Sea: when a creature in your aura hits you, it must succeed on a Strength save or be knocked prone. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An aura barbarian — the storm around you does damage and protects your allies.
