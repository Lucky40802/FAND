# College of Ash

## Overview
College of Ash expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Bard class.

## Theme
A bard who performs funeral rites and battlefield dirges.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Dirge | A performance that weakens enemies' resolve with a minor fear effect. |
| 6 | Ashen Echo | Recall a fallen ally's voice for a small bonus to another ally. |
| 10 | Cinderpath | Allies moving through the performance gain temporary fire resistance. |
| 14 | Requiem | A powerful performance that weakens foes and steadies allies at once. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
