# Way of the Empty Palm

## Overview
Way of the Empty Palm expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Monk class.

## Theme
A monk who trains around having nothing left to hold onto.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Empty Grip | Disarm attempts no longer provoke opportunity attacks and succeed more easily. |
| 6 | Hollow Guard | After seeing an attack, briefly gain resistance to its damage type. |
| 10 | Formless Strike | Once per rest, an unarmed strike that can't be parried or blocked. |
| 14 | Nothing to Hold | Briefly immune to being grappled, restrained, or disarmed. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
