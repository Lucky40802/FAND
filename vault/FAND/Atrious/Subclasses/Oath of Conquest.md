# Oath of Conquest

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Paladins who seek to crush chaos with fear and iron will.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Conquering Presence | Frighten creatures of your choice within 30 ft. |
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Guided Strike | Add +10 to an attack roll. |
| 7 | Aura of Conquest | Frightened creatures near you cannot move and take psychic damage each turn. |
| 15 | Scornful Rebuke | Creatures that hit you take psychic damage. |
| 20 | Invincible Conqueror | For 1 minute: resistance to all damage, an extra attack, and improved critical range. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A fear-and-control paladin.
