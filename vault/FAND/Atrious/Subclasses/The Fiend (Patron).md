# The Fiend (Patron)

*Official SRD subclass — Warlock — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

*Tied to the [[FAND/Runes/Fear|Fear]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
A pact with a devil, demon, or other fiend from the lower planes — a patron who rewards dealing death with resilience and, eventually, the power to banish enemies outright.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Expanded Spell List | Additional spells (such as Burning Hands, Command, Fireball, Suggestion) always available to choose when learning warlock spells. |
| 1 | Dark One's Blessing | When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, gain temporary hit points equal to Charisma modifier + warlock level (minimum 1). |
| 6 | Dark One's Own Luck | Add 1d10 to one ability check or saving throw, once per short/long rest. |
| 10 | Fiendish Resilience | Choose one damage type at the end of a short/long rest; resistant to that type until the choice changes. |
| 14 | Hurl Through Hell | On hitting a creature with an attack, banish it to the lower planes for a moment; on return it takes 10d10 psychic damage unless it's a fiend already familiar with those planes. Usable once per long rest. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A durability- and damage-focused patron — thrives in prolonged fights, with a devastating (if once-per-day) execute-style capstone.
