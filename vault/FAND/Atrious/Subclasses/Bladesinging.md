# Bladesinging

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Elven-style wizards who fuse swordplay with spellcasting.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Training in War and Song | Light armor, one one-handed melee weapon, and Performance. |
| 2 | Bladesong | Bonus action: for 1 minute gain AC, speed, and Acrobatics/concentration bonuses from Intelligence. |
| 6 | Extra Attack | Attack twice; replace one attack with a cantrip. |
| 10 | Song of Defense | Reaction: spend a spell slot to reduce damage you take. |
| 14 | Song of Victory | Add Intelligence modifier to melee damage while using Bladesong. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A gish wizard with excellent defense.
