# Diamond's Patron

## Overview
Diamond's Patron is an Otherworldly Patron for the [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Warlock|Warlock]] class — the patron being [[Diamond]]'s shattered remains rather than a Fiend or Great Old One.

## Theme
Unlocked by acquisition rather than level, following the [[Class]] system's Primary/Secondary/Tertiary tiers. A Diamond Patron warlock treats Diamond himself, not [[Gnome]], as the progenitor.

## Acquisition Tiers
- **Primary** — an actual shard of [[Diamond]]'s shattered body. Lets the warlock harden any part of themselves to true diamond hardness for a moment, enough to no-sell a single hit rather than just blunt it.
- **Secondary** — something Diamond wore or shaped before he was broken, rather than a piece of him directly. Lets the warlock refract light off their own skin, hard enough to blind at close range.
- **Tertiary** — anything else cut from or shaped like diamond, real or cheap imitation. Raises power only, no unique ability.
