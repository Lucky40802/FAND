# Path of the Battlerager

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Armored brawlers who fight in spiked armor. (Originally dwarf-only in the book; many tables drop that restriction.)

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Battlerager Armor | While raging in spiked armor, make a bonus-action spike attack, and grappling deals piercing damage. |
| 6 | Reckless Abandon | Gain temporary hit points when you use Reckless Attack while raging. |
| 10 | Battlerager Charge | Dash as a bonus action while raging. |
| 14 | Spiked Retribution | Creatures that hit you in melee while you rage in spiked armor take piercing damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A grappler who turns their own body into a weapon.
