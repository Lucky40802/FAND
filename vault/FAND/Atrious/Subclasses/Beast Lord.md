# Beast Lord

## Overview
Beast Lord expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Ranger class.

## Theme
A ranger who leads a pack rather than a single companion.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Pack Bond | Gain a second beast companion, each individually weaker. |
| 6 | Coordinated Strike | Both companions can attack the same target for a small bonus. |
| 10 | Alpha's Call | Companions gain temporary hit points when the Ranger drops one. |
| 14 | Feral Legion | Briefly summon a small pack of minor beasts. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
