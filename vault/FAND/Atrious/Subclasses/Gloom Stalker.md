# Gloom Stalker

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Rangers who hunt in darkness and strike first.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Dread Ambusher | Bonus to initiative and an extra attack and damage on your first turn. |
| 3 | Umbral Sight | Darkvision; unseen by creatures relying on darkvision. |
| 7 | Iron Mind | Proficiency in Wisdom saves (or another save). |
| 11 | Stalker's Flurry | Once per turn on a missed attack, make another attack. |
| 15 | Shadowy Dodge | Reaction: impose disadvantage on an attack against you. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An ambush-first ranger.
