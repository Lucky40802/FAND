# Storm Reaver

## Overview
Storm Reaver expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Barbarian class.

## Theme
A barbarian whose rage calls down lightning rather than just muscle.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Storm's Fury | Raging attacks occasionally arc lightning to a nearby foe. |
| 6 | Thunderclap | A shout that briefly deafens nearby enemies. |
| 10 | Static Charge | Resistance to lightning while raging. |
| 14 | Eye of the Storm | Once per rest, a burst of lightning damage to all nearby enemies. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
