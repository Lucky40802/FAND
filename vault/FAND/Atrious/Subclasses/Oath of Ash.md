# Oath of Ash

## Overview
Oath of Ash expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Paladin class.

## Theme
A paladin sworn to scorched earth rather than mercy.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Ashen Smite | Smites deal extra fire damage and leave lingering cinders. |
| 6 | Cinder Ward | Resistance to fire, once per rest. |
| 10 | Scorched Aura | Enemies near the paladin take minor fire damage each turn. |
| 14 | Immolation | A final burst of fire released the moment the paladin is dropped to 0 hit points. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
