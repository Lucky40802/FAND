# The Hexblade (Patron)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A pact with a sentient weapon from the Shadowfell.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hexblade's Curse | Bonus action curse on a creature: bonus damage, better crits, and hit points when it dies. |
| 1 | Hex Warrior | Proficiency with medium armor, shields, and martial weapons; use Charisma for one weapon attack stat. |
| 6 | Accursed Specter | A slain humanoid can rise as a specter ally until your next long rest. |
| 10 | Armor of Hexes | A hexed target that attacks you may miss automatically. |
| 14 | Master of Hexes | When a cursed creature dies, transfer the curse to another creature. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A melee warlock using Charisma to hit.
