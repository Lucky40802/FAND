# School of Enchantment (Wizard)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards who charm and bewilder.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Enchantment Savant | Copy enchantment spells cheaply. |
| 2 | Hypnotic Gaze | Charm and incapacitate a nearby creature. |
| 6 | Instinctive Charm | Reaction: redirect an attack against you to another creature. |
| 10 | Split Enchantment | Single-target enchantments can target two creatures. |
| 14 | Alter Memories | Make charmed creatures forget the charm. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A social-control wizard.
