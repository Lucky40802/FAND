# Way of the Ascendant Dragon

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Fizban's Treasury of Dragons. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Monks who emulate dragons.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Draconic Disciple | Change unarmed damage to an element; reroll failed social checks. |
| 3 | Breath of the Dragon | Replace an attack with an elemental breath. |
| 6 | Wings Unfurled | Fly while using Step of the Wind. |
| 11 | Aspect of the Wyrm | Aura of fear or elemental resistance. |
| 17 | Ascendant Aspect | Stronger breath and resistance aura. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An elemental, mobile monk.
