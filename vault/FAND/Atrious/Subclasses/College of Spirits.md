# College of Spirits

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Storytellers who channel spirits through their tales.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Guiding Whispers | Learn the guidance cantrip at longer range. |
| 3 | Spiritual Focus | Use a candle, crystal ball, skull, or similar as a focus; later it boosts damage and healing spells. |
| 3 | Tales from Beyond | Spend Bardic Inspiration to roll on a table of spirit tales and hold one for later use. |
| 6 | Spirit Session | Hold a ritual with others to learn a divination or necromancy spell temporarily. |
| 14 | Mystical Connection | Roll twice on the tales table and choose. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A random-but-flavorful spooky bard.
