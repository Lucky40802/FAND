# Circle of Spores

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Druids who embrace decay as part of the natural cycle.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Halo of Spores | Reaction: a creature that moves into or starts its turn within 10 ft takes necrotic damage unless it passes a Constitution save. |
| 2 | Symbiotic Entity | Spend a Wild Shape use to gain temporary hit points and empower your halo and melee attacks for 10 minutes. |
| 6 | Fungal Infestation | When a small or medium creature dies near you, raise it as a spore-zombie for an hour (limited uses). |
| 10 | Spreading Spores | While your entity is active, hurl spores into a 10 ft cube that keeps damaging creatures inside. |
| 14 | Fungal Body | Immune to being blinded, deafened, frightened, or poisoned; critical hits against you become normal hits. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An aura-damage druid with a small undead army.
