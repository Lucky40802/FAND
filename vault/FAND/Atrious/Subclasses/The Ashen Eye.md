# The Ashen Eye

## Overview
The Ashen Eye expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Warlock class.

## Theme
A patron that is little more than a burnt-out watcher, seeing everything and wanting little.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Cinder Sight | See through smoke, ash, and fog without penalty. |
| 6 | Ashen Bolt | Eldritch blast leaves a lingering ember that burns at the start of the target's next turn. |
| 10 | Watcher's Warning | Once per rest, sense danger a moment before it happens. |
| 14 | Eye of Ruin | Briefly see the entire surrounding area, even through walls. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
