# School of Evocation

*Official SRD subclass — Wizard — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

*Tied to the [[Frost]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
A wizard specialized in raw destructive magic, with the rare ability to protect allies from their own area spells.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Evocation Savant | Half the gold and time cost to copy an evocation spell into your spellbook. |
| 2 | Sculpt Spells | When you cast an evocation spell that affects other creatures you can see, choose a number of them equal to 1 + the spell's level; they automatically succeed on the save and take no damage if they would normally take half on a success. |
| 6 | Potent Cantrip | Creatures that succeed on a save against your cantrips still take half damage (if the cantrip deals damage). |
| 10 | Empowered Evocation | Add Intelligence modifier to the damage of one evocation spell you cast. |
| 14 | Overchannel | Cast a spell of 5th level or lower at maximum damage; the second time in the same day you do this with the same spell (and every time after), you take necrotic damage equal to twice the spell's level. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The purest blaster wizard subclass — allies stand inside your fireballs safely, and Overchannel gives one huge guaranteed-max-damage nova per encounter.
