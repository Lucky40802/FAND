# The Genie (Patron)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A patron who is a noble genie of one of the four elements.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Genie's Vessel | A small vessel you can enter to rest; add elemental damage once per turn. |
| 6 | Elemental Gift | Resistance based on genie kind; fly briefly. |
| 10 | Sanctuary Vessel | Bring allies into the vessel; resting there is faster. |
| 14 | Limited Wish | Cast any 6th-level-or-lower spell once per long rest. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A flexible, elemental warlock.
