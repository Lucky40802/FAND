# Ashen Commander

## Overview
Ashen Commander expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Fighter class.

## Theme
A battlefield leader wreathed in ash and embers.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Ashen Banner | Allies within reach gain advantage on their next save against fear. |
| 6 | Scorched Ground | Where the commander stands, the ground burns anything that crosses it. |
| 10 | Rally Through Fire | Spend a reaction to let a nearby ally act again, at reduced effect. |
| 14 | Last Stand | Below half health, deal extra damage and shrug off one condition per turn. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
