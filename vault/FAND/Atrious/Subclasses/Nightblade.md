# Nightblade

## Overview
Nightblade expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Rogue class.

## Theme
A rogue who moves through shadow rather than crowds.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Shadow Step | Teleport a short distance between dim light or darkness, once per rest. |
| 6 | Silent Strike | A hit from stealth briefly silences the target. |
| 10 | Umbral Cloak | Briefly turn invisible while in dim light or darkness. |
| 14 | Executioner's Dark | A sneak attack against a badly wounded foe can drop it outright. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
