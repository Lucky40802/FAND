# College of Dreams

## Overview
College of Dreams expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Bard class.

## Theme
A bard who plays on sleep and dream rather than waking emotion.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Lullaby | A performance that can lightly drowse a weak enemy. |
| 6 | Dreamwalk | Briefly view a sleeping creature's dream for information. |
| 10 | Nightmare Verse | Force a save or the target suffers disadvantage from intrusive visions. |
| 14 | Waking Dream | Briefly blur reality, granting each ally advantage on one roll. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
