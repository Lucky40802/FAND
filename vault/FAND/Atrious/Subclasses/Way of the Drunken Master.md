*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Monks with an unpredictable, swaying style.

| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Bonus Proficiencies | Performance and brewer's supplies. |
| 3 | Drunken Technique | Flurry of Blows grants Disengage and extra speed. |

| 17 | Intoxicated Frenzy | Flurry of Blows can make extra attacks against different creatures. |
