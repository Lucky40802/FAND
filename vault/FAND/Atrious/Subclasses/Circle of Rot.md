# Circle of Rot

## Overview
Circle of Rot expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Druid class.

## Theme
A druid who tends decay the way others tend growth.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Withering Touch | Melee attacks briefly sicken the target. |
| 6 | Spore Cloud | A small area that damages and blinds. |
| 10 | Rotstep | Wild Shape into blighted forms with a minor extra effect. |
| 14 | Bloom of Decay | An area that heals allies while damaging enemies over time. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
