# Forge Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Clerics of smithing gods who craft and armor.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonus Proficiencies | Heavy armor and smith's tools. |
| 1 | Blessing of the Forge | Once per long rest, make a weapon or armor +1 until your next long rest. |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Artisan's Blessing | Hour-long ritual to create a simple metal item. |
| 6 | Soul of the Forge | Fire resistance and +1 AC in heavy armor. |
| 8 | Divine Strike | Extra fire damage once per turn on weapon hits. |
| 17 | Saint of Forge and Fire | Fire immunity and resistance to nonmagical weapon damage in heavy armor. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The highest-AC cleric; a front-line smith.
