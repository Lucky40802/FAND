# Eldritch Knight

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A fighter who supplements martial skill with wizard spells.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Spellcasting | Third-caster using wizard spells (mostly abjuration and evocation), Intelligence-based. |
| 3 | Weapon Bond | Bond with weapons so they cannot be disarmed, and summon them to your hand as a bonus action. |
| 7 | War Magic | After casting a cantrip, make one weapon attack as a bonus action. |
| 10 | Eldritch Strike | A creature you hit has disadvantage on its next save against a spell you cast before the end of your next turn. |
| 15 | Arcane Charge | When you use Action Surge, you can teleport up to 30 ft. |
| 18 | Improved War Magic | After casting any spell, make one weapon attack as a bonus action. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The classic sword-and-spell hybrid.
