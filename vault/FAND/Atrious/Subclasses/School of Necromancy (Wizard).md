## Overview
Wizards who study the magic of death and undeath. (File is named to avoid clashing with this vault's Necromancy Rune and spell school.)

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Necromancy Savant | Halve the cost and time to copy necromancy spells. |
| 2 | Grim Harvest | When you kill a creature with a spell, regain hit points equal to twice the spell's level (thrice for necromancy). |
| 6 | Undead Thralls | Animate dead extra corpses; your undead gain hit points and damage. |
| 10 | Inured to Undeath | Resist necrotic damage; your hit point maximum can't be reduced. |
| 14 | Command Undead | Take control of an undead creature that fails a Charisma save. |
