# Path of Ruin

## Overview
Path of Ruin expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Barbarian class.

## Theme
A barbarian who rages to destroy, not just to fight.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Ruinous Blow | Critical hits while raging also wreck a small part of the surroundings. |
| 6 | Wake of Destruction | Difficult terrain doesn't slow the barbarian while raging. |
| 10 | Collapse | A heavy attack can knock every creature in a small area prone. |
| 14 | Cataclysm | Once per long rest, a single massive attack against everything adjacent. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
