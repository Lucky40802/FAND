# Champion

*Official SRD subclass — Fighter — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

## Overview
The simplest and most consistent Fighter subclass — raw physical excellence rather than a trick or a resource to manage.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Improved Critical | Weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. |
| 7 | Remarkable Athlete | Add half proficiency bonus (round up) to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution checks not already proficient; running long jump distance increases by Strength modifier in feet. |
| 10 | Additional Fighting Style | Learn a second Fighting Style option. |
| 15 | Superior Critical | Critical hit range expands to 18–20. |
| 18 | Survivor | At the start of each turn, regain hit points equal to 5 + Constitution modifier if at or below half hit point maximum (and above 0). |

## Gameplay Identity
- No resource management, no complexity — the subclass for players who want to just hit things reliably and well.
