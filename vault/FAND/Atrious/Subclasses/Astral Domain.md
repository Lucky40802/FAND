# Astral Domain

## Overview
Astral Domain expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Cleric class.

## Theme
A cleric who channels starlight rather than a single god's word.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Starlight Ward | A bonus-action shield that absorbs a small amount of damage. |
| 6 | Constellation Sight | See further in darkness and faintly sense invisible creatures. |
| 10 | Gravity Well | Pull nearby enemies toward a point, once per rest. |
| 14 | Astral Form | Briefly become incorporeal, immune to non-magical damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
