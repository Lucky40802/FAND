# Beast Master

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Rangers who bond with a beast companion.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Ranger's Companion | Gain a beast companion (CR 1/4 or lower) that acts on your command. |
| 7 | Exceptional Training | Command your beast to Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Help as a bonus action; its attacks count as magical. |
| 11 | Bestial Fury | Your beast can attack twice. |
| 15 | Share Spells | Spells you cast on yourself also affect your companion. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A pet-class ranger.
