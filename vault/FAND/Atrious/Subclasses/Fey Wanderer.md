# Fey Wanderer

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Rangers marked by the Feywild — charming, mobile, and unsettling.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Dreadful Strikes | Once per turn, weapon hits deal extra psychic damage. |
| 3 | Otherworldly Glamour | Add Wisdom modifier to Charisma checks and gain a social skill proficiency. |
| 7 | Beguiling Twist | Advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened; redirect the effect on a failed attempt. |
| 11 | Fey Reinforcements | Cast summon fey once per long rest without material or concentration. |
| 15 | Misty Wanderer | Cast misty step a limited number of times, bringing an ally along. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A mobile, social-friendly ranger.
