# Clockwork Soul

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Sorcerers infused with the power of cosmic order.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clockwork Magic | Bonus order-themed spells. |
| 1 | Restore Balance | Reaction: cancel advantage or disadvantage on a roll. |
| 6 | Bastion of Law | Spend sorcery points to create a ward that absorbs damage. |
| 14 | Trance of Order | Treat low d20 rolls as 10 and attacks against you lose advantage. |
| 18 | Clockwork Cavalcade | Heal, repair, and end spells in an area. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A defensive, order-themed sorcerer.
