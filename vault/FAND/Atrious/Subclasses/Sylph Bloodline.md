# Sylph Bloodline

## Overview
Sylph Bloodline is a Sorcerous Origin for the [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] class, tracing its power to [[Air]] and the dead air spirit [[Sylph]].

## Theme
Unlocked by acquisition rather than level, following the [[Class]] system's Primary/Secondary/Tertiary tiers.

## Acquisition Tiers
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Sylph]]'s own remains. Lets the sorcerer fall as slowly as they like, landing from any height without being hurt by it.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to one of Sylph's children — [[Boreas]], [[Zephyrus]], [[Notus]], [[Eurus]], or [[Vortex]] itself — rather than Sylph directly. Grants a small taste of that specific child's own power rather than plain wind.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Air]]: ordinary wind, a feather, open sky. Raises power only, no unique ability.
