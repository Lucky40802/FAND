# Graviturgy Magic

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Explorer's Guide to Wildemount. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Gravity]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Wizards who manipulate gravity.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Adjust Density | Concentrate to halve or double an object or creature's weight. |
| 6 | Gravity Well | Once per turn when you hit a creature with a spell, move it 5 ft. |
| 10 | Violent Attraction | Reaction: increase damage from a weapon hit or fall near you. |
| 14 | Event Horizon | Once per long rest, crush and pull nearby creatures toward you, damaging them. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A positioning-control wizard.
