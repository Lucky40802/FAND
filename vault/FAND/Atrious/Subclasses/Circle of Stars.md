# Circle of Stars

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Druids who draw power from the constellations.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Star Map | A star-map spellcasting focus; learn guidance and guiding bolt (free castings). |
| 2 | Starry Form | Spend a Wild Shape use to take on a constellation for 10 minutes: Archer (ranged radiant attack), Chalice (extra healing on nearby ally), or Dragon (stability on concentration and Int/Wis/Con checks). |
| 6 | Cosmic Omen | After a long rest, get a Weal or Woe omen: add or subtract a d6 from a nearby creature's roll as a reaction. |
| 10 | Twinkling Constellations | Constellation dice improve to 2d8 and you can swap forms each turn. |
| 14 | Full of Stars | While in Starry Form, you resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A flexible support-and-blast druid built around swapping constellations.
