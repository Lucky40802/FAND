# Soul Mechanist

## Overview
Soul Mechanist expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Artificer class.

## Theme
An artificer who powers constructs with captured fragments of spirit rather than raw mechanism.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Bound Spirit | A minor construct companion powered by a captured wisp of spirit. |
| 6 | Soul Battery | Spend hit points to power an extra infusion charge. |
| 10 | Animate Vessel | The bound spirit can briefly possess a nearby inanimate object. |
| 14 | Greater Vessel | The construct companion gains a significant, temporary power boost. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
