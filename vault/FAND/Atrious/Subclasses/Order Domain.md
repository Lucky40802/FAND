# Order Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Clerics of law, discipline, and authority.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonus Proficiency | Heavy armor. |
| 1 | Voice of Authority | When you cast a spell of 1st level or higher on an ally, that ally can use its reaction to make a weapon attack. |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Order's Demand | Creatures of your choice within 30 ft must pass a Wisdom save or be charmed until the end of your next turn and drop what they hold. |
| 6 | Embodiment of the Law | Cast an enchantment spell you normally cast as an action as a bonus action instead (limited uses per long rest). |
| 8 | Divine Strike | Once per turn, weapon hits deal extra 1d8 psychic damage (2d8 at 14th). |
| 17 | Order's Wrath | A creature you hit with Divine Strike is cursed; the next ally to hit it deals extra 2d8 psychic damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A command-and-control cleric that turns allies into extra attackers.
