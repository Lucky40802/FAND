# Rune Sage

## Overview
Rune Sage expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Wizard class.

## Theme
A wizard who studies runes as a second, slower spellbook.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Runic Focus | Inscribe a minor rune granting a small stat bonus for a short time. |
| 6 | Ward Rune | A temporary rune that absorbs a small amount of damage. |
| 10 | Runic Recall | Once per rest, recover a spell slot by consuming a prepared rune. |
| 14 | Master Glyph | Inscribe a rune carrying one powerful, one-time effect. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
