# Way of Mercy

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Monks who wear masks and act as both healers and executioners.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Implements of Mercy | Proficiency in Insight and Medicine and the herbalism kit. |
| 3 | Hand of Healing | Spend ki to heal a creature by touch (Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier). |
| 3 | Hand of Harm | Once per turn on a hit, spend ki to deal extra necrotic damage. |
| 6 | Physician's Touch | Hand of Healing ends several conditions; Hand of Harm can poison. |
| 11 | Flurry of Healing and Harm | Replace Flurry of Blows strikes with free Hand of Healing uses, and Hand of Harm once per flurry for free. |
| 17 | Hand of Ultimate Mercy | Spend 5 ki to revive a creature dead for under 24 hours (once per long rest). |

## Gameplay Identity
- A healer-monk with a killer instinct.
