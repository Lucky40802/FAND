# Battle Master

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A martial tactician with a pool of combat maneuvers.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Combat Superiority | Gain superiority dice (d8) and learn maneuvers; spend a die to add it to damage and trigger the maneuver's effect. |
| 3 | Student of War | Proficiency with one artisan's tool. |
| 7 | Know Your Enemy | After observing a creature for a minute out of combat, learn how it compares to you in two chosen stats. |
| 10 | Improved Combat Superiority | Superiority dice become d10; learn more maneuvers. |
| 15 | Relentless | Regain a superiority die when you roll initiative with none left. |
| 18 | Improved Combat Superiority | Superiority dice become d12. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The most tactical fighter — many small tricks instead of one big one.
