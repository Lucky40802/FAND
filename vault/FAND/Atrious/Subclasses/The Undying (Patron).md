# The Undying (Patron)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A patron who has cheated death.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Among the Dead | Learn spare the dying; undead have trouble attacking you. |
| 6 | Defy Death | Regain hit points when you succeed on a death save or stabilize someone. |
| 10 | Undying Nature | Need no food, water, or air; age slowly. |
| 14 | Indestructible Life | Bonus action: regain hit points and reattach limbs. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A durable, death-flavored warlock.
