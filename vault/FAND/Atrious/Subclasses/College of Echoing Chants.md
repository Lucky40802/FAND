# College of Echoing Chants

## Overview
College of Echoing Chants expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Bard class.

## Theme
A bard whose magic repeats and layers rather than striking once.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Echoed Word | A cast spell can be repeated at half effect against a second target. |
| 6 | Layered Verse | Performances last a round longer than normal. |
| 10 | Resonant Chant | Allies affected by the performance also gain minor temporary hit points. |
| 14 | Grand Chorus | The next performance affects everyone in a wide radius. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
