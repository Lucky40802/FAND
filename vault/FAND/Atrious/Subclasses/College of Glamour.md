# College of Glamour

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Happiness]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Bards who wield fey enchantment — beauty, charm, and command.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Mantle of Inspiration | Spend a Bardic Inspiration die as a bonus action: several creatures gain temporary hit points and may move without provoking opportunity attacks. |
| 3 | Enthralling Performance | After a 1-minute performance, humanoids who fail a Wisdom save are charmed for an hour and think of you as a friend. |
| 6 | Mantle of Majesty | Bonus action: for 1 minute, cast command each turn as a bonus action without expending a slot (once per long rest). |
| 14 | Unbreakable Majesty | Bonus action: for 1 minute, creatures must pass a Charisma save before they can attack you or target you with a harmful spell. |

## Gameplay Identity
- Crowd control by charm and command rather than damage.
