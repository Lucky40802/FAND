# College of Dance

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook (2024). Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Bards whose performance is movement: dancers who fight as they perform.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Dazzling Footwork | While unarmored and without a shield, add Charisma to AC; your unarmed strikes use your Bardic Inspiration die and can use Dexterity. |
| 6 | Inspiring Movement | Reaction when an enemy ends its turn near you: move without provoking, and an ally can move too. |
| 6 | Tandem Footwork | When you roll initiative, spend Bardic Inspiration to add the die to your and your allies' initiative. |
| 14 | Leading Evasion | You and nearby allies take no damage on successful Dexterity saves against area effects, and half on failures. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A mobile melee bard who fights unarmored.
