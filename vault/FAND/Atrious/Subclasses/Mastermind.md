# Mastermind

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Rogues who play the game of intrigue and command from the back line.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Master of Intrigue | Proficiency with disguise kit, forgery kit, and a gaming set; learn two languages and mimic speech patterns. |
| 3 | Master of Tactics | Use the Help action as a bonus action from 30 ft away. |
| 9 | Insightful Manipulator | After observing a creature for a minute, learn how it compares to you in two stats. |
| 13 | Misdirection | Reaction: redirect an attack aimed at you to a creature giving you cover. |
| 17 | Soul of Deceit | Your thoughts can't be read; you can present false thoughts and evade truth-detecting magic. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A support rogue built on info and coordination.
