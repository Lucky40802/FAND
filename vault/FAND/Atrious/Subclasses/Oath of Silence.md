# Oath of Silence

## Overview
Oath of Silence expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Paladin class.

## Theme
A paladin sworn to a quiet, unseen justice.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Hushed Step | No stealth disadvantage while in medium armor. |
| 6 | Silent Smite | A smite that provokes no opportunity attacks and makes no sound. |
| 10 | Muted Ward | Briefly silence an area, blocking spellcasting there. |
| 14 | Unseen Judgment | Once per rest, an attack against an unaware target lands as a critical hit. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
