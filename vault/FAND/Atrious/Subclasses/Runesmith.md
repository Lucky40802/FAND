# Runesmith

## Overview
Runesmith expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Artificer class.

## Theme
An artificer who etches power into gear rather than building it in.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Runic Etching | Imbue a weapon or armor with a minor temporary bonus. |
| 6 | Dual Rune | Etch two items at once, each at reduced potency. |
| 10 | Lasting Rune | Etchings now last until the next long rest instead of one hour. |
| 14 | Grand Etching | One etched item gains a significant, if temporary, magical property. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
