# Circle of the Sea

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook (2024). Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Druids of ocean and storm, carrying the sea's fury with them.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Wrath of the Sea | Spend a Wild Shape use to wrap yourself in an aura of ocean spray; creatures in it take cold damage and can be pushed away. |
| 6 | Aquatic Affinity | The aura grows, and you gain a swimming speed. |
| 10 | Stormborn | While the aura is active, you gain a flying speed and resistance to cold, lightning, and thunder damage. |
| 14 | Oceanic Gift | Grant the aura to an ally instead of yourself, or to both of you at a higher cost. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A storm-and-sea controller druid.
