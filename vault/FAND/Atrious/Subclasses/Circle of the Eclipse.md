# Circle of the Eclipse

## Overview
Circle of the Eclipse expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Druid class.

## Theme
A druid balanced between a light aspect and a dark one.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Umbral Shift | Swap between a light-boon and dark-boon stance once per turn. |
| 6 | Eclipse Step | Teleport a short distance through shadow, once per rest. |
| 10 | Twin Aspect | Wild Shape gains a minor radiant or necrotic effect depending on stance. |
| 14 | Total Eclipse | Briefly blind every creature in a radius except allies. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
