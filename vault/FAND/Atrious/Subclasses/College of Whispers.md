# College of Whispers

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Bards who trade in fear, secrets, and stolen identities.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Psychic Blades | Once per turn on a weapon hit, spend a Bardic Inspiration die to deal extra psychic damage (scaling with level). |
| 3 | Words of Terror | After talking with a humanoid for a minute, it must pass a Wisdom save or be frightened of you or another creature you name (once per rest). |
| 6 | Mantle of Whispers | When a humanoid dies near you, capture its shadow and later take on its appearance for an hour. |
| 14 | Shadow Lore | Charm a creature that can hear you for 8 hours if it fails a Wisdom save; it obeys as if you were a friend (once per long rest). |

## Gameplay Identity
- An infiltration and intimidation bard.
