# The Hollow King

## Overview
The Hollow King expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Warlock class.

## Theme
A patron that is an empty crown and an empty throne, nothing more.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Hollow Command | Briefly compel a weak-willed creature to hesitate. |
| 6 | Empty Throne | Gain temporary hit points when an ally within sight drops to 0. |
| 10 | Crownless Authority | Once per rest, advantage on Intimidation and Persuasion. |
| 14 | Reign of Ash | Force several nearby enemies to kneel at once. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
