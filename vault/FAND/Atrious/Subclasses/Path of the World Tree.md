# Path of the World Tree

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook (2024). Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Barbarians who draw on the cosmic tree whose roots and branches connect the realms.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Vitality of the Tree | When you rage, gain temporary hit points; while raging, you can give an ally temporary hit points at the start of your turns. |
| 6 | Branches of the Tree | Reaction while raging: pull a creature you can see toward you by teleporting it next to you, and reduce its speed if it fails a save. |
| 10 | Battering Roots | Your reach with heavy and versatile melee weapons grows by 10 ft, and those hits can push or topple foes. |
| 14 | Travel Along the Tree | While raging, teleport a short distance as a bonus action; once per rage, teleport farther and bring allies. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A battlefield-controlling barbarian who moves friends and foes. In FAND, the World Tree's roots make natural sense as part of the Inner Realm.
