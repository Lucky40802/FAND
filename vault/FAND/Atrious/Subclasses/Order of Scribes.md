# Order of Scribes

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards whose spellbook is awakened and alive.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Wizardly Quill | A magic quill that copies spells quickly. |
| 2 | Awakened Spellbook | Swap a spell's damage type and cast rituals quickly. |
| 6 | Manifest Mind | Project the book's mind as a spectral point to cast from. |
| 10 | Master Scrivener | Create a scroll from your book after a rest. |
| 14 | One with the Word | Use the book to avoid damage, at the cost of forgetting spells temporarily. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A flexible wizard built around the spellbook.
