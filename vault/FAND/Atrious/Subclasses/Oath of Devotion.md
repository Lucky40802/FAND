# Oath of Devotion

*Official SRD subclass — Paladin — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

*Tied to the [[FAND/Runes/Heal|Heal]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
The classic paladin ideal — honesty, courage, and protecting the weak, backed by radiant power that scales into a genuine holy champion.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Sacred Weapon | Action: for 1 minute, add Charisma modifier to attack rolls with one weapon (minimum +1) and it emits bright light in a 20ft radius (dim light 20ft further). |
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Turn the Unholy | Action: each fiend or undead within 30ft that can see/hear you must succeed on a Wisdom save or be turned for 1 minute. |
| 7 | Aura of Devotion | You and allies within 10ft (30ft at 18th) can't be charmed while you're conscious. |
| 15 | Purity of Spirit | You are permanently under the effect of a protection from evil and good spell. |
| 20 | Holy Nimbus | Action: emanate sunlight for 1 minute — 30ft bright light, enemies starting their turn in it take 10 radiant damage, and you have advantage on saves against spells cast by fiends or undead. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The baseline paladin fantasy — reliable protection auras and a genuine capstone that turns you into a walking beacon.
