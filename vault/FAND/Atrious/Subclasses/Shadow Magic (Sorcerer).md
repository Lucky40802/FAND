# Shadow Magic (Sorcerer)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Sorcery touched by the Shadowfell. (File is named to avoid clashing with this vault's Shadow Magic spell school.)

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eyes of the Dark | Darkvision; later cast darkness cheaply and see through it. |
| 1 | Strength of the Grave | Once per long rest, when damage would drop you to 0, make a Charisma save to stay at 1 hit point instead. |
| 6 | Hound of Ill Omen | Spend sorcery points to summon a spectral hound that hunts a target. |
| 14 | Shadow Walk | Bonus action: teleport 120 ft between dim or dark spaces. |
| 18 | Umbral Form | Spend sorcery points to become shadow, resistant to nearly all damage and able to pass through creatures and objects. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A tough, darkness-based sorcerer that cheats death.
