# Way of the Open Hand

*Official SRD subclass — Monk — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

*Tied to the [[Balance]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
The archetypal martial artist monk — every Flurry of Blows hit can knock an opponent down, push them back, or shut off their reactions.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Open Hand Technique | On a Flurry of Blows hit, impose one: target makes a Dexterity save or falls Prone; target makes a Strength save or is pushed 15ft; target can't take reactions until the end of your next turn. |
| 6 | Wholeness of Body | Action: regain hit points equal to three times monk level, once per long rest. |
| 11 | Tranquility | At the end of a long rest, gain the effect of the sanctuary spell until the start of the next long rest (ends early if you attack or cast a spell). |
| 17 | Quivering Palm | Spend 3 ki points on a hit to set up lethal vibrations; later, as an action within a number of days equal to monk level, force a Constitution save or the target drops to 0 hit points (half damage instead on success). |

## Gameplay Identity
- The control-focused monk — every unarmed strike threatens to reposition or disable, culminating in a genuine execute at 17th level.
