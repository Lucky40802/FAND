# Artillerist

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Artificers who deploy magical cannons.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tool Proficiency | Woodcarver's tools. |
| 3 | Eldritch Cannon | Create a small cannon (flamethrower, force ballista, or protector) that acts on your command for an hour. |
| 5 | Arcane Firearm | A wand, staff, or rod used as focus adds extra damage to a spell. |
| 9 | Explosive Cannon | Cannon damage improves and it can detonate. |
| 15 | Fortified Position | You and allies near the cannon have half cover; you can create a second cannon. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A ranged-artillery artificer.
