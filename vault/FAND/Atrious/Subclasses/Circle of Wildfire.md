# Circle of Wildfire

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Druids of destructive and renewing flame.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Summon Wildfire Spirit | Spend Wild Shape to summon a fiery spirit that fights and can teleport allies. |
| 6 | Enhanced Bond | Fire and healing spells gain a bonus die and can originate from the spirit. |
| 10 | Cauterizing Flames | When creatures die near you, create flames that heal or burn. |
| 14 | Blazing Revival | When you drop to 0 HP, sacrifice the spirit to return with half your hit points. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A fire-and-healing druid with a pet.
