# Void Stalker

## Overview
Void Stalker expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Ranger class.

## Theme
A ranger who hunts things that came from the Void rather than the Mortal Realm.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Void Sight | See invisible and incorporeal creatures within a short range. |
| 6 | Null Arrow | Once per rest, one shot ignores resistance. |
| 10 | Step Between | A short teleport as part of movement, once per rest. |
| 14 | Erase | A called shot against a badly wounded foe deals heavy bonus damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
