# Knowledge Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Clerics of lore, learning, and secrets.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blessings of Knowledge | Two extra languages and expertise in two knowledge skills. |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Knowledge of the Ages | Gain proficiency with any skill or tool for 10 minutes. |
| 6 | Channel Divinity: Read Thoughts | Read a creature's surface thoughts and cast suggestion on it. |
| 8 | Potent Spellcasting | Add Wisdom modifier to cantrip damage. |
| 17 | Visions of the Past | Meditate to see the history of an object or place. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An investigator and skill-monkey cleric.
