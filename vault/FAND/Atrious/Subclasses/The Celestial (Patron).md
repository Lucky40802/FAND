# The Celestial (Patron)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A pact with a being from the Upper Planes.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonus Cantrips | Gain light and sacred flame. |
| 1 | Healing Light | A pool of d6s you spend to heal creatures within 60 ft as a bonus action. |
| 6 | Radiant Soul | Resist radiant damage; add Charisma modifier to fire and radiant spell damage. |
| 10 | Celestial Resilience | Gain temporary hit points after rests and share some with allies. |
| 14 | Searing Vengeance | Once per long rest, instead of a death save, stand up with half hit points and blast nearby enemies. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A support warlock with real healing.
