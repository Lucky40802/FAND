# Grave Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Sadness]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Clerics who watch the line between life and death and enforce it.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Circle of Mortality | Healing spells used on creatures at 0 hit points roll maximum dice; gain the spare the dying cantrip. |
| 1 | Eyes of the Grave | Action: sense undead within 60 ft (limited uses per long rest). |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Path to the Grave | Curse a creature within 30 ft; the next attack against it before the end of your next turn treats it as vulnerable. |
| 6 | Sentinel at Death's Door | Reaction: turn a critical hit against a creature within 30 ft into a normal hit. |
| 8 | Potent Spellcasting | Add Wisdom modifier to cleric cantrip damage. |
| 17 | Keeper of Souls | When an enemy dies within 60 ft, you or an ally there regains hit points equal to the enemy's hit dice (once per turn). |

## Gameplay Identity
- A support cleric that denies the enemy their crits and keeps allies from dying.
