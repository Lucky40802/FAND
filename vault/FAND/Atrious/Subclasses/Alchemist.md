# Alchemist

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Artificers who create potions and reagents through experimental science.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tool Proficiency | Alchemist's supplies. |
| 3 | Experimental Elixir | After a long rest, create elixirs with random beneficial effects (healing, swiftness, resilience, boldness, flight, transformation). |
| 5 | Alchemical Savant | Add Intelligence modifier to acid, fire, necrotic, or poison damage or healing on spells cast through alchemist's supplies. |
| 9 | Restorative Reagents | Elixirs grant temporary hit points; cast lesser restoration for free. |
| 15 | Chemical Mastery | Resist acid and poison damage; cast greater restoration and heal once each without slots. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A support artificer with thrown consumables.
