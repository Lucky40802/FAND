# Way of Ruin

## Overview
Way of Ruin expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Monk class.

## Theme
A monk who breaks things rather than redirects them.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Fracture Strike | Attacks briefly reduce a target's armor class. |
| 6 | Ruinous Palm | A heavy strike that trades accuracy for extra damage. |
| 10 | Shatterpoint | A critical hit briefly stuns the target. |
| 14 | Collapse | A single overwhelming strike against a badly damaged foe. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
