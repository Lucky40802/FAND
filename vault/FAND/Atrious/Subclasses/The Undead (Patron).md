# The Undead (Patron)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A patron that is a powerful undead being.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form of Dread | Transform to gain temporary hit points, fear immunity, and a fear-inflicting hit. |
| 6 | Grave Touched | Change attack damage to necrotic and add a die once per turn. |
| 10 | Necrotic Husk | Necrotic resistance; explode into necrotic energy when you drop to 0. |
| 14 | Spirit Projection | Project your spirit, flying and passing through objects. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A horror-themed warlock.
