# Cavalier

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A fighter who protects allies and controls the battlefield, often on horseback.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Bonus Proficiency | A skill or language. |
| 3 | Born to the Saddle | Advantage on saves against being dismounted; mounting costs little movement. |
| 3 | Unwavering Mark | Marking a creature by hitting it makes it disadvantaged attacking others and lets you punish it when it does. |
| 7 | Warding Maneuver | Reaction: add 1d8 to an ally's AC or saving throw. |
| 10 | Hold the Line | Creatures that move within your reach provoke an opportunity attack; a hit stops their movement. |
| 15 | Ferocious Charger | After moving 10 ft toward a creature and hitting it, it must pass a Strength save or fall prone. |
| 18 | Vigilant Defender | You get a special reaction each turn for opportunity attacks. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A protector and mark-based controller.
