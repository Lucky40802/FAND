# Wild Magic (Sorcerer)

*Official SRD subclass — Sorcerer — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

*Tied to the [[Spin]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Magic drawn from raw chaos rather than any bloodline — unpredictable, occasionally spectacular, and never fully controlled.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wild Magic Surge | After casting a sorcerer spell of 1st level or higher, the DM may have you roll a d20; on a 1, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table for a random magical effect. |
| 1 | Tides of Chaos | Gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw before a long rest; doing so may trigger a Wild Magic Surge at the DM's discretion. |
| 6 | Bend Luck | Reaction: spend 2 sorcery points to add or subtract 1d4 from another creature's attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. |
| 14 | Controlled Chaos | When you roll on the Wild Magic Surge table, roll twice and choose which result to use. |
| 18 | Spell Bombardment | When you roll damage for a spell and roll the maximum on any damage die, roll that die again and add it to the total (once per turn). |

## Gameplay Identity
- The most volatile sorcerer subclass — real risk/reward built into every significant spell cast, with the two capstones giving more control over the chaos.
