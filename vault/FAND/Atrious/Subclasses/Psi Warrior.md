# Psi Warrior

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Fighters who augment strikes with psionic power.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Psionic Power | Psionic energy dice fuel a protective field, a psionic strike, and telekinetic movement. |
| 7 | Telekinetic Adept | Psi-powered leap (brief flight) and telekinetic thrust (knock prone). |
| 10 | Guarded Mind | Resistance to psychic damage; end charm or fright by spending a die. |
| 15 | Bulwark of Force | Grant half cover to allies. |
| 18 | Telekinetic Master | Cast telekinesis and attack while concentrating on it. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A flexible psionic fighter.
