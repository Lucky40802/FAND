# Monster Slayer

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Hunters of supernatural threats.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Hunter's Sense | Learn a creature's immunities, resistances, and vulnerabilities. |
| 3 | Slayer's Prey | Mark a creature to deal extra damage to it once per turn. |
| 7 | Supernatural Defense | Add a bonus to saves and grapple escapes against your prey. |
| 11 | Magic-User's Nemesis | Reaction: foil a creature's spell or teleport. |
| 15 | Slayer's Counter | Reaction: attack your prey when it forces you to save. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An anti-monster, anti-caster ranger.
