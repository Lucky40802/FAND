# Light Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Star]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Clerics of dawn, fire, and truth who wield radiance as a weapon.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonus Cantrip | Gain the light cantrip. |
| 1 | Warding Flare | Reaction: impose disadvantage on an attack against you (limited uses per long rest). |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Radiance of the Dawn | Dispel magical darkness within 30 ft; hostile creatures there take radiant damage (2d10 + cleric level, half on a Constitution save). |
| 6 | Improved Flare | Warding Flare can protect allies within 30 ft. |
| 8 | Potent Spellcasting | Add Wisdom modifier to cleric cantrip damage. |
| 17 | Corona of Light | Action: for 1 minute, emit sunlight (60 ft bright); enemies in it have disadvantage on saves against your fire and radiant spells. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A blaster cleric with protective reactions.
