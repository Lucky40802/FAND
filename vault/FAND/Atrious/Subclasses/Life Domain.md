# Life Domain

*Official SRD subclass — Cleric — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

*Tied to the [[Divine]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
The purest healing-focused cleric — every restoration spell goes further, and the domain's whole identity is keeping a party standing.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonus Proficiency | Proficiency with heavy armor. |
| 1 | Disciple of Life | Whenever a spell of 1st level or higher restores hit points to a creature, that creature regains additional hit points equal to 2 + the spell's level. |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Preserve Life | As an action, restore a number of hit points equal to five times cleric level, divided as chosen among creatures within 30ft, up to half their hit point maximum each. |
| 6 | Blessed Healer | When you cast a spell of 1st level or higher that restores hit points to another creature, you regain hit points equal to 2 + the spell's level. |
| 8 | Divine Strike | Once per turn, a weapon hit deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage (2d8 at 14th level). |
| 17 | Supreme Healing | Instead of rolling for healing dice, use the highest possible number for every die. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The most straightforward support cleric — every healing spell simply does more, with a strong emergency burst-heal at 2nd level.
