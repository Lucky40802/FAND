# School of Conjuration (Wizard)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards who summon creatures and objects.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Conjuration Savant | Copy conjuration spells cheaply. |
| 2 | Minor Conjuration | Create a small nonmagical object for an hour. |
| 6 | Benign Transposition | Teleport or swap places with an ally. |
| 10 | Focused Conjuration | Damage can't break concentration on conjuration spells. |
| 14 | Durable Summons | Summoned creatures gain temporary hit points. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A summoner wizard.
