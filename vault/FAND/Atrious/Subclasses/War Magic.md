# War Magic

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards who blend spells and defense on the battlefield.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Arcane Deflection | Reaction: gain +2 AC or +4 on a save; afterward you can only cast cantrips until your next turn. |
| 2 | Tactical Wit | Add Intelligence modifier to initiative. |
| 6 | Power Surge | Store surge charges and spend one to add force damage to a spell. |
| 10 | Durable Magic | While concentrating, gain +2 AC and saving throws. |
| 14 | Deflecting Shroud | When you use Arcane Deflection, nearby creatures take force damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A durable, aggressive blaster.
