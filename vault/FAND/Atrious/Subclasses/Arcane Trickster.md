# Arcane Trickster

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Rogues who add illusion and enchantment magic to their tricks.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Spellcasting | Third-caster using wizard spells (mostly enchantment and illusion), Intelligence-based. |
| 3 | Mage Hand Legerdemain | Your mage hand is invisible and can pick pockets and use tools at range. |
| 9 | Magical Ambush | If you're hidden when you cast a spell on a creature, it has disadvantage on its save. |
| 13 | Versatile Trickster | Use mage hand to distract a creature, giving you advantage against it. |
| 17 | Spell Thief | Reaction: steal a spell targeting you, negating it and learning it for a while. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A trickster-caster rogue.
