# Scout

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Skirmishers and outdoor experts.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Skirmisher | Reaction: move half your speed when an enemy ends its turn nearby, without provoking. |
| 3 | Survivalist | Expertise in Nature and Survival. |
| 9 | Superior Mobility | Extra walking, climbing, and swimming speed. |
| 13 | Ambush Master | Advantage on initiative; the first target you hit is easier for allies to hit. |
| 17 | Sudden Strike | Bonus-action attack that can use a second Sneak Attack on a different target. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A mobile hit-and-run rogue.
