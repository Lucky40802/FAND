# Oath of Eternity

## Overview
Oath of Eternity expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Paladin class.

## Theme
A paladin sworn to outlast rather than to strike first.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Steadfast Vow | Advantage on saves against being frightened or charmed. |
| 6 | Timeless Guard | Remove one instance of exhaustion per long rest. |
| 10 | Undying Oath | Once per long rest, ignore an instant-death effect. |
| 14 | Eternal Stand | Briefly cannot be reduced below 1 hit point. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
