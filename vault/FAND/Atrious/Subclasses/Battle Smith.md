# Battle Smith

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Artificers who fight alongside a steel defender.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tool Proficiency | Smith's tools. |
| 3 | Battle Ready | Martial weapons; use Intelligence for magic weapon attacks. |
| 3 | Steel Defender | A construct companion that can deflect attacks aimed at nearby allies. |
| 5 | Extra Attack | Attack twice with the Attack action. |
| 9 | Arcane Jolt | When you or your defender hit, add force damage or heal a nearby creature. |
| 15 | Improved Defender | Arcane Jolt improves and the defender is tougher. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A companion-focused artificer.
