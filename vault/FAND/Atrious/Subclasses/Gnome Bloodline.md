# Gnome Bloodline

## Overview
Gnome Bloodline is a Sorcerous Origin for the [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] class, tracing its power to [[Earth]] and the dead earth spirit [[Gnome]].

## Theme
Unlocked by acquisition rather than level, following the [[Class]] system's Primary/Secondary/Tertiary tiers.

## Acquisition Tiers
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Gnome]]'s own remains. Lets the sorcerer sense anything moving through solid ground for a fair distance around them, the earth itself reporting back.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Iron]] or [[Diamond]], Gnome's children, rather than Gnome directly. Hardens the sorcerer's skin partway toward metal or crystal permanently, blunting blows without Earth ever being cast.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Earth]]: ordinary dirt, stone, clay. Raises power only, no unique ability.
