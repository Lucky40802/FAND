# Circle of the Shepherd

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Druids who speak for the wild and lead summoned animals and fey.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Speech of the Woods | Learn Sylvan and speak with beasts. |
| 2 | Spirit Totem | Bonus action: create a 30 ft spirit aura (Bear: temp HP and Strength check advantage; Hawk: reaction to grant advantage on an attack; Unicorn: bonus healing and detection). |
| 6 | Mighty Summoner | Beasts and fey you conjure have extra hit points and magical natural attacks. |
| 10 | Guardian Spirit | Summoned beasts and fey that end their turn in your aura regain hit points. |
| 14 | Faithful Summons | If you drop to 0 hit points, four beasts appear to protect you for an hour (once per long rest). |

## Gameplay Identity
- A summoner druid that makes conjured allies sturdier and more useful.
