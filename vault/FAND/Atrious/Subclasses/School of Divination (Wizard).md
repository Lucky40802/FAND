# School of Divination (Wizard)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards who read the future.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Divination Savant | Copy divination spells cheaply. |
| 2 | Portent | Roll two d20s after each long rest and swap them in for any creature's rolls. |
| 6 | Expert Divination | Casting divination spells regains lower-level slots. |
| 10 | The Third Eye | Choose a sense such as darkvision or seeing invisibility. |
| 14 | Greater Portent | Roll three Portent dice. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The fate-bending wizard.
