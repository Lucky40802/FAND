# Path of the Berserker

*Official SRD subclass — Barbarian — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

## Overview
The simplest, most violent barbarian path — rage turned into more damage and more attacks, at the cost of vulnerability once it ends.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Frenzy | While raging, may frenzy: make a single melee weapon attack as a bonus action each turn; causes one level of exhaustion when the rage ends. |
| 6 | Mindless Rage | Can't be charmed or frightened while raging; if already charmed/frightened when you rage, the effect is suspended for the duration. |
| 10 | Intimidating Presence | Action: frighten one creature within 30ft (Wisdom save) for 1 minute; can attempt again on other creatures on later turns. |
| 14 | Retaliation | Reaction: when hit by a melee attack, make a melee weapon attack against the attacker if within reach. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The purest damage-focused barbarian — Frenzy trades an exhaustion level for real extra offense, with no complexity beyond that trade-off.
