# Path of the Zealot

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A barbarian whose rage is religious fervor, empowered by a god and hard to put down.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Divine Fury | While raging, the first creature you hit each turn takes extra radiant or necrotic damage (1d6 + half your barbarian level). |
| 3 | Warrior of the Gods | A pool of d12s you can spend as a bonus action to heal yourself. |
| 6 | Fanatical Focus | Once per rage, reroll a failed saving throw. |
| 10 | Zealous Presence | Bonus action: up to ten creatures within 60 ft gain advantage on attack rolls and saves until your next turn (once per long rest, or by expending a rage). |
| 14 | Rage Beyond Death | While raging, dropping to 0 hit points does not knock you out; you die only if you still have 0 hit points when the rage ends. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A damage-heavy, hard-to-kill frontliner with strong party buffing.
