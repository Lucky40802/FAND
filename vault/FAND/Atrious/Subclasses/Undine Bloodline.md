# Undine Bloodline

## Overview
Undine Bloodline is a Sorcerous Origin for the [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] class, tracing its power to [[Water]] and the dead water spirit [[Undine]].

## Theme
Unlocked by acquisition rather than level, following the [[Class]] system's Primary/Secondary/Tertiary tiers.

## Acquisition Tiers
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Undine]]'s own remains. Lets the sorcerer breathe and see clearly underwater without needing to cast anything for it.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Ice]] or [[Frost]] rather than Undine directly. Keeps a thin permanent layer of ice across the sorcerer's skin, blunting blows before any Water is even cast.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Water]]: ordinary rain, a filled waterskin, damp cloth. Raises power only, no unique ability.
