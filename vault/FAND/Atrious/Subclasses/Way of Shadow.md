# Way of Shadow

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Monks trained in stealth, darkness, and infiltration.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Shadow Arts | Spend ki to cast darkness, darkvision, pass without trace, or silence; minor illusion for free. |
| 6 | Shadow Step | Bonus action: teleport up to 60 ft between dim or dark spaces, with advantage on your next melee attack. |
| 11 | Cloak of Shadows | Action: become invisible in dim light or darkness until you attack or cast. |
| 17 | Opportunist | Reaction: attack a creature just hit by someone else within 5 ft. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A stealthy assassin-monk.
