# School of Abjuration (Wizard)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards of protective and warding magic.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Abjuration Savant | Copy abjuration spells cheaply. |
| 2 | Arcane Ward | Create a ward that absorbs damage and recharges when you cast abjuration spells. |
| 6 | Projected Ward | Use the ward to protect an ally. |
| 10 | Improved Abjuration | Add proficiency to counterspell and dispel magic checks. |
| 14 | Spell Resistance | Advantage on saves against spells and resistance to spell damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A durable defensive wizard.
