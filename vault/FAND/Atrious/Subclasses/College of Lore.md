# College of Lore

*Official SRD subclass — Bard — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

## Overview
A bard who collects secrets and knowledge rather than fighting spirit — versatile magic and social utility over any single trick.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Bonus Proficiencies | Gain proficiency with three skills of choice. |
| 3 | Cutting Words | Reaction: expend a Bardic Inspiration die to subtract its roll from a creature's attack roll, ability check, or damage roll within 60ft. |
| 6 | Additional Magical Secrets | Learn two spells of choice from any class's spell list; they count as bard spells. |
| 14 | Peerless Skill | When making an ability check, may expend a Bardic Inspiration die and add it to the roll. |

## Gameplay Identity
- The most flexible bard subclass — leans into skills, spell versatility, and shutting down enemy rolls rather than combat presence.
