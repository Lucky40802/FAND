# Chronomancer

## Overview
Chronomancer expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Wizard class.

## Theme
A wizard who nudges moments rather than commanding time outright.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Borrowed Second | Once per rest, take an extra reaction. |
| 6 | Slow Step | Force a target's speed to drop briefly. |
| 10 | Echo Cast | Recast a 2nd-level or lower spell at reduced cost, once per rest. |
| 14 | Fractured Moment | Briefly act twice in one round, though only one turn can include spellcasting. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
