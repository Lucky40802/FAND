# Way of the Astral Self

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Singularity]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Monks who manifest an astral form of their own body.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Arms of the Astral Self | Spend ki to manifest spectral arms: extra reach, Wisdom-based strikes, and Wisdom saves in place of Strength/Dexterity. |
| 6 | Visage of the Astral Self | Spend ki for an astral face granting darkvision, better Intimidation/Insight, and telepathic communication. |
| 11 | Body of the Astral Self | Astral arms and visage improve, granting a bonus to defense and extra damage. |
| 17 | Awakened Astral Self | Spend ki to become a full astral form with extra attacks and stronger defenses. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A reach-and-durability monk built around a temporary astral form.
