# School of Transmutation (Wizard)

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Wizards who change matter and form.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Transmutation Savant | Copy transmutation spells cheaply. |
| 2 | Minor Alchemy | Temporarily change a material into another. |
| 6 | Transmuter's Stone | Craft a stone that grants a chosen benefit. |
| 10 | Shapechanger | Cast polymorph on yourself without a slot once per rest. |
| 14 | Master Transmuter | Destroy the stone for a major effect such as restoring youth or raising the dead. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A utility wizard. Pairs well with this vault's Alchemy-style professions.
