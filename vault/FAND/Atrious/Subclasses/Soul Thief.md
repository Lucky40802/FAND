# Soul Thief

## Overview
Soul Thief expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Rogue class.

## Theme
A rogue who steals more than valuables.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Petty Theft | A successful sneak attack steals a small amount of hit points. |
| 6 | Steal Skill | Briefly copy one minor trait from a creature just defeated. |
| 10 | Hollow Mark | A marked target's saves weaken against the rogue's attacks. |
| 14 | Grand Larceny | Steal a significant amount of hit points or a resource from a single foe. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
