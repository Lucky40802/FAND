# Hunter

*Official SRD subclass — Ranger — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

## Overview
The straightforward combat ranger — a menu of tactical options against different threats (single strong enemies, groups of weak enemies, and everything between).

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Hunter's Prey | Choose one: Colossus Slayer (extra 1d8 damage once per turn to a damaged creature), Giant Killer (reaction attack against a Large+ creature that hits you), or Horde Breaker (once per turn, attack a second creature within 5ft of your first target). |
| 7 | Defensive Tactics | Choose one: Escape the Horde (opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage), Multiattack Defense (+4 AC against a creature's subsequent attacks the same turn), or Steel Will (advantage on saves against being frightened). |
| 11 | Multiattack | Choose one: Volley (make a ranged attack against every creature within 10ft of a point in range) or Whirlwind Attack (make a melee attack against every creature within 5ft). |
| 15 | Superior Hunter's Defense | Choose one: Evasion, Stand Against the Tide (redirect a missed attacker's attack to another creature), or Uncanny Dodge (halve damage from one attack per turn). |

## Gameplay Identity
- Modular martial ranger — each tier is a real choice between single-target, crowd-control, and defensive playstyles.
