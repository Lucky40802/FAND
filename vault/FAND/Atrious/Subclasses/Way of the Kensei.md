# Way of the Kensei

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Monks who master specific weapons.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Path of the Kensei | Chosen kensei weapons count as monk weapons; gain AC and damage tricks with them. |
| 6 | One with the Blade | Kensei weapons count as magical; spend ki for extra damage. |
| 11 | Sharpen the Blade | Spend ki to give a weapon a temporary bonus. |
| 17 | Unerring Accuracy | Reroll a missed monk weapon attack once per turn. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A weapon-focused monk.
