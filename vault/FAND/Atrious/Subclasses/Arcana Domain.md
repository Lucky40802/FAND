# Arcana Domain

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Clerics of a god of magic, blending divine and arcane.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arcane Initiate | Arcana proficiency and two wizard cantrips. |
| 2 | Channel Divinity: Arcane Abjuration | Turn, and later banish, celestials, elementals, fey, and fiends. |
| 6 | Spell Breaker | Healing an ally can also end a spell affecting them. |
| 8 | Potent Spellcasting | Add Wisdom modifier to cantrip damage. |
| 17 | Arcane Mastery | Add one wizard spell of each of 6th through 9th level to your domain spells. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A cleric with a wizard's toolbox.
