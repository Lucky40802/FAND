# Divine Soul

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Sorcery inherited from a celestial parent or ancestor.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Divine Magic | Choose from the cleric spell list as well; gain an extra affinity spell. |
| 1 | Favored by the Gods | Add 2d4 to a failed save or missed attack roll once per rest. |
| 6 | Empowered Healing | Spend a sorcery point to reroll healing dice on nearby spells. |
| 14 | Otherworldly Wings | Bonus action: sprout wings and fly. |
| 18 | Unearthly Recovery | Once per long rest, when below half hit points, regain half your maximum as a bonus action. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A sorcerer that doubles as a healer.
