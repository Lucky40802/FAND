# Celestial Ruin

## Overview
Celestial Ruin expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Sorcerer class.

## Theme
A sorcerer descended from a star that fell rather than one that still burns.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Falling Light | A small burst of radiant damage, once per rest. |
| 6 | Ruin's Mark | A struck target takes extra damage from the sorcerer's next spell. |
| 10 | Starfall | Call down a delayed burst of radiant damage. |
| 14 | Collapse | Briefly ignore resistance to the radiant damage dealt. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
