# Drakewarden

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Fizban's Treasury of Dragons. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Rangers bonded to a young drake companion.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Draconic Gift | Learn thaumaturgy and Draconic. |
| 3 | Drake Companion | Summon a small drake that fights alongside you and adds elemental damage. |
| 7 | Bond of Fang and Scale | The drake grows; ride it and fly; its bite deals extra damage. |
| 11 | Drake's Breath | Exhale or have the drake exhale a cone of elemental damage. |
| 15 | Perfected Bond | The drake becomes Large and can grant resistance with its reaction. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A pet-and-mount ranger.
