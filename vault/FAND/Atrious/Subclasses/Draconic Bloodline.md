# Draconic Bloodline

*Official SRD subclass — Sorcerer — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

## Overview
Magic inherited from a dragon ancestor — extra durability and a growing elemental affinity tied to that dragon's damage type.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dragon Ancestor | Choose a dragon type, which sets your associated damage type (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Acid, or Poison); you can speak, read, and write Draconic. |
| 1 | Draconic Resilience | Extra hit points equal to sorcerer level; AC equals 13 + Dexterity modifier when not wearing armor. |
| 6 | Elemental Affinity | Add Charisma modifier to the damage of one spell that deals your draconic damage type; spend 1 sorcery point for resistance to that damage type for 1 hour. |
| 14 | Dragon Wings | Bonus action: sprout dragon wings, gaining a fly speed equal to current speed (no heavy armor). |
| 18 | Draconic Presence | Spend 5 sorcery points to exude an aura of awe or fear in a 60ft radius for 1 minute; affected creatures make a Wisdom save or are charmed (awe) or frightened (fear). |

## Gameplay Identity
- Durable, elemental-flavored sorcery — extra hit points from level 1 and a growing physical resemblance to the ancestor dragon.
