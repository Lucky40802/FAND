# Oath of the Watchers

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Guardians against extraplanar threats.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Watcher's Will | Advantage on mental saves for you and several allies. |
| 3 | Channel Divinity: Abjure the Extraplanar | Turn aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, and fiends. |
| 7 | Aura of the Sentinel | Bonus to initiative for you and nearby allies. |
| 15 | Vigilant Rebuke | When an ally succeeds on a mental save, deal force damage to the source. |
| 20 | Mortal Bulwark | Truesight, advantage against extraplanar foes, and banish them on a hit. |

## Gameplay Identity
- An anti-caster, anti-outsider paladin.
