# Chronurgy Magic

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Explorer's Guide to Wildemount. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

*Tied to the [[Time]] Rune in this vault's Class acquisition system.*

## Overview
Wizards who manipulate time.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Chronal Shift | Reaction: force a creature to reroll an attack, check, or save (twice per long rest). |
| 2 | Temporal Awareness | Add Intelligence modifier to initiative. |
| 6 | Momentary Stasis | Action: a creature within range that fails a Constitution save is incapacitated with speed 0 until your next turn. |
| 10 | Arcane Abeyance | Store a spell of 4th level or lower in a bead that another creature can trigger. |
| 14 | Convergent Future | Reaction: decide the outcome of an attack, check, or save — at the cost of exhaustion. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A control wizard with reroll and stasis tools.
