# Ignis Bloodline

## Overview
Ignis Bloodline is a Sorcerous Origin for the [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] class, tracing its power to [[Fire]] and the dead fire spirit [[Ignis]] rather than a dragon.

## Theme
Unlike the other 39 subclasses in this vault, Ignis Bloodline isn't unlocked by character level — it's unlocked by what the sorcerer has acquired from [[Ignis]], following the [[Class]] system's Primary/Secondary/Tertiary tiers.

## Acquisition Tiers
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Ignis]]'s own remains. Grants immunity to being burned by any Fire but the sorcerer's own, and lets their own flames burn hotter than their raw strength should allow.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Heat]] or [[Lava]] rather than Ignis directly. Grants a lasting warmth around the sorcerer strong enough to melt frost and ice nearby, no casting required.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Fire]]: ash, a forged blade, ordinary embers. Raises power only, no unique ability.
