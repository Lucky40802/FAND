# Thief

*Official SRD subclass — Rogue — used under CC-BY-4.0 (SRD 5.1/5.2)*

## Overview
The generalist rogue — better at using items, moving through the world, and staying hidden than any other rogue archetype.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Fast Hands | Cunning Action can be used to make a Sleight of Hand check, use thieves' tools to disarm a trap or open a lock, or take the Use an Object action. |
| 3 | Second-Story Work | Climbing no longer costs extra movement; a running long jump covers extra feet equal to Dexterity modifier. |
| 9 | Supreme Sneak | Advantage on Stealth checks if you move no more than half your speed on the same turn. |
| 13 | Use Magic Device | Ignore all class, race, and level requirements for using magic items. |
| 17 | Thief's Reflexes | Take two turns during the first round of combat — one at your normal initiative, one at initiative minus 10 (usable only once per combat). |

## Gameplay Identity
- The most flexible rogue for exploration and item interaction — less about killing, more about doing everything else better than anyone else at the table.
