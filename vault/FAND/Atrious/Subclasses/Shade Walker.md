# Shade Walker

## Overview
Shade Walker expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Rogue class.

## Theme
A rogue who treats shadow as a second set of streets.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Shadow Meld | Advantage on stealth checks in dim light or darkness. |
| 6 | Shortcut Through Shade | Once per rest, treat two connected shadowed areas as adjacent for movement. |
| 10 | Fade | Briefly turn invisible when taking damage. |
| 14 | One With the Dark | Immune to being tracked by non-magical means. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
