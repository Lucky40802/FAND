# Aether Engineer

## Overview
Aether Engineer expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Artificer class.

## Theme
A tinkerer who trades grounded machinery for lift and drift.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Levitation Rig | A short hover, usable once per rest. |
| 6 | Aether Battery | A stored charge that powers one extra infusion use per day. |
| 10 | Skybound Frame | Armor or a construct companion gains sustained flight. |
| 14 | Ascendant Engine | Briefly grants nearby allies a shared hover during combat. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
