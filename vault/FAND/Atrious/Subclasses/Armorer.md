# Armorer

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
Artificers who build a magical suit of armor and fight in it.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Tools of the Trade | Heavy armor proficiency and smith's tools. |
| 3 | Arcane Armor | Turn armor into arcane armor with no Strength requirement that can't be removed against your will. |
| 3 | Armor Model | Choose Guardian (thunder gauntlets, defensive field) or Infiltrator (lightning launcher, speed, stealth). |
| 5 | Extra Attack | Attack twice with the Attack action. |
| 9 | Armor Modifications | Infuse different parts of the armor separately. |
| 15 | Perfected Armor | Guardian: pull creatures in. Infiltrator: bonus lightning damage. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A frontline artificer.
