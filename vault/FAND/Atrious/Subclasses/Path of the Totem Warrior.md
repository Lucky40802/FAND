# Path of the Totem Warrior

*Unofficial summary of an official subclass — Player's Handbook. Paraphrased from memory in my own words; check the book for exact wording and numbers.*

## Overview
A barbarian who channels an animal spirit — bear, eagle, or wolf — into their rage.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Spirit Seeker | Cast beast sense and speak with animals as rituals. |
| 3 | Totem Spirit | Pick a spirit. Bear: resistance to all damage except psychic while raging. Eagle: enemies have disadvantage on opportunity attacks against you and you can Dash as a bonus action while raging. Wolf: allies have advantage on melee attacks against enemies adjacent to you while raging. |
| 6 | Aspect of the Beast | A permanent perk matching the totem (Bear: greater carrying capacity and strength feats; Eagle: keen long-range sight; Wolf: superior tracking and stealthy travel). |
| 10 | Spirit Walker | Cast commune with nature as a ritual. |
| 14 | Totemic Attunement | A stronger rage benefit matching the totem (Bear: enemies near you have disadvantage attacking others; Eagle: gain a flying speed while raging; Wolf: knock a Large-or-smaller creature prone with a melee hit as a bonus action). |

## Gameplay Identity
- A choose-your-role barbarian: tank (Bear), skirmisher (Eagle), or party enabler (Wolf).
