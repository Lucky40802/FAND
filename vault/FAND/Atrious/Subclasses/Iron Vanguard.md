# Iron Vanguard

## Overview
Iron Vanguard expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Fighter class.

## Theme
A heavy-armor fighter built to hold a line rather than break one.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Iron Stance | While stationary, gain damage resistance. |
| 6 | Bulwark Step | Intercept an attack aimed at a nearby ally. |
| 10 | Unyielding | Once per rest, reduce an incoming critical hit to a normal hit. |
| 14 | Immovable Object | Briefly immune to forced movement and being knocked prone. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
