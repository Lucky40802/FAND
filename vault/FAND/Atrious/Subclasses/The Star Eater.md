# The Star Eater

## Overview
The Star Eater expands the fantasy and gameplay style of the Warlock class.

## Theme
A patron whose hunger consumes light itself.

## Level Unlocks
| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Devouring Bolt | Eldritch blast against a badly wounded foe heals the warlock slightly. |
| 6 | Starved Void | Resistance to radiant damage. |
| 10 | Consume | Briefly gain a stolen ability from a significant foe just defeated. |
| 14 | Feast of Stars | A burst that damages foes and heals the warlock proportionally. |

## Gameplay Identity
- A simpler, lighter subclass than the vault's fully custom Classes (no relic acquisition system) — a solid, modest option rather than a flagship one.
