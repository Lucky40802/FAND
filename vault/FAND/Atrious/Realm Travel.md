# Realm Travel

How characters get from the [[Mortal Realm]] to the other realms, what it costs, and what happens to them when they arrive. The higher a realm ranks in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]], the harder it is to reach and the harder it is to survive in.

## Realm Pressure
Every realm pushes back on visitors who don't belong. Each realm has a **Pressure**, and each traveler has an **Attunement**.

| Realm | Pressure |
|---|---|
| Human ([[Mortal Realm]]) | 0 |
| [[Mysterious Realm\|Mysterious]] | 1 |
| [[Hell]] / [[Heaven]] | 2 |
| [[Chaos Realm\|Chaos]] | 3 |
| [[Abyss]] | 4 |
| [[Inner Realm]] | 5 |
| [[Outer Realm]] | 6 |
| [[Void]] | 7 |
| [[Inner Void]] | 8 |
| [[Primordial Realm\|Primordial]] | 9 |

**Attunement** is the highest of these:
- character level ÷ 4, rounded down;
- [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|contract]] rank ÷ 2, rounded down (Minor = 1 up to 1st Finger = 10);
- the Potency of a worn or carried item made with a material from that realm (see [[FAND/Atrious/Material Properties|Material Properties]]);
- the realm's own natives, and anyone invited by a god whose home realm it is, count as fully attuned.

**If Pressure is higher than Attunement**, the difference is the **strain**:

| Strain | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Disadvantage on Constitution saves |
| 2 | Also gain 1 level of exhaustion at the end of each day |
| 3 | Also no benefit from long rests |
| 4+ | 1 level of exhaustion every hour. Leave quickly |

This is why gear made from a realm's own materials matters: a Hell 7 cloak (Potency 2) lets a level-4 character stand in Hell without strain.

## Ways to travel

| Method | Reaches | Requirement | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thin places** (fog, fey rings, old mirrors) | Mysterious | Find one; Survival or Arcana DC 12 | Arrive somewhere random; time may pass differently |
| **Astral crossing** | Mysterious, Hell, Heaven, Outer | An [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Navigation\|Navigation]] check (DC 10 + 3 × Pressure) each day | Failure: lost a day, or arrive at the wrong realm on a natural 1 |
| **A god's invitation** | That god's home realm (see [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster\|God Roster]]) | A contract of Major rank or higher, or a direct summons | None on the way; the god expects something in return |
| **Death** | Hell or Heaven | Dying; returning needs resurrection or a god's favor | Your body stays behind |
| **Gates** | Hell, Heaven | A guarded gate on the realm's border (for Hell, the [[Fiend Realm]]); a pass, a bribe, or a fight | Guards of the realm's grade |
| **Rifts** | Chaos, Abyss | A rift left by a god's death or a failed summoning; it closes in 1d6 days | No control over where you land |
| **The Fall** | Abyss | Travel down through Hell to the Rim | Falling; climbing back out is a quest |
| **Law-veins** | Inner Realm | A place where one Law is unusually strong, and a guide who knows the way (usually a contractor) | Anchor-wardens attack anyone who touches a vein |
| **Beyond the Astral** | Outer Realm | An Astral crossing that keeps going, at least a week | Pressure 6 with no rest stops |
| **Void ritual** | Void, Inner Void | A ritual using a Void-tier material as the key, consumed on use | Returning needs a second key |
| **God-falls** | Primordial | Stand where an original god died, or descend through the deepest [[Spirit Realm]] | A campaign goal, not a trip |

## Getting home
Leaving a realm uses the same methods in reverse. Carrying an **anchor**, something from the realm you want to return to, grants advantage on any check to get back there. Anyone who arrived by rift, the Fall, or death has no easy way back; getting home is its own adventure.

## Travel and crafting
- A **Realm Forge** (see [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]) is always inside or connected to its realm, so Abyss-grade crafting and above means travel.
- Gathering is done in the realm itself; see each realm's Gathering page from the [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]].
- Contractors of Apostle rank and above can use their god's home realm as a Realm Forge.
