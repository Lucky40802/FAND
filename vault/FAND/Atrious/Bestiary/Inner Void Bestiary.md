# Inner Void Bestiary

The Inner Void, near the source of Emptiness and Zero. What lives here is defined by what it lacks.

Realm: [[Inner Void]] (tier 9). A monster's grade is its strength within this realm, from 1 to 10. Its drops use the same scale; see [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]] for how grades turn into Item Level. Stripping a corpse for its drops uses the [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] profession.
