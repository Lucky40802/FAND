# Void Bestiary

The few things that exist in the Void, where nothing has ever lived. Most are Law-shapes: Spin, Time, Gravity, and Balance given a body.

Realm: [[Void]] (tier 8). A monster's grade is its strength within this realm, from 1 to 10. Its drops use the same scale; see [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]] for how grades turn into Item Level. Stripping a corpse for its drops uses the [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] profession.
