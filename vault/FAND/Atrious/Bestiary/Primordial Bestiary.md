# Primordial Bestiary

What moves among the remains of the progenitors and original gods. Most are echoes or guardians, not living things.

Realm: [[Primordial Realm]] (tier 10). A monster's grade is its strength within this realm, from 1 to 10. Its drops use the same scale; see [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]] for how grades turn into Item Level. Stripping a corpse for its drops uses the [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] profession.
