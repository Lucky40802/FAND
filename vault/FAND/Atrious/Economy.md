# Economy

What things cost in FAND: materials, Blueprints, crafting, and services. Prices use the standard coins (1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp). Every material's price is listed on its note in the [[FAND/Atrious/Materials/Materials.base|Materials database]].

## Material prices
A material's price comes from its realm and grade (see [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]]):

> **Price = realm base × grade**, doubled for peak materials (grade 9 and 10).

| Realm | Base (gp) | Grade 1 | Grade 5 | Grade 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human | 5 | 5 | 25 | 100 |
| Mysterious | 50 | 50 | 250 | 1,000 |
| Hell / Heaven | 500 | 500 | 2,500 | 10,000 |
| Chaos | 2,500 | 2,500 | 12,500 | 50,000 |
| Abyss | 10,000 | 10,000 | 50,000 | 200,000 |
| Inner Realm | 50,000 | 50,000 | 250,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Outer Realm | 100,000 | 100,000 | 500,000 | 2,000,000 |
| Void, Inner Void, Primordial | — | not sold | not sold | not sold |

- **Supplies** (oils, inks, wire, tools) cost a fifth of that, minimum 1 gp.
- **Void and above** have no market price. They change hands only between gods, their Fingers, and people desperate enough to trade what gods want.
- Examples: Steel (Human 4) is 20 gp; a Seraph Feather (Heaven 8) is 4,000 gp; Adamantine (Human 10) is 100 gp.

## Buying and selling
| Where | Buys at | Sells at | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary merchant | Half price | Full price | Human materials only; Mysterious in large cities |
| Specialist (a guild or a profession's master) | Half price | Full price | Up to Hell / Heaven materials |
| God's temple | Half price | Full price, minus contract discount | Its own signature materials (see [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster\|God Roster]]); contractors get the rewards in [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts\|God Contracts]] |
| Black market (Fiend Realm, the Rim of the Abyss) | Three-quarters price | Double price | Hell, Abyss, and Chaos materials; no questions asked, no guarantees |
| Other realms | Their own prices | — | Materials native to a realm cost half there, and foreign materials cost double |

Selling in bulk (more than ten of one material) floods the local market: the price drops by 10% for each further ten sold in the same town that month.

## Blueprints
Buying a Blueprint buys the knowledge, not the item (see [[FAND/Atrious/Crafting Rules|Crafting Rules]]).

| Rarity | Blueprint price |
|---|---|
| Common | 25 gp |
| Uncommon | 100 gp |
| Rare | 500 gp |
| Very Rare | 2,500 gp |
| Legendary | 10,000 gp |
| Artifact | Not for sale; found, earned, or reverse-engineered |

## Finished items and crafting
- **An item's value** is twice the cost of its materials, plus the crafting labor.
- **Labor**, when you hire someone: 10 gp × the item's level per day of crafting time. The crafter's profession level must meet the item's level.
- **Custom Blueprint design** (see [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]): a hired designer charges the Blueprint price for the item's rarity, and the design check still has to succeed.
- **Refining** (see [[FAND/Atrious/Material Refining|Material Refining]]): a hired refiner charges 10% of the result's price.
- **Repairing Damaged armor** (see [[FAND/Atrious/Armor Class|Armor Class]]): one unit of the armor's materials plus a day's labor.

## Services
| Service | Cost |
|---|---|
| Guide to a thin place (Mysterious Realm) | 50 gp |
| Astral Navigator for a crossing | 500 gp per day |
| Passage through a guarded gate (Hell / Heaven) | 1,000 gp bribe, or a favor |
| Realm Forge rental | 1% of the item's value per day |
| Realm-sealed storage for foreign materials | 5 gp per material per month |
| Gathering rights on claimed land | 10% of what you gather |

## Contract tithes
Gods expect a share from their contractors (see [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|God Contracts]]):
- **Minor and Normal:** a tenth of income from work done in the god's name.
- **Major and Senior:** a tenth, plus the god's tasks.
- **Apostle and above:** no tithe. The god provides; the contractor provides the god.
