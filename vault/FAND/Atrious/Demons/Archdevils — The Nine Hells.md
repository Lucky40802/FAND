# Archdevils — The Nine Hells

## Vaskarion - Warlord of the Scorched Gate
Rightful ruler of Ashfall Reach, the first layer of Hell, a fallen angel who chose to lead Hell's armies rather than serve in Heaven's.

## Malithrax - Sovereign of the Burning Throne
Supreme overlord of all nine layers of Hell, the being every other archdevil ultimately answers to, and the architect of the entire infernal contract system.

## Cindravox - Master of the Ashen Pact
Ruler of the Frostspire, the eighth layer, a master strategist and dealmaker whose contracts are legendarily airtight.

## Ferrovain - Duke of the Iron Spires
Ruler of Ferrothorne, the second layer, an obsessive, paranoid tyrant who governs a city of iron built in imitation of his own rigid mind.

## Aurivex - Hoarder of the Golden Deep
Ruler of the Sunken Hoard, the third layer, whose greed for wealth and secrets is so total it borders on self-destructive.

## Duskarn - Lord of the Fourth Circle
Co-ruler of the Cinderreach alongside his daughter Phlethis, an old power whose influence has faded as hers has grown.

## Cryovant - the Frozen Prince
Ruler of the Frozen Strait, the fifth layer, frozen in a block of ice by Malithrax himself as punishment, ruling in that state ever since.

## Myrkuth - Lord of the Swarm
Ruler of the Fly-Marsh, the seventh layer, once a beautiful angel before Malithrax's corruption twisted him into a bloated, fly-ridden wretch.

## Sableryn - Princess of the Burning Court
Malithrax's own daughter, who usurped the Bleeding Road, the sixth layer, from Grendrax and now rules it in his place.

## Grendrax - the Twice-Fallen
Former ruler of the Bleeding Road, cast down by Sableryn and reduced to wandering Hell as a lesser devil, damned even by the standards of the damned.

## Trivask - the Wild Beast of the Pit
Former ruler of the Frozen Strait before Cryovant usurped him, now a broken, wandering figure stripped of his old dominion.

## Phlethis - Mistress of the Molten Sea
Co-ruler of the Cinderreach alongside her father Duskarn, whose growing influence has largely eclipsed his.

## Korvenn - Marshal of the Endless War
Not a figure I can verify against published lore of any kind — likely a homebrew or very obscure addition rather than a core member of this hierarchy.
