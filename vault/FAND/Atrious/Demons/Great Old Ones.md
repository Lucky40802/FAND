# Great Old Ones

## Cthulhu - The Dreaming Corruptor
A Lovecraftian entity adapted into D&D's Far Realm lore — a vast, ancient horror that sleeps, and whose mere dreams can drive mortal minds to madness.

## Hastur - The Unspeakable King
A Lovecraftian Great Old One adapted into D&D lore, associated with forbidden knowledge and a name best left unspoken.

## Tharzeth - The Chained Corruptor
An Elder Evil, a god of entropy and madness bound away by the other gods for being too destructive even for them.

## Vermikhan - The Worm That Walks
A demigod of undeath and corruption, whose worship spreads through parasitic worms that reanimate and consume the dead.

## Ghulvath - The Ancient Slime
A deity of oozes, slimes, and things that lurk unseen, worshipped in secret by those drawn to formless corruption.
