# Demon Princes — The Abyss

## Xazareth - Dark Prince of the Shattered Realm
Demon lord of seduction and dark magic, ruler of a triple-layered realm, as cunning as he is cruel.

## Skarrgoth - Herald of the Howling Pack
Demon lord and creator of a savage beastfolk race, worshipped by them as a terrible, ravenous god of slaughter.

## Cerneth - Horned Sovereign of the Maze
Minotaur-like demon lord of beasts and the labyrinth, ancient rival of Skarrgoth for dominance among savage cults.

## Vylumex - the Faceless Ooze
Demon lord of ooze and formlessness, an amorphous horror worshipped mostly by cultists who crave the erasure of the self.

## Velsara - Queen of the Silken Court
Demon queen of lust and seduction, ruler of a realm built entirely around temptation and indulgence.

## Sporemara - Lady of the Rot
Demon lady of fungi and rot, whose realm spreads spores and decay wherever her influence reaches.

## Nyxweaver - Queen of the Black Web
Demon queen of spiders, patron and tyrant of a race of dark elves, who demands worship through betrayal and cruelty.

## Bathyrax - Prince of the Drowned Deep
Demon lord of the deepest, coldest oceans, rival to Nyxweaver for standing among the Abyss's ruling demon lords.

## Vashkiri - Emperor of the Scaled Host
Demon lord claimed as a dark patron by some lizardfolk tribes, embodying their most savage, predatory instincts.

## Krutherak - the Destroyer
Demon lord of ogres and trolls, worshipped as a god of destruction and brute regeneration.

## Thornivax - Prince of the Blighted Root
Demon lord of poisonous and carnivorous plant life, ruling a realm of hungry, toxic overgrowth.

## Emberask - the Burning Terror
A demon lord associated with fire and fear, said to take the shape of a great cat wreathed in flame.
