# Material Grading

Every crafting material in FAND has a **grade**: the **realm** it comes from, then a **number from 1 to 10** for how strong it is within that realm. Examples: `Human 4`, `Hell 7`, `Abyss 10`.

Materials include both **ores** (metal, stone, crystal, wood) and **monster materials** (hide, bone, scale, blood, organs, essences). A Hell 3 ore and a Hell 3 monster fang have the same grade and power. What differs is what they're good for (see Material Types).

Grades drive [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]: the grade of an item's primary material sets its level, rarity, and crafting DC.

## Realm ranking (weakest to strongest)

| Tier | Realm | What it is | Material Rank | Item Level | Rarity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Human** | The [[Mortal Realm]]: ordinary ores, beasts, and plants | 1–10 | 1–5 | Common (1–5), Uncommon (6–10) |
| 2 | **[[Mysterious Realm\|Mysterious]]** | The in-between places: the [[Astral]], the [[Mirror Realm]], fey pockets, and the edges of the [[Spirit Realm]] | 11–20 | 6–10 | Uncommon (1–5), Rare (6–10) |
| 3 | **Hell / Heaven** | [[Hell]] (including the [[Fiend Realm]]) and [[Heaven]]. Same tier, opposite natures | 21–30 | 11–15 | Rare (1–5), Very Rare (6–10) |
| 4 | **[[Chaos Realm\|Chaos]]** | Places where [[Chaos]] has broken reality into constant change | 31–40 | 16–20 | Very Rare |
| 5 | **[[Abyss]]** | The bottomless dark below Hell, where what falls never lands | 41–50 | 21–25 | Very Rare (1–5), Legendary (6–10) |
| 6 | **[[Inner Realm]]** | The core layers every realm rests on, where the Laws are anchored | 51–60 | 26–30 | Legendary |
| 7 | **Outer Realm** | The [[Outer Realm]], past the Astral, where no god has confirmed what's there | 61–70 | 31–35 | Legendary |
| 8 | **Void** | The [[Void]], where Spin, Time, Gravity, and Balance were pulled from. Nothing has ever lived there | 71–80 | 36–40 | Artifact |
| 9 | **Inner Void** | The [[Inner Void]], the deepest Void, near the source of Emptiness and Zero | 81–90 | 41–45 | Artifact |
| 10 | **[[Primordial Realm\|Primordial]]** | Remains of the original gods and dead progenitors, found only in the depths of the [[Spirit Realm]] or on the corpse of a god | 91–100 | 46–50 | Artifact |

Tiers 8–10 (Void, Inner Void, Primordial) are new additions to finish your list above Outer Realm.

**Material Rank** = (tier − 1) × 10 + grade. Hell 7 = 20 + 7 = **Rank 27**.
**Item Level** = Material Rank ÷ 2, rounded up. Hell 7 → **Level 14**.

Item Levels 1–20 cover the same range as the existing [[FAND/Atrious/Blueprints Index|Blueprints]], from Human through Chaos. Anything from the Abyss upward goes beyond mortal gear.

## Grade 1–10 within a realm

| Grade | Meaning | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Common for that realm | Surface ore veins, weak local creatures |
| 4–6 | Uncommon | Deep veins, average monsters, elites |
| 7–9 | Rare | Named monsters, guarded deposits, rare plants |
| 10 | Unique | A realm's boss, a one-of-a-kind deposit, a god's leftovers |

**Grading monster materials:** grade the creature by how strong it is *for its realm*. A hellhound is a common Hell beast (Hell 2–3). A Hell general is Hell 9. The same creature always gives the same grade, but a DM can raise a grade by 1 for an exceptional specimen.

## Material types
The type decides which profession works a material and what traits it can add.

| Type | Examples | Usually worked by | Typical traits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ore / Metal** | Iron, silver, brimstone iron, nullsteel | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Blacksmithing\|Blacksmithing]] | Damage, AC, durability |
| **Wood / Plant** | Oak, wisp-willow, ash of a burnt god-tree | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving\|Carving]], [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)\|Alchemy]] | Reach, range, spell focus |
| **Crystal / Gem** | Quartz, mirror shard, zero-glass | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving\|Carving]] | Spell power, stored charges |
| **Stone** | Granite, anchorstone | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving\|Carving]] | AC, weight, shields |
| **Hide / Cloth / Fiber** | Wolf pelt, fey silk, shadow thread | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Tailoring\|Tailoring]] | Light armor, stealth, resistances |
| **Bone / Fang / Horn / Scale** | Imp horn, deepmaw tooth, drake scale | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Carving\|Carving]] | Crits, piercing, natural toughness |
| **Blood / Organ** | Troll blood, hellhound gland | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Alchemy (Profession)\|Alchemy]] | Regeneration, poison, elemental damage |
| **Essence / Soul / Core** | Elemental core, wraith essence, law-ash | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Enchanting\|Enchanting]] | Elemental damage, magic effects |
| **Mechanism** | Springs, gears, clockwork hearts | [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Engineering\|Engineering]] | Moving parts, repeating effects |

[[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Harvesting|Harvesting]] gathers ores, stone, crystals, and plants; [[FAND/Atrious/Professions/Scavenging|Scavenging]] strips hide, bone, blood, organs, and essences from corpses.
