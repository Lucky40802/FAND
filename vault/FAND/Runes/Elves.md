A [[Race]] of the spiritual bloodline. Elves, together with [[Shaman]], draw more readily than anyone on what remains of the progenitor spirits: [[Ignis]], [[Undine]], [[Gnome]], and [[Sylph]]. An elf casting [[Fire]], [[Water]], [[Earth]], or [[Air]] gets more out of it than a human would from the same Rune.

That closeness goes both ways. Elves feel the [[Spirit Realm]] the way others feel weather, and many live long enough to watch a Law weaken over generations. Some gods court elves for exactly this reason: they notice when something in the Laws changes.

Elves are also in the D&D sense of the word: long-lived, keen-sensed, and at home in the fey pockets of the [[Mysterious Realm]], which some of their oldest lines call home.

**D&D class:** — a Race, not a class. Any class; elves favor Sorcerer bloodlines tied to a progenitor and Druid.
