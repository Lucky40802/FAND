The Law of horns: the body growing its own weapon from bone. A body Law like [[Muscle]] and [[Breath]], it lets anything with a skull push something hard and sharp out of it, and it's why horned creatures heal their horns when nothing else about them heals.

In practice it's a Law of charging and goring. Horn users lower their heads and go through what's in front of them, and their horns can be regrown after being broken.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Barbarian|Barbarian]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Path of the Beast|Path of the Beast]], reflavored so the rage grows horns instead of claws or a tail.

Horn materials in the crafting system (such as imp horn or minotaur horn) are ordinary monster parts; they aren't tied to this Law unless the DM says so. See [[FAND/Atrious/Materials/Horn (Material)|Horn (Material)]].
