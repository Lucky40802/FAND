Sylph is the spirit of Air, one of the progenitors who lived in the [[Spirit Realm]] before her corpse became the [[Air]] Law. The old records call her the only progenitor that never stayed in one place long enough to be drawn: a voice, a pressure, a direction the grass bent.

Sylph left more children than any other progenitor: the four winds [[Boreas]], [[Zephyrus]], [[Notus]], and [[Eurus]], and [[Vortex]], which may be less a child than the shape of her dying. Her children took her death harder than any other progenitor's heirs. Boreas still holds enough power to challenge a god, and Eurus went as far as [[Infinatas]] trying to bring her back.

Those of the spiritual bloodline, [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]] chief among them, draw more readily on what's left of Sylph than most. What remains of her lies in the [[Primordial Realm]] as a wind that never stops.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Sylph Bloodline|Sylph Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], power tied to Sylph directly requires something bound to the spirit itself.
- **Primary:** a piece of Sylph's own remains, such as [[FAND/Atrious/Materials/Sylph Breath|Sylph Breath]]. Its unique ability lets the wielder become weightless at will, drifting on any wind.
- **Secondary:** a relic tied to one of her children. Its unique ability depends on the child: Boreas gives cold, Zephyrus gentleness and speed, Notus storms, Eurus renewal, and Vortex pull.
- **Tertiary:** anything touched by Sylph at one remove: a feather from a high peak, a bell rung only by wind. Raises power only.
