Undead summoning types, roughly ordered from weakest to strongest by how much of the original's power a corpse can hold onto. A fresher, more intact, or more deliberately preserved corpse always keeps more than a bare skeleton does — and the strongest two on this list, [[Lich]] and [[Grim Reaper]], aren't corpse-summons made by someone else at all.
