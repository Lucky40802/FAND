The regions where the Law of [[Chaos]] has won outright and reality never settles. It's tier 4 (**Chaos**) in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]], above [[Hell]] and [[Heaven]].

**D&D class:** — a realm, not a class.

## Overview
Chaos is [[Dark]]'s answer to [[Serenity]]. It's a Law that resists stillness. Where its Rune power pooled after a god's death, it turned a whole region into a place that can't hold its shape. Mountains turn liquid, rivers run upward, and a road is only a road until you stop looking at it. Hell and Heaven are at least *ordered*, even if the order is cruel. The Chaos Realm has no order at all, which is why it ranks above both.

## Regions
- **The Churn:** the outer band, where change is slow enough to survive for a few days.
- **Stormglass flats:** plains of glass fused by storms that change element every hour.
- **The Unmade Core:** the center, where nothing stays the same from one breath to the next. No confirmed visitor has come back.

## Inhabitants
Warpbeasts whose bodies change as they move, chaos-spawn, and the occasional [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Wild Magic (Sorcerer)|Wild Magic]] sorcerer looking for the source of their power.

## Getting there
Wild-magic surges, broken rifts left by the death of a Chaos-aligned god, or simply walking far enough into a region that has started to warp.

## Materials (Chaos 1–10)
Shifting ore, stormglass, warpbeast hide, chaos-spawn eyes. Items made from Chaos materials deal a random damage type each hit (see [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]).

## Dangers
Your equipment, spells, and even your body can change without warning. The DM may roll a wild surge whenever a character rests here.
