Runes are the power of lost/weakened gods. These entity have died leaving behind multiples runes that hold their power. All gods now are mere replicas of pre - existing gods power. Due to this gods are constantly killed and usurped for their power. [[Infinatas]] is one of the last original gods. 

Gods are able to increase their power by either waging war on other gods and stealing their power or making other follow the rules, nature or law of their power to make it stronger, this is usually done through lower or other realms. But when this contact is done many end up killed by the scheming entity looking for their power. Gods are also known to bet on mortal matter such as war and succession war, but they normally conspire to make their side win.

[[Spell]] are things created by deriving a law of the universe. Most spell are concocted by those close to godhood. Due to this people who are close or are in godhood can create spell. The one exception is that of the use of runes of god to temporarily create a gods power to create a spell.

Mortals gain more lasting access to a Law through a [[Class]], built by acquiring something tied to that Law's progenitor rather than casting it on the fly.
