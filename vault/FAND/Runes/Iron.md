Iron is the power of [[Gnome]] first child being strong and versatile in a majority of tasks. While being [[Gnome]] favorite child his power is consider the most potent among [[Gnome]]'s child. It is a subsidy of [[Earth]]

Due to unknown causes he has been stuck in the spirit realm being unable to have an influence in the [[Mortal Realm]]

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Warlock|Warlock]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Iron's Patron|Iron's Patron]].

An Iron class treats [[Iron]] himself, not [[Gnome]], as its progenitor — but since he's still locked in the spirit realm rather than dead, a Primary link is effectively impossible to obtain, which is why no confirmed Iron class exists in the current age.
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Iron]] himself, were he ever freed or killed rather than merely sealed away. Unique ability unknown, since none has ever been acquired.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Steel]], [[Iron]]'s own evolved descendant. Its unique ability lets the wielder reshape a weapon's edge mid-fight, trading it between a duller, tougher form and a sharper, more brittle one at will.
- **Tertiary** — anything else forged from or touched by [[Iron]]: ordinary tools, common ore. Raises power only.
