The collective name for the in-between places that aren't quite the [[Mortal Realm]] and aren't quite anywhere else. It's tier 2 (**Mysterious**) in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

**D&D class:** — a realm, not a class.

## Overview
The Mysterious Realm isn't one place. It's every pocket, fold, and edge where reality goes thin without fully breaking. Scholars group them together because they share three traits:
- time and distance don't behave quite right there;
- things there are half-real;
- no god has ever managed to claim one for long.

## Regions
- **The [[Astral]]:** the thin space between realms that gods and spirits pass through.
- **The [[Mirror Realm]]:** pockets born from [[Reflection]] used too often in one place. They show a version of the Mortal Realm that answers back.
- **Fey pockets:** hidden groves and courts where seasons and moods have more weight than physics.
- **Spirit edges:** the outer rim of the [[Spirit Realm]], where lesser spirits drift. It's far from the progenitor remains in its depths.

## Inhabitants
Wisps, fey, reflections that have gained a will of their own, wandering spirits, and mortals who got lost and stayed.

## Getting there
Mostly by accident: a thin place, a mirror, a fey ring, or a wrong turn in fog. Leaving is harder than arriving.

## Materials (Mysterious 1–10)
Wisp glow-glands, moonsilver, mirror shards, fey silk, astral dust, fey stag antler.

## Dangers
Losing time: a night inside can be a year outside. Your own reflection trying to swap places with you. Fey bargains that are technically fair.

## Adventure hooks
- A village's mirrors have all started showing the same stranger.
- A fey court offers a Mysterious 9 material in exchange for a name.
