Water is the law that is formed from the corpse of [[Undine]] the spirit of Water, part of the power that comes from the [[Spirit Realm]] , the power hold more power to those more attuned to the spirit like those of the spiritual bloodline like [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]].

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Undine Bloodline|Undine Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], a Water class needs something tied to [[Undine]] rather than just casting the Law on the fly.
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Undine]]'s own remains. Its unique ability lets the wielder breathe and see clearly underwater without needing to cast anything for it.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Ice]] or [[Frost]] rather than [[Undine]] directly. Its unique ability keeps a thin permanent layer of ice across the wielder's skin, blunting blows before any Water is even cast.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Water]]: ordinary rain, a filled waterskin, damp cloth. Raises power only.
