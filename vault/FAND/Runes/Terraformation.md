The Law of reshaping land at scale: raising hills, draining swamps, turning desert to field. Where [[Earth]] moves stone and [[Gnome]]'s remains let the wielder pass through it, Terraformation changes what a place *is*. It sits between [[Natural]] and [[Man-Made]]: the result looks natural, but something decided it.

It's slow. A Terraformation user reshapes a field in a day and a valley in a season. Gods prize it because changing land changes who can live there, and who lives there decides who they worship.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Druid|Druid]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Circle of the Land|Circle of the Land]], reflavored to shape the land rather than draw on it.
