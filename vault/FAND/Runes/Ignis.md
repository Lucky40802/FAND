Ignis is the spirit of Fire, one of the progenitors who lived in the [[Spirit Realm]] before its corpse became the [[Fire]] Law. The old records describe it as hungry rather than cruel: it consumed whatever it touched because that was all it knew how to do, and it gave off warmth as freely as it destroyed.

Ignis's death left two children: [[Heat]] and [[Lava]]. Heat is the part of Ignis that spreads without burning. Lava is the part that stayed heavy and slow, fire that forgot how to rise.

Those of the spiritual bloodline, [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]] chief among them, draw more readily on what's left of Ignis than most, as they do with every progenitor spirit. What remains of its body lies in the [[Primordial Realm]], where standing too close still means burning.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Ignis Bloodline|Ignis Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], power tied to Ignis directly (rather than the diluted Fire Law, or Heat and Lava once removed) requires something bound to the spirit itself.
- **Primary:** a piece of Ignis's own remains, such as an [[FAND/Atrious/Materials/Ignis Cinder|Ignis Cinder]]. Its unique ability lets the wielder become living flame for a few seconds: untouchable by anything that can burn.
- **Secondary:** a relic tied to [[Heat]] or [[Lava]]. Its unique ability lets the wielder feel every heat source nearby, including living bodies, through walls.
- **Tertiary:** anything touched by Ignis at one remove: a forge fire, volcanic glass, a burnt god-tree. Raises power only.
