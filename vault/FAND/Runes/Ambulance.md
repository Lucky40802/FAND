The Law of carrying the wounded to safety. It doesn't heal (that's [[FAND/Runes/Heal|Heal]]); it moves. Someone using it can lift a fallen ally and cross a battlefield without being struck, as long as they're carrying someone who can't carry themselves.

Soldiers call it the coward's Law until it saves them.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Monk|Monk]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Way of Mercy|Way of Mercy]], reflavored around rescuing rather than healing.
