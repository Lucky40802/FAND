Gnome is the spirit of Earth, one of the progenitors dwelling in the [[Spirit Realm]] before its corpse became the [[Earth]] Law. Not the small folk of the same name — an entity of stone and root that predates them by an age, said to have been slow-moving, patient to the point of stillness, and utterly without malice even when it finally died.

Gnome fathered two children before its death: [[Iron]] and [[Diamond]], both of whom carry a fraction of its old patience even now. [[Iron]] in particular is said to still be trapped in the [[Spirit Realm]] rather than having died outright — whether that makes Iron more or less powerful than its parent is disputed among those who study the Laws closely.

Those of the spiritual bloodline — [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]] chief among them — draw more readily on what's left of Gnome than most, the same way they do with every progenitor spirit.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Gnome Bloodline|Gnome Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], acquiring power tied to Gnome directly (rather than the diluted Earth Law, or Iron/Diamond once removed) requires something genuinely bound to the spirit itself.
- **Primary** — a piece of Gnome's own remains. Its unique ability lets the wielder pass through solid stone and earth as though it were still water, slowly, without disturbing what's above or below.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Iron]] or [[Diamond]] rather than Gnome directly. Its unique ability grants an unnatural physical resilience — Gnome's patience made permanent, resisting anything that would break a body outright.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by Gnome at one remove: worked stone, a smith's iron, a cut diamond. Raises power only.
