The world mortals are born into, and the weakest realm in the [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]] ranking (tier 1, **Human**). It's also the one every god wants most, because it's where the believers are.

**D&D class:** — a realm, not a class.

## Overview
The Mortal Realm is the middle ground that [[Heaven]] and [[Hell]] mirror each other across. It has almost no Laws of its own. Nearly all of its magic comes from Runes left by dead gods and from contracts and pacts with living ones. That makes mortals weak, but also useful: a god grows by getting others to follow its Law, and the Mortal Realm holds more potential followers than any other realm.

## Regions
- **Settled lands:** kingdoms, cities, and farmland. This is where most worship and most succession wars happen, and gods bet on both.
- **Wilds:** forests, mountains, and seas with ordinary beasts and the occasional stray from a higher realm.
- **Thin places:** spots where the [[Astral]], [[Mirror Realm]], or [[Fiend Realm]] press close. Most mortal contact with other realms starts at one.

## Inhabitants
Humans and the other mortal peoples, beasts, and monsters born here. Also [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|contractors]] of every god, all quietly working against each other.

## Materials (Human 1–10)
Iron, steel, silver, ordinary woods and hides at the low end. Starfall iron and wyvern scale at the top. See [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]].

## Dangers
Being mortal: other gods' contractors, devils offering [[Pacts]], and things from higher realms that slip through thin places.
