Infinatas is one of the last original gods: not a replica built from dead gods' Runes, but a god who was never killed and never usurped. Even so, he wasn't always a god (see [[God]]). What he was before is not recorded.

Because he was never broken apart, Infinatas still holds Laws no one else can: [[Life]] and [[Death]] together, [[FAND/Runes/Resurrection|Resurrection]], [[Cycle of Life]], [[Eternal]], and [[FAND/Runes/Wish|Wish]]. Every other god holds scraps; Infinatas holds pairs. That makes him the most valuable target in existence, and the reason he survives is not known.

He offers no known [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|contracts]] and has no known Hand (see the [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]]). Those who seek him out usually want something only he can give. [[Eurus]] went to him to save her mother [[Sylph]]; whether he answered is disputed.

**D&D class:** none. Infinatas-tier power is reserved and not something a player character starts with.

## Why he matters
- He is proof that a god can survive without stealing. Every other god wants to learn how, or take what he has.
- If he ever dies, the Runes he leaves behind would include Laws no one has held separately before.
- The [[Primordial Realm]] holds the remains of gods like him who didn't survive.
