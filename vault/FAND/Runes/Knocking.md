The Law of knocking: something struck is pushed back, knocked down, or knocked open. It turns a blow into movement, so a hit under this Law sends a body flying and a knock on a door opens it whether it's locked or not.

Guards fear it more for the doors than for the blows.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Fighter|Fighter]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Battle Master|Battle Master]] (pushing and trip attacks).
