Not a Law but a category: Runes that only one god holds, either because that god was never broken apart or because it took every piece of a dead god for itself.

## Held by Infinatas
[[Infinatas]], one of the last original gods, still holds Laws no one else can reach, because they were never pulled from a corpse:
- [[Life]] and [[Death]] together
- [[FAND/Runes/Resurrection|Resurrection]]
- [[Cycle of Life]]
- [[Eternal]]
- [[FAND/Runes/Wish|Wish]]

## Uncontested Runes
In the [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]], most gods share at least one Rune with a rival, and those Runes are fought over. A Rune that only one god holds is safe for now; when that god dies, it goes to whoever reaches the remains first (see [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|God Contracts]]).

**D&D class:** — a category, not a class.
