Air is the law that is formed from the corpse of [[Sylph]] the spirit of Water, part of the power that comes from the [[Spirit Realm]] , the power hold more power to those more attuned to the spirit like those of the spiritual bloodline like [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]].

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Sylph Bloodline|Sylph Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], an Air class needs something tied to [[Sylph]] rather than just casting the Law on the fly.
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Sylph]]'s own remains. Its unique ability lets the wielder fall as slowly as they like, landing from any height without being hurt by it.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to one of [[Sylph]]'s children — [[Boreas]], [[Zephyrus]], [[Notus]], [[Eurus]], or [[Vortex]] itself — rather than [[Sylph]] directly. Its unique ability grants a small taste of that specific child's own power rather than plain wind.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Air]]: ordinary wind, a feather, open sky. Raises power only.
