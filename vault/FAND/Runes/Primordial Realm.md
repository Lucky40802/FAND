Where what's left of the original gods and dead progenitors lies. It's tier 10 (**Primordial**), the strongest in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]]. It's less a place you visit than a depth you reach.

**D&D class:** — a realm, not a class.

## Overview
Every god alive today is a replica built from the power of gods who came before (see [[Runes]]). The Primordial Realm holds the originals: the progenitors whose bodies became the Laws, and the first gods, of whom [[Infinatas]] is one of the last still standing. Most of it lies in the deepest parts of the [[Spirit Realm]], far below the edges that lesser spirits drift through. Fragments of it also turn up wherever an original god died, which is why a god's corpse counts as Primordial ground.

## Regions
- **The Progenitor Remains:** the bodies of beings like [[Ignis]], [[Gnome]], [[Undine]], and [[Sylph]]. They're closer to residue than living presences, but still the purest source of their Laws.
- **The Cage of [[Iron]]:** where Iron is trapped, still alive and not yet a Law. Nobody knows what keeps him there.
- **God-falls:** the places where original gods died. Their remains leave a Primordial scar in whatever realm it happened.

## Inhabitants
Almost nothing lives here. Remains, echoes of the progenitors, and the occasional god or 1st Finger searching for a Primary piece (see [[Class]]).

## Getting there
Through the depths of the Spirit Realm, or by standing where an original god died. Either way, it's a campaign goal, not a trip.

## Materials (Primordial 1–10)
Law-ash (3), progenitor bone (7), heart shard of an original god (10). Any Primordial material that comes from a progenitor also counts as a **Primary** acquisition toward that progenitor's class.

## Dangers
The remains still exert their Laws. Standing near Ignis's remains means burning. Mortals who take a piece often become the next target of every god who wants it.
