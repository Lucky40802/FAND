Fire is the law that is formed from the corpse of [[Ignis]] the spirit of fire, part of the power that comes from the [[Spirit Realm]] , the power hold more power to those more attuned to the spirit like those of the spiritual bloodline like [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]].

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Ignis Bloodline|Ignis Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], a Fire class needs something tied to [[Ignis]] rather than just casting the Law on the fly.
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Ignis]]'s own remains. Its unique ability makes the wielder immune to being burned by any Fire but their own, and lets their own flames burn just as hot even past what their raw strength should allow.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Heat]] or [[Lava]] rather than [[Ignis]] directly. Its unique ability keeps a lasting warmth around the wielder strong enough to melt frost and ice nearby on its own, no casting required.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Fire]]: ash, a forged blade, ordinary embers. Raises power only.
