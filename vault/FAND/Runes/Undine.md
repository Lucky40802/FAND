Undine is the spirit of Water, one of the progenitors dwelling in the [[Spirit Realm]] before its corpse became the [[Water]] Law. Depicted in what little survives of the old records as formless and restless — never the same shape twice, always moving even when nothing around it did.

Undine's death produced two known children: [[Ice]] and [[Frost]], both stiller and more fixed than their parent ever was, as though something about dying settled Undine's restlessness into them permanently.

Those of the spiritual bloodline — [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]] chief among them — draw more readily on what's left of Undine than most, the same way they do with every progenitor spirit.

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Undine Bloodline|Undine Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], acquiring power tied to Undine directly (rather than the diluted Water Law, or Ice/Frost once removed) requires something genuinely bound to the spirit itself.
- **Primary** — a piece of Undine's own remains. Its unique ability lets the wielder's body flow like water for a few seconds at will — enough to slip through a gap no solid body could fit, or shrug off a blow that should have connected.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Ice]] or [[Frost]] rather than Undine directly. Its unique ability keeps the wielder's blood from ever fully freezing or overheating, steady regardless of the surrounding cold or heat.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by Undine at one remove: river ice, morning frost, still water. Raises power only.
