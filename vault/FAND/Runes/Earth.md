Earth  is the law that is formed from the corpse of [[Gnome]] the spirit of Earth, part of the power that comes from the [[Spirit Realm]] , the power hold more power to those more attuned to the spirit like those of the spiritual bloodline like [[Shaman]] and [[Elves]].

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Gnome Bloodline|Gnome Bloodline]].

Like every other [[Class]], an Earth class needs something tied to [[Gnome]] rather than just casting the Law on the fly.
- **Primary** — a piece of [[Gnome]]'s own remains. Its unique ability lets the wielder sense anything moving through solid ground for a fair distance around them, the earth itself reporting back.
- **Secondary** — a relic tied to [[Iron]] or [[Diamond]], [[Gnome]]'s children, rather than [[Gnome]] directly. Its unique ability hardens the wielder's skin partway toward metal or crystal permanently, blunting blows without Earth ever being cast.
- **Tertiary** — anything else touched by [[Earth]]: ordinary dirt, stone, clay. Raises power only.
