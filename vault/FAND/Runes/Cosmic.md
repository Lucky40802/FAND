Cosmic is not a category — it is a single, specific entity, as old as the universe itself and never fully killed the way the elemental spirits were, only ever siphoned from piece by piece. Nothing has ever gotten close enough to take a Primary or Secondary piece of it cleanly, which is why no [[Class]] is built on Cosmic directly; everything anyone actually wields is one of the pieces siphoned off it, each its own separate entity in its own right: [[Plasma]], [[Star]], [[Dimension]], and [[Singularity]].
