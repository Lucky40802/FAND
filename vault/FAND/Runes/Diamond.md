Another child of [[Gnome]] being the strongest and most prestigious  one but was unwanted by [[Gnome]]. Due to this he less common in the [[Earth]] power. So when one uses [[Earth]], they need considerable experience to tap into the connection of [[Diamond]]. The entity behind this power is long gone being broken to pieces by unknown causes

**D&D class:** [[FAND/Atrious/Classes/Warlock|Warlock]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Diamond's Patron|Diamond's Patron]].

A Diamond class treats [[Diamond]] itself, not [[Gnome]], as its progenitor.
- **Primary** — an actual shard of [[Diamond]]'s shattered body. Its unique ability lets the wielder harden any part of themselves to true diamond hardness for a moment, enough to no-sell a single hit rather than just blunt it.
- **Secondary** — something [[Diamond]] wore or shaped before he was broken, rather than a piece of him directly. Its unique ability lets the wielder refract light off their own skin, hard enough to blind at close range.
- **Tertiary** — anything else cut from or shaped like diamond, real or cheap imitation. Raises power only.
