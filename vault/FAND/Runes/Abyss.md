The bottomless dark below [[Hell]], where anything that falls keeps falling. It's tier 5 (**Abyss**) in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]]. It's the first realm above the power range of the ordinary Blueprints, and the realm of the [[FAND/Atrious/Demons/Demon Princes — The Abyss|Demon Princes]].

**D&D class:** — a realm, not a class.

## Overview
Hell is built on rules, contracts, and ranks. The Abyss is what's underneath those rules: a vertical realm with no floor, made of endless layers. Each layer is a realm of its own shaped by whoever rules it. The Demon Princes hold the largest layers and war with each other without end. Their wars are one of the few things Hell's devils and Heaven's gods agree on: better down there than up here.

## Regions
- **The Rim:** where the Abyss meets the bottom of Hell. Devils guard it to keep demons from climbing up.
- **The Layers:** hundreds of stacked realms, one per ruler. Examples include the Shattered Realm (triple-layered, ruled by Xazareth), the Drowned Deep (Bathyrax), and the Silken Court (Velsara).
- **The Fall:** the open dark between layers. You can fall through it for days.

## Inhabitants
Demons of every kind, the [[FAND/Atrious/Demons/Demon Princes — The Abyss|Demon Princes]] and their courts, and the lightless leviathans that swim the Fall.

## Getting there
Down through Hell, or through a rift opened by a demon summoning gone wrong. There's no easy way back up. Climbing is the main danger.

## Materials (Abyss 1–10)
Drowned iron, Abyss pearl, deepmaw tooth, lightless-leviathan scale. From here upward, crafting needs a **Realm Forge** (see [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]). The most famous one is a forge on the Rim that devils and demons both claim.

## Dangers
Falling forever. Demon lords who collect mortals the way gods collect contractors. Darkness that drains light sources.

## Adventure hooks
- A Demon Prince is offering a contract to anyone who can bring back a rival's heart (Abyss 10).
- The Rim forge has changed hands again, and whoever holds it now is taking commissions.
