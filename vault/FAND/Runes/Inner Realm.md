The core layers that every other realm rests on, where the Laws are anchored to reality. It's tier 6 (**Inner Realm**) in [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]], the inward counterpart to the [[Outer Realm]].

**D&D class:** — a realm, not a class.

## Overview
When a god dies and its power breaks into Runes, those Runes still have to hold onto *something*. The Inner Realm is where they hold on. It's a vast structure of anchors, veins, and roots under every realm. The Laws run through it like ore through rock. Damage it, and a Law weakens everywhere at once. Gods know this, so the Inner Realm is heavily guarded and rarely spoken of.

## Regions
- **The Law-veins:** tunnels where raw Law runs visibly through the rock. Each vein carries a single Law.
- **The Anchor Halls:** chambers holding the anchors of the most important Laws, each guarded by an anchor-warden.
- **The Root:** the deepest point, where every vein meets. It's said to sit directly against the [[Void]].

## Inhabitants
Anchor-wardens (ancient constructs whose makers no one remembers), Law-beasts that feed on raw veins, and a few [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|Fingers]] sent by gods to guard, or quietly steal, what's anchored here.

## Getting there
Only through deep places where a Law is unusually strong, or with the help of a god's contractor who already knows the way.

## Materials (Inner Realm 1–10)
Law-vein ore, anchorstone, anchor-warden plate, coreheart crystal. Mining a Law-vein weakens that Law slightly, everywhere. Crafters are expected to know this and do it anyway.

## Dangers
Anchor-wardens attack anyone who touches a vein. Raw Law can overwrite the mind of whoever gets too close.

## Adventure hooks
- A Law is weakening across the Mortal Realm, and someone is mining its vein.
- A god wants a specific anchor moved closer to its own realm.
