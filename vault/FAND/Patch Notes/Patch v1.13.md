# v1.13 — Material Properties and the Undead King

*2026-09-27 · Materials, Crafting, Bestiary*

**Added**
- [[FAND/Atrious/Material Properties|Material Properties]]: every material now has a property it gives to Custom Blueprints. There are 48 properties in all, such as Shapeshifting (mimic parts), Sovereign of the Dead (lich, vampire, and Undead King parts), Eye Ray, Burning, and Piercing Edge. Strength scales with Potency (1–10), which comes from the material's grade.
- Every material's property is shown in the [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]] and on its Bestiary and Gathering tables.
- New Mysterious 9 monster: the Undead King, which drops the Undead King's Heart and Crown.

**Changed**
- [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]: traits now come from material properties. The Cinderfang example was updated, and a new Staff of the Dead Court example was added.
