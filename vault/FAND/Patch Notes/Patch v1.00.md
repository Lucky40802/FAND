# v1.00 — Baseline: everything before the Patch Notes page

*Baseline, backfilled · Subclasses, Professions, Spells, Blueprints, Lore*

### Subclasses
- Added the 13 SRD-licensed subclasses (Champion, College of Lore, Life Domain, Circle of the Land, Path of the Berserker, Way of the Open Hand, Oath of Devotion, Hunter, Thief, Draconic Bloodline, Wild Magic, The Fiend, School of Evocation).
- Added ~46 further subclasses across all 13 classes (later converted to official names, see above). Total: 102 subclass notes.
- Every subclass referenced in the Rune acquisition table now has a real note.

### Professions and crafting
- Added six professions: Cooking, Scavenger, Navigation, Farming, Building, Masonry (31 total). Each cross-links to its near-namesake (Cooking/Spirit Cooking, Navigation/Astral Navigation, Masonry/Stonecutting, Building/Woodworking).
- Added [[FAND/Atrious/Crafting Rules|Crafting Rules]]: Blueprint + materials + profession level, rarity-based DCs, time by tier, reverse-engineering.
- Renamed Runic Craft to Sigilcraft to stop clashing with the Rune system.

### Spells
- 2,332 spells total across 20 schools.
- Original pyramid (Cantrip / Tertiary / Secondary / Primary) rebuilt with varied mechanics: control, buffs, debuffs, utility, forced movement, damage-over-time, and seal inscriptions. Names now match their effects; duplicate descriptions removed.
- Added 300 cantrips, 20 school hub notes, and tier tags for graph coloring.
- Added 350 SRD 5.1 spells and 35 SRD 5.2 spells, remapped into the vault's 20 schools, with CC-BY-4.0 attribution.
- Added 25 original spells inspired by non-SRD supplement mechanics.
- Added [[FAND/Atrious/Spells/Spell Slots|Spell Slots]] (full casters get 3 first-level slots at level 1) and the School of Resonance (Bard).
- Exported the full spell index and the Necromancy school as files.

### Blueprints
- 6,293 blueprints across the professions, in database format (individual notes plus per-profession Bases).
- Added ~2,000 generated items using real materials and 191 historical weapons.
- Weapon damage now scales with level; elemental names match damage types.
- Fixed 438 corrupted or placeholder entries and removed franchise-derived item names.

### Lore and cleanup
- `FAND.html` and vault notes cleansed of trademarked names (archmage-named spells, demon lords, franchise items). Tiamat note is now Vyrekala.
- Added Rune pages for Gnome and Undine, plus Spirit Realm, Shaman, and Master Professions.
- Added Party bios and merged in the School of Resonance from the archive.
- Fixed orphaned notes and broken links across the vault.
