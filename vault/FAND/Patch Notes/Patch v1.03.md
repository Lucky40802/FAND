# v1.03 — 58 official subclasses

*2026-09-26 · Subclasses*

**Added**
- 58 official subclasses as paraphrased summaries, each labeled with its source book. Subclass total: 160.
  - Barbarian: Ancestral Guardian, Beast, Wild Magic, Battlerager, Giant. Bard: Creation, Eloquence, Swords, Spirits.
  - Cleric: Arcana, Forge, Knowledge, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, War, Peace. Druid: Moon, Dreams, Wildfire.
  - Fighter: Arcane Archer, Samurai, Echo Knight, Psi Warrior, Rune Knight, Banneret. Monk: Drunken Master, Kensei, Sun Soul, Long Death, Ascendant Dragon.
  - Paladin: Glory, Watchers, Redemption, Crown, Oathbreaker. Ranger: Horizon Walker, Monster Slayer, Swarmkeeper, Drakewarden.
  - Rogue: Inquisitive, Scout, Swashbuckler, Phantom, Soulknife. Sorcerer: Clockwork Soul, Lunar Sorcery.
  - Warlock: Fathomless, Genie, Undying, Undead (Patron). Wizard: the six remaining PHB schools (suffixed "(Wizard)") and Order of Scribes.

**Changed**
- All 12 affected Class pages list the new subclasses.
- Subclasses that share a theme with an existing Rune (Knowledge, Nature, War, Moon, Dreams, Creation, Spirits, Sun) link to it. They are not added to the Class acquisition table.
