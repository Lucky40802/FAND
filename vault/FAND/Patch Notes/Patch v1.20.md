# v1.20 — Player's Guide, Economy, Profession Progression, Monster Stats, Realm Map, more Blueprints, trademark cleanup

*2026-10-04 · Documentation, Economy, Professions, Bestiary, Realms, Blueprints, Materials, Cleanup, FAND.html*

**Added**
- [[FAND/Atrious/Player's Guide|Player's Guide]]: a plain-language tour of Runes and classes, Armor Class, material grades and properties, getting materials, crafting, contracts, and realm travel, with a quick-reference table.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Economy|Economy]]: material prices (realm base × grade, doubled for grades 9–10; Human 5 gp base up to Outer Realm 100,000 gp; Void and above aren't sold), where to buy and sell, Blueprint prices by rarity, crafting labor, services, and contract tithes.
- Every material note now has a `price` field and a Price line; the Materials database shows price as a column.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Profession Progression|Profession Progression]]: learning a profession, Profession XP from crafting, designing, refining, and gathering; 100 × the next level to level up; teachers and milestones.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Monster Stats|Monster Stats]] and stats for all 103 homebrew monsters in the Bestiaries: Level, HP, Natural AC, attacks, and save DC from their grade, a signature trait from their best drop's property, and boss rules for grade 10. Official monsters convert with Natural AC = 2 × (book AC − 10) + CR.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Realm Map|Realm Map]]: a Mermaid diagram of how every realm connects and the routes between them.
- 101 more Blueprints for the six newer professions: Cooking, Scavenger, Navigation, and Farming +18 each, Building +15, Masonry +14. Each now has 26–31. The Blueprints Index lists 6,470.

**Changed**
- Official-monster sections in every Bestiary explain the AC conversion.
- Hub links: [[FAND]] lists the new pages.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Realm Travel|Realm Travel]] links the Realm Map.

**Fixed**
- Trademark scan across the vault and `FAND.html`. The remaining franchise and WotC-specific names were replaced:

  | Old name | New name |
  |---|---|
  | Tharizdun | Tharzeth |
  | Kyuss | Vermikhan |
  | Ghaunadaur | Ghulvath |
  | Hyrsam | Hyrrel |
  | Prince of Frozen Sighs | The Frostsigh Prince |
  | Elven Blade of Rivendell | Elven Blade of the Hidden Vale |
  | The Master Sword | The Dawnbane Sword |
  | Dragon Slayer (manga-derived description) | Wyrmcleaver Slab, with a new description |

- Removed dangling `FAND.html` links to Demogorgon and Kostchtchie, a "Guts of Berserk" reference, and a Forgotten Realms mention.
- Book titles used as source labels (Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes, Bigby Presents) are kept as attribution.
