# v1.09 — Realm lore pages

*2026-09-26 · Realms*

**Added**
- Realm lore pages for the Material Grading tiers that had none: [[Mysterious Realm]], [[Chaos Realm]], [[Abyss]], [[Inner Realm]], and [[Primordial Realm]]. Each covers overview, regions, inhabitants, how to get there, materials, dangers, and adventure hooks.
- The Abyss page ties in the existing Demon Princes, and the Primordial page ties in the progenitors, Iron's cage, and Infinatas.

**Fixed**
- [[Mortal Realm]] was an empty stub. It now has a full lore page.

**Changed**
- [[Runes]] lists the new realm pages. [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]] links each tier to its realm page.
