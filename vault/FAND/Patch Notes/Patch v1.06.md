# v1.06 — AC on armor and shield Blueprints

*2026-09-26 · Armor Class, Blueprints*

**Added**
- All 837 Armor and Shield Blueprints now have `ac`, `armorType`, and `slot` fields.
  - Body armor: 190 (32 Light, 84 Medium, 74 Heavy).
  - Pieces: 355 (Head, Hands, Feet, Shoulders, Back).
  - Shields: 270 (78 Bucklers, 105 Shields, 87 Tower Shields).
  - Also 8 Enhancements and 14 Barding.
- The 19 profession Bases that include armor show the new fields as columns.

**Changed**
- [[FAND/Atrious/Armor Class|Armor Class]]: AC sources now cover armor pieces by slot, three shield sizes, enhancements, and barding, with a full-kit example.
