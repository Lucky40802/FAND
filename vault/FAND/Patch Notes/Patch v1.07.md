# v1.07 — Material Grading and Custom Blueprints

*2026-09-26 · Materials, Crafting*

**Added**
- [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]]: every material is graded by realm, then 1–10 within that realm. There are 10 realm tiers: Human, Mysterious, Hell/Heaven, Chaos, Abyss, Inner Realm, Outer Realm, and three new top tiers (Void, Inner Void, Primordial). Material Rank (1–100) sets Item Level (1–50). Covers ores and monster materials, with 9 material types and example materials for each realm.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprints|Custom Blueprints]]: players design their own gear in five steps (concept, materials, crafter, design check, stats). The item form decides the crafter, and realm specialists handle Hell, Heaven, and Void metals. Items from the Abyss upward need a Realm Forge. Includes a worked example.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Custom Blueprint Template|Custom Blueprint Template]] and a `Blueprints/Custom/` folder for player-made Blueprints.

**Changed**
- [[FAND]] and [[FAND/Atrious/Crafting Rules|Crafting Rules]] link to the new pages.
