# v1.15 — God Roster and contract rewards

*2026-09-27 · Gods, Contracts*

**Added**
- [[FAND/Atrious/God Roster|God Roster]]: all 70 gods as contract-giving replicas.
  - Each has Runes held, a home realm, what it wants, three signature materials (Normal, Senior, Apostle), a Finger relic, how many Hand seats are filled, and rivals.
  - Gods that hold the same Rune are rivals; 31 Runes are contested, such as Lightning between Zeus, Indra, and Thor, and Sun between five gods.
  - The 8 pantheon leaders have named Fingers: Zeus, Odin, Ra, Brahma, Amaterasu, Quetzalcoatl, Dagda, and Anu.
- [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|God Contracts]]: Rewards section with a reward ladder from Minor to 1st Finger (materials, crafting access, Realm Forge, relics).

**Changed**
- [[FAND]] and the [[FAND/Atrious/Gods Index|Gods Index]] link to the roster.
