# v1.11 — 620 official D&D monsters

*2026-09-27 · Bestiary, Policy*

**Added**
- 620 official D&D monsters in the realm Bestiaries, from the Monster Manual, Volo's Guide to Monsters, Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes, and Fizban's Treasury of Dragons.
  - Each has CR, source, a grade derived from CR, a one-line description in my own words, and graded drops. There are no stat blocks.
  - Placement by realm: beasts, giants, and most monstrosities in Human; dragons, undead, fey, elementals, and Underdark aberrations in Mysterious; devils and yugoloths in Hell; demons in the Abyss; celestials in Heaven; slaadi and githzerai in Chaos; modrons and maruts in the Inner Realm; star spawn in the Outer Realm.
- 890 new graded drop materials. The [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]] now lists 1,558 materials.

**Changed**
- Content policy: official D&D monster names, including trademarked ones such as beholder and mind flayer, are now allowed (user decision). Named unique characters (deities, demon lords, archdevils) keep their renamed versions.
