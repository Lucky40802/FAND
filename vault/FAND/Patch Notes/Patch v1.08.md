# v1.08 — God Contracts

*2026-09-26 · Gods, Contracts*

**Added**
- [[FAND/Atrious/God Contracts|God Contracts]]: ten ranks of contract with a god, weakest to strongest: Minor, Normal, Major, Senior, Apostle, 5th Finger, 4th Finger, 3rd Finger, 2nd Finger, 1st Finger.
  - Each rank has its own holder limit: every god has 12 Apostles and one holder per Finger, and the five Fingers form the god's Hand.
  - Each rank also has its own spell access, Natural AC, duties, and a rule for who it can grant contracts to.
  - Apostles get a Secondary-tier link to the god's Rune and Fingers get a Primary-tier fragment. The 2nd Finger and above can create new spells.
  - Covers rising in rank (Fingers can be taken by defeating the holder), exclusivity from Major upward, penalties for breaking a contract, and what happens to contracts when a god dies.

**Changed**
- [[God]], [[Pacts]], [[Worship]], and [[FAND]] link to the new page.
