# v1.14 — Materials database

*2026-09-27 · Materials*

**Added**
- Materials database: 1,597 material notes in `Atrious/Materials/` plus [[FAND/Atrious/Materials/Materials.base|Materials.base]].
  - Each note has realm, tier, grade, rank, Item Level, rarity, type, property, Potency, source, what drops it, who gathers it, where it's found, and how many Blueprints use it.
  - The Base has views for all materials, monster drops, gathered materials, Blueprint materials, and one view per realm.
- Eight material notes are suffixed "(Material)" to avoid clashing with existing notes: Bronze, Diamond, Horn, Iron, Obsidian, and Steel (Runes), and Crystal Spellshard and Sunstone (Blueprints).

**Changed**
- [[FAND]] and the [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]] link to the database.
