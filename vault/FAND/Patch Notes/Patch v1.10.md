# v1.10 — Graded Blueprint materials; Bestiary and Gathering pages; Material Index

*2026-09-26 · Materials, Bestiary, Blueprints*

**Added**
- Graded all 359 materials used by the existing Blueprints. Every Blueprint that lists materials (5,379) now has `materialGrade` and `materialRank` for its strongest material.
- Bestiary pages for all 11 realms, in `Atrious/Bestiary/`: 102 original monsters, each with a grade, habitat, description, and drops.
- Gathering pages for all 11 realms, in `Atrious/Gathering/`: ores, stone, crystals, plants, and supplies, with where to find them and which profession gathers them.
- 309 new graded materials across those pages.
- [[FAND/Atrious/Material Index|Material Index]]: all 668 materials by realm, with rank, Item Level, type, and source.

**Changed**
- Each realm page links its Bestiary and Gathering page. [[FAND/Atrious/Material Grading|Material Grading]] and [[FAND]] link the Material Index.
