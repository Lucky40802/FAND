# v1.18 — 2024 subclasses, Blueprint properties, Potency tuning, FAND.html sync

*2026-09-27 · Subclasses, Blueprints, Materials, FAND.html, Patch Notes*

**Added**
- 3 new subclasses from the 2024 Player's Handbook: [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Path of the World Tree|Path of the World Tree]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/College of Dance|College of Dance]], [[FAND/Atrious/Subclasses/Circle of the Sea|Circle of the Sea]] (163 total). Each class page now notes which of its subclasses the 2024 book revised; in 2024, every class picks its subclass at level 3.
- Weapon, armor, and shield Blueprints (1,925) carry the property of their strongest material: new `property` and `potency` fields, a Material Property section, and columns in every profession Base.
- `FAND.html` synced: all 1,597 materials, now grouped by realm with grades and properties; a new Bestiary tab with 738 monsters; 124 more subclasses (183 total); FAND roster details on all 70 gods; the 76 new profession Blueprints; and classes filled on 231 matching spells. Gods and other entities now show their descriptions when opened. Backup in the session scratchpad.
- The full old entries were kept in a Patch Notes Archive note (later replaced by the Patch Notes database in v1.19).

**Changed**
- [[FAND/Atrious/Material Properties|Potency]]: peak materials are stronger, with grade 9 at +1 and grade 10 at +2 (maximum 10). The Undead King's Heart is now Potency 3. The materials database, Bestiaries, Gathering pages, and Material Index were regenerated.
- Patch Notes condensed back to one page.
