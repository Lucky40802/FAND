Inane is [[Infinatas]]'s split personality: the same being, turned the other way. Where Infinatas keeps the balance of existence through [[FAND/Runes/Creation|Creation]], Inane keeps it through [[Destruction]].

**D&D class:** none. Inane-tier power is reserved and not something a player character starts with.

## What is known
- Infinatas cares for one thing above all: balance. He keeps it by creating; Inane keeps it by destroying.
- The gods' original world eroded away because of Inane. To the gods it was an unknown entity, and they called it only "Inane".
- After their world was gone, Infinatas allowed the surviving gods into this realm, but only under strict regulation.

## Open questions
- Does Inane ever act on its own, or only when the balance calls for it?
- Do any of the gods know that Inane and Infinatas are one being?
- What would happen to the realm if the gods learned what Inane is?
