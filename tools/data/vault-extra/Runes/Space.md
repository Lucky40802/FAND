Space is the room that everything else sits in: the distance between two things, and the inside of every container. It is held by [[Infinatas]] alone.

**D&D class:** none. Space is not granted through a contract.

## Fragments of Space
- A **Fragment of Space** is a sliver of the Rune: a knot of folded room that fits in a palm. Every one in existence is a gift from Infinatas, usually given to keep the balance somewhere it was slipping.
- Bound into a bag together with a **Fragment of Time**, it makes a **Bag of Holding** (20 extra items). Each further Fragment of Space bound into the bag adds 10 more.
