# Hidden Classes

Besides the basic classes there are **hidden classes**. No one picks one at character creation: they are unlocked only through **quests** and by climbing the [[Towers]] that stand in every realm.

## Tiers
- **Unique**: one of a kind. Only one person in the world can hold a given Unique class at a time; when they die or give it up, it can be claimed again.
- **Epic**: a specialisation of a basic class or subclass. A Rogue's assassin skills, for example, can be honed into a class built around poison.
- **Legendary**: classes that follow the **progenitors** of the Runes, the original holders of the Laws.

## Where they are found
- Epic classes wait about halfway up a Tower; Unique classes three quarters of the way; Legendary classes at the top.
- Some can also be unlocked by quests outside the Towers.

The full list, with each class's requirements, unlock, features, and price, is on the website's Hidden classes page.
