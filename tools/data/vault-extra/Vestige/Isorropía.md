Isorropía is the third personality of [[Infinatas]]. Where Infinatas keeps balance through creation and [[Inane]] keeps it through destruction, Isorropía keeps it by *changing* things: adding to the realm so that it never settles into something that would have to be destroyed.

## Two vessels
- Infinatas and Inane share one vessel. That is why killing Infinatas lets Inane take over: they are the same body turned two ways.
- Isorropía made a vessel of its own and left, to go on its own mission. It walks the realm, makes new things in it, and reshapes what is already there.
- This is how FAND changes: new materials, new creatures, new places, and new rules appear because Isorropía put them there. Each change is meant to keep the balance from tipping.

## The Runes it keeps
- **Balance** belongs to Infinatas. The gods who hold Balance hold a share he allows.
- **Destruction** belongs to Inane. The gods who hold Destruction hold what Inane has not yet taken back.
- **The Runes of creation** (Creation, Origin, Rebirth, and the Original) keep their own holders, but Isorropía holds a fragment of each one. With it, Isorropía can keep those Runes in balance, so that no holder can create without limit.
