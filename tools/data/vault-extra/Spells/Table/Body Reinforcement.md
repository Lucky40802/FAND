# Body Reinforcement

As an action, you harden your muscles and bones. You add +1 to the next Strength-based roll you make before the start of your next turn (a Strength attack, ability check, or saving throw). The strain locks your body afterwards: on your next turn you are petrified, until the end of that turn.
