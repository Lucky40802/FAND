# Toxic

Your voice turns sweet and sickly, and the sound seeps into one creature you can see within range. The target makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, it takes 2d6 poison damage and is poisoned until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is not poisoned.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
