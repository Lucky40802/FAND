# Bohemian Rhapsody

You perform a song in several parts, and the magic shifts with each part. When you cast the spell, and as a bonus action on each of your later turns, choose one movement you haven't used yet in this casting. Once all four are used, the spell ends.

**Ballad.** Up to three creatures of your choice within range regain 3d8 hit points.

**Opera.** Each creature of your choice in a 20-foot-radius sphere within range makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it takes 4d8 psychic damage and can't take reactions until the start of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage.

**Hard Rock.** One creature within range makes a Constitution saving throw. It takes 4d8 thunder damage and is pushed 15 feet away on a failed save, or takes half damage and isn't pushed on a success.

**Coda.** Each creature of your choice within range gains 10 temporary hit points.

**At Higher Levels:** The healing and damage of each movement increase by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
