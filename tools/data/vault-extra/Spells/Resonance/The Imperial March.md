# The Imperial March

You sing a heavy, menacing march that announces your arrival. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, a creature takes 5d10 psychic damage and is frightened of you for the duration. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the fear on a success. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't frightened.

For the duration, your allies within 30 feet of you march in step. They gain 10 feet of speed and can't be frightened.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 5th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
