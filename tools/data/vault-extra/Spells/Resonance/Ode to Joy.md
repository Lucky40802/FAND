# Ode to Joy

You sing a vast, joyful hymn that fills everyone who hears it. Choose up to twelve creatures you can see within range.

Each living target regains all its hit points. Each target is cured of all diseases and of the blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, and stunned conditions. Its exhaustion level is reduced by 1.

Each target that died within the last minute returns to life with all its hit points, as long as its body is mostly whole and it didn't die of old age. A creature returned this way can't be returned by this spell again for 7 days.

This spell has no effect on undead or constructs. After you cast it, you can't cast it again until you finish a long rest.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
