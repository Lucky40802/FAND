# Running Up That Hill

You sing a plea to trade places. Choose two creatures you can see within range, one of which can be you. The two creatures swap positions by teleporting.

An unwilling creature makes a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC. If it succeeds, the spell fails. The swap also fails if either creature can't fit in the other's space.

**At Higher Levels:** The range increases by 30 feet for each slot level above 2nd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
