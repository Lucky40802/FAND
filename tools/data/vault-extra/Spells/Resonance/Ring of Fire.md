# Ring of Fire

You sing a burning chorus, and a ring of fire rises around a point you choose within range. The ring has a radius of up to 20 feet, is 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick. It lasts for the duration.

When the ring appears, each creature in its space makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. It takes 5d8 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

Choose whether the inside or the outside of the ring burns. A creature takes 5d8 fire damage when it ends its turn within 10 feet of that side, or when it enters the ring for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there. The other side deals no damage. The ring blocks sight.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 4th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
