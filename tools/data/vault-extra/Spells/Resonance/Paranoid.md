# Paranoid

You sing a jagged, nervous riff that turns friends into suspects. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within range makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, a creature takes 6d6 psychic damage and becomes paranoid for the duration. A paranoid creature treats every other creature as an enemy. It can't take reactions or benefit from the Help action. On each of its turns, it must use its action to attack or harm the nearest creature it can reach, or move away from everyone if it can't.

A paranoid creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. On a success, a creature takes half damage and isn't paranoid.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 6th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
