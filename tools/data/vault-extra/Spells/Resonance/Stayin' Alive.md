# Stayin' Alive

You sing a steady four-on-the-floor beat to keep a heart going.

The creature that triggered the spell drops to 1 hit point instead of 0. This doesn't work if the damage would kill it outright.

**At Higher Levels:** The target also gains 5 temporary hit points for each slot level above 2nd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
