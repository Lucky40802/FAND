# Lose Yourself

You lose yourself in the music and seize the moment.

Until the spell ends, you are immune to being charmed and frightened. If you are already charmed or frightened, those conditions end.

Once before the spell ends, when you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

**At Higher Levels:** At 4th level or higher, you can use the d20 benefit twice before the spell ends.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
