# The Sound of Silence

You sing one last note, and all sound collapses inward. Choose a 40-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within range.

Each creature of your choice in the sphere makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failed save, it takes 10d8 thunder damage and is deafened and stunned until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is only deafened.

For the duration, no sound can be made or heard inside the sphere. Creatures inside are deafened, and spells with Verbal components can't be cast there, including your own Resonance spells.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 8th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
