# Fly Me to the Moon

One willing creature you can see within range feels light, as if the ground matters less.

Until the spell ends, the target's jump distance is tripled. If it falls, it descends at 60 feet per round and takes no falling damage.

**At Higher Levels:** You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
