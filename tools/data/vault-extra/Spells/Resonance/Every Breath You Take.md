# Every Breath You Take

You attune your hearing to the breathing of one creature you can see within range. An unwilling target makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. On a success, the spell fails.

On a failed save, for the duration you always know the direction and distance to the target as long as it is within 1 mile of you and on the same plane. It can't benefit from being hidden from you, though invisibility still hides it from your sight.

**At Higher Levels:** At 4th level or higher, the duration becomes 8 hours and the range of the tracking becomes 5 miles.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
