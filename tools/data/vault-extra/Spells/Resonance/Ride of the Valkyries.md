# Ride of the Valkyries

You sing a charging war theme, and three spectral riders swoop across the battlefield.

Make a ranged spell attack for each rider. You can aim them at one creature or at several creatures you can see within range. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 force damage.

If a rider's attack reduces a creature to 0 hit points, one ally of your choice within 60 feet of you regains 2d8 hit points.

**At Higher Levels:** One additional rider appears for each slot level above 5th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
