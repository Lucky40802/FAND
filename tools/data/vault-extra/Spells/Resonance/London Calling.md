# London Calling

You sing a short message of up to 25 words toward one creature you are familiar with. The message reaches the target wherever it is on the same plane.

The target hears the message in your voice, recognizes you, and can answer right away in up to 25 words. Its answer reaches you.

If the target is on another plane, there is a 5 percent chance the message doesn't arrive. Creatures with an Intelligence of 1 understand the tone of the message but not the words.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
