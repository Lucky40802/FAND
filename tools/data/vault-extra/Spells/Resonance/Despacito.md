# Despacito

You slow the tempo of one creature you can see within range to a crawl. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, the target's speed is halved. It also takes a -2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws, and it can't take reactions. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

On a success, nothing happens.

**At Higher Levels:** You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
