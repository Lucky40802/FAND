# In the Hall of the Mountain King

You begin a quiet, creeping melody that grows faster and louder each round. Choose a point within range. A 20-foot-radius sphere centered there starts to rumble.

When you cast the spell, and at the start of each of your turns for the duration, each creature of your choice in the sphere makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. It takes thunder damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

The damage is 2d6 the first time and grows by 2d6 each time after that, up to a maximum of 8d6. When the damage reaches 8d6, the spell ends after that round.

**At Higher Levels:** The maximum damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
