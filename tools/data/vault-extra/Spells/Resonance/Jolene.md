# Jolene

You plead with one humanoid you can see within range, and name one other creature you want it to leave alone. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. It has advantage if you or your allies are fighting it.

On a failed save, the target is charmed by you for the duration. While charmed this way, it can't attack, grapple, or target the named creature with harmful spells.

The spell ends early if the named creature or you damage the target. On a success, nothing happens.

**At Higher Levels:** You can target one additional humanoid for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
