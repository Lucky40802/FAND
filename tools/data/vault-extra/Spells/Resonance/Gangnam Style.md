# Gangnam Style

You belt out an infectious beat. Each creature of your choice in a 15-foot cube within range makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, the creature breaks into an awkward dance until the end of its next turn. Its speed is halved, it can't take reactions, and it has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

On a success, nothing happens. Creatures that can't hear you automatically succeed.

**At Higher Levels:** The cube grows by 5 feet on each side for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
