# Hound Dog

A spectral hound bays at one creature you can see within range. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, it takes 1d6 psychic damage. Its speed is also reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn, because it feels hunted. On a success, nothing happens.

The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
