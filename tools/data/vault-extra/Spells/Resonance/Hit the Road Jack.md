# Hit the Road Jack

You tell one creature you can see within range to leave and not come back. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, the target must use its movement on each of its turns to move away from you by the safest route. It can't willingly move within 30 feet of you while the effect lasts. It can still take actions.

The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. On a success, nothing happens. Creatures that can't hear you automatically succeed.

**At Higher Levels:** You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
