# One Love

You sing a song of unity that binds up to six willing creatures you can see within range, one of which can be you.

For the duration, the linked creatures gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws. They can speak to each other telepathically while within 1 mile of each other.

When a linked creature takes damage, that damage is split evenly among all linked creatures within 1 mile, rounded up. A creature that drops to 0 hit points leaves the link.

**At Higher Levels:** You can link two additional creatures for each slot level above 6th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
