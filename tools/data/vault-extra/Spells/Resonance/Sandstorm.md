# Sandstorm

You sing a fast, pounding pulse, and a whirling storm of sand fills a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within range.

The sphere is heavily obscured and is difficult terrain for the duration.

When a creature enters the sphere for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. It takes 2d6 slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
