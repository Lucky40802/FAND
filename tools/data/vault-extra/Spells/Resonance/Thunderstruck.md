# Thunderstruck

You shout a sharp note, and a small bolt of lightning drops onto one creature you can see within range. The target makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, it takes 1d8 lightning damage. If it is wearing metal armor, it also can't take reactions until the start of its next turn. On a success, it takes no damage.

The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
