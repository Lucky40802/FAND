# Purple Rain

You sing a slow, soaring ballad, and a violet rain falls in a 40-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on a point within range.

When the rain begins, each creature of your choice in the area makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. It takes 6d8 radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

At the start of each of your later turns, each creature you chose that is in the area takes 2d8 radiant damage. Each other creature in the area regains 2d8 hit points. The area is lightly obscured for the duration.

**At Higher Levels:** The initial damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 7th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
