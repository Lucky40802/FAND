# Respect

You demand the respect you are owed. One creature you can see within range makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, the target can't attack you or target you with harmful spells. It can still attack other creatures. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns and whenever you or your allies damage it, ending the effect on a success.

On a success, nothing happens. Creatures that can't hear you automatically succeed.

**At Higher Levels:** You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
