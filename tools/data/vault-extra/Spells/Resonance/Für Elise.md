# Für Elise

You dedicate a short, gentle melody to one willing creature you can see within range.

Once before the spell ends, the target can roll a d4 and add it to one ability check it makes. It can roll the die before or after the check. The spell then ends.

A creature can benefit from only one casting of this spell at a time.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
