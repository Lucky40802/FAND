# Hotel California

You sing a hazy, welcoming tune, and an invisible lobby forms in a 20-foot cube within range. Creatures can enter it freely.

A creature inside the cube can't leave it by any means, including teleportation or travel to another plane. When it tries, it makes a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC. On a success, it can leave once. On a failure, it can't leave and can't try again until its next turn.

When the spell is cast, each creature already in the cube makes the same save. Each creature in the cube has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws for the duration. The cube can't be dispelled by Dispel Magic cast at lower than 7th level.

**At Higher Levels:** The cube grows by 10 feet on each side for each slot level above 7th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
