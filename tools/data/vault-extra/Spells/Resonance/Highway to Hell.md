# Highway to Hell

You sing a roaring riff, and a road of fire 60 feet long and 10 feet wide burns along the ground from you in a direction you choose. The road lasts for the duration.

When a creature enters the road for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. It takes 3d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

You and creatures you choose when you cast the spell are immune to the road's fire. While on the road, they also gain 10 feet of speed.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
