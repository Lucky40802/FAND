# Under Pressure

You sing a low, crushing note that makes the air press down. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within range makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, a creature takes 5d8 bludgeoning damage and its speed is halved until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half as much damage and its speed is not reduced.

**At Higher Levels:** The damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 3rd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
