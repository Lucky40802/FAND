# Smooth Criminal

A soft, sly rhythm settles into your movements.

Until the spell ends, you have advantage on the next Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) or Dexterity (Stealth) check you make. The spell then ends.

For the duration, you can also lean forward or backward at a steep angle without falling over, as long as your feet stay on the ground.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
