# Bring Me to Life

You sing a desperate call into a creature that has died within the last minute. The creature returns to life with 1 hit point.

The spell can't return a creature that died of old age. It also can't restore missing body parts.

The returned creature gains one level of exhaustion. Because the spell needs no costly material, this exhaustion can't be removed by any means except a long rest.

**At Higher Levels:** At 6th level or higher, the creature returns with hit points equal to your Bard level instead of 1, and it doesn't gain exhaustion.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
