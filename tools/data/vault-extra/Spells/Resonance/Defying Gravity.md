# Defying Gravity

You sing a rising anthem that pulls free of the ground. Choose up to two willing creatures you can see within range, one of which can be you.

Each target gains a flying speed of 60 feet for the duration. While it has this speed, it can't be knocked prone and can't be forced to fall by effects that pull it toward the ground.

When the spell ends, a target still in the air floats down at 60 feet per round and lands safely.

**At Higher Levels:** You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 4th.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
