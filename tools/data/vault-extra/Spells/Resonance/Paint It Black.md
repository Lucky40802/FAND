# Paint It Black

You drain the color and light out of a 10-foot cube within range.

The cube is filled with magical darkness for the duration. Nonmagical light can't brighten it. Creatures with darkvision can see through it normally.

If the darkness overlaps an area of light created by a spell of 1st level or lower, that light is suppressed while the darkness lasts.

**At Higher Levels:** The cube grows by 5 feet on each side for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
