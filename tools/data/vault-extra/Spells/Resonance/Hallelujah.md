# Hallelujah

You sing a single swelling note of praise toward one creature you can see within range.

The target regains hit points equal to 2d4 + your spellcasting ability modifier. If the target is frightened, that condition also ends.

This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.

**At Higher Levels:** The healing increases by 2d4 for each slot level above 1st.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
