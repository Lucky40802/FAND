# Sweet Dreams

You hum a hypnotic loop. Each creature of your choice in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point within range makes a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC.

On a failed save, a creature falls into a magical sleep and is unconscious for the duration. It wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature uses an action to shake it awake.

On a success, nothing happens. Undead, creatures immune to being charmed, and creatures that can't hear you automatically succeed.

**At Higher Levels:** The radius increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 3rd.

Resonance: needs the Bard's Resonance Phrase (a chant); registers as no school to Detect Magic.
